Spaghetti with Pumpkin and Sage Cream

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

An enveloping autumnal recipe where the pumpkin becomes a silky sauce that hugs the spaghetti. Sage fried in oil provides a persistent scent and a sophisticated aromatic note.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti320 g
  • Mantuan or butternut squash (Mantuan squash is a starchy, sweet Italian variety)500 g
  • Fresh sage6 leaves
  • Red onion1/2 pc
  • Vegetable stock200 ml

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Peel the squash, remove the seeds and cut it into small cubes to speed up the cooking phase.
  2. Finely chop the onion and sauté it in a pan with oil and the whole sage leaves until golden.
  3. Add the squash, season with salt and pepper and cover with the vegetable stock, leaving it to cook for about 15-20 minutes until very tender.
  4. Remove the sage leaves, transfer the squash to a tall container and blend until you obtain a smooth cream.
  5. Cook the spaghetti in plenty of salted water, draining it 'al dente' (about 2 minutes before the time indicated).
  6. Put the pumpkin cream back into the pan and add the spaghetti along with a ladle of starch-rich cooking water.
  7. Toss the pasta over a high heat until the cream thickens and perfectly coats every strand of spaghetti.
  8. Serve immediately, decorating, if you like, with a few fresh or lightly toasted sage leaves.
