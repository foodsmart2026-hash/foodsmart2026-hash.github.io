Mascarpone cream

4 servings · Prep 50 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • cold mascarpone500 g
  • powdered sugar100 g
  • medium eggs3 count
  • vanilla extract1 teaspoon

Allergens

Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Gluten.

Method

  1. Carefully separate the yolks from the whites, using very fresh eggs and keeping the utensils perfectly clean.
  2. For a safer preparation, use pasteurized eggs or correctly pasteurize yolks and whites with a specific and controlled process.
  3. Whip the yolks with the sugar until you obtain a light and fluffy cream.
  4. Add the cold mascarpone a little at a time, mixing gently until obtaining a smooth cream without lumps.
  5. Whip the whites to stiff peaks with perfectly clean whisks, using pasteurized egg whites.
  6. Fold the egg whites into the cream in three batches, using a spatula with movements from bottom to top.
  7. Add the vanilla and transfer the cream to the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before using it.
  8. Use for tiramisu, semifreddos, and spoon desserts.

Chef's tips

Tips, errors, and alternatives:

- Do not use mascarpone that is too warm: the cream could become liquid.

- Do not overwork the mascarpone with the whisks.

- To avoid using raw eggs, you can prepare a version with 250 ml of whipped cream and 250 g of mascarpone, adding 80–100 g of powdered sugar.

- You can flavor it with coffee, cocoa, orange zest, or liqueur.

- Keep the cream in the refrigerator at 4 °C or less and consume it quickly.
