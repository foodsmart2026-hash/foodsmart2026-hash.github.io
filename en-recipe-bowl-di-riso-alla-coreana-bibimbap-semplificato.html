Korean-Style Rice Bowl (Simplified Bibimbap)

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A colourful and balanced rice bowl, ideal for using various seasonal vegetables. The finishing touch of a fried egg makes the dish rich and creamy.

Ingredients

  • Sushi rice or Originario rice (a versatile Italian short-grain rice)300 g
  • Fresh spinach200 g
  • Bean sprouts100 g
  • Carrots2 pcs
  • Eggs4 pcs
  • Soy sauce30 ml
  • Sesame oil (or vegetable oil)20 ml
  • Sesame seedsq.b.

Allergens

Eggs, Soy, Sesame

Method

  1. Cook the rice in boiling salted water following the package instructions, then drain and keep warm.
  2. Blanch the spinach for 2 minutes, squeeze out the excess water well and season with a drop of sesame oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Cut the carrots into matchsticks and sauté them in a pan with a little oil for 4 minutes until they are tender but still bright.
  4. Sauté the bean sprouts in the same pan for 2 minutes with a tablespoon of soy sauce.
  5. In another frying pan, cook the 4 eggs sunny-side up, leaving the yolks very runny.
  6. Assemble the bowls by placing the rice at the base and arranging the spinach, carrots, and sprouts on top in a radial pattern.
  7. Place the egg in the centre, sprinkle with sesame seeds and a drizzle of soy sauce before serving and mixing everything together.
