Cumin-scented Millet and Carrot Bake

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A gluten-free single-course dish using millet, an inexpensive cereal rich in minerals. The sweetness of the carrots pairs perfectly with the exotic aroma of cumin.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🌍 International

Ingredients

  • Hulled millet200 g
  • Carrots400 g
  • Red onion1 pcs
  • Ground cumin5 g
  • Sunflower seeds20 g
  • Vegetable stock500 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Rinse the millet under running water and toast it in a pot with a drizzle of oil for 2 minutes to enhance its nutty flavour.
  2. Add the vegetable stock, cover, and cook by absorption for about 15-20 minutes (without stirring too much).
  3. In the meantime, grate the carrots using the large holes of a grater and sauté them in a pan with the sliced onion and cumin.
  4. Once the millet is cooked and has absorbed all the liquid, fluff it up with a fork and let it cool slightly.
  5. In a bowl, combine the millet with the sautéed carrots and add the sunflower seeds for some crunch.
  6. Transfer the mixture into an oiled baking dish, levelling the surface well with the back of a spoon.
  7. Bake at 190°C for about 25 minutes until the edges begin to pull away from the dish and the surface is golden.
  8. For the best result, turn on the grill for the last 3 minutes to create a tasty crust.
  9. Cut into large cubes and serve warm, accompanied by a fresh salad.
