Baked Ricotta and Apple Pulp Cream

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A very delicate spoon dessert reminiscent of a small cheesecake, but without added sugar and much more economical. The cooked apple acts as a natural sweetener, blending perfectly with the cow's milk ricotta.

Ingredients

  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (a creamy Italian whey cheese)250 g
  • Golden Delicious apples200 g
  • Egg1 pcs
  • Ground cinnamon1 g
  • Butter for the moulds5 g

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Peel the apples, cut them into small cubes, and cook them in a small saucepan with a tablespoon of water until they are very soft.
  2. Mash the apples with a fork or blend them until you have a smooth purée and leave it to cool completely.
  3. In a bowl, sift the ricotta through a fine-mesh sieve to remove any lumps and make it silky.
  4. Combine the apple purée with the ricotta and add the egg, whisking vigorously with a hand whisk.
  5. Add a pinch of cinnamon to fragrance the mixture without using sugar.
  6. Lightly butter four small ovenproof ramekins and pour in the mixture, levelling it well.
  7. Place the ramekins in a high-sided baking tray filled halfway with hot water for a bain-marie cooking in the oven.
  8. Bake at 160°C for about 25-30 minutes: the dessert is ready when the centre is firm but still slightly wobbly.
  9. Remove from the oven, let cool to room temperature, and serve directly in the ramekin or turned out onto a plate.
