Baked Polenta with Mushrooms and Taleggio

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A rustic one-pot dish that uses instant polenta to speed up cooking without sacrificing taste. Taleggio cheese creates an enveloping creaminess.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌾 Gluten-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • instant polenta flour250 g
  • frozen mixed mushrooms450 g
  • Taleggio (a soft, pungent Northern Italian cheese)150 g
  • garlic1 spicchio
  • butter20 g
  • parsleyq.b.

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. In a large pan, sauté the mushrooms from frozen with the crushed garlic clove and oil until all the water has evaporated.
  2. Deglaze the mushrooms with a splash of white wine (optional) or water and cook for a further 10 minutes, then add the parsley.
  3. Prepare the instant polenta by bringing 1 litre of salted water to the boil and pouring in the flour gradually, stirring for 5 minutes or as indicated on the packet.
  4. Spread half of the still-hot polenta on the bottom of a rectangular baking tray, levelling it with a wet spatula.
  5. Distribute the sautéed mushrooms and the Taleggio cut into small cubes over the top.
  6. Cover with the remaining polenta and level the surface again.
  7. Add a few dots of butter on the surface to help with browning.
  8. Bake at 200°C using the grill function for 10 minutes, until a crispy crust forms and the cheese inside starts to ooze from the sides.
  9. Let it rest for 5 minutes before cutting into square slabs.
