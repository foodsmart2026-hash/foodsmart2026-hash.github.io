Vegetable purée

2 servings · 40 min · Easy

Broth and blended vegetables, a weaning staple.

🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

A home-cooking classic, warm and comforting, perfect to enjoy on cooler days. This soup combines carrots, potatoes, and zucchini in a balanced mix, great on its own or with small pasta shapes.

Ingredients

  • Carrot80 g
  • Potato120 g
  • Zucchini100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash all vegetables under running water to remove any soil residue.
  2. Peel the carrot and potato with a vegetable peeler to remove the outer skin, which is less digestible for infants.
  3. Trim the zucchini and cut all vegetables into uniform cubes of about 1.5 cm for even cooking.
  4. Place the vegetables in a stainless steel saucepan and cover with 500 ml of cold low-mineral water.
  5. Bring to a boil, then cover with a lid and simmer over low heat for about 25-30 minutes.
  6. Check for doneness: the vegetables should be very tender when pressed with a fork.
  7. Drain the vegetables, reserving the cooking liquid, and blend them with an immersion blender until a perfectly smooth cream is obtained.
  8. Add small amounts of the cooking broth until the desired consistency for the child's age is reached.
  9. Allow the puree to cool until it reaches a temperature of approximately 37°C (98°F).
  10. Complete each portion by adding one teaspoon (5g) of raw extra virgin olive oil before serving.

Tips

  • Carefully peel the carrots and potatoes to achieve a vibrant color and a fiber-free texture.
  • Keep the heat low and the lid closed during cooking to prevent too much broth from evaporating.
  • Blend the vegetables directly in the pot with an immersion blender until you reach your preferred density.

Variations

  • Add a few fresh basil leaves at the end of cooking to give the dish a Mediterranean aroma.
  • Serve the soup with pan-toasted bread croutons to add a crunchy note.
