Caldo verde

Portugal · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

Portuguese potato and kale soup with slices of chouriço.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes600 g
  • Kale250 g
  • Onion100 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Chouriço120 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Water1300 ml
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Gluten, Mustard, Sulphites.

Method

  1. Sauté the onion and garlic in 30 ml of oil for 4 minutes until softened.
  2. Add the chopped potatoes and water, season with salt and cook for 20 minutes.
  3. Blend the soup until completely smooth.
  4. Shred the kale into very thin strips and cook it in the soup for 5 minutes.
  5. Brown the sliced chouriço in a frying pan with the remaining oil.
  6. Serve the soup topped with the chouriço and a drizzle of oil.
