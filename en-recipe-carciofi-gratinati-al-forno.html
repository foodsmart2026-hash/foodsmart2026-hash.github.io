Baked Gratin Artichokes

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

🌿 Seasonal recipe🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • artichokes8
  • breadcrumbs100 g
  • grated parmesan50 g
  • garlic1 clove
  • extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Parsleyto taste
  • Lemon juice1
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Milk
May contain traces of: Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Clean the artichokes, remove the tough outer leaves and cut them in half.
  2. Remove any internal choke and soak them in water acidified with lemon.
  3. Boil them in boiling salted water for about 8–10 minutes, then drain and let them cool slightly.
  4. Prepare the breading by mixing breadcrumbs, parmesan, minced garlic, and parsley.
  5. Add oil, salt, and pepper and mix until the mixture is slightly moist.
  6. Arrange the artichokes in a greased baking pan, with the cut side facing up.
  7. Distribute the breading on the surface, pressing lightly to make it adhere.
  8. Finish with a drizzle of oil and bake at 200 °C for about 15–20 minutes, until golden brown

Chef's tips

Tips and alternatives:

- Do not overcook the artichokes during boiling.

- For a crunchier crust, use coarse breadcrumbs.

- You can add pecorino, lemon zest, or mint.

- For a lactose-free version, omit the parmesan.
