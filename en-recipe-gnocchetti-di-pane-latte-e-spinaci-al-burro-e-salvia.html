Bread, Milk, and Spinach Gnocchetti with Butter and Sage

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A traditional rural 'recovery' recipe, incredibly soft and easy to chew. These little gnocchi literally melt in your mouth.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Stale bread (crumb only)150 g
  • Whole milk150 ml
  • Cooked and squeezed spinach80 g
  • Type 0 flour (refined white wheat flour)40 g
  • Butter15 g
  • Sage2 leaves

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Break the breadcrumb into pieces and place in a bowl with warm milk for about 15 minutes.
  2. Squeeze the cooked spinach well and chop finely with a knife until it is almost a pulp.
  3. Lightly squeeze the bread to remove excess milk and mix it with the spinach in a bowl.
  4. Add the flour and knead briefly by hand: the mixture should be soft but workable with a spoon.
  5. Bring a pot of water to the boil and, using two teaspoons, form small balls (quenelles) to drop into the water.
  6. The gnocchetti are ready when they float to the surface (this takes about 2-3 minutes).
  7. In a small pan, melt the butter with the sage without letting it sizzle too much.
  8. Drain the gnocchetti with a slotted spoon and gently toss them in the melted butter.
  9. Serve a few gnocchetti at a time, potentially cutting them in half for younger children.
