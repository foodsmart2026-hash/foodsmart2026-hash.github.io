Rice, Milk and Spinach Soup

💰 Save Euro · about €0.55 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A nostalgic dish, delicate and nutritious, which uses milk to make the rice incredibly creamy. Spinach adds iron and colour to this economical and balanced meal.

Ingredients

  • Originario rice (a short-grain rice variety ideal for soups)280 g
  • Whole or semi-skimmed milk500 ml
  • Fresh or frozen spinach300 g
  • Nutmeg1 pinch
  • Water500 ml

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. In a large pot, combine the water and milk, add a pinch of salt and bring almost to the boil.
  2. Pour the rice into the liquid and cook over a gentle heat, stirring occasionally so it doesn't stick to the bottom.
  3. If using fresh spinach, wash and roughly chop it; if using frozen, defrost and squeeze out the excess water.
  4. Halfway through the rice cooking time (after about 8-10 minutes), add the spinach to the pot and mix well.
  5. Continue cooking until the rice is very soft and the liquid has transformed into a thick cream.
  6. Add a generous pinch of grated nutmeg, which will enhance the flavour of the milk and spinach.
  7. Check the seasoning and, if the soup is too thick, add a splash of warm milk at the end.
  8. Serve the soup warm, finishing with a drizzle of olive oil for a touch of shine and flavour.
