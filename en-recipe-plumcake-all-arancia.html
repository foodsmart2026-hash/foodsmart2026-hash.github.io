Orange Loaf Cake

8 servings · Prep 60 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

Great for breakfast, snack time, and for children

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Sugar250 g
  • Eggs3
  • Sunflower oil100 g
  • Natural yogurt125 g
  • All-purpose flour250 g
  • Baking powder1 sachet
  • Orange1
  • Powdered sugarto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Put the sugar, the orange cut into pieces with all the zest, and the eggs into the blender bowl and blend for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the sunflower oil and the yogurt and mix.
  3. Combine the all-purpose flour, baking powder, and salt, then mix for a few minutes.
  4. Grease and flour a loaf pan.
  5. Transfer the batter into the pan and sprinkle with some sugar.
  6. Bake for 35 - 40 minutes at 180 degrees Celsius.
  7. Remove from the oven and let it rest for a few minutes in the pan.
  8. Remove from the pan and serve, dusting with powdered sugar.
