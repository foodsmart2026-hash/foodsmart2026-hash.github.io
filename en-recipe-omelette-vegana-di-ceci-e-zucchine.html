Vegan chickpea and courgette omelette

2 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

An egg-free omelette made with chickpea flour, courgettes and spring onion.

Ingredients

  • Chickpea flour120 g
  • Water200 ml
  • Courgettes200 g
  • Spring onion40 g
  • Turmeric1 g
  • Nutritional yeast flakes5 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Fine salt3 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Mix the chickpea flour, water, turmeric, nutritional yeast and salt; leave to rest for 10 minutes.
  2. Grate the courgettes and slice the spring onion.
  3. Sauté the vegetables in half of the oil for 4 minutes.
  4. Pour the batter over the vegetables in a 24 cm non-stick frying pan.
  5. Cook covered over medium-low heat for 6 minutes, flip using a plate and cook for a further 4 minutes.
  6. Serve hot or warm.
