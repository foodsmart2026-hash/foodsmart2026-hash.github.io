Crispy baked tofu with vegetables

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

Marinated tofu cubes baked with courgettes, peppers and red onion.

Ingredients

  • Firm tofu400 g
  • Courgettes300 g
  • Red pepper200 g
  • Red onion100 g
  • Soy sauce30 ml
  • Cornflour15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Smoked paprika3 g
  • Fine salt3 g

Allergens

Gluten, Soy

Method

  1. Start by taking the 400 g of firm tofu and wrap it carefully in paper towels to remove excess moisture, pressing lightly with your hands. Then cut it into regular cubes of about 2 cm; this step is essential because the less water the tofu contains, the crunchier it will become in the oven.
  2. Transfer the tofu cubes to a bowl and drizzle them with the 30 ml of soy sauce, mixing gently so as not to break them. Let them rest and marinate for 10 minutes: the tofu will act like a sponge, absorbing the saltiness and the brown color of the sauce.
  3. In a food bag or a large bowl, combine the 15 g of cornstarch and the 3 g of smoked paprika. Add the tofu cubes well-drained from the marinade and shake vigorously until each side is perfectly coated by the powder, thus ensuring an aromatic and thick crust.
  4. While the tofu finishes its preparation, focus on the vegetables: cut the 300 g of zucchini into one-centimeter rounds, the 200 g of red bell pepper into strips, and the 100 g of red onion into thin wedges. Try to keep similar sizes among the different vegetables to allow for uniform cooking within the same timeframe.
  5. Collect all the cut vegetables in a bowl and season them with 20 ml of extra virgin olive oil (exactly half of the indicated dose) and the 3 g of fine salt. Mix well with your hands to coat every piece; the salt will help the vegetables release their natural sweetness during the caramelization in the oven.
  6. Line a large baking tray with parchment paper and distribute the tofu cubes and vegetables in a single layer, avoiding overlapping to allow hot air to circulate. Drizzle the surface with the remaining 20 ml of extra virgin olive oil, which will help the tofu brown without drying out too much on the inside.
  7. Bake in a preheated conventional oven at 210 °C and cook for the first 15 minutes. Halfway through, take out the tray and turn the ingredients with a spatula; you will smell the pungent aroma of the onion and the toasted scent of the paprika, a sign that the crust is forming correctly.
  8. Continue cooking for another 10-15 minutes until the tofu is firm and golden on the outside, and the vegetables have slightly charred edges. Remove from the oven as soon as the texture has become pleasantly firm to the bite and serve it hot, perhaps accompanied by grains for a complete meal.
