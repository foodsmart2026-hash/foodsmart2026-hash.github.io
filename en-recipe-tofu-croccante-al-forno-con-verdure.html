Crispy baked tofu with vegetables

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

Marinated tofu cubes baked with courgettes, peppers and red onion.

Ingredients

  • Firm tofu400 g
  • Courgettes300 g
  • Red pepper200 g
  • Red onion100 g
  • Soy sauce30 ml
  • Cornflour15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Smoked paprika3 g
  • Fine salt3 g

Allergens

Gluten, Soy

Method

  1. Pat the tofu dry, cut into cubes and marinate with the soy sauce for 10 minutes.
  2. Toss the tofu in the cornflour and smoked paprika.
  3. Chop the vegetables into pieces and season with half of the oil and the salt.
  4. Arrange the tofu and vegetables on a baking tray lined with baking parchment and drizzle with the remaining oil.
  5. Bake at 210 °C for 25-30 minutes, turning halfway through.
  6. Serve hot, optionally with rice or couscous.
