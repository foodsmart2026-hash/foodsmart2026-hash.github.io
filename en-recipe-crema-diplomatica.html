Diplomat cream

4 servings · Prep 60 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • For the pastry cream
  • whole milk400 ml
  • sugar100 g
  • egg yolks3
  • cornstarch35 g
  • Vanillato taste
  • To finish
  • fresh heavy cream250 ml
  • powdered sugar30 g

Allergens

Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Gluten, Soy.

Method

Preparing the pastry cream

  1. Heat the milk with the vanilla in a small saucepan, without bringing it to a boil.
  2. In a bowl, work the egg yolks with the sugar, then incorporate the sifted cornstarch.
  3. Gradually pour the hot milk over the mixture, stirring constantly.
  4. Transfer back into the saucepan and cook over medium heat until a thick and smooth cream is obtained.
  5. Pour the cream into a shallow container, cover with plastic wrap in direct contact, and cool quickly in the refrigerator.
  6. When the pastry cream is completely cold, whisk it briefly to make it smooth.

To finish

  1. Whip the cold cream with the powdered sugar until a soft but stable consistency is achieved.
  2. Incorporate the cream into the pastry cream in several additions, using a spatula and gentle movements from bottom to top.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- The pastry cream must be completely cold before adding the cream.

- Do not over-whip the cream: it must remain soft and creamy.

- For a firmer cream, slightly increase the proportion of pastry cream.

- For a flavored version you can use coffee, lemon or chocolate.

- Ideal for mille-feuille, cream puffs, cakes and tarts.
