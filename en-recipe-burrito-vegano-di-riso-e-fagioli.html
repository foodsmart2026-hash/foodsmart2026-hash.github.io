Vegan rice and bean burrito

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

Flour tortillas filled with rice, spiced beans, avocado, sweetcorn and tomato salsa.

Ingredients

  • Flour tortillas (lard-free)4 pieces (240 g)
  • Basmati rice150 g
  • Cooked borlotti beans300 g
  • Avocado200 g
  • Sweetcorn150 g
  • Tomatoes200 g
  • Red onion60 g
  • Lime juice20 ml
  • Ground cumin2 g
  • Paprika2 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Mustard, Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Start by rinsing 150g of basmati rice under running water to remove excess starch. Boil it in lightly salted water for about 10-12 minutes until tender yet firm, then drain well and fluff it with a fork to prevent it from becoming sticky.
  2. In a non-stick pan, heat 20ml of extra virgin olive oil over medium heat. Add the 300g of boiled borlotti beans along with 2g of cumin, 2g of paprika, and half of the salt (2.5g), sautéing for about 5 minutes until they release an intense, spicy aroma.
  3. While the beans are sautéing, mash about a third of them with a fork directly in the pan. Stir for another 3-4 minutes until you reach a creamy consistency that will act as a binder for the filling, while keeping some beans whole for texture.
  4. Prepare the fresh salsa by dicing 200g of tomatoes and finely chopping 60g of red onion. Mix them in a bowl with the remaining 2.5g of salt and 20ml of lime juice, letting it marinate for 10 minutes so the acidity softens the onion's bite.
  5. Prepare the other filling components by slicing 200g of avocado into thin strips and thoroughly draining 150g of sweet corn. Having all ingredients ready on your workspace will allow you to assemble the burritos quickly, preventing the warm components from cooling down.
  6. Heat a large pan and lightly toast the 4 wheat tortillas for about 30 seconds per side. They should become warm and flexible to the touch without drying out or becoming crispy, which is crucial to prevent them from breaking during folding.
  7. Place a tortilla on a clean surface and layer a portion of basmati rice and spiced beans in the center. Top with the corn, a few avocado slices, and a generous spoonful of the tomato and onion salsa, leaving about two centimeters clear from the edges.
  8. Fold the two sides of the tortilla inward and roll from the bottom, tucking the filling in tightly. Place the burritos in the pan with the seam side down and heat for 1 minute per side over medium-high heat until they are golden brown and sealed.
