Vegan rice and bean burrito

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

Flour tortillas filled with rice, spiced beans, avocado, sweetcorn and tomato salsa.

Ingredients

  • Flour tortillas (lard-free)4 pieces (240 g)
  • Basmati rice150 g
  • Cooked borlotti beans300 g
  • Avocado200 g
  • Sweetcorn150 g
  • Tomatoes200 g
  • Red onion60 g
  • Lime juice20 ml
  • Ground cumin2 g
  • Paprika2 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Mustard, Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Boil the rice and drain it.
  2. Heat the beans in the oil with the cumin, paprika and half of the salt, mashing some of them.
  3. Prepare a salsa with the diced tomatoes and onion, lime juice and the remaining salt.
  4. Slice the avocado.
  5. Warm the tortillas and fill them with the rice, beans, sweetcorn, avocado and salsa.
  6. Roll them up, tucking in the sides, and heat for 1 minute per side in a pan.
