Penne Rigate with Potatoes, Turmeric and Pancetta

💰 Save Euro · about €0.67 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

An energetic first course playing on yellow tones. The potatoes partially melt to create a thick sauce, while the pancetta adds a bold smoky note.

Ingredients

  • Penne Rigate (ridged penne pasta)320 g
  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes300 g
  • Diced smoked pancetta (Italian cured pork belly)60 g
  • Red onion1 pcs
  • Turmeric powder5 g
  • Fresh parsley1 ciuffo

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into very small cubes (about 1 cm) to ensure they cook quickly and release starch.
  2. In a large non-stick pan, brown the pancetta without adding fats until it becomes crispy.
  3. Remove the pancetta and, in the same rendered fat, sauté the chopped onion and the cubed potatoes.
  4. Add a glass of water and the turmeric, cover, and cook until the potatoes start to break down under the pressure of a fork.
  5. Boil the pasta in salted water, drain when al dente, and transfer it to the pan with the potato dressing.
  6. Add the crispy pancetta set aside earlier and toss everything over a high heat.
  7. If the dressing seems too dry, add a ladle of cooking water to create a bright yellow cream.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the chopped parsley, and serve immediately.
