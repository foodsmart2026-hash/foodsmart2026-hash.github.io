Potato, Mackerel and Onion Bake

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 60 min · Hard

Canned mackerel is an excellent source of Omega-3 and costs less than tuna. In this rustic bake, it pairs perfectly with potatoes to create a hearty dish.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Floury potatoes800 g
  • Canned grilled mackerel (drained)250 g
  • Golden onions2 pcs
  • Breadcrumbs40 g
  • Eggs1 pcs
  • Parsley1 mazzetto

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Fish

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes whole with their skins on for about 30-40 minutes; the skin protects the starch and makes the final mash less watery.
  2. While the potatoes are cooking, slice the onions and stew them in a pan with oil and salt until very soft and slightly caramelised.
  3. Peel the potatoes while still hot, mash them with a potato masher into a bowl, and let them cool slightly.
  4. Add the egg, chopped parsley, flaked mackerel, and stewed onions to the mash, stirring to combine everything.
  5. Grease a circular baking tin and dust it with half of the breadcrumbs, then pour in the mixture, levelling it with the back of a spoon.
  6. Sprinkle with the remaining breadcrumbs and a drizzle of oil, which will help form a golden crust during cooking.
  7. Bake at 200°C for about 20 minutes, until the edges appear pulled away from the tin and the surface is well toasted.
  8. Leave the bake to cool for 10 minutes before serving: resting allows the flavours to stabilise and the slice to remain firm.
