Braised cavolo nero with orange, ginger and almonds

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

An aromatic and healthy side dish that balances the earthy notes of cavolo nero with the freshness of orange and a spicy touch of ginger.

Ingredients

  • Cavolo nero600 g
  • Orange (juice and zest)1
  • Freshly grated ginger15 g
  • Flaked almonds40 g
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Extra virgin olive oil3 tbsp
  • Salt and chilli flakesto taste

Allergens

Tree nuts

Method

  1. Prep the cavolo nero by removing the central rib and cutting the leaves into pieces of about 4 cm.
  2. Wash the leaves well and leave them slightly damp.
  3. In a large frying pan, heat the oil with the unpeeled (smashed) garlic clove and a pinch of chilli flakes.
  4. Add the cavolo nero to the pan, season with salt and cover immediately with a lid.
  5. Leave to braise over a medium heat for about 8-10 minutes, using the residual washing water to create steam.
  6. Remove the garlic and add the grated ginger and orange zest.
  7. Pour in the orange juice and continue cooking uncovered for another 5 minutes, until the liquid has almost entirely evaporated.
  8. In a separate small pan, toast the flaked almonds for a couple of minutes until golden and fragrant.
  9. Remove the cavolo nero from the heat and transfer it to a serving dish.
  10. Garnish with the toasted almonds and serve as an accompaniment to meat or legume dishes.
