Small Pasta Shells with Red Lentils and Mantuan Squash

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

An enveloping cream that hides split lentils, perfect for children's digestion. The squash provides a natural sweetness that makes the dish very popular.

Ingredients

  • Small pasta shells (conchigliette)120 g
  • Split red lentils80 g
  • Cleaned squash (Mantuan squash is a sweet, firm Italian variety)200 g
  • Small golden onion1/2 pc
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Rinse the red lentils well under running water to remove any impurities and excess starch.
  2. Cut the squash into small cubes and finely chop the onion to make it almost unnoticeable after cooking.
  3. In a pot, place the squash, onion, and lentils, covering with about 500ml of cold water.
  4. Bring to the boil and simmer over low heat for 20 minutes: the lentils and squash should almost fall apart.
  5. Blend the mixture with a hand blender or mash it well with a fork to obtain a smooth, thick cream.
  6. Cook the small shells directly in the cream, adding a little hot water if necessary, stirring often so it doesn't stick to the bottom.
  7. The pasta is ready when it is very tender (slightly beyond the classic 'al dente' for adults).
  8. Turn off the heat and stir in the raw extra virgin olive oil to preserve its nutritional properties.
  9. Check the temperature before serving; the dish should have a velvety consistency that helps with swallowing.
