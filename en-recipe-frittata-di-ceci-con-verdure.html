Chickpea Frittata with Vegetables

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A plant-based take on the classic frittata, prepared with legume flour for a balanced and protein-rich meal. This recipe is ideal for an economical family dinner, using humble but nutrient-rich ingredients typical of the Mediterranean diet.

Ingredients

  • Chickpea flour (Gram flour)250 g
  • Still water600 ml
  • Green courgettes400 g
  • Tropea red onion (sweet Italian red onion)100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Fine salt6 g
  • Ground black pepper2 g
  • Fresh or dried marjoram2 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. In a large bowl, sift the chickpea flour to avoid lumps and gradually pour in the water, whisking vigorously with a hand whisk until you get a smooth, liquid batter.
  2. Let the batter rest for at least 40-60 minutes at room temperature; this rest is essential for the flour to absorb the water, improving the final texture and digestibility.
  3. Wash the courgettes, trim them, and cut them into small cubes of about half a centimetre; peel the onion and slice it thinly to ensure even cooking.
  4. In a large non-stick pan, heat 10 ml of extra virgin oil and sauté the onion over a low heat for 5 minutes until it is transparent and sweet.
  5. Add the courgettes to the onion, salt lightly, and cook over a high flame for 7-8 minutes: they should soften and brown slightly without losing their crunch.
  6. Remove any foam that has formed on the surface of the batter with a spoon, add the salt, pepper, marjoram, and the sautéed vegetables, mixing everything well.
  7. Grease a round baking tin (24-26 cm diameter) with the remaining oil, pour in the mixture, and level it with a spatula so the thickness is even throughout.
  8. Bake in a preheated static oven at 200°C for about 25-30 minutes: the frittata is ready when the surface appears golden, small cracks form, and the edges pull away from the tin.
