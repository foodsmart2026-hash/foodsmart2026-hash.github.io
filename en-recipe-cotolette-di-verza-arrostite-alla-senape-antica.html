Roasted Savoy Cabbage Steaks with Wholegrain Mustard

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

Thick slices of Savoy cabbage roasted in the oven until tender and caramelised, served with a savoury mustard glaze.

Ingredients

  • Whole, firm Savoy cabbage1 (approx. 800 g)
  • Wholegrain mustard3 tbsp
  • Acacia honey1 tbsp
  • Extra virgin olive oil4 tbsp
  • Garlic powder1 tsp
  • Fresh thyme2 sprigs
  • Salt and pepperto taste
  • Breadcrumbs (optional)2 tbsp

Allergens

Mustard
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan).
  2. Remove only the damaged outer leaves of the cabbage and wash the whole head.
  3. Cut the cabbage vertically into slices about 2.5 cm thick, ensuring the core keeps the leaves together in each 'steak'.
  4. Arrange the slices on a baking tray lined with baking parchment, making sure they do not overlap.
  5. In a bowl, prepare the glaze by mixing the oil, mustard, honey, garlic powder, and thyme leaves.
  6. Generously brush the tops of the cabbage steaks with half of the glaze, then season with salt and pepper.
  7. Bake for 15 minutes, then remove the tray, carefully flip the slices with a spatula, and brush the other side with the remaining glaze.
  8. Finish cooking for another 10 minutes until the edges are brown and crispy and the heart of the cabbage is tender.
