Braised Savoy Cabbage with Vinegar and Fennel Seeds

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A humble winter side dish that is rich in flavour thanks to the acidity of the vinegar which balances the sweetness of the cabbage. Fennel seeds provide a fresh aroma and aid digestion.

Ingredients

  • Savoy cabbage800 g
  • Golden onion1 pcs
  • White wine vinegar30 ml
  • Fennel seeds5 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Remove the toughest outer leaves of the cabbage, cut it in half, and remove the hard central core to ensure even cooking.
  2. Slice the cabbage into thin strips about 5 mm wide and rinse them under running water to remove any traces of soil.
  3. Prep the onion and slice it finely; heat the oil in a large pan and let it soften over low heat for about 5 minutes until it becomes translucent.
  4. Add the cabbage to the pan, slightly increase the heat, and sauté for 3-4 minutes, stirring often so it picks up the flavours evenly.
  5. Deglaze with the white wine vinegar, let the alcohol evaporate for a minute, then add a pinch of salt and the fennel seeds.
  6. Cover the pan with a lid and cook over low heat for about 20 minutes; the cabbage should be tender but not mushy.
  7. If the bottom gets too dry during cooking, add a couple of tablespoons of warm water to maintain the necessary moisture.
  8. Check the cooking: the cabbage is ready when it changes colour, becoming darker and losing volume while still maintaining a slight bite.
