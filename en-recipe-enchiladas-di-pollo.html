Chicken Enchiladas

Mexico · 4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Tortillas filled with shredded chicken, covered in red sauce and cheese, then baked until golden.

Ingredients

  • Corn tortillas8 pieces (240 g)
  • Chicken breast500 g
  • Tomato passata400 ml
  • Onion100 g
  • Chipotle chilli powder2 g
  • Ground cumin2 g
  • Grated cheddar cheese120 g
  • Sour cream100 g
  • Vegetable oil20 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Gluten, Mustard.

Method

  1. Cook the 500g of chicken breast in boiling salted water (using a portion of the total 6g of salt) for about 20 minutes over medium heat. You will know it is ready when the meat is tender to the touch and easily separates with a fork; boiling the whole breast keeps the fibers juicy inside.
  2. Drain the chicken and, while still warm, shred it finely using two forks or your fingers. Aim for thin, uniform strands, as a larger surface area will allow the meat to better absorb the seasonings in the subsequent steps.
  3. Finely chop the 100g of onion and let it soften in a large pan with 20ml of vegetable oil for about 5 minutes over low heat. The onion should become translucent and release a sweet aroma without browning, creating a delicate aromatic base for the sauce.
  4. Add the 400ml of tomato passata, 2g of chipotle powder, 2g of cumin, and the remaining salt to the onion, cooking for 10 minutes over medium heat. The sauce will be ready when it has thickened slightly, taking on a deep red color and a rich, spicy aroma.
  5. Take about one-third of the freshly prepared sauce and mix it thoroughly with the shredded chicken in a large bowl. This step is essential to ensure that the filling of your enchiladas remains moist and flavorful even after baking.
  6. Briefly warm the 8 corn tortillas in a non-stick pan for about 15-20 seconds per side until they become very flexible. Fill the center of each tortilla with the chicken mixture, roll them tightly, and place them in a baking dish with the seam facing down to prevent them from opening.
  7. Evenly distribute the remaining sauce over the rolled tortillas and finish by sprinkling the 120g of grated cheddar cheese over the entire surface. The sauce topping will protect the dough from direct oven heat, while the cheese will create a delicious golden crust.
  8. Place the dish in a preheated static oven at 200°C for about 15 minutes, until the cheddar is completely melted and begins to form small bubbles. This final gratin is the secret to binding all the flavors and giving the dish its characteristic texture.
  9. Carefully remove from the oven and serve the enchiladas hot, topping each portion with the 100g of cold sour cream. The thermal contrast and the acidity of the cream will perfectly balance the spicy note of the chipotle and the saltiness of the cheese.
