Chicken Enchiladas

Mexico · 4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Tortillas filled with shredded chicken, covered in red sauce and cheese, then baked until golden.

Ingredients

  • Corn tortillas8 pieces (240 g)
  • Chicken breast500 g
  • Tomato passata400 ml
  • Onion100 g
  • Chipotle chilli powder2 g
  • Ground cumin2 g
  • Grated cheddar cheese120 g
  • Sour cream100 g
  • Vegetable oil20 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Gluten, Mustard.

Method

  1. Boil the chicken in salted water for 20 minutes, then shred it.
  2. Sauté the onion in the oil, add the passata, cumin, chipotle and salt, then cook for 10 minutes.
  3. Mix the chicken with a third of the sauce.
  4. Warm the tortillas, fill them with the chicken and roll them up.
  5. Place them in a baking dish and cover with the remaining sauce and cheese.
  6. Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 15 minutes and serve with sour cream.
