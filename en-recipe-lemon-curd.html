Lemon curd

1 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🌾 Gluten-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • medium eggs3
  • sugar150 g
  • lemon juice120 ml
  • Grated zest of organic lemons2
  • butter100 g
  • salt1 pinch

Allergens

Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Sulphites.

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the lemons, dry them, and finely grate the zest, avoiding the white pith.
  2. Squeeze the lemons, strain the juice, and pour it into a small saucepan along with the sugar and zest.
  3. Add the eggs and salt, whisking until the mixture is uniform.
  4. Cook in a double boiler or over very low heat, stirring continuously to prevent the eggs from curdling.
  5. Continue cooking until it reaches about 82–84 °C and a creamy consistency.
  6. Remove from heat and incorporate the cold butter cut into cubes, stirring until completely melted.
  7. Strain the cream through a sieve to remove any zest residue and lumps.
  8. Transfer to a clean container, cover with plastic wrap directly touching the surface, and chill quickly in the refrigerator.

Chef's tips

Tips, common mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not use high heat: the eggs might scramble.

- If the cream has small lumps, strain it immediately.

- You can replace the lemon with orange or lime, adjusting the sugar according to the acidity.

- It is ideal for tarts, tartlets, cheesecakes, and fillings.

- Store in the refrigerator at 4 °C or lower and consume within 3–4 days.
