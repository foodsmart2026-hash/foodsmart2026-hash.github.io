Spaghetti Carbonara with Peas and Crispy Pancetta

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

An affordable twist on the classic carbonara that incorporates peas for a touch of freshness and volume. The egg creates a delicious sauce that pairs perfectly with the savouriness of the browned pancetta.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti320 g
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Frozen peas150 g
  • Smoked pancetta cubes (cured Italian pork belly)60 g
  • Small onion1/2 pcs

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil for the pasta and, in the meantime, finely chop the onion to make it almost imperceptible to the palate.
  2. In a large non-stick frying pan, brown the pancetta over medium heat without adding extra fat until it turns golden and becomes crispy.
  3. Add the chopped onion and frozen peas to the pancetta, pour in half a glass of water, and cook covered for 8-10 minutes until the peas are tender.
  4. In a bowl, whisk the two eggs with a pinch of salt and plenty of black pepper until you have a smooth and slightly frothy mixture.
  5. Cook the spaghetti in the boiling water following the instructions on the packet, making sure to keep at least two ladles of the cooking water before draining.
  6. Drain the pasta al dente and pour it directly into the pan with the pea and pancetta mixture, tossing over high heat for a minute to distribute the flavours.
  7. Turn off the heat (a vital step to prevent the egg from scrambling) and pour the egg mixture over the hot pasta.
  8. Gradually add a little cooking water and stir quickly with tongs until the residual heat of the pasta transforms the egg into a thick and glossy cream.
  9. Serve immediately when you notice the cream completely coats the spaghetti without pooling at the bottom of the plate.
