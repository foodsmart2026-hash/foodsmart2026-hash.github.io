Frangipane cream

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • softened butter100 g
  • sugar100 g
  • almond flour100 g
  • medium eggs2
  • all-purpose flour30 g
  • vanilla extract1 tsp
  • salt1 pinch

Allergens

Tree nuts, Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Soy.

Method

  1. Let the butter soften at room temperature until it is easily workable.
  2. Transfer it to a bowl and cream it with the sugar until you obtain a creamy mixture.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing until fully absorbed.
  4. Fold in the almond flour and the sifted all-purpose flour.
  5. Add vanilla and salt and mix until you obtain a uniform cream.
  6. Cover the bowl and let it rest in the refrigerator for about 30 minutes.
  7. Use the cream to fill tarts, tartlets, or puff pastry bases.
  8. Bake inside the dessert according to the recipe, until the frangipane is puffed, golden, and completely cooked in the center.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- The butter must be soft but not melted.

- Do not add the eggs all at once to prevent the cream from separating.

- You can substitute the almond flour with hazelnut flour.

- A small amount of orange zest makes the cream particularly fragrant.

- Be careful of the tree nut allergen.
