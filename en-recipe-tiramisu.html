Tiramisù

6 servings · Prep 0 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

The world's most beloved Italian dessert.

Ingredients

  • Ladyfingers (Savoiardi)300 g
  • Mascarpone cheese500 g
  • Fresh eggs (approx. 4 large)220 g
  • Granulated sugar100 g
  • Espresso or moka coffee (unsweetened)300 ml
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder15 g
  • Fine salt1 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Prepare the coffee using a moka pot or espresso machine, pour it into a wide bowl, and let it cool completely to room temperature.
  2. Carefully separate the yolks from the whites. Ensure the bowl for the whites is perfectly clean and grease-free.
  3. In a stand mixer or with electric beaters, whisk the yolks with the sugar for 5-7 minutes until the mixture is pale, fluffy, and reaches the 'ribbon stage'.
  4. Briefly soften the mascarpone with a spatula, then gently fold it into the yolk mixture at low speed or by hand until smooth.
  5. Whisk the egg whites with a pinch of salt until stiff peaks form. Gently fold them into the mascarpone cream using slow bottom-to-top movements.
  6. Quickly dip the ladyfingers into the cold coffee (about 1-2 seconds per side) to prevent them from becoming overly soaked and soggy.
  7. Arrange a layer of ladyfingers at the bottom of a rectangular dish, cover with half of the mascarpone cream, and level with a spatula.
  8. Repeat with a second layer of ladyfingers and finish with the remaining cream.
  9. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours (ideally 12 hours) to allow the flavors to meld and the structure to set.
  10. Just before serving, evenly dust the surface with sifted unsweetened cocoa powder.
