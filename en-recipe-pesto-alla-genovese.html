Genoese Pesto

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • fresh basil leaves50 g
  • extra virgin olive oil70 ml
  • pine nuts35 g
  • grated parmesan cheese60 g
  • grated pecorino cheese20 g
  • garlic1 clove
  • coarse salt2 g

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Tree nuts.

Method

  1. Gently wash the basil and dry it perfectly with a clean kitchen towel.
  2. Place the garlic and coarse salt in the mortar and crush them until a paste is formed.
  3. Add the pine nuts and continue crushing until they are reduced to a smooth paste.
  4. Incorporate the basil a little at a time, working it with circular motions to avoid overheating it.
  5. Add the parmesan and pecorino and blend gently.
  6. Pour in the oil in a thin stream, stirring until a creamy sauce is obtained.
  7. If using a mixer, work with short pulses to limit the heating of the blades.
  8. Use immediately or transfer to a clean container, covering the surface with a thin layer of oil and refrigerating.

Chef's tips

Tips, errors, and alternatives:

- Do not use wet basil: water alters the consistency.

- Avoid overheating the mixture.

- You can replace the pine nuts with walnuts or almonds to create a variation.

- For a milder flavor, reduce the garlic.
