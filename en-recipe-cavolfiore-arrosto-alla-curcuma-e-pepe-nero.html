Roasted Cauliflower with Turmeric and Black Pepper

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A very simple preparation that transforms cauliflower into a tasty and visually inviting side dish thanks to its golden colour. Turmeric adds an exotic note without impacting the budget.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🌍 International

Ingredients

  • White cauliflower800 g
  • Turmeric powder1 tsp
  • Garlic powder1/2 tsp

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan assisted) for quick and effective browning.
  2. Clean the cauliflower by removing the leaves and the central core, then break it into medium-small florets.
  3. Wash the florets well and dry them thoroughly with a cloth; if they remain wet, the cauliflower will boil rather than roast.
  4. In a large bowl, mix the florets with 2 tablespoons of oil, turmeric, garlic powder and plenty of black pepper.
  5. Massage the florets with your hands so each piece is evenly coated with the spice and oil mix.
  6. Spread the cauliflower on a baking tray lined with baking paper, avoiding overlapping the pieces to allow air to circulate.
  7. Bake and cook for about 20-25 minutes, turning halfway through with a spatula.
  8. The cauliflower is ready when the tips of the florets appear slightly charred and the texture is tender but firm.
  9. Remove from the oven, adjust the salt and serve preferably warm to enhance the aroma of the turmeric.
