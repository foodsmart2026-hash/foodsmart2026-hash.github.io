Mezze Maniche with Broccoli and Almond Pesto

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

A clever way to get even the most skeptical to eat broccoli, creating a silky sauce without any cream. The almonds add a toasted and crunchy note.

Ingredients

  • mezze maniche pasta (short tube-shaped Italian pasta)320 g
  • broccoli (florets and tender stalks)500 g
  • blanched almonds40 g
  • grated Pecorino Romano (salty Italian sheep's milk cheese)40 g
  • extra virgin olive oil50 ml
  • lemon zestq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Tree nuts

Method

  1. Divide the broccoli into small florets and peel the stalk, cutting it into small cubes to ensure even cooking.
  2. Boil the broccoli in plenty of salted water for about 6-7 minutes until very tender when pierced with a fork.
  3. In the same broccoli water, drop the pasta and cook according to the package instructions.
  4. While the pasta cooks, place the broccoli (drained with a slotted spoon) into a blender with the almonds, oil, and Pecorino.
  5. Blend while adding a ladle of pasta cooking water until you achieve a smooth and thick cream.
  6. Toast any extra almonds if you want a final crunchy touch, crushing them roughly.
  7. Drain the pasta al dente, reserving a little water, and toss in the pan with the broccoli cream.
  8. Turn off the heat, add a grating of lemon zest to cleanse the palate, and a final drizzle of raw oil.
