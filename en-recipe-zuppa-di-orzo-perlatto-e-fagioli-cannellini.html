Pearl Barley and Cannellini Bean Soup

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A perfect comfort food for cold days, rich in complex carbohydrates and protein. It is an affordable dish as it uses dried or canned pulses and budget-friendly grains.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Pearl barley200 g
  • Cooked cannellini beans400 g
  • Carrot, celery, onion (soffritto mix)150 g
  • Tomato passata (sieved tomatoes)100 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Bay leaves2 foglie
  • Water or stock1.5 l

Allergens

Gluten, Celery

Method

  1. Rinse the barley well under running water to remove any impurities.
  2. Prepare a soffritto by finely chopping the carrot, celery and onion and browning them with the oil in a deep pot.
  3. Add the tomato passata and bay leaves, letting the flavours combine for 2 minutes.
  4. Pour the barley into the pot and toast it briefly, as you would with rice.
  5. Cover with the boiling water or stock and cook for about 30 minutes over a medium heat.
  6. Add the cannellini beans (if canned, rinse them well) and continue cooking for another 10 minutes.
  7. Mash some of the beans against the side of the pot to make the soup thicker and creamier.
  8. Turn off the heat, let it rest for 5 minutes and serve with a drizzle of raw olive oil and fresh pepper.
