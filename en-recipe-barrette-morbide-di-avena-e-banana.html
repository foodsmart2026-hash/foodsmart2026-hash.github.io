Soft Oat and Banana Bars

💰 Save Euro · about €0.76 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A healthy snack, without added refined sugars, which exploits the natural sweetness of ripe bananas. Excellent to prepare in advance for the whole week.

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats200 g
  • Very ripe bananas3 pcs
  • Dark chocolate chips40 g
  • Chopped walnuts30 g
  • Peanut butter (optional)20 g
  • Salt1 pizzico

Allergens

Gluten, Tree nuts, Peanuts

Method

  1. Peel the bananas and mash them with a fork in a bowl until you get an almost liquid puree without large chunks.
  2. Add the rolled oats to the puree and mix well so that the oats absorb all the moisture from the fruit.
  3. Join the chocolate chips, roughly chopped walnuts, and a pinch of salt to enhance the flavour of the chocolate.
  4. If you want a more binding and rich consistency, add the peanut butter and mix everything together.
  5. Line a small rectangular baking tin (about 20x20 cm) with baking parchment and pour the mixture, levelling it with the back of a spoon.
  6. Compact the mixture well by pressing firmly: this will prevent the bars from crumbling after baking.
  7. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C for about 20-25 minutes until the edges are slightly dark and crunchy.
  8. Remove the tin and leave to cool completely; this step is essential for the bars to solidify.
  9. Cut the block into 8-10 rectangular bars and store them in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
