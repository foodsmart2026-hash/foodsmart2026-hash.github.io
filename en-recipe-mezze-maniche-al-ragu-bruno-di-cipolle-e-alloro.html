Mezze Maniche with Brown Onion and Bay Leaf Ragù

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A vegan first course of humble origins where the slow caramelisation of onions creates a creamy and flavourful sauce. The cost is very low thanks to the use of basic pantry ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Mezze Maniche (short, wide ribbed pasta tubes)320 g
  • Large golden onions500 g
  • Tomato purée (concentrate)30 g
  • Dried bay leaves2 foglie
  • Vegetable stock (from a cube or fresh)250 ml
  • Brown sugar5 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Slice the onions very thinly with a sharp knife or a mandoline to ensure even cooking.
  2. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a large pan, add the onions and sugar, and cook over a very low heat for 15 minutes.
  3. Add the bay leaves and a pinch of salt to help the onions release their natural juices.
  4. When the onions have softened and start to turn golden, add the tomato purée dissolved in the hot vegetable stock.
  5. Cover and continue cooking for another 10 minutes until the base becomes a thick, dark cream (brown ragù).
  6. Boil the pasta in plenty of salted water, draining it two minutes before the time indicated on the package.
  7. Toss the pasta in the onion sauce, adding a ladle of cooking water to emulsify the oils with the starches.
  8. Remove the bay leaves and serve the pasta hot, ensuring every piece is coated in the onion cream.
