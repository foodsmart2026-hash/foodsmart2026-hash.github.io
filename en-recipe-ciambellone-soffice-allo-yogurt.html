Fluffy Yogurt Bundt Cake

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

The classic Italian breakfast cake (Ciambellone), tall and very soft, ideal for dunking in milk or tea. The recipe uses the yogurt pot as a measuring cup, making it incredibly simple.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Plain yogurt125 g
  • Plain flour (Type 00 flour, finely milled for Italian cakes)250 g
  • Caster sugar150 g
  • Eggs3 pcs
  • Sunflower oil100 ml
  • Baking powder (Lievito per dolci, often vanilla-scented in Italy)16 g
  • Lemon (zest)1 pcs

Allergens

Eggs, Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Start by whisking the eggs with the sugar using an electric whisk for at least 5 minutes until they become pale and fluffy.
  2. Add the room-temperature yogurt and continue mixing at a low speed so as not to deflate the eggs.
  3. Pour in the sunflower oil in a steady stream, incorporating it slowly to maintain the emulsion of the mixture.
  4. Add the finely grated lemon zest, avoiding the bitter white pith of the peel.
  5. Sift the flour with the baking powder and add it to the mixture in three stages, folding gently with a spatula from bottom to top.
  6. Grease and flour a 24 cm bundt tin and pour in the batter, levelling it with the spatula.
  7. Bake in a preheated static oven at 180°C for 35-40 minutes, without ever opening the door for the first 30 minutes.
  8. Check the cake with a skewer: if it comes out clean, the cake is perfectly baked.
  9. Leave to cool completely before removing from the tin to prevent it from breaking due to its fluffiness.
