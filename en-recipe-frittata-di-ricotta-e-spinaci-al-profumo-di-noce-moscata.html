Baked Ricotta and Spinach Frittata with Nutmeg

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A tall and fluffy baked frittata that resembles the filling of ravioli but served as a main course. The addition of ricotta makes it moist and perfect for eating cold as well.

Ingredients

  • Fresh eggs6 pcs
  • Cow's milk ricotta (a soft, creamy Italian whey cheese)250 g
  • Frozen leaf spinach300 g
  • Parmigiano Reggiano (an aged, hard Italian cow's milk cheese)40 g
  • Nutmeg1 pizzico
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 ml
  • Salt and Pepperq.b.

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Boil the frozen spinach in a little boiling water for 5 minutes, then drain and squeeze very well to remove all the water.
  2. Coarsely chop the spinach with a knife to distribute it more evenly through the mixture.
  3. In a bowl, work the ricotta with a whisk until it becomes a smooth cream, removing any lumps.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time to the ricotta, incorporating them with slow movements so as not to deflate the mixture.
  5. Add the spinach, Parmigiano, nutmeg, salt, and pepper, stirring until everything is homogeneous.
  6. Grease a 22 cm round baking tin with oil and pour in the mixture, levelling it with a spatula.
  7. Bake at 180°C (conventional oven) for about 25 minutes until the surface appears puffed and golden.
  8. Check the cooking by inserting a skewer: it should come out dry and the frittata should feel springy in the centre.
  9. Remove from the oven and let it rest for 5 minutes before cutting into wedges to prevent it from breaking.
