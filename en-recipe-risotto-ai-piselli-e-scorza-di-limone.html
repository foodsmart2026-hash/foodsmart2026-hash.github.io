Pea and Lemon Zest Risotto

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A fresh and affordable variation of the classic Venetian 'Risi e Bisi'. The hint of lemon elevates the simple ingredients, making the dish refined.

Ingredients

  • Risotto rice (Arborio or Carnaroli)320 g
  • Fine frozen peas300 g
  • Vegetable stock1.2 l
  • Fresh spring onions2 pcs
  • Butter30 g
  • Organic lemon (zest)1 pcs
  • Grated Parmesan cheese40 g

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Prepare the vegetable stock and keep it simmering on a low heat throughout the recipe.
  2. Finely chop the spring onions and sauté them in a saucepan with half the butter for 3 minutes over a low heat.
  3. Add the rice and toast it for 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the grains become glossy and translucent at the edges.
  4. Add the frozen peas to the rice and begin adding the stock, one ladle at a time, waiting for it to be absorbed before adding the next.
  5. Continue cooking for about 16-18 minutes, stirring often to release the starch and create the typical creaminess.
  6. When the rice is 'al dente', turn off the heat and stir in the remaining butter, the Parmesan, and the grated lemon zest.
  7. Cover with a lid and let the risotto rest for 2 minutes (mantecatura) to stabilise the flavours.
  8. Serve immediately, garnishing with an extra grating of fresh lemon zest to enhance the aroma.
