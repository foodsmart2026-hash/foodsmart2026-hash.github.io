Sponge Cake

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • medium eggs5
  • sugar150 g
  • all-purpose flour150 g
  • salt1 pinch
  • vanilla extract1 teaspoon

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Milk, Soy.

Method

  1. Bring the eggs to room temperature. Crack them into a large bowl together with the sugar and salt.
  2. Whip with electric beaters for about 12–15 minutes, until the mixture is very pale, voluminous, and fluffy.
  3. Add the vanilla and continue to mix briefly.
  4. Sift the flour and fold it in a little at a time, using a spatula and gentle movements from bottom to top so as not to deflate the mixture.
  5. Grease and flour a 22–24 cm mold, then gently pour in the batter.
  6. Level the surface without tapping the mold on the work surface.
  7. Bake in a preheated static oven at 170 °C for about 35–40 minutes. Avoid opening the oven during the first 30 minutes.
  8. Check for doneness with a toothpick, remove from the oven and let cool completely before removing from the mold and slicing into layers.

Chef's tips

Tips, errors, and alternatives:

- Traditional Sponge Cake does not require baking powder: the volume comes from the air incorporated into the eggs.

- If the cake sinks, it might have been deflated while folding in the flour or taken out of the oven too early.

- For a softer version you can replace 30 g of flour with potato starch.

- For a cocoa version replace 25 g of flour with unsweetened cocoa powder.

- To cut even layers, let the cake rest until completely cold.
