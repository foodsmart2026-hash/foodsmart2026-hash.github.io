Tuscan Ribollita

Toscana · 6 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 90 min · Medium

A rustic peasant soup of cavolo nero, beans, and stale bread, re-boiled before serving.

Ingredients

  • Cooked cannellini beans500 g
  • Cavolo nero300 g
  • Savoy cabbage200 g
  • Potatoes200 g
  • Carrot100 g
  • Celery80 g
  • Red onion100 g
  • Peeled plum tomatoes200 g
  • Stale Tuscan bread250 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Water1500 ml
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

Gluten, Celery
May contain traces of: Mustard, Soy.

Method

  1. Sauté the finely chopped onion, carrot, and celery in the oil for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the shredded cavolo nero and savoy cabbage, the diced potatoes, and the peeled tomatoes.
  3. Blend half of the beans and mix the purée with the water.
  4. Simmer over a low heat for 60 minutes, then add the whole beans and season with salt.
  5. In a pot, alternate layers of sliced bread and soup; leave to rest for at least 2 hours.
  6. Bring back to the boil (re-boil) for 10 minutes and serve with a drizzle of raw oil.
