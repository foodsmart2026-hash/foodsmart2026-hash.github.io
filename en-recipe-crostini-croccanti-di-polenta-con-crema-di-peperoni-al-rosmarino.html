Crispy Polenta Crostini with Rosemary Bell Pepper Cream

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A tasty starter that plays on the contrast between the crunchy maize base and the softness of a sweet pepper mousse. Entirely free from common allergens, it is a quick and budget-friendly solution perfect for welcoming guests with flavour.

Ingredients

  • Instant polenta (precooked cornmeal)150 g
  • Water600 ml
  • Red and yellow bell peppers350 g
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Fresh rosemary2 sprigs
  • Fine salt5 g
  • Ground black pepper1 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Bring the salted water to the boil in a pot; the water is ready when the surface shows large, dense bubbles.
  2. Gradually whisk the polenta into the boiling water, stirring quickly for about 5 minutes until the mixture is thick and pulls away cleanly from the bottom.
  3. Spread the polenta onto a flat baking tray covered with baking parchment, levelling it to a thickness of 1 cm with a damp spatula, then place it in the freezer for 5 minutes to firm up quickly.
  4. Meanwhile, wash the peppers and cut them into small cubes of about 5 mm to ensure very quick cooking in the pan.
  5. Heat 15 ml of oil in a pan with the whole garlic clove (for aroma only) and one rosemary sprig; add the peppers and sauté over a high heat for 8 minutes until tender and lightly golden.
  6. Remove the garlic and rosemary, transfer the peppers to a beaker and blend with a stick blender until a smooth cream is formed, adding a drizzle of oil if necessary.
  7. Take the now firm polenta, cut it into squares of about 5 cm and grill them in the same pan used for the peppers with the remaining oil for 2 minutes per side, until a crispy crust forms.
  8. Assemble the starter by placing a spoonful of pepper cream on each polenta crostino and finish with a pinch of pepper and a few fresh rosemary needles.
