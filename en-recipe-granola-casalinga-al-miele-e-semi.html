Homemade Honey and Seed Granola

💰 Save Euro · about €0.56 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A crunchy mix of cereals and seeds made at home, much cheaper and healthier than industrial versions rich in hydrogenated fats. Perfect for dipping into yogurt or cold milk.

Ingredients

  • Whole grain rolled oats250 g
  • Sunflower seeds40 g
  • Honey60 g
  • Sunflower oil30 ml
  • Raisins40 g
  • Salt1 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. In a large bowl, mix the oats with the sunflower seeds and a pinch of salt.
  2. In a small saucepan, lightly heat the honey with the oil until they become fluid and easy to pour.
  3. Pour the liquid mix over the cereals and stir well with a spoon so that every oat flake is well coated.
  4. Line a baking tray with baking parchment and spread the mixture in an even, thin layer.
  5. Bake at 160°C (static oven) for about 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through for even browning.
  6. Pay close attention in the final minutes: the granola goes from golden to burnt very quickly due to the honey.
  7. Remove the tray from the oven and add the raisins (do not add them earlier as they would toughen too much in the oven).
  8. Leave to cool completely on the tray: the granola will only become crunchy once cold.
  9. Break the large pieces with your hands and store in an airtight glass jar to maintain freshness for up to 2 weeks.
