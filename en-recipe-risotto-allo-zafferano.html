Saffron Risotto

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A creamy, golden risotto, finished with butter and Parmesan.

Ingredients

  • Carnaroli rice320 g
  • Meat stock (beef and chicken, strained)1200 ml
  • Butter60 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano PDO60 g
  • White onion40 g
  • Dry white wine80 ml
  • Saffron strands0.3 g
  • Fine salt4 g

Allergens

Milk, Sulphites
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Bring the stock to a gentle simmer and keep it warm over a low heat.
  2. Place the saffron strands in a small cup with 2 tablespoons of hot stock and leave to infuse.
  1. Finely chop the onion.
  2. In a saucepan, melt 20 g of the butter and sauté the onion over a low heat for 3-4 minutes until softened, without letting it brown.
  3. Add the rice and toast for 2 minutes, stirring, until the grains become glossy.
  4. Deglaze with the white wine and allow it to evaporate completely.
  5. Add the hot stock a ladleful at a time, stirring often and waiting for it to be absorbed before adding more.
  6. Halfway through cooking (about 9 minutes), add the saffron with its liquid and season with salt.
  7. After 17-18 minutes, when the rice is al dente and still moist, turn off the heat.
  8. Beat in the remaining butter and Parmesan, cover for 1 minute and serve immediately.
