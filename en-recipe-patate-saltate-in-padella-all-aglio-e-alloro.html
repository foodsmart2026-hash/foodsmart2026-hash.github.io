Pan-Fried Potatoes with Garlic and Bay Leaves

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A rustic and fragrant side dish, a budget-friendly alternative to classic roast potatoes. The combination of garlic and bay leaves provides an intense aroma without extra costs.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes800 g
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Fresh or dried bay leaves3 leaves

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into regular cubes of about 2 cm to ensure even cooking.
  2. Put the cubes in a bowl with cold water for 10 minutes to remove excess starch, preventing them from sticking to the pan.
  3. Drain and dry the potatoes perfectly with a clean tea towel; any residual moisture would prevent the formation of a crust.
  4. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a large non-stick pan along with the crushed garlic and bay leaves until the oil is fragrant.
  5. Pour the potatoes in a single layer and cook over a medium-high heat without turning them for the first 5 minutes to seal the surface.
  6. Gently turn the potatoes and continue cooking for another 15-20 minutes over a medium heat, tossing them occasionally.
  7. Cover with a lid for the last 5 minutes if you want a softer interior, while still keeping the heat lively.
  8. The potatoes are ready when they appear golden and crispy on the outside and a fork enters easily without resistance.
  9. Remove the garlic and bay leaves, season generously with salt and pepper before serving very hot.
