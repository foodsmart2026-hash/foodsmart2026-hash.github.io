Mediterranean Potato, Mackerel and Vegetable Dish

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A nutritionally balanced proposal that combines high-quality oily fish protein with the complex carbohydrates of potatoes and legume fibre. A quick and affordable one-dish meal, a pillar of the modern Mediterranean diet for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes600 g
  • Mackerel fillets in brine (sustainably caught)250 g
  • Cooked cannellini beans (drained)240 g
  • Medium courgettes400 g
  • Cherry tomatoes200 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Red onion60 g
  • Dried oregano and salt5 g

Allergens

Fish

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the potatoes, peel them and cut them into cubes of about 2 cm to ensure even and rapid cooking.
  2. Parboil the potato cubes in lightly salted water for about 5-6 minutes; they should be tender when pierced with a fork but not falling apart.
  3. While the potatoes are cooking, finely slice the red onion and cut the courgettes into rounds about half a centimetre thick.
  4. In a large non-stick frying pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil and soften the onion over a low heat until it becomes glossy and translucent.
  5. Drain the potatoes and add them to the pan along with the courgettes, slightly increasing the heat to brown them evenly for about 10 minutes until golden.
  6. Rinse the cannellini beans from their liquid and add them to the pan: this step provides the necessary portion of plant protein and fibre for the meal.
  7. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and the roughly flaked mackerel fillets, cooking everything for another 4 minutes to let the flavours blend.
  8. Adjust the salt, sprinkle with plenty of oregano and serve the dish hot, ensuring the cherry tomatoes are just softened but still whole.
