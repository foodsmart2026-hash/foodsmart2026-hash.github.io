Meat broth

8 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 180 min · Easy

Homemade meat broth: the base for risottos, tortellini, passatelli and soups.

🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Beef (brisket or shank)500 g
  • Beef bones300 g
  • Carrot1 pz
  • Onion1 pz
  • Celery1 costa
  • Coarse saltq.b.
  • Cold water3 l

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Put the meat and bones in a large pot and cover with the cold water.
  2. Bring to a simmer over medium-low heat and skim off the foam that rises to the surface.
  3. Add the whole carrot, onion and celery, washed and trimmed.
  4. Cook over very low heat for about 3 hours with the lid ajar: the broth should barely simmer, not boil.
  5. Season with salt towards the end, then strain through a fine sieve.

Chef's tips

Always start from cold water: the meat releases more flavour.

Do not boil it hard, so the broth stays clear.

For a lighter broth, let it cool and remove the layer of fat on top.

It keeps 3 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
