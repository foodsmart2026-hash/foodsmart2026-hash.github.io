Whipped cream

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • fresh whipping cream (at least 35% fat)500 ml
  • powdered sugar (optional)40 gr

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Chill the cream in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, keeping it very cold until the moment of use.
  2. Place the bowl and the beaters in the refrigerator for about 15 minutes, to make whipping easier.
  3. Pour the cream into the bowl and start whipping at medium-low speed.
  4. When it starts to thicken, gradually increase the speed.
  5. If you want sweet cream, add the sifted powdered sugar a little at a time.
  6. Continue to whip until you reach the desired consistency: soft to accompany desserts or firmer to decorate cakes.
  7. Stop the beaters immediately when the cream holds its shape and shows well-defined peaks.
  8. Use immediately or store in the refrigerator in a closed container.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not confuse cooking cream and whipping cream: you need a product with sufficient fat content.

- If you whip for too long, the cream can separate, turning into butter.

- For an unsweetened version, eliminate the sugar.

- For more stable decorations, use a specific stabilizer.

- Whipped cream is perfect for cakes, ice cream, hot chocolate and spoon desserts.
