Pasta with Soft Cod and Peeled Tomato Sauce

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A simple and budget-friendly way to introduce fish into the family diet, using cod fillets that flake perfectly into the tomato sauce. The texture is tender and safe, ideal for children starting to chew small pieces.

Ingredients

  • Small short pasta (such as maccheroncini)160 g
  • Cod fillet (fresh or frozen)150 g
  • Peeled tomatoes or fine tomato pulp200 g
  • Dried oregano1 pinch
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Fish

Method

  1. Carefully check the cod fillet with your fingers to ensure there are no bones, then chop it roughly with a knife.
  2. In a non-stick pan, heat the tomato pulp with a splash of water and the oregano for about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the chopped cod to the tomato and cover with a lid.
  4. Cook over a medium-low heat for 10 minutes; the fish will cook quickly and start to flake into the sauce.
  5. Use a wooden spoon to break up the fish pieces further until they become tiny crumbs integrated into the sauce.
  6. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in plenty of unsalted water, draining it one minute before the time indicated.
  7. Toss the pasta in the pan with the cod sauce for the final minute, adding a tablespoon of the cooking water if necessary.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the raw extra virgin olive oil and stir well.
  9. Serve the dish well-combined and lukewarm; adults can add salt and chilli flakes to taste.
