Rustic pearled spelt, chicory and black chickpea soup

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Puglia · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A hearty soup where the bitterness of wild chicory meets the texture of black chickpeas and pearled spelt in an autumnal embrace.

Ingredients

  • Wild chicory or catalogna700g
  • Pearled spelt200g
  • Black chickpeas (already soaked and boiled)250g
  • Vegetable stock1.2L
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Fresh chilli1
  • Extra virgin olive oil60ml
  • Grated Pecorino Romano PDO40g
  • Bay leaves2 leaves
  • Salt and black pepperto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Milk
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Thoroughly clean the chicory, removing the tough ends of the stalks and cutting the leaves in half lengthways if they are too large.
  2. Blanch the chicory in plenty of lightly salted water for about 6-7 minutes to temper the excessive bitterness, then drain well.
  3. In a large pot, preferably earthenware, heat the extra virgin olive oil with the crushed garlic cloves and finely chopped chilli.
  4. Add the pearled spelt and toast it for 2 minutes over a high heat, stirring frequently so it does not stick.
  5. Add the pre-cooked black chickpeas and the bay leaves, allowing them to flavour for another 2 minutes so the pulses absorb the base aromas.
  6. Cover everything with the boiling vegetable stock and simmer over a low heat for about 20 minutes (or as indicated on the spelt packaging).
  7. Halfway through cooking the spelt, add the blanched and squeezed chicory to the pot, stirring gently to distribute it evenly.
  8. Continue cooking until the spelt is tender but still al dente and the broth has reduced slightly, while maintaining a loose consistency.
  9. Remove the garlic and bay leaves, season with salt and turn off the heat.
  10. Stir in the Pecorino Romano and a final drizzle of olive oil, then let the soup rest covered for 3 minutes before serving.
