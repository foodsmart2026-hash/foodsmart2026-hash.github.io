Mezze Maniche with Creamy Rosemary Chickpeas

💰 Save Euro · about €0.56 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A thick and nutritious pasta dish that uses legumes to create a velvety, flavourful sauce. Fresh rosemary provides an unmistakable Mediterranean aroma that enhances the sweetness of the chickpeas.

Ingredients

  • Mezze Maniche pasta (short, ridged tube-shaped pasta)320 g
  • Canned pre-cooked chickpeas480 g
  • Fresh rosemary2 sprigs
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Tomato pulp100 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Drain the chickpeas from their canning liquid and rinse them well under running water to remove excess salt.
  2. In a frying pan, heat the oil with the unpeeled garlic clove and the finely chopped rosemary leaves to release their essential oils.
  3. Add the chickpeas and the tomato pulp, salt lightly and leave to simmer over a medium heat for about 10 minutes, adding half a glass of water.
  4. Take half of the chickpeas from the pan and blend them with a hand blender until you get a smooth, thick cream.
  5. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil and cook the mezze maniche according to the pack instructions for an 'al dente' texture.
  6. Combine the chickpea cream with the remaining whole chickpeas in the pan and stir well to blend the two textures.
  7. Drain the pasta directly into the sauce, adding a little cooking water to make it extremely creamy.
  8. Toss for a minute over a high heat so the sauce adheres well to the surface of the pasta and serve with a drizzle of raw olive oil.
