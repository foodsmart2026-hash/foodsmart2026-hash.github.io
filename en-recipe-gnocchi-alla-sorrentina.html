Gnocchi alla sorrentina

Campania · 4 servings · 60 min · Medium

Baked gnocchi with tomato sauce and gooey mozzarella.

🇮🇹 Italian

Typical of Campania, these gnocchi offer an embrace of tomato, stringy mozzarella, and fresh basil. The final gratin creates an irresistible crust that contrasts with the softness of the potatoes.

Ingredients

  • Starchy potatoes1000 g
  • All-purpose flour (00 type)250 g
  • Medium eggs1 piece
  • Tomato purée (passata)500 ml
  • Fresh mozzarella cheese250 g
  • Fresh basil15 g
  • Parmigiano Reggiano cheese50 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Fine salt12 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Wash the potatoes and boil them with their skins on in cold water brought to a boil for about 30-40 minutes, until tender to the core.
  2. Peel the potatoes while still hot, mash them with a potato ricer onto a work surface, and let the steam evaporate for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the sifted flour, egg, and 5g of salt; knead quickly by hand until the dough is smooth, soft, and no longer sticky.
  4. Roll the dough into long cylinders about 2 cm thick, cut into chunks, and ridge them using a gnocchi board or the prongs of a fork.
  5. In a large pan, heat the tomato purée with olive oil, the remaining salt, and 5g of basil; simmer over low heat for 15 minutes.
  6. Drop the gnocchi into boiling salted water and drain them with a slotted spoon as soon as they float to the surface (about 2 minutes).
  7. Transfer the gnocchi to the pan with the sauce, add the mozzarella cut into 1 cm cubes, and toss gently.
  8. Pour everything into a baking dish, sprinkle with Parmigiano Reggiano and the remaining basil, then gratinate in a static oven at 200°C for 10 minutes until golden brown.

Tips

  • Use old, starchy potatoes because they absorb less flour and make the gnocchi much softer.
  • Work the dough quickly while the potatoes are still warm to prevent it from becoming sticky.
  • Drain the mozzarella well before adding it to prevent it from releasing too much liquid during baking.

Variations

  • You can enrich the sauce with a pinch of fresh chili pepper for a slightly spicy note.
  • Try using smoked provola cheese instead of mozzarella for a more intense and rustic flavor.
