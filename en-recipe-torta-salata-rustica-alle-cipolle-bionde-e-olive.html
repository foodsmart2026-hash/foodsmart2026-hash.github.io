Rustic Onion and Olive Tart

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

Inspired by the French pissaladière but in a vegetarian version, this tart relies on the sweetness of slow-cooked onions.

Ingredients

  • rectangular puff pastry1 rotolo
  • yellow onions800 g
  • pitted black olives60 g
  • cream cheese100 g
  • brown sugar1 cucchiaino
  • dried thymeq.b.
  • extra virgin olive oil30 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Thinly slice the onions and place them in a large pan with the oil, a pinch of salt, and the sugar.
  2. Cover and cook over very low heat for at least 20 minutes, stirring often; the onions should become almost like an amber jam.
  3. Unroll the puff pastry onto a baking tray keeping its baking paper and prick the bottom with a fork.
  4. Spread the cream cheese over the base, leaving a 2 cm border on all sides.
  5. Distribute the onion compote evenly over the cheese and add the halved olives.
  6. Dust with plenty of thyme and fold the pastry edges inwards to create a frame.
  7. Bake at 200°C for 20-25 minutes until the pastry edges are well puffed and golden.
  8. Allow to cool slightly before slicing so the filling can set properly.
