White Cauliflower Risotto with a Mild Melting Cheese Heart

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A risotto where the cauliflower disappears into the creamy base, making the dish very sweet and nutritious. Ideal for encouraging children to enjoy cruciferous vegetables.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Risotto rice (Originario type - a short-grain Italian rice)160 g
  • Cauliflower (florets only)250 g
  • Mild caciotta cheese (a soft, semi-cooked Italian cheese)40 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml
  • Small brown onion1/2 pc

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Wash the cauliflower and cut it into very tiny florets, almost minced, to speed up the cooking.
  2. Finely chop the onion and stew it in a pot with a drizzle of water until it becomes transparent.
  3. Add the rice and the cauliflower florets, toasting them lightly with the onion for 2 minutes.
  4. Cover with hot water (or salt-free vegetable stock) and begin cooking, stirring often.
  5. Add water gradually as it is absorbed; the cauliflower will begin to break down, creating a cream.
  6. Continue for about 15-18 minutes until the rice is well cooked and the cauliflower is completely soft.
  7. Cut the caciotta into tiny cubes or grate it coarsely.
  8. Turn off the heat and 'mantecare' (beat in) the cheese and olive oil until the cheese melts.
  9. Let it rest covered for 2 minutes before serving to achieve a 'wave-like' consistency perfect for little ones.
