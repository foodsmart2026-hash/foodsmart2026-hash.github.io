Caramel

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 10 min · Medium

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • sugar150 g
  • water50 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Pour the sugar into a heavy-bottomed saucepan and spread it evenly
  2. Add the water and gently tilt the saucepan to moisten all the sugar.
  3. Turn on medium heat and let it cook without stirring with utensils, avoiding crystallization.
  4. When the sugar begins to change color, monitor the cooking carefully.
  5. Continue until you achieve a uniform amber color, without reaching a shade that is too dark.
  6. Turn off the heat and use the caramel immediately to coat molds or prepare desserts.
  7. If you want a soft sauce, you can add hot cream in another preparation, with extreme caution because the mixture can splatter violently.
  8. Allow to cool before using or storing in a suitable container.

Chef's tips

Tips, errors, and alternatives:

- Caramel reaches very high temperatures: never taste it hot and do not touch it.

- Do not leave the saucepan unattended: the color changes rapidly.

- For a soft caramel sauce, use a specific recipe with cream and butter, not just water.

- You can add a pinch of salt for a salted variant.
