Soft Apple and Ricotta Sweet Frittata Bites

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.51 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 8 min · Easy

These fluffy squares combine egg proteins with the lightness of ricotta and the sweetness of grated apple. Perfect for children starting to feed themselves, they offer a soft and safe texture.

Ingredients

  • Large fresh eggs2 pcs
  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (a soft, creamy Italian whey cheese)120 g
  • Golden Delicious apple1 pcs
  • Butter5 g

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Wash, peel, and grate the apple using the large holes of a grater, then squeeze it gently between your hands to remove excess water.
  2. In a bowl, work the ricotta with a fork until it is creamy and free of large lumps.
  3. Add the eggs to the ricotta and whisk everything with a hand whisk or a fork until the mixture appears uniform and slightly frothy.
  4. Incorporate the grated apple into the egg and ricotta mix, stirring well to distribute it evenly.
  5. Heat a small non-stick frying pan with the knob of butter, spreading it over the entire surface with a brush or kitchen paper.
  6. Pour the mixture into the hot pan, cover with a lid, and cook over a very low heat for about 5 minutes: the steam will help keep the frittata fluffy and soft.
  7. When the surface looks set and no longer liquid, flip the frittata using the lid and cook for another 2 minutes on the other side.
  8. Slide the frittata onto a cutting board, let it cool slightly, and cut it into small cubes or strips of about 1-2 cm, which are easy to chew.
