Seasonal Pan-Fried Vegetables

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A colourful and nutritious side dish that combines the crunch of fresh vegetables with the creaminess of legumes. This recipe highlights extra virgin olive oil and fibre, following the pillars of the Mediterranean diet for a balanced meal.

Ingredients

  • Dark courgettes400 g
  • Medium carrots300 g
  • Canned precooked chickpeas (drained weight)240 g
  • Tropea red onion (a sweet red onion variety from Calabria)100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Fresh rosemary5 g
  • Iodised salt3 g
  • Ground black pepper1 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Washing and preparation: Thoroughly wash the courgettes and carrots under running water; trim and cut them into uniform 1.5 cm cubes to ensure even cooking in a short time.
  2. Healthy sauté: Finely slice the onion and heat the extra virgin olive oil in a large non-stick frying pan; add the onion and let it soften over a low heat for 4 minutes until translucent.
  3. Cooking the carrots: Add the carrots to the pan, as they are the hardest vegetable; sauté for 5 minutes, adding two tablespoons of hot water to help soften the fibres without using excess fat.
  4. Cooking the courgettes: Add the courgettes and increase to medium-high heat; cook for 7-8 minutes uncovered, stirring often until they show a slight golden colour on the surface but remain firm at the core.
  5. Integrating the legumes: Drain the chickpeas from their liquid, rinse them well under water and add them to the vegetables; this step enriches the dish with plant-based proteins and iron, making it nutritionally complete.
  6. Aromatising: Add the finely chopped rosemary and a grind of black pepper; sauté everything for another 3 minutes to allow the essential oils of the herb to penetrate the ingredients.
  7. Final adjustment: Salt the mixture only at the end to prevent the vegetables from releasing too much water and losing texture; they are ready when the carrots can be pierced with a fork but still offer slight resistance.
  8. Resting and serving: Turn off the heat and let the side dish rest for 2 minutes covered; this allows the flavours to stabilise and the internal moisture to make the chickpeas even more tender before serving.
