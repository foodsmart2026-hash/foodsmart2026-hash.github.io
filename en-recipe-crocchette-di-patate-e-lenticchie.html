Potato and Lentil Croquettes

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A nutritious snack that transforms pulses into a finger food loved by the whole family thanks to its delicious texture. This budget-friendly recipe uses basic Italian pantry staples to create a balanced, high-fibre starter.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes500 g
  • Cooked lentils (boiled or canned, well drained)240 g
  • Egg1 pz
  • Wholemeal breadcrumbs100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Chopped fresh parsley10 g
  • Organic lemon zest5 g
  • Unrefined sea salt3 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Wash the potatoes and boil them whole with their skins in plenty of lightly salted water for about 25-30 minutes, until a fork pierces them easily.
  2. Drain the potatoes, peel them while still hot and mash them with a potato ricer into a large bowl, then leave to cool slightly to avoid cooking the egg prematurely.
  3. Thoroughly rinse the pre-cooked lentils under running water to remove the canning liquid and excess salt, then pat them dry well with kitchen paper.
  4. Take half of the lentils and coarsely blend them with a hand blender, leaving the other half whole to add texture and variety to the bite.
  5. Add the lentils (both the puree and the whole ones), the egg, parsley, lemon zest, and 40 g of wholemeal breadcrumbs to the mashed potatoes.
  6. Knead vigorously by hand until you obtain a smooth and firm mixture; if it feels too sticky, add an extra tablespoon of breadcrumbs.
  7. Shape into cylindrical croquettes or walnut-sized flattened patties, then coat them evenly in the remaining breadcrumbs (60 g), pressing down well.
  8. Arrange the croquettes on a baking tray lined with baking parchment, drizzle with a little extra virgin olive oil and bake at 200°C (fan oven) for 20 minutes, turning them halfway through until they are golden brown.
