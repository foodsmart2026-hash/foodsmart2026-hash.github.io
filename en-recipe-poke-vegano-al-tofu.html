Vegan tofu poke

United States · 2 servings · Prep 0 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

Hawaiian poke with marinated tofu and edamame.

Ingredients

  • Sushi rice200 g
  • Tofu200 g
  • Shelled edamame (frozen or fresh)100 g
  • Avocado1 piece (about 150 g pulp)
  • Cucumber1 piece (about 200 g)
  • Soy sauce45 ml
  • Sesame seeds10 g
  • Rice vinegar30 ml
  • Granulated sugar5 g
  • Fine salt3 g
  • Sesame oil10 ml
  • Fresh grated ginger5 g

Allergens

Gluten, Sesame, Soy

Method

  1. Rice washing: Rinse the rice in cold water 4-5 times until the water runs completely clear to remove excess starch.
  2. Rice cooking: Place rice in a pot with 240 ml of water. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on very low heat for 12 minutes. Turn off heat and let rest covered for 10 minutes.
  3. Rice seasoning (Sushi-su): In a small pan, lightly heat the rice vinegar with sugar and salt until dissolved. Gently fold the liquid into the rice using cutting motions, without mashing it.
  4. Marinating: Cut tofu into even 1.5 cm cubes. Marinate in a bowl with soy sauce and grated ginger for at least 15 minutes.
  5. Edamame: Blanch edamame in lightly salted water for 3-4 minutes. Drain and immediately cool in ice water to maintain the bright green color.
  6. Vegetable prep: Peel the avocado and slice it thinly. Wash the cucumber and slice into rounds or thin ribbons using a mandoline.
  7. Tofu searing: Heat sesame oil in a non-stick pan. Sauté the tofu cubes with their marinade for 5-6 minutes over medium-high heat until golden and slightly caramelized.
  8. Assembly: Divide the rice into two bowls. Neatly arrange tofu, avocado, cucumber, and edamame on top. Garnish with sesame seeds.
