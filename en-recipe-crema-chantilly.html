Chantilly Cream

2 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • fresh whipping cream (at least 35% fat)500 ml
  • powdered sugar60 g
  • vanilla extract1 tsp

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Place the cream in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours before preparation. The bowl and the whisk attachments should also be very cold.
  2. Pour the cold cream into the bowl and start whipping it at medium-low speed.
  3. When the cream begins to gain volume, gradually add the sifted powdered sugar.
  4. Incorporate the vanilla seeds or extract, continuing to whip.
  5. Slightly increase the speed and work until you reach a light and stable consistency, with soft or medium peaks according to the intended use.
  6. Stop the whisking as soon as the cream holds its shape: continuing to whip could turn it into butter.
  7. Transfer to a piping bag or a covered container.
  8. Store in the refrigerator and preferably use within a few hours to obtain the best consistency.

Chef's tips

Tips, errors, and alternatives:

- In Italian pastry the term crema chantilly is often also used for crema diplomatica, prepared by combining pastry cream and whipped cream.

This recipe is instead the classic chantilly.

- Do not use cream at room temperature.

- For a more stable chantilly you can use a specific stabilizing product for cream.

- You can flavor it with orange zest or cinnamon.

- Do not freeze the preparation if you want to maintain its original consistency.
