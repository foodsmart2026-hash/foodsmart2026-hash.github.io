Chickpea and Sweet Paprika Shakshuka

💰 Save Euro · about €1.26 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A nutritious and filling version of the Middle Eastern classic, enriched with pulses for a complete meal. Perfect for a convivial family dinner using simple cupboard staples.

Ingredients

  • Large eggs4 pcs
  • Canned chickpeas400 g
  • Tomato pulp (chopped canned tomatoes)500 g
  • Brown onion1 pcs
  • Red pepper1 pcs
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Sweet paprika10 g
  • Ground cumin5 g
  • Salt and pepperq.b.

Allergens

Eggs

Method

  1. Finely slice the onion and cut the pepper into small cubes of about 1 cm to ensure even cooking.
  2. In a large frying pan, heat the oil and sauté the onion and pepper over medium heat for 8 minutes until tender and lightly golden.
  3. Add the paprika and cumin, toasting the spices for 1 minute to release the essential oils and intensify the dish's aroma.
  4. Pour in the tomato pulp and the rinsed chickpeas; season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 15 minutes to allow the flavours to meld and the sauce to thicken.
  5. Using a spoon, create four small wells in the sauce and gently crack an egg into each space, taking care not to break the yolk.
  6. Cover with a lid and cook for 5-7 minutes: the white should be opaque and firm, while the yolk should remain creamy and runny.
  7. Turn off the heat when you see a thin white film covering the yolk, a sign that the egg is cooked but still soft inside.
