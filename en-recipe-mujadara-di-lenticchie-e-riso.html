Lentil and Rice Mujadara

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A pillar of Middle Eastern cuisine, humble yet incredibly rich in flavour thanks to caramelised onions. It is a complete protein dish thanks to the combination of grains and legumes.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🌍 International

Ingredients

  • Basmati or long-grain rice200 g
  • Dried brown lentils200 g
  • Large yellow onions3 pcs
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Cumin seeds or powder5 g
  • Ground cinnamon1 g
  • Saltq.b.

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils and cook them in plenty of water for about 15-20 minutes; they should be tender but not mushy.
  2. While the lentils are cooking, slice the onions into very thin rings for even caramelisation.
  3. In a large frying pan, heat the oil and fry the onions over medium-low heat for 20 minutes until they become dark brown and crispy.
  4. Remove half of the onions and set them aside on kitchen paper; these will be used for the final garnish.
  5. In the pan with the remaining onions, add the raw rice and spices (cumin and cinnamon), toasting for 2 minutes.
  6. Add the cooked and drained lentils to the rice, cover with 400 ml of boiling water and season with salt.
  7. Cover with a tight-fitting lid and cook on the lowest heat for 12-15 minutes, until the water has been completely absorbed.
  8. Turn off the heat, let it rest covered for 5 minutes and serve decorated with the crispy onions set aside.
