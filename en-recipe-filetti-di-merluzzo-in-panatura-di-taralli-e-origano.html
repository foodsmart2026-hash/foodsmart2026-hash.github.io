Cod Fillets in a Taralli and Oregano Crust

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 18 min · Medium

Transform simple frozen fish fillets into a gourmet dish using leftover or affordable taralli. The crunchy and aromatic crust protects the white cod meat, keeping it juicy.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Cod fillets (fresh or frozen)600 g
  • Classic oil taralli (crunchy Italian savoury snack rings)100 g
  • Dried oregano1 cucchiaino
  • Lemon zest1 pcs
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Saltq.b.

Allergens

Fish, Gluten

Method

  1. If using frozen cod, let it defrost completely in the fridge and pat it very dry with kitchen paper.
  2. Place the taralli in a food bag and crush them with a rolling pin until you get a coarse crumb.
  3. In a bowl, combine the taralli crumbs, oregano, grated lemon zest and a drizzle of oil, stirring to coat the crumbs.
  4. Arrange the fish fillets on a baking tray lined with parchment paper and salt them lightly.
  5. Cover the surface of each fillet with a generous layer of the taralli mixture, pressing with your fingers to make it stick.
  6. Bake at 190°C for about 15-18 minutes, depending on the thickness of the fillets.
  7. Switch on the grill for the last 2 minutes to make the taralli crust extremely crunchy and amber-coloured.
  8. The fish is ready when the flesh flakes easily with a fork but remains white and glistening.
  9. Serve immediately with a slice of fresh lemon to cleanse the palate.
