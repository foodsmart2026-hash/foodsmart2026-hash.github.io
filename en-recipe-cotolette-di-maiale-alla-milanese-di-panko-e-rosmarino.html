Panko and Rosemary Pork Milanese Cutlets

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

An affordable version of the classic Milanese cutlet, using pork loin instead of veal. The 'panko' style breading (obtained by coarsely crushing dry bread) guarantees an unbeatable crunch.

Ingredients

  • Pork loin slices4 pcs
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Coarsely grated stale bread120 g
  • Fresh rosemary1 rametto
  • Plain flour (Type 0 flour)30 g
  • Vegetable oil for frying100 ml
  • Saltq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Make small cuts on the edges of the pork loin slices to prevent them from curling during cooking.
  2. Flatten the meat with a meat mallet between two sheets of parchment paper until it doubles in surface area.
  3. Prepare three plates: one with flour, one with beaten and salted eggs, and one with the crushed bread mixed with finely chopped rosemary.
  4. Pass the meat first through the flour (shake off the excess), then the egg, and finally the bread, pressing firmly with the palm of your hand.
  5. Let the breaded cutlets rest in the fridge for 10 minutes: this trick helps the breading stay attached.
  6. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan; it must be very hot (about 170°C) so as not to soak the meat in grease.
  7. Fry one cutlet at a time for about 3 minutes per side until they reach a deep hazelnut colour.
  8. Drain on kitchen paper to remove excess oil and salt only an instant before serving.
  9. Accompany with a fresh salad or lemon wedges to balance the fried flavours.
