Savoy Cabbage Carpaccio with Lemon, Pears and Pecorino

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 45 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

A fresh and crunchy starter where raw Savoy cabbage is softened by a citrus marinade and paired with the sweetness of pears.

Ingredients

  • Savoy cabbage (heart only)400 g
  • Abate pears2
  • Pecorino Romano PDO shavings80 g
  • Walnut halves40 g
  • Lemon (juice)1
  • Extra virgin olive oil4 tbsp
  • Fine saltto taste
  • Ground black pepperto taste

Allergens

Tree nuts, Milk

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the cabbage and remove the tougher outer leaves.
  2. Use a mandoline to slice the cabbage heart into very fine, almost transparent strips.
  3. In a small bowl, prepare an emulsion by mixing the lemon juice, extra virgin olive oil, salt and pepper.
  4. Place the cabbage in a large bowl and drizzle with the prepared emulsion.
  5. Massage the cabbage with your hands for about 2 minutes: this step is essential to break down the fibres and make it tender.
  6. Cover the bowl and leave to marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes.
  7. Just before serving, peel the pears and cut them into very thin slices, coating them with a few drops of lemon juice to prevent browning.
  8. Assemble the dish by arranging the marinated cabbage at the base, adding the pear slices, pecorino shavings and roughly chopped walnuts.
