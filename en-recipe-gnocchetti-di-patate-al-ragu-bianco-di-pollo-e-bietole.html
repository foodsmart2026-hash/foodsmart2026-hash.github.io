Potato Gnocchetti with White Chicken Ragù and Swiss Chard

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

Soft homemade or store-bought gnocchetti (salt-free), seasoned with a very light ragù. The Swiss chard adds iron and colour in a delicate way.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Small potato gnocchetti (small Italian potato dumplings)300 g
  • Chicken breast140 g
  • Swiss chard (leaves only)100 g
  • 30-month aged Parmigiano Reggiano (hard Italian cow's milk cheese)15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Finely chop the chicken with a knife or pulse it briefly in a food processor to obtain a texture suitable for little chewers.
  2. Wash the chard, remove the tough stalks and finely chop the tender leaves into thin strips.
  3. In a pan, cook the chicken with a tablespoon of water and the chard, covering with a lid for about 8-10 minutes.
  4. If the sauce dries out too much, add a little of the pasta cooking water to keep it creamy.
  5. Bring a pot of water (without salt) to the boil and drop in the gnocchetti.
  6. Drain the gnocchetti as soon as they rise to the surface, using a slotted spoon so they don't break.
  7. Toss the gnocchetti in the pan with the chicken and chard ragù for a minute so they absorb the flavours well.
  8. Complete with the extra virgin olive oil and the very finely grated Parmigiano, which will act as a natural flavour enhancer.
  9. Cut the gnocchetti in half before serving if they are still too large for the child.
