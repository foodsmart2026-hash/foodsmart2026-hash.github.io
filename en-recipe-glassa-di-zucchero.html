Sugar glaze

2 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • icing sugar150 g
  • hot water25 ml
  • lemon juice5 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Gluten, Milk.

Method

  1. Sift the icing sugar into a bowl to remove any lumps.
  2. Add the lemon juice and mix with a spatula.
  3. Pour in the hot water a little at a time, continuing to mix.
  4. Work until you obtain a smooth and homogeneous glaze.
  5. Check the consistency: it should drip slowly from the spatula without being completely liquid.
  6. If it is too thick, add a few drops of water; if it is too runny, incorporate more icing sugar.
  7. Use immediately to decorate cookies, donuts, and cakes.
  8. Allow to dry completely before stacking or packaging the sweets.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not add too much water all at once.

- For a whiter glaze, omit the lemon juice.

- For a colored variation, use food coloring.

- You can replace the water with orange juice.
