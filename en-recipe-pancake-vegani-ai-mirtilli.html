Vegan blueberry pancakes

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Fluffy pancakes made with plant-based milk and blueberries, without eggs or dairy.

These pancakes are a soft plant-based breakfast alternative, perfect for starting the day with sweetness. Their spongy texture holds fresh blueberries that burst with flavor in every bite, complemented by the touch of maple syrup.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour200 g
  • Oat milk280 ml
  • Cane sugar25 g
  • Baking powder10 g
  • Sunflower oil25 ml
  • Blueberries120 g
  • Maple syrup60 ml

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. In a large bowl, carefully sift 200 g of 00 flour together with 10 g of baking powder and add 25 g of brown sugar. Mix well with a hand whisk to aerate the powders and ensure the baking powder is evenly distributed, avoiding the formation of unpleasant lumps in the final result.
  2. In a separate container, combine 280 ml of oat milk and 25 ml of sunflower oil, mixing them briefly with a fork. This liquid emulsion is essential to obtain a homogeneous batter and a velvety final texture without weighing down the pancake's structure.
  3. Gradually pour the liquids into the bowl of powders, mixing with a whisk using quick but gentle movements. Stop as soon as the ingredients are combined and you see the traces of flour disappear: do not overwork the batter to keep the pancakes incredibly soft and light as clouds.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat and, if necessary, lightly grease it with a drop of sunflower oil. Wait until the surface is hot before proceeding, so that the batter starts cooking immediately while maintaining a perfect and regular circular shape.
  5. Pour a small ladle of batter into the center of the hot pan and immediately place a handful of the 120 g of fresh blueberries on top. Let it cook for about 2 minutes without touching the pancake, until you see small bubbles appear on the surface and the edges begin to look slightly dry and matte.
  6. With the help of a spatula, flip the pancake with a firm movement and continue cooking for about 1 minute on the other side. You will know it is ready when both sides have taken on a beautiful, even golden color and the aroma of freshly baked sweets fills the kitchen.
  7. Transfer the ready pancake to a warm plate and repeat the process until the batter is finished, keeping the heat of the pan constant. If you notice the pancakes browning too quickly, lower the heat slightly to ensure even cooking and a perfectly cooked center.
  8. Assemble your breakfast by stacking the pancakes and garnishing them with the remaining blueberries from the 120 g total. Finally, generously pour the 60 ml of maple syrup over the entire stack, letting its enveloping sweetness penetrate between the fluffy layers for a truly unforgettable experience.

Tips

  • Mix the batter only as much as needed to combine the ingredients, leaving a few small lumps for extra fluffiness.
  • Add the blueberries directly onto the batter once poured into the pan to ensure an even distribution.
  • You can store cooked pancakes in the fridge for two days and reheat them briefly in a toaster to restore their fragrance.

Variations

  • Swap the blueberries for dark chocolate chips for an even more indulgent version.
  • Use almond milk instead of oat milk for a more intense toasted aftertaste.
