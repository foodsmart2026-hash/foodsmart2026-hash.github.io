Vegan blueberry pancakes

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Fluffy pancakes made with plant-based milk and blueberries, without eggs or dairy.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour200 g
  • Oat milk280 ml
  • Cane sugar25 g
  • Baking powder10 g
  • Sunflower oil25 ml
  • Blueberries120 g
  • Maple syrup60 ml

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. Add the oat milk and oil and mix without overworking the batter.
  3. Heat a lightly greased non-stick frying pan.
  4. Pour in a small ladleful of batter, add a few blueberries, and cook for 2 minutes until bubbles appear.
  5. Flip and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Serve with the maple syrup and remaining blueberries.
