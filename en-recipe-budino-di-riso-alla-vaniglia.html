Vanilla Rice Pudding

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A thick and nutritious breakfast inspired by grandmother's rice treats, very economical and filling. Excellent both warm in winter and cold in summer.

Ingredients

  • Originario rice (A short-grain Italian rice variety ideal for puddings)150 g
  • Whole milk800 ml
  • Sugar60 g
  • Vanilla extract5 ml
  • Lemon (whole zest peel)1 pcs
  • Ground cinnamon1 pizzico

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Place the milk in a large saucepan along with the sugar, the lemon zest (one large piece that is easy to remove), and the vanilla.
  2. Bring the milk to a light boil over medium heat, stirring occasionally to dissolve the sugar.
  3. Pour the rice into the milk and lower the heat to minimum, letting it cook very slowly.
  4. Stir often, especially towards the end of cooking, as the rice releases starch and tends to stick to the bottom.
  5. Cook for about 25-30 minutes until the rice is very tender and the milk has turned into a thick cream.
  6. Remove the lemon zest before the mixture becomes too firm.
  7. If the pudding seems too dry, add a splash of hot milk at the end to soften it.
  8. Distribute the rice into 4 small bowls and dust the surface with a pinch of cinnamon for aroma.
  9. Serve warm for a comforting breakfast, or leave to cool in the fridge for a firmer texture.
