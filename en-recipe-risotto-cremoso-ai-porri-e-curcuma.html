Creamy Leek and Turmeric Risotto

💰 Save Euro · about €1.14 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A risotto with an intense golden colour and a delicate flavour. Turmeric adds antioxidant properties without weighing down the family budget.

Ingredients

  • Originario rice (an affordable short-grain variety for risotto)320 g
  • Large leeks2 pcs
  • Turmeric powder1 cucchiaino
  • Vegetable stock1.2 L
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Nutritional yeast or Parmesan20 g
  • Salt and pepperq.b.

Allergens

latte_(se_usato_parmigiano)

Method

  1. Slice the leeks into thin rings after removing the base and the toughest green part.
  2. In a saucepan, sauté the leeks with the oil and a pinch of salt for 10 minutes over a gentle heat until they are almost creamy.
  3. Add the rice and let it toast with the leeks for a couple of minutes until it looks translucent.
  4. Add the turmeric powder and stir well to distribute the colour evenly.
  5. Begin adding the hot stock one ladle at a time, waiting for it to be absorbed before adding more.
  6. Stir regularly: constant movement helps the rice release its starch for a natural creaminess.
  7. When the rice is cooked but still firm, turn off the heat and adjust the salt.
  8. Stir in the cheese or nutritional yeast and let the risotto rest covered for a minute before serving.
