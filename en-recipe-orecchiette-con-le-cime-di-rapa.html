Orecchiette with turnip tops

Puglia · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A Puglian classic with turnip tops, garlic, anchovies and chilli.

A hallmark of Apulian cuisine, these orecchiette combine savory anchovies with the slightly bitter notes of turnip tops. The pasta's textured surface perfectly captures the spicy, garlic-infused dressing.

Ingredients

  • Semolina orecchiette360 g
  • Cleaned turnip tops800 g
  • Anchovy fillets in oil20 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Chilli1 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil50 ml
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish
May contain traces of: Soy.

Method

  1. Begin by thoroughly washing the 800 g of cleaned broccoli rabe under fresh running water. Carefully separate the tender leaves and florets from the tougher stalks, cutting the larger florets in half. This step is essential to ensure even cooking across the different parts of the vegetable.
  2. Fill a large pot with plenty of water and bring it to a vigorous boil over high heat. Once boiling, add the 8 g of fine salt, stirring until completely dissolved. Salting the water at this stage allows both the orecchiette and the greens to absorb the flavor correctly from the start.
  3. Submerge the broccoli rabe in the boiling water and let them cook for about 3 minutes over medium heat. You will notice the green of the leaves becoming brighter and the texture starting to soften slightly. Cooking the vegetables before the pasta ensures they release their characteristic aromas directly into the cooking water.
  4. Add the 360 g of semolina orecchiette to the same pot with the broccoli rabe, stirring immediately to prevent them from sticking together. Let them cook for about 10-12 minutes, or until the pasta is al dente. This 'all-in-one' cooking method allows the pasta to soak up the mineral and distinctive flavor of the greens.
  5. While the pasta cooks, peel the 6 g of garlic and finely slice the 1 g of chili pepper. Take a large skillet and pour in the 50 ml of extra virgin olive oil, heating it over low heat. Preparing the aromatic base in advance will ensure the seasoning is ready as soon as the pasta is drained.
  6. Add the garlic, chili, and 20 g of anchovy fillets to the skillet with the hot oil. Mash the anchovies with a wooden spoon until they have completely melted, creating a fragrant and savory paste. Be careful not to burn the garlic; it should only turn lightly golden to release its aroma without becoming bitter.
  7. About one minute before the pasta finishes cooking, reserve a ladleful of the cooking water and set it aside. Drain the orecchiette and broccoli rabe together using a slotted spoon or a colander, being careful not to remove all the residual moisture. The pasta should still be firm to the bite to withstand the final toss in the pan.
  8. Pour the pasta and vegetables into the skillet with the anchovy base, sautéing everything over high heat for about 1 minute. If the seasoning seems too dry, add a splash of the reserved cooking water to bind the ingredients. You will see the oil and starch create a perfect emulsion that coats every orecchietta evenly.
  9. Turn off the heat and give a final vigorous stir to distribute the broccoli rabe and the intense flavor of the anchovies. Serve your orecchiette immediately while hot, ensuring each plate gets its fair share of seasoning and chili. The pungent and inviting aroma will confirm that the dish is ready to be enjoyed in all its authenticity.

Tips

  • Cook the pasta and vegetables in the same water so the semolina absorbs the full flavor of the turnip tops.
  • Melt the anchovies thoroughly in the hot oil until they form a fragrant cream before tossing the pasta.
  • For a milder taste, remove the garlic germ before slicing and sautéing it.

Variations

  • For a crunchy texture, top the dish with breadcrumbs toasted in a pan just before serving.
  • Substitute fresh chili with chili oil to achieve a more persistent heat throughout the dressing.
