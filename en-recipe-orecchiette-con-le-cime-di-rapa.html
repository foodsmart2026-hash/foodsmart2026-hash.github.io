Orecchiette with turnip tops

Puglia · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A Puglian classic with turnip tops, garlic, anchovies and chilli.

Ingredients

  • Semolina orecchiette360 g
  • Cleaned turnip tops800 g
  • Anchovy fillets in oil20 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Chilli1 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil50 ml
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish
May contain traces of: Soy.

Method

  1. Wash the turnip tops and separate the tender leaves and florets.
  2. Bring salted water to the boil, add the turnip tops and, after 3 minutes, the orecchiette.
  3. Meanwhile, heat the oil with the garlic and chilli and melt the anchovies over a low heat.
  4. Drain the pasta and vegetables together when the pasta is al dente.
  5. Toss them in the pan with the seasoned oil for 1 minute and serve.
