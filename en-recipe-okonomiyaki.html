Okonomiyaki

Japan · 2 servings · 30 min · Easy

Japanese cabbage pancake with sauce and mayonnaise.

Often called Japanese pizza, okonomiyaki is a hearty savory pancake made with cabbage and pork belly. Its soft interior and crispy exterior make it a unique dish loved by everyone.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour150 g
  • Dashi stock (or water)150 ml
  • Medium eggs2 pieces
  • Green cabbage300 g
  • Thinly sliced pork belly100 g
  • Okonomiyaki sauce60 g
  • Japanese mayonnaise40 g
  • Sunflower oil15 ml
  • Fine salt2 g
  • Katsuobushi (bonito flakes)4 g
  • Aonori (dried seaweed powder)2 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish, Eggs
May contain traces of: Molluscs, Mustard, Soy.

Method

  1. Wash the cabbage, remove the tough central core, and shred it into very thin julienne or small 5mm cubes.
  2. In a large bowl, sift the flour and add salt; gradually pour in the cold dashi while whisking until a smooth, lump-free batter forms.
  3. Add the eggs to the batter and beat lightly; do not overmix, as the batter should remain airy and light.
  4. Fold the shredded cabbage into the batter using a spatula until every piece is evenly coated.
  5. Heat a non-stick pan or griddle (teppan) over medium heat (approx. 180°C/350°F) with a thin layer of oil.
  6. Pour half of the mixture to form a disc about 2-2.5 cm thick; lay 3-4 slices of pork belly side-by-side on top.
  7. Cover with a lid and cook for 5 minutes. Once the bottom is golden brown, flip the okonomiyaki using two spatulas.
  8. Cook for another 5 minutes uncovered, pressing down slightly to ensure even cooking, until the pork is crispy.
  9. Flip one last time for 1-2 minutes, then transfer to a serving plate.
  10. Garnish by drizzling okonomiyaki sauce and mayonnaise in a grid pattern; finish with a sprinkle of aonori and katsuobushi.

Tips

  • Do not press down on the pancake with the spatula while cooking to keep the texture light and airy.
  • Ensure the cabbage is julienned very thinly so it cooks evenly within the diet.
  • Apply the sauce and mayonnaise in thin strips for a classic street food presentation.

Variations

  • Add pieces of squid to the batter for a seafood-style version.
  • Use grated cheese over the pork belly for an even richer and gooier result.
