Sautéed Broccoli with Breadcrumbs and Anchovies

💰 Save Euro · about €0.97 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A tasty and crunchy way to enjoy broccoli, using simple ingredients from the Italian larder. The saltiness of the anchovies enhances the sweetness of the cabbage, making it irresistible.

Ingredients

  • Broccoli (florets)800 g
  • Stale bread (breadcrumbs)60 g
  • Anchovy fillets in oil4 pcs
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Dried chilli flakes1 pinch
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Fish

Method

  1. Divide the broccoli into equal-sized florets and wash them under running water to remove any soil residue.
  2. Blanch the florets in lightly salted water for 4-5 minutes: they should turn bright green and remain crunchy, not mushy.
  3. Drain the broccoli and immediately plunge them into cold water to stop the cooking and maintain the vivid colour.
  4. In a large pan, heat half the oil with the crushed garlic and chilli, then add the anchovy fillets until they dissolve completely.
  5. In a separate small pan, toast the coarsely crumbled breadcrumbs with the rest of the oil until they become golden and very crunchy.
  6. Add the broccoli to the pan with the anchovy base and sauté over a high heat for 3-4 minutes, allowing the florets to absorb the flavour well.
  7. Check the cooking: the tips of the broccoli should start to take on a light brown roasted colour.
  8. Remove the garlic, transfer to a serving dish, and sprinkle generously with the crunchy breadcrumbs just before serving.
