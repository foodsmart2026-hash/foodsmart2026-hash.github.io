Grandma's Baked Apple Fritters

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 18 min · Medium

A lighter and healthier version of the classic fried fritters. The apple remains soft inside a fragrant batter, making them perfect for children.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Large apples (Fuji or Gala type)2 pcs
  • Plain flour (Type 00, a finely ground Italian flour)150 g
  • Semi-skimmed milk120 ml
  • Medium egg1 pcs
  • Sugar30 g
  • Baking powder (Italian 'lievito per dolci')5 g
  • Cinnamonq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. In a bowl, prepare the batter by whisking the egg with the sugar and gradually adding the milk.
  2. Sift the flour with the baking powder and cinnamon, then incorporate it into the liquid mixture, whisking until you have a thick cream.
  3. Peel the apples, remove the core with a corer, and cut them into round slices (rings) about 1 cm thick.
  4. Dip each apple slice into the batter, ensuring it is completely coated on both sides.
  5. Place the apple rings on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, spacing them slightly apart.
  6. Bake in a fan oven at 190°C for about 15-18 minutes, turning them halfway through if necessary for even browning.
  7. The fritters are ready when the batter appears puffed up, golden, and slightly crispy at the edges.
  8. Remove from the oven and let them cool for a couple of minutes to allow the structure to stabilise.
  9. Serve with a final dusting of icing sugar or a drizzle of warm honey.
