Baked Bread and Chocolate Pudding

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

An anti-waste rescue recipe that transforms stale bread into a creamy, delicious dessert. Reminiscent of childhood flavours and costing just a few pence per serving.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Stale bread (crusty loaf type)200 g
  • Whole milk500 ml
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Caster sugar50 g
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder30 g
  • Dark chocolate chips20 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Cut the stale bread into approximately 2 cm cubes, removing any overly hard crusts if necessary, and place them in a large bowl.
  2. Heat the milk without boiling it and pour it hot over the bread; mash with a fork to allow all the liquid to be absorbed.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs with the sugar and sifted cocoa until you obtain a frothy, dark mixture.
  4. Combine the egg mix with the soaked bread, stirring well to create a homogeneous mass without dry bread lumps.
  5. Add the chocolate chips which will provide a crunchy and intense note to the finished dessert.
  6. Lightly butter an ovenproof dish and pour in the mixture, levelling it with the back of a spoon.
  7. Bake in the oven at 180°C for about 40 minutes: the pudding should puff up slightly and form a crust on the surface.
  8. To check the cooking, press the centre: it should feel firm but still soft, not excessively dry.
  9. Serve lukewarm or cold; it is perfect accompanied by a dusting of icing sugar or a thin layer of orange marmalade.
