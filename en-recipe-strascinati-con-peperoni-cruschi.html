Strascinati with crispy Senise peppers

Basilicata · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Fresh pasta from Basilicata with crunchy dried peppers, garlic and toasted breadcrumbs.

Ingredients

  • Semolina strascinati pasta360 g
  • Peperoni cruschi (dried Senise peppers)30 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Garlic6 g
  • Breadcrumbs40 g
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Soy, Eggs.

Method

  1. Boil the strascinati in plenty of salted water.
  2. Meanwhile, remove the seeds and stems from the dried peppers.
  3. Fry them for a few seconds per side in 40 ml of hot oil until they become crunchy; drain and crumble them.
  4. In the same oil, brown the garlic and toast the breadcrumbs with the remaining oil for 2 minutes.
  5. Drain the pasta and toss it in the pan with the infused oil.
  6. Serve topped with the crumbled peppers and toasted breadcrumbs.
