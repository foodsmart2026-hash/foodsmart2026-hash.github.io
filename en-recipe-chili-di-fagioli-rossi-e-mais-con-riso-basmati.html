Red Kidney Bean and Sweetcorn Chilli with Basmati Rice

💰 Save Euro · about €1.14 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

An international 'comfort food' classic revisited with an affordable plant-based twist. The red beans and rice form a complete protein, making it a filling and energetic meal.

Ingredients

  • Tinned red kidney beans (drained)480 g
  • Tinned sweetcorn140 g
  • Tomato passata400 ml
  • Basmati or Ribe rice240 g
  • Red bell pepper1 pcs
  • Red onion1 pcs
  • Ground cumin1 cucchiaino

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Start by rinsing the rice well under running water to remove excess starch, which ensures the grains stay separate and fluffy after cooking.
  2. Boil the rice in plenty of salted water according to the packet instructions, drain it and keep it warm covered with a tea towel.
  3. Chop the onion and cut the pepper into small cubes; sauté both in a pot with a tablespoon of oil for about 7-8 minutes until softened.
  4. Add the cumin and tomato passata to the vegetables, season with salt and leave to simmer over a low heat for 10 minutes to reduce the sauce.
  5. Add the red beans and sweetcorn to the pot, having rinsed them well from their canning liquid, which may contain excess sodium.
  6. Continue cooking for another 10 minutes, allowing the beans to absorb the flavour of the tomato and spices; the consistency should be thick and not watery.
  7. Taste and adjust the salt or chilli according to personal preference before serving.
  8. Serve the chilli piping hot in large bowls, accompanied by the white rice on the side to allow diners to mix the flavours gradually.
