Tomato sauce

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • tomato passata800 g
  • extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • small onion1
  • garlic1 clove
  • fresh basil leaves8
  • Saltto taste
  • sugarto taste

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Peel the onion and finely chop it. Lightly crush the garlic without mashing it into a pulp.
  2. Pour the oil into a saucepan and add the onion. Let it soften over low heat for about 5 minutes without letting it burn.
  3. Add the garlic, if using, and let it sauté for about 30 seconds to release its aroma.
  4. Pour in the tomato passata, stir, and slowly bring to a boil.
  5. Lower the heat, add salt, and let it cook for 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. If the tomatoes are overly acidic, consider adding a pinch of sugar, without using it automatically.
  7. Once cooking is finished, turn off the heat and add the hand-torn basil.
  8. Stir and use the sauce to season pasta, gnocchi, meatballs, or baked dishes.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not overcook the onion: it should become soft and translucent, not brown.

- Do not cook over too high heat: the tomato sauce might reduce excessively.

- For a rustic sauce, use crushed peeled tomatoes.

- For a richer version, add olives, capers, or vegetables.

- For a milder flavor, you can replace the onion with a shallot.
