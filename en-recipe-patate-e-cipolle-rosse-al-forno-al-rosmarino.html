Roasted Potatoes and Red Onions with Rosemary

💰 Save Euro · about €0.47 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A rustic and crispy side dish that enhances the sweetness of caramelised red onions along with the savouriness of potatoes. It is the perfect choice to accompany any main course with minimal expense.

Ingredients

  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes800 g
  • Red onions200 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Fresh or dried rosemary2 sprigs

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into roughly 2 cm cubes to ensure even cooking.
  2. Trim the red onions and cut them into thick wedges so they won't burn during the long cooking time.
  3. Put the vegetables in a large bowl and season them with oil, salt, pepper, and the rosemary needles.
  4. Mix well with your hands to distribute the seasoning over every piece of vegetable.
  5. Line a baking tray with baking parchment and spread the potatoes and onions in a single layer without overlapping them.
  6. Bake at 200°C in a fan oven for about 20 minutes until the edges of the potatoes start to turn golden.
  7. Take the tray out and gently turn the vegetables with a spatula to encourage even browning.
  8. Continue cooking for another 15 minutes until the potatoes are crispy on the outside and tender on the inside.
  9. Serve the side dish piping hot to appreciate the contrast between the softness of the onion and the crunchiness of the potato.
