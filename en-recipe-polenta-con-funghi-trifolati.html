Polenta with sautéed mushrooms

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Easy

Soft polenta with button mushrooms and porcini sautéed with garlic and parsley.

Ingredients

  • Cornmeal300 g
  • Water1200 ml
  • Button mushrooms400 g
  • Dried porcini mushrooms20 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Parsley10 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Coarse salt10 g
  • Fine salt3 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Soak the porcini in lukewarm water for 20 minutes, squeeze them dry and chop them.
  2. Bring the salted water to the boil and pour in the cornmeal in a steady stream, whisking constantly.
  3. Cook the polenta for 40 minutes, stirring frequently.
  4. Sauté the garlic in the oil, add the sliced button mushrooms and porcini, and cook for 10 minutes over a high heat.
  5. Season with salt and finish with the chopped parsley.
  6. Serve the polenta on plates topped with the mushrooms.
