Polenta with sautéed mushrooms

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Easy

Soft polenta with button mushrooms and porcini sautéed with garlic and parsley.

Ingredients

  • Cornmeal300 g
  • Water1200 ml
  • Button mushrooms400 g
  • Dried porcini mushrooms20 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Parsley10 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Coarse salt10 g
  • Fine salt3 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Begin by soaking the 20 g of dried porcini mushrooms in a bowl of lukewarm water for about 20 minutes. Once rehydrated, squeeze them carefully to remove excess water and chop them coarsely on a cutting board. This process allows the porcini to release all their intense aroma during the subsequent cooking phase.
  2. While the porcini are soaking, carefully clean the 400 g of button mushrooms (champignons) by removing any soil and slice them thinly. Finely mince the 6 g of garlic and 10 g of fresh parsley. Having all ingredients ready will help you better manage the cooking times for both the polenta and the mushrooms.
  3. Pour the 1200 ml of water into a cauldron or a large, thick-bottomed pot and add the 10 g of coarse salt. Bring everything to a boil over high heat. The consistent heat of the water is essential for the correct hydration of the cornmeal granules from the very start.
  4. As soon as the water boils, lower the heat slightly and rain in the 300 g of cornmeal very slowly. Stir vigorously with a hand whisk to prevent any unpleasant lumps from forming. Keep stirring until the mixture begins to thicken slightly and becomes creamy.
  5. Cook the polenta for about 40 minutes over low heat, stirring frequently with a wooden spoon. You will know it is ready when it pulls away easily from the sides of the pot and has a velvety, dense consistency. Slow cooking is the secret to removing any raw flour taste and making it perfectly digestible.
  6. About 15 minutes before the polenta is ready, heat the 60 ml of extra virgin olive oil in a large non-stick skillet. Add the 6 g of minced garlic and the previously soaked porcini, sautéing them over medium heat for a couple of minutes. You will smell an irresistible woody aroma that will serve as the flavorful base of the dish.
  7. Add the 400 g of sliced button mushrooms to the skillet and increase the heat. Cook over high heat for about 10 minutes, tossing them often so the mushrooms turn golden and lose their vegetation water. The mushrooms should be tender but still firm to the bite, not overly soft.
  8. Towards the end of the mushroom cooking time, add the 3 g of fine salt and the 10 g of chopped parsley you prepared earlier. Stir well to distribute the flavors evenly throughout the seasoning. Adding the parsley at the last moment ensures it keeps its bright green color and fresh herbal scent.
  9. Turn off the heat for the polenta and let it rest for just one minute in the pot to stabilize its structure. Distribute the hot, steaming polenta into deep plates and place a generous portion of the sautéed mushrooms on top. Serve immediately to enjoy the contrast between the creaminess of the corn and the savory taste of the sautéed mushrooms.
