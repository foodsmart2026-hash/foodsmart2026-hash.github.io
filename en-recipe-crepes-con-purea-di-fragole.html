Crêpes with strawberry purée

Kid-friendly

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Thin pancakes filled with fresh strawberry purée, with no added sugar in the filling. Note: contains eggs, milk and gluten; cut into thin strips.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour100 g
  • Whole milk250 ml
  • Egg50 g
  • Sugar10 g
  • Butter10 g
  • Strawberries300 g
  • Ripe banana100 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Soy.

Method

  1. Blend the flour, milk, egg and sugar until you have a smooth batter; leave to rest for 10 minutes.
  2. Grease a non-stick frying pan with a light coating of butter.
  3. Cook one small ladle of batter at a time, for 1 minute per side.
  4. Blend the washed strawberries with the banana.
  5. Spread the purée onto the crêpes and fold them into fans.
  6. For younger children, cut into thin strips.
