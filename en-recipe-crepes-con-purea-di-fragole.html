Crêpes with strawberry purée

Kid-friendly

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Thin pancakes filled with fresh strawberry purée, with no added sugar in the filling. Note: contains eggs, milk and gluten; cut into thin strips.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour100 g
  • Whole milk250 ml
  • Egg50 g
  • Sugar10 g
  • Butter10 g
  • Strawberries300 g
  • Ripe banana100 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Soy.

Method

  1. In a large bowl, sift 100 g of 00 flour and add 10 g of sugar, mixing well with a hand whisk. Add the 50 g egg and slowly pour in 250 ml of whole milk, whisking vigorously to avoid the formation of annoying lumps. You will obtain a fluid, velvety, and homogeneous batter, which is essential for successful thin and flexible crêpes.
  2. Let the batter rest at room temperature for about 10 minutes, covering the bowl with a clean cloth or cling film. This waiting time allows the gluten to relax and small air bubbles to disappear, ensuring an elastic consistency that will not crack during pan-cooking.
  3. While the batter rests, prepare the natural filling by thoroughly washing 300 g of strawberries and peeling 100 g of ripe banana. Using an immersion blender, reduce the two fruits into a smooth and thick puree; the sweetness of the ripe banana will balance the acidity of the strawberries without the need for added sugar.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan of about 20-22 cm over medium heat and grease the surface with a thin layer of the total 10 g of butter, using paper towels to help. The pan is at the ideal temperature when, by pouring a drop of batter, it sizzles instantly without darkening too quickly.
  5. Pour a small ladle of batter into the center of the hot pan and swirl it quickly with a circular motion of the wrist to distribute the liquid over the entire surface. Cook for about 60 seconds over moderate heat until the edges begin to lift on their own and the surface appears dry and matte.
  6. Carefully flip the crêpe using a thin spatula and continue cooking on the other side for about 30-45 seconds. You will know it is perfectly cooked when you see the typical amber spots appearing and smell a delicate scent of biscuit and butter.
  7. Place the ready crêpe on a flat plate and generously spread two tablespoons of the strawberry and banana puree over one half of the surface. Fold the crepe first into a semi-circle and then into a triangle to obtain the classic fan shape, which perfectly encloses the creamy filling.
  8. For an elegant and safe presentation, especially if intended for children, cut the filled crêpes crosswise to obtain thin strips. This technique makes the dish easier to consume and allows you to enjoy the balance between the softness of the dough and the freshness of the fruit in every single bite.
