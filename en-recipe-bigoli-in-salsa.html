Bigoli in salsa

Veneto · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

A long, thick Venetian pasta served with a sauce of braised onions and anchovies.

Ingredients

  • Bigoli360 g
  • White onions300 g
  • Anchovy fillets in oil60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Water100 ml
  • Fine salt4 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish
May contain traces of: Soy, Eggs.

Method

  1. Thinly slice the onions.
  2. Braise them in the oil over a low heat with the water for 20 minutes, until they have reached an almost creamy consistency.
  3. Add the anchovies and stir until they have dissolved into the sauce.
  4. Boil the bigoli in lightly salted water.
  5. Drain and toss them in the sauce for 1 minute with a small ladleful of the cooking water.
