Bigoli in salsa

Veneto · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

A long, thick Venetian pasta served with a sauce of braised onions and anchovies.

Ingredients

  • Bigoli360 g
  • White onions300 g
  • Anchovy fillets in oil60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Water100 ml
  • Fine salt4 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish
May contain traces of: Soy, Eggs.

Method

  1. Start by peeling the 300g of white onions and slicing them very thinly, almost transparent, using a sharp knife. This step is fundamental because the onions must almost disappear into the sauce during cooking, turning into a sweet and velvety base without any coarse pieces.
  2. In a large pan, pour the 60ml of extra virgin olive oil and add the sliced onions. Turn the heat to a very low intensity: the onions should not fry or brown, but only start to soften slowly to release their natural sugars without burning.
  3. Pour the 100ml of water into the pan with the onions and cover with a lid, letting them stew for about 20 minutes over minimum heat. You will know they are ready when they have become very tender, translucent, and almost reduced to a cream, having absorbed the liquid and lost any pungency.
  4. Add the 60g of anchovy fillets in oil to the stewed onions and stir with a wooden spoon. Gently press the fillets against the bottom of the pan until they have completely melted, creating a smooth sauce with an amber color and an intense, savory aroma.
  5. While the sauce finishes flavoring, bring a large pot of water to a boil and add the 4g of fine salt. I recommend using very little salt because the anchovies are already very sapid, and you would risk making the dish too salty.
  6. Submerge the 360g of bigoli in the boiling water and cook them according to the package instructions, making sure to drain them 'al dente'. The rough and full-bodied texture of this pasta is perfect for capturing and holding the onion and anchovy sauce.
  7. Drain the bigoli directly into the sauce pan, reserving a small ladle of cooking water if necessary. Sauté the pasta over high heat for about 1 minute, stirring vigorously so that the oil and starch create a silky emulsion that perfectly coats every strand.
