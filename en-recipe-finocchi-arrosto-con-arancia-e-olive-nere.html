Roasted Fennel with Orange and Black Olives

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A light and elegant side dish that enhances fennel through caramelisation in the oven. The Sicilian pairing with orange and olives makes it perfect for fish dishes.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Large fennel bulbs3 pcs
  • Orange (juice and zest)1 pcs
  • Pitted black olives50 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Dried thyme1 tsp
  • Saltq.b.

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Clean the fennel by removing the green fronds (keep them for decoration) and the tougher outer layers; cut them into 1 cm thick vertical slices.
  2. In a small bowl, prepare an emulsion with the extra virgin olive oil, the strained orange juice, salt, and thyme.
  3. Arrange the fennel slices on a baking tray in a single layer to encourage even browning.
  4. Brush the fennel generously with the orange emulsion, ensuring the liquid penetrates between the fibres.
  5. Add the whole or halved black olives, distributing them among the fennel on the tray.
  6. Bake at 190°C for about 25 minutes; the fennel should become tender and show slightly charred, sweet edges.
  7. Halfway through cooking, gently turn the fennel slices and baste them with the cooking juices that have formed.
  8. Remove from the oven and finish with the grated orange zest and the reserved green fronds for an intense citrus aroma.
