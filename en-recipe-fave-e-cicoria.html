Broad Bean Purée and Chicory

Puglia · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 120 min · Easy

Dried broad bean purée with sautéed chicory: a rustic, vegan dish from Puglia.

Ingredients

  • Dried hulled broad beans300 g
  • Chicory800 g
  • Potato100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Garlic4 g
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Soak the broad beans for 12 hours, then rinse them.
  2. Cook them with the chopped potato, covered with water, over a low heat for about 2 hours, skimming the surface.
  3. Mash the broad beans and potato with a wooden spoon, add 30 ml of oil and season with salt.
  4. Boil the chicory in salted water for 8 minutes and drain.
  5. Sauté it in a frying pan with the remaining oil and the garlic for 3 minutes.
  6. Serve the purée with the chicory on the side and a drizzle of oil.
