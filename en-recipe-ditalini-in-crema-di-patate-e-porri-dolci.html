Ditalini in Creamy Potato and Sweet Leek Sauce

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A thick and velvety soup that uses leek for a delicate but characteristic aroma, replacing the onion. The boiled potato creates a soft base that coats the pasta, making it very easy to swallow.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🧒 Kid-friendly

Ingredients

  • Small ditalini pasta (short, tube-shaped pasta)160 g
  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes250 g
  • Leek (white part only)80 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano (a hard, aged Italian cheese)20 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into very small cubes (about 1 cm) to speed up cooking.
  2. Slice the leek into very thin rounds and wash well to remove any traces of soil.
  3. In a pot, heat a tablespoon of oil and add the leek with two tablespoons of water; let it soften over a very low heat until it becomes translucent.
  4. Add the potatoes and cover with about 700 ml of water; bring to a boil and cook for 20 minutes.
  5. Mash most of the potatoes with a fork directly in the pot to create a coarse cream.
  6. Pour the ditalini directly into the potato cream and cook for the indicated time, stirring often to prevent it from sticking to the bottom.
  7. The pasta should be 'risotto-style', with very little residual liquid and plenty of creaminess.
  8. With the heat off, add the Parmigiano Reggiano and the remaining oil, stirring vigorously.
  9. Serve lukewarm; for adults, a grind of black pepper and a pinch of salt will complete the flavour profile.
