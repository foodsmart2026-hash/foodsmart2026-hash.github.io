Béchamel sauce

6 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Classic homemade béchamel, smooth and lump-free: for lasagne, baked pasta, crêpes and gratins.

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Whole milk1 l
  • Butter80 g
  • Plain flour80 g
  • Nutmegq.b.
  • Fine saltq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Heat the milk in a small pan without boiling it.
  2. In another pan melt the butter over low heat, add the flour all at once and whisk for 1-2 minutes: this is the roux.
  3. Remove from the heat and pour in the hot milk in a thin stream, whisking constantly.
  4. Return to the heat and cook for 5-10 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens and coats the spoon.
  5. Season with salt and a grating of nutmeg.

Chef's tips

Hot roux and hot milk, but poured slowly off the heat: that is the secret to avoiding lumps.

If lumps form, blitz the sauce with a stick blender for a few seconds.

For a thicker béchamel (lasagne) use 100 g butter and 100 g flour per litre.

Cover it with cling film touching the surface so no skin forms.
