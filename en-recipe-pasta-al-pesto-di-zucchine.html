Pasta with zucchini pesto

Kid-friendly

2 servings · 20 min · Easy

A delicate garlic-free pesto, ideal for children.

A delicate and creamy first course, perfect for a quick lunch during the summer season. Steamed zucchinis maintain a vibrant color and a velvety texture that perfectly coats the short pasta.

Ingredients

  • Short pasta (such as ditalini or mini penne)100 g
  • Medium zucchinis200 g
  • Fresh basil4 leaves
  • Parmigiano Reggiano PDO aged 30 months20 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the zucchinis, trim the ends, and cut into uniform cubes of about 1 cm.
  2. Steam the zucchinis for approximately 8-10 minutes until they are very tender to the touch.
  3. Bring a pot of water to a boil without adding salt, as required for the specified age group.
  4. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions, ensuring the texture is soft and easy for a toddler to chew.
  5. While the pasta is cooking, place the steamed zucchinis, washed basil leaves, Parmigiano Reggiano, and extra virgin olive oil into a blender jar.
  6. Blend the ingredients until a smooth and consistent cream is formed; if necessary, add a tablespoon of pasta cooking water to adjust the texture.
  7. Drain the pasta while keeping it slightly moist and transfer it to a warm bowl.
  8. Season immediately with the zucchini pesto, stirring well to incorporate the cream.
  9. Check that the pasta temperature is suitable for consumption (around 37°C/98°F) before serving.

Tips

  • Cool the zucchinis before blending to prevent the basil from darkening due to the heat.
  • Add a ladle of pasta cooking water to make the zucchini cream even smoother.
  • Consume the dish immediately to enjoy the freshness of the ingredients and the texture of the pasta.

Variations

  • You can replace Parmigiano Reggiano with Pecorino Romano for a bolder flavor.
  • Add toasted pine nuts or flaked almonds at the end for a pleasant crunchy note.
