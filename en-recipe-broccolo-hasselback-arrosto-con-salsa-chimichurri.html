Roasted Hasselback Broccoli with Chimichurri Sauce

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A visually striking technique applied to whole broccoli, slow-roasted in the oven and served with an aromatic and punchy chimichurri sauce.

Ingredients

  • Whole medium broccoli heads2
  • Fresh parsley1 bunch
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Dried oregano1 tablespoon
  • Dried chilli flakes1 pinch
  • Red wine vinegar30ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil100ml
  • Coarse saltto taste

Allergens

Sulphites

Method

  1. Clean the broccoli by removing only the outer leaves and the woody base of the stalk, keeping the head intact.
  2. Using a sharp knife, make deep vertical cuts across the entire surface of the broccoli, spaced 1 cm apart, without cutting all the way through to the base.
  3. Blanch the whole broccoli upside down in salted water for 5 minutes, then drain and dry them well.
  4. Prepare the chimichurri by finely chopping the parsley and garlic by hand; combine with the oregano, chilli flakes, vinegar and oil.
  5. Place the broccoli on a baking tray lined with baking parchment and brush generously with half of the sauce, making sure it gets into the cuts.
  6. Cover the tray with a sheet of aluminium foil and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  7. Remove the foil and continue cooking for a further 15-20 minutes until the edges are well-browned and crispy.
  8. Remove from the oven and leave the broccoli to rest for 5 minutes.
  9. Serve the broccoli whole or cut in half, drizzling with the rest of the fresh chimichurri prepared earlier.
