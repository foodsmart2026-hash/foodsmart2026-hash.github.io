Bavette with Savoy Cabbage Pesto and Breadcrumbs

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A recipe that transforms a humble vegetable like Savoy cabbage into a refined and creamy dressing, balanced by the crunchiness of pan-toasted bread.

Ingredients

  • Bavette or Linguine (long, flat pasta strands)320 g
  • Savoy cabbage (inner leaves)400 g
  • Stale bread60 g
  • Dried chilli flakes1 pizzico
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Lemon juice10 ml

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Clean the Savoy cabbage by removing the tougher central ribs and cut the leaves into thin strips.
  2. Blanch the cabbage in salted water for 5 minutes, then drain it, keeping the cooking water to cook the pasta.
  3. Blend the cooked cabbage with 2 tablespoons of oil, the lemon juice, and a bit of the cooking water until you get a smooth pesto.
  4. Coarsely crumble the stale bread and toast it in a pan with a drizzle of oil, garlic, and chilli until golden and crispy.
  5. Cook the bavette in the cabbage water following the times on the package.
  6. Drain the pasta and toss it in the pan with the cabbage pesto, adding water if necessary to make it fluid.
  7. Plate up by sprinkling the spicy breadcrumbs over the top only at the last moment so they don't go soggy.
  8. Serve immediately to enjoy the contrast between the creaminess of the pesto and the crunch of the bread.
