Pearl Spelt Soup with Escarole and Bread Dumplings

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

An ancient and simple comfort food, where escarole adds freshness and bread dumplings make the dish hearty and loved by children.

Ingredients

  • Pearl spelt (a nutty, chewy ancient grain)250 g
  • Fresh escarole (a slightly bitter leafy green)500 g
  • Stale bread100 g
  • Egg1 pcs
  • Grated Parmesan (an aged Italian hard cheese)30 g
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Vegetable stock1.5 L
  • Milk50 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Soak the stale bread in the milk; once soft, squeeze it out well and place it in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg, Parmesan, and a pinch of salt to the bread; knead and form small balls the size of olives.
  3. Wash the escarole and cut it into thick strips.
  4. In a large pot, heat a drizzle of oil with the garlic, then add the escarole and let it wilt for 5 minutes.
  5. Pour in the boiling stock and the previously rinsed pearl spelt.
  6. Cook over medium heat for about 20 minutes (check the package instructions for the spelt).
  7. In the final 5 minutes of cooking, gently drop the bread dumplings into the boiling soup.
  8. The dumplings are ready when they float to the surface and become firm; serve the soup very hot.
