Baked Red Radicchio with Walnuts and Balsamic

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

The heat of the oven softens the bitterness of the radicchio, making it almost sweet. The crunchiness of the walnuts and the acidity of the balsamic vinegar create an elegant and affordable pairing.

Ingredients

  • Red radicchio (Trevigiano or round variety - a bitter Italian leaf vegetable)2 heads
  • Walnut halves40 g
  • Balsamic Vinegar of Modena30 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Tree nuts

Method

  1. Wash the heads of radicchio, dry them well and cut them into four wedges each, keeping the base attached so the leaves don't fall apart.
  2. Arrange the radicchio wedges on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, without overlapping them too much.
  3. Drizzle the radicchio with olive oil, salt, and pepper, lightly massaging the leaves to distribute the seasoning.
  4. Bake at 200°C for about 10-12 minutes: the radicchio should wilt and the tips of the leaves should become slightly crispy.
  5. Halfway through cooking, carefully turn the wedges to ensure even heat distribution.
  6. Remove from the oven and immediately drizzle with balsamic vinegar, which will create a delicious emulsion with the residual heat.
  7. Roughly break the walnut halves over the hot radicchio to preserve their essential oils and crunchiness.
  8. Serve immediately as an ideal accompaniment for creamy cheeses or polenta dishes.
