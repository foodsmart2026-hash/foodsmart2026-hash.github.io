Savoy Cabbage and Apple Stew with Cumin

💰 Save Euro · about €0.82 per person

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

Inspired by Alpine cuisine, this side dish pairs winter Savoy cabbage with the sweetness of apples and the digestive aroma of cumin. Perfect for accompanying roasts or matured cheeses.

Ingredients

  • Savoy cabbage1 pcs
  • Golden Delicious apples2 pcs
  • White onion1 pcs
  • Cumin seeds1 tsp
  • White wine vinegar20 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Vegetable stock100 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Remove the tougher outer leaves of the Savoy cabbage, cut it into quarters, remove the central core, and slice it into thin strips.
  2. Finely slice the white onion and peel the apples, cutting them into cubes of about 1.5 cm so they maintain their shape during cooking.
  3. In a large saucepan, heat the oil and sauté the onion together with the cumin seeds until the onion becomes transparent and the cumin releases its aroma.
  4. Add the sliced cabbage and sauté for 5 minutes, stirring often until it starts to wilt significantly.
  5. Deglaze with the white wine vinegar over a high heat, letting the pungent smell evaporate for about a minute.
  6. Add the apple cubes and the hot vegetable stock, then cover with a lid and lower the heat to minimum.
  7. Cook for about 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the cabbage is tender and the liquid is almost completely absorbed.
  8. Season with salt and black pepper, serving the side dish hot to appreciate the contrast between sharp and sweet.
