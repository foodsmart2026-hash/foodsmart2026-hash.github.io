Fennel Cutlets in Wholemeal Sesame Breadcrumbs

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 15 min · Medium

A crispy transformation of a vegetable often relegated to a boiled side dish. These vegetable cutlets are ideal for getting children to eat greens thanks to the golden breading.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Large fennel bulbs2 pcs
  • Gram flour (Chickpea flour)100 g
  • Cold water150 ml
  • Wholemeal breadcrumbs150 g
  • Sesame seeds30 g
  • Sunflower oil50 ml
  • Sweet paprika5 g

Allergens

Sesame, Gluten

Method

  1. Clean the fennel and cut them into slices about 1 cm thick lengthways, keeping the core intact so they don't fall apart.
  2. Blanch the fennel slices in salted water for 5 minutes: they should soften slightly but remain firm.
  3. Prepare a fluid batter by mixing the gram flour with the water, a pinch of salt, and the paprika, ensuring there are no lumps.
  4. On a flat plate, mix the wholemeal breadcrumbs with the sesame seeds for an aromatic and protein-rich coating.
  5. Dip each fennel slice first into the chickpea batter, removing any excess, and then into the breadcrumb mix.
  6. Press down well with your hands on the breading so that it adheres perfectly to the damp surface of the fennel.
  7. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan and fry the cutlets for 4 minutes per side over a medium heat.
  8. They are ready when the outer crust is brown and crispy, and the sesame seeds are toasted.
  9. Drain on kitchen paper and serve immediately with a wedge of lemon.
