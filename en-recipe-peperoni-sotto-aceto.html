Pickled Bell Peppers

Sicilia · 2 servings · Prep 45 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

Excellent as a winter preserve

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Oil1/2 lt.
  • Vinegar1/2 lt.
  • Sugar500 gr.
  • Yellow bell peppers500 gr.
  • Red bell peppers500 gr.

Allergens

Sulphites

Method

  1. Cut the peppers into strips
  2. In a saucepan, pour the oil, vinegar, and sugar and bring to a boil
  3. Add the peppers and boil for a few minutes
  4. Pour into jars and turn them upside down to create a vacuum seal.
