Milk Buns

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • bread flour500 gr
  • whole milk250 ml
  • sugar50 gr
  • softened butter50 gr
  • medium egg1
  • fresh yeast7 gr
  • salt8 gr
  • 1 egg yolk and 15 ml of milk for brushingas needed

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Slightly warm the milk and dissolve the yeast and sugar in it.
  2. Pour the flour into a bowl, add the egg and the milk with the yeast. Start kneading until you get a uniform mixture.
  3. Incorporate the salt and then the softened butter, a little at a time, waiting for each portion to be absorbed.
  4. Work for about 10–12 minutes until you get a smooth, elastic, and slightly soft dough.
  5. Cover and let rise in a warm place for about 2 hours, until doubled in volume.
  6. Divide the dough into 8 equal portions, form regular balls and place them on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  7. Cover and let rise for another 45–60 minutes. Gently brush with egg yolk and milk.
  8. Bake in a preheated static oven at 180 °C for 18–20 minutes, until you get a golden surface. Let cool on a wire rack.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not use milk that is too hot: it could compromise the yeast activity.

- Add the butter gradually to avoid making the dough greasy.

- For softer buns, cover them with a kitchen towel while cooling.

- You can replace 30 g of sugar with honey.

- For a savory version, reduce the sugar to 15 g and eliminate the sweet brushing.
