Cavolo nero and ricotta malfatti with sage butter

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Lombardia · 4 servings · Prep 40 min · Cooking 10 min · Medium

Naked gnocchi made with a base of cavolo nero and ricotta, soft and flavourful, finished with brown butter.

Ingredients

  • Cavolo nero (leaves only)500 g
  • Well-drained cow's milk ricotta250 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano100 g
  • Plain flour80 g
  • Eggs2
  • Nutmeg1 pinch
  • Butter60 g
  • Fresh sage6 leaves
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Boil the cavolo nero leaves in salted boiling water for 10 minutes.
  2. Drain the leaves, let them cool, and squeeze them very thoroughly with your hands; they must be almost dry.
  3. Finely chop the cavolo nero with a knife or a pulse mixer (do not blend to a purée).
  4. In a large bowl, combine the chopped cavolo nero, ricotta, eggs, parmesan, nutmeg and a pinch of salt.
  5. Gradually add the flour, mixing until you have a thick and slightly sticky dough.
  6. Shape into small quenelles or walnut-sized balls using two spoons or floured hands.
  7. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil and, meanwhile, melt the butter in a large frying pan with the sage leaves until it turns nut-brown.
  8. Drop the malfatti into the water; when they rise to the surface (about 2-3 minutes), let them cook for a further 30 seconds.
  9. Gently lift them out with a slotted spoon directly into the pan with the butter.
  10. Toss gently for one minute and serve with an extra dusting of parmesan.
