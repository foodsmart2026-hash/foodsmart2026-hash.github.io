Soft carrot cake

Kid-friendly

8 servings · Prep 0 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

Soft cake with carrots and almonds.

Ingredients

  • Carrots (cleaned and finely grated)300 g
  • All-purpose flour (00)200 g
  • Blanched almonds (ground into flour)80 g
  • Medium eggs3 pieces
  • Brown sugar80 g
  • Sunflower oil80 ml
  • Baking powder16 g

Allergens

Tree nuts, Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (345°F) in static mode and grease a 22 cm baking pan.
  2. Wash, peel, and finely grate the carrots; if they are too watery, squeeze them slightly between your hands.
  3. Grind the almonds in a mixer with a tablespoon of the sugar until they become a very fine powder to ensure child safety.
  4. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs with the remaining brown sugar for at least 5 minutes until the mixture is light and fluffy.
  5. Slowly pour in the sunflower oil while gently mixing with a hand whisk.
  6. Gradually incorporate the sifted flour and baking powder, alternating with the almond flour.
  7. Finally, add the grated carrots and fold them in with a spatula using bottom-to-top movements to keep the batter airy.
  8. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 40 minutes, checking with a toothpick inserted in the center.
  9. Remove from the oven and let cool completely before cutting into small pieces suitable for the child to pick up and chew.
