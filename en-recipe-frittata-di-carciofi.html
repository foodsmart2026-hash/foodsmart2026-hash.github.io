Artichoke frittata

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

🌿 Seasonal recipe🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • eggs6
  • artichokes5
  • parmesan cheese40 g
  • garlic1 clove
  • extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Parsleyto taste
  • Salt and pepperto taste
  • lemon1

Allergens

Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Clean the artichokes, remove the tough leaves and cut them into thin slices.
  2. Immerse them in water and lemon to prevent oxidation.
  3. Heat the oil in a pan with the garlic and add the drained artichokes.
  4. Cook for about 15 minutes with a splash of water, until they are soft.
  5. In a bowl, whisk the eggs with parmesan, salt, pepper and chopped parsley.
  6. Remove the garlic and pour the eggs over the artichokes, distributing them evenly.
  7. Cook over low heat with the lid on for about 7–8 minutes.
  8. Flip the frittata using a plate and complete the cooking on the other side.

Chef's tips

Tips and alternatives:

- Do not add raw artichokes directly to the eggs: they might remain tough.

- For a lighter version, bake the frittata in the oven at 180 °C.

- You can add scamorza, ricotta or diced cooked ham.
