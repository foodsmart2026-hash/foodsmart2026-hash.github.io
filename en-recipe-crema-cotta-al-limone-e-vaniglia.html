Lemon and Vanilla Cooked Cream

💰 Save Euro · about €0.61 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A silky and refreshing spoon dessert made with basic pantry staples. Very budget-friendly, it is inspired by the tradition of homemade Italian custard.

Ingredients

  • Whole milk500 ml
  • Egg yolks4 pcs
  • Caster sugar100 g
  • Cornflour (Maizena)40 g
  • Lemon (zest)1 pcs
  • Vanilla essenceq.b.

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. In a large saucepan, heat the milk together with the lemon zest cut into large strips (avoid the bitter white pith) and the vanilla.
  2. In a bowl, whisk the egg yolks with the caster sugar until the mixture is slightly pale.
  3. Add the sifted cornflour to the yolks and mix well to remove any possible lumps.
  4. When the milk is just about to boil, remove the lemon zest and pour a ladle of it into the egg mixture to loosen it.
  5. Pour the entire egg mixture back into the saucepan with the remaining milk and return to a medium-low heat.
  6. Continue to stir constantly with a whisk until the cream thickens noticeably, coating the back of a spoon.
  7. Remove from the heat as soon as you see the first bubbles of boiling to prevent the eggs from developing too strong a flavour.
  8. Pour the cream into 4 individual dessert bowls and immediately cover with cling film touching the surface to prevent a skin from forming.
  9. Leave to cool first at room temperature and then in the fridge for at least 2 hours before serving.
