Peeled Aubergines with Tomato and Oregano

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.30 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Aubergines cooked for a long time until they almost become a sauce, peeled to avoid any swallowing discomfort. The tomato and oregano recall the Mediterranean flavours of pizza.

Ingredients

  • Large aubergines350 g
  • Sweet tomato passata (smooth tomato purée)150 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 g
  • Dried oregano1 pinch
  • Water50 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Completely peel the aubergines with a vegetable peeler to remove the purple skin.
  2. Cut the flesh into very small cubes, about half a centimetre per side.
  3. In a pan, combine the tomato passata, water, and extra virgin olive oil.
  4. Heat over medium-low heat and add the aubergine cubes.
  5. Cover with a lid and cook for at least 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. If the sauce reduces too much, add a splash of water: the aubergine should stew, not fry.
  7. By the end of cooking, the aubergine should look almost broken down and integrated into the tomato.
  8. Add a pinch of dried oregano, rubbing it between your fingers to release the aroma.
  9. Leave to rest for a few minutes before serving, so the flavours blend perfectly.
