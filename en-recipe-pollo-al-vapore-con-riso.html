Steamed chicken with rice

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

Tender steamed chicken with ginger, jasmine rice, Chinese cabbage and pickled cucumber.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Jasmine rice300 g
  • Water (for the rice)450 ml
  • Chinese cabbage (napa cabbage)300 g
  • Cucumber250 g
  • Fresh ginger15 g
  • Rice vinegar20 ml
  • Sunflower oil20 g
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and cook it with the water and 2 g of salt in a covered pan for 12 minutes, then leave it to rest.
  2. Slice the cucumber thinly and season it with rice vinegar and a pinch of salt; leave it to marinate.
  3. Peel the ginger and slice it thinly.
  4. Rub the chicken with 2 g of salt and place it in the steamer basket, covered with the ginger slices.
  5. Steam for 18-20 minutes, until the meat is white and cooked through to the centre.
  6. Slice the Chinese cabbage into strips.
  7. Add the cabbage to the basket for the final 5 minutes of cooking.
  8. Leave the chicken to rest for 3 minutes, then slice it.
  9. Heat the oil and pour it hot over the sliced chicken to infuse it.
  10. Serve the chicken with the rice, cabbage and pickled cucumber.
