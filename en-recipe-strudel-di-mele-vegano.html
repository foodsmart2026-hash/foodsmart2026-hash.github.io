Vegan Apple Strudel

8 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A strudel made with plant-based puff pastry, apples, raisins, pine nuts, and cinnamon, without butter or eggs.

A plant-based reimagining of the famous South Tyrolean dessert, filled with juicy apples, pine nuts, and fragrant cinnamon. The vegetable puff pastry ensures an outer crunch that holds a soft and spiced heart, ideal for autumn days.

Ingredients

  • Vegan puff pastry (butter-free)250 g
  • Apples700 g
  • Raisins40 g
  • Pine nuts30 g
  • Brown sugar50 g
  • Breadcrumbs30 g
  • Ground cinnamon3 g
  • Lemon juice15 ml
  • Soya milk20 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Soy, Sulphites
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Sesame.

Method

  1. Start by carefully peeling the 700g of apples, removing the cores and cutting them into very thin and uniform slices. Place them in a large bowl: this step is fundamental to ensure even cooking and a melting consistency inside your strudel.
  2. Immediately drizzle the apples with the 15ml of lemon juice to prevent them from browning, then add the 50g of brown sugar and 3g of cinnamon powder. Mix well with a spoon so that every slice is thoroughly coated; you will immediately smell that spicy aroma typical of traditional desserts.
  3. Add the 40g of raisins (previously soaked and well squeezed) and the 30g of pine nuts to the mixture, distributing them evenly in the bowl. Let the filling rest for about 10 minutes so that the flavors can meld and the apples can slightly release their natural juices, making it all juicier.
  4. Take the 250g of chilled vegetable puff pastry and roll it out on a sheet of parchment paper, maintaining a precise rectangular shape. Sprinkle the central part of the pastry with the 30g of breadcrumbs; this ingredient is the chef's trick to absorb the moisture from the apples during cooking, preventing the base from becoming too soft.
  5. Distribute the apple filling over the layer of breadcrumbs, trying to create a central cylinder and leaving a couple of centimeters free from the edges. Gently roll up the pastry starting from the long side, using the parchment paper to help you, and seal the ends well by pressing with your fingers to prevent the filling from leaking.
  6. Place the strudel on a baking tray and brush the entire surface evenly with the 20ml of soy drink, which will provide a beautiful amber color despite the absence of egg yolk. Make small transverse cuts on the upper part with a sharp knife to allow steam to escape, preventing the pastry from breaking.
  7. Bake in a preheated static oven at 190 °C and cook for about 35-40 minutes until the pastry is puffed and has an intense golden color. Once ready, let it cool on a wire rack before slicing: the internal structure will stabilize and the scent of apple and cinnamon will win over your entire table.

Tips

  • Squeeze the soaked raisins very well to avoid releasing too much moisture inside the pastry.
  • The breadcrumbs on the bottom are essential for absorbing apple juices and keeping the base crispy.
  • Let the strudel cool slightly before slicing to prevent the filling from leaking and ruining the slices.

Variations

  • Replace the pine nuts with coarsely chopped walnut halves for a bolder flavor.
  • Add some dried cranberries along with the raisins for a contrasting note of acidity.
