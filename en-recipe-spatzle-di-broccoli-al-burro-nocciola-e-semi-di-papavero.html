Broccoli spätzle with brown butter and poppy seeds

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Trentino-Alto Adige · 4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 15 min · Medium

Small Tyrolean dumplings made with a broccoli-based batter, served with brown butter and poppy seeds for a rich, comforting flavour.

Ingredients

  • Broccoli (trimmed weight)400g
  • Plain flour250g
  • Medium eggs2
  • Whole milk50ml
  • Butter40g
  • Poppy seeds1 tbsp
  • Nutmeg1 pinch
  • Grated Parmesan cheese30g
  • Saltto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Boil the broccoli florets in salted water for about 8-10 minutes, until they are very tender.
  2. Drain the broccoli and blend with a stick blender until you have a smooth, thick purée.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the eggs with the milk, a pinch of salt, and the nutmeg.
  4. Incorporate the broccoli purée into the egg mixture, mixing well with a whisk.
  5. Add the sifted flour a little at a time, working the batter until it is smooth and sticky.
  6. Leave the batter to rest for 15 minutes at room temperature.
  7. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil and place a spätzle maker (or a potato ricer with large holes) over it.
  8. Drop the dumplings into the water and remove them with a slotted spoon as soon as they rise to the surface.
  9. In a large frying pan, melt the butter until it takes on an amber colour and a nutty aroma.
  10. Toss the spätzle in the butter, add the poppy seeds and Parmesan, then serve immediately.
