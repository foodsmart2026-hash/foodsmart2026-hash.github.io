Cavatelli with sausage ragù

Molise · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

Semolina pasta shapes from Molise served with a sausage ragù and pecorino cheese.

Ingredients

  • Semolina cavatelli360 g
  • Pork sausage300 g
  • Tomato passata500 ml
  • Onion60 g
  • Red wine60 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Grated pecorino cheese40 g
  • Fine salt4 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Sulphites
May contain traces of: Celery, Mustard.

Method

  1. Remove the casings from the sausage and crumble the meat.
  2. Sauté the chopped onion in the oil, then add the sausage and cook for 6 minutes.
  3. Pour in the red wine and allow it to evaporate.
  4. Add the passata, season with salt and simmer over a low heat for 30 minutes.
  5. Boil the cavatelli in salted water, then drain while still al dente.
  6. Toss the pasta with the ragù and finish with the pecorino.
