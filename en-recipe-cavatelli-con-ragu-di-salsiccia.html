Cavatelli with sausage ragù

Molise · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

Semolina pasta shapes from Molise served with a sausage ragù and pecorino cheese.

Ingredients

  • Semolina cavatelli360 g
  • Pork sausage300 g
  • Tomato passata500 ml
  • Onion60 g
  • Red wine60 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Grated pecorino cheese40 g
  • Fine salt4 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Sulphites
May contain traces of: Celery, Mustard.

Method

  1. Begin by preparing the meat base: remove the casing from the 300 g of pork sausage and crumble it finely with your hands or using a fork. This step is essential to ensure the meat browns evenly and releases all its flavor into the sauce; try to achieve small, uniform pieces.
  2. Move on to the sauté by finely chopping the 60 g of onion and placing it in a large pan with 20 ml of extra virgin olive oil. Turn the heat to medium-low and let it soften gently for about 4 minutes until the onion becomes translucent and tender without browning too much, thus maintaining a delicate sweetness.
  1. Slightly increase the heat and add the crumbled sausage to the pan, cooking it for about 6 minutes. Stir frequently with a wooden spoon to break up any remaining clumps until the meat has changed color and appears well-browned and fragrant; browning is the secret to giving depth and character to your ragù.
  2. Pour the 60 ml of red wine over the browned meat and let the alcohol evaporate completely over high heat for a couple of minutes. You will know it is ready when you no longer smell the sharp note of alcohol, but only the fruity aroma of the wine which has created a flavorful base with the meat juices.
  3. Add the 500 ml of tomato passata and 4 g of fine salt, stirring well to combine the ingredients. Lower the heat to a minimum, partially cover the pan, and let it simmer gently for 30 minutes: the ragù will be ready when it looks thick, rich, and a beautiful deep red, signaling that the tomato has concentrated properly.
  4. While the sauce finishes cooking, bring a pot of water to a boil and have your 360 g of semolina cavatelli ready. Toss the pasta in and cook it according to the package directions, making sure to taste it often to drain it 'al dente' so it maintains a firm and pleasant bite.
  5. Drain the cavatelli and pour them directly into the pan with the hot sausage ragù, tossing them over high heat for one minute to let the flavors meld. Turn off the heat, add the 40 g of grated pecorino, and stir one last time vigorously to create a creamy finish that will perfectly bind the pasta to the sauce.
