Fennel gratin

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Easy

A delicate side dish of blanched fennel baked with a Parmesan crust.

Ingredients

  • Fennel1000 g
  • Butter40 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano PDO60 g
  • Breadcrumbs30 g
  • Fine salt6 g
  • Ground black pepper0.5 g
  • Water (for blanching)2000 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk
May contain traces of: Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Remove the tougher outer layers of the fennel and the base of the core.
  3. Cut the fennel into wedges about 1.5 cm thick, leaving a bit of the core to keep them together.
  4. Bring the water to a boil with 4 g of salt.
  5. Blanch the fennel for 6-7 minutes, then drain thoroughly.
  6. Grease a baking dish with 10 g of butter.
  7. Arrange the wedges slightly overlapping and season with salt and pepper.
  8. Sprinkle with the mixed Parmigiano and breadcrumbs.
  9. Dot the remaining butter over the surface in small knobs.
  10. Gratinate in the oven for 20-25 minutes, until a golden crust forms.
