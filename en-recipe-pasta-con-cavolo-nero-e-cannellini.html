Pasta with cavolo nero and cannellini beans

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A complete main course that marries the rustic nature of cavolo nero with the creaminess of pulses, perfect for the cold season. This recipe balances complex carbohydrates, fibre and plant-based proteins in a single affordable and flavourful dish.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Wholewheat short pasta (e.g. fusilli or rigatoni)320 g
  • Cavolo nero (Italian black kale, cleaned weight)300 g
  • Pre-cooked cannellini beans (drained weight)240 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Garlic cloves1 pz
  • Dried chilli1 g
  • Wholegrain sea salt5 g
  • Black pepper1 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Vegetable preparation: Wash the cavolo nero, remove the tough central rib from each leaf and cut the leaves into strips of about 2 cm; this step ensures there are no woody parts in the final dish.
  2. Initial boiling: Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to the boil; using a single pot for the vegetables and pasta saves energy and preserves nutrients.
  3. Blanching the kale: Drop the cavolo nero strips into the boiling water and cook for 5 minutes; the leaves should soften slightly but maintain a deep green colour.
  4. Cooking the pasta: Add the wholewheat pasta to the same water as the kale and cook according to the package instructions, but drain it 2 minutes before the suggested time to finish cooking in the pan.
  5. Aromatic base: While the pasta is cooking, heat the extra virgin olive oil in a large pan with the crushed garlic and chilli for 2-3 minutes, until the garlic turns golden and releases its aroma.
  6. Preparing the pulses: Rinse the cannellini beans from their canning liquid, add them to the pan with a ladle of pasta cooking water and allow to infuse over medium heat for 3 minutes.
  7. Creating the cream: Mash a small portion of the beans with a fork directly in the pan; this trick creates a creamy sauce without having to add cream or cheese.
  8. Tossing the pasta: Drain the pasta and kale with a slotted spoon and transfer them to the pan; toss over high heat for 2 minutes, stirring continuously to coat the pasta in the cannellini cream.
  9. Finishing and serving: Turn off the heat, remove the garlic and add a drizzle of raw oil and a grinding of black pepper; the dish is ready when the pasta looks glossy and is perfectly bound to the sauce.
