Privacy policy – Food Smart

Food Smart app and website www.food-smart.it · Last updated: 12 November 2026 ·

In short: Food Smart collects only the data needed to run the app (account, recipes, preferences and, if you allow it, your location) and to show ads according to your consent. We do not sell your data and you can delete your account and all your data at any time.

This policy is written in accordance with EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email, nickname and password, stored only as a bcrypt hash and never in plain text. With Sign in with Google or Apple: the account identifier and, if provided, name and profile photo (never shown publicly).
  • Location: coordinates and city, only while you use the app and with your consent, stored on our server to show nearby stores and estimate prices. No background tracking.
  • Content: recipes and photos you publish (camera or gallery, only with consent) and the menus you choose to share.
  • Usage data: favourites, shopping list, pantry, personal menus, ingredients viewed, language preference.
  • Advertising (Android app): Google AdMob may use the device advertising ID, according to the consent you give in the privacy form shown at launch.

2. What other users see

Only your nickname, the avatar you choose, the recipes you publish, the menus you share with the community and your author page listing your recipes are public. Your email is never visible to other users. You can stop sharing a menu at any time. Community recipes are checked by an administrator before they enter the Salva Euro section.

3. Advertising and cookies

In the Android app ads are provided by Google AdMob. On www.food-smart.it ads are provided by Google AdSense and your consent is asked through Google's banner before advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookies" link at the bottom of every page of the website. We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting tools.

4. Third-party services

  • Google: Google sign-in, AdMob and AdSense (ads), Places API (receives coordinates to find real nearby stores).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple.
  • Artificial intelligence (Google Gemini via Emergent): receives ingredient names, city/coordinates, shopping list and the text of published recipes to generate ingredient cards, price estimates, nutrition values, estimated allergens and automatic translations.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: city search.
  • Emergent: hosting, database, photo storage, email delivery (password reset codes).
  • RevenueCat (subscription management): the component is included in the app but is not active and receives no data until Premium subscriptions become available. At that point it will receive your user identifier and purchase data, and this policy will be updated before activation.

These providers process data only to deliver the service. Prices shown are AI estimates, not real offers. Nutrition, allergen and health information is AI-generated for general guidance and does not replace medical advice. We do not sell your data. All communications are encrypted (HTTPS).

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is active. Deleting your account erases your profile, published recipes, the content of your photo files, menus (shared ones included), favourites, shopping list, pantry and sessions. You can delete your account and all related data in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on the web: account deletion page.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Children

Kids' recipes are intended for parents. The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 14.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we process data, we will update this page and its date before the changes take effect.

9. Contact

For privacy requests write to supporto@food-smart.it.

Website: "Notify me at launch" (service closed)

The "Coming soon" page of www.food-smart.it is no longer active and we no longer collect e-mails through that form. If you left your e-mail there in the past, we collected it with your consent only to tell you about the launch of the Food Smart app: we do not share it with third parties or use it for anything else. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to supporto@food-smart.it.
