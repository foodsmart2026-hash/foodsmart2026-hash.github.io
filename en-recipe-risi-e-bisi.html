Risi e bisi

Veneto · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A thick Venetian soup made with rice and fresh peas, cooked in stock.

Ingredients

  • Vialone Nano rice280 g
  • Fresh shelled peas400 g
  • Pancetta50 g
  • Onion50 g
  • Butter30 g
  • Vegetable stock1300 ml
  • Grated Grana Padano40 g
  • Parsley5 g

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Celery, Eggs.

Method

  1. Sauté the chopped onion and diced pancetta in half of the butter for 4 minutes until softened.
  2. Add the peas and 200 ml of stock and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the rice and the remaining hot stock a little at a time.
  4. Cook for 16-18 minutes: the result should be soft, almost like a soup.
  5. Beat in the remaining butter, the Grana Padano and chopped parsley.
