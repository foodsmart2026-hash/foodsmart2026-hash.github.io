Sautéed Courgettes with Thyme Breadcrumbs

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A clever way to use stale bread and make courgettes crunchy in a few minutes. An affordable side dish that the whole family will love.

Ingredients

  • Medium courgettes600 g
  • Coarsely grated stale bread40 g
  • Dried or fresh thyme1 tsp

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Wash the courgettes, trim the ends and cut into small cubes about 1 cm wide for quick cooking.
  2. In a non-stick frying pan, toast the breadcrumbs with a drizzle of oil and the thyme until golden and crunchy.
  3. Set the toasted crumbs aside in a small bowl to prevent them from losing their crunch while the vegetables cook.
  4. In the same pan, heat another 2 tablespoons of oil and add the courgettes over a very high heat.
  5. Sauté the courgettes for 8-10 minutes, stirring often to prevent burning but allowing them to take on some colour.
  6. The courgettes should remain firm and not release too much water; the high temperature is essential for this result.
  7. Salt only at the end of cooking to prevent the courgettes from becoming soft due to osmosis.
  8. Add the crunchy breadcrumbs to the courgettes and sauté everything for one last minute to combine the flavours.
  9. Serve immediately to enjoy the contrast between the softness of the courgettes and the crunch of the bread.
