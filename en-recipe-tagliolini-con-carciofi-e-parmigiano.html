Tagliolini with artichokes and parmesan

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

🌿 Seasonal recipe🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Tagliolini320 g
  • artichokes5
  • garlic1 clove
  • extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • parmesan50 g
  • cooking water100 ml
  • Parsleyto taste
  • Salt and pepperto taste
  • lemon1

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Mustard, Soy.

Method

  1. Clean the artichokes, cut them into thin slices and soak them in water and lemon.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and sauté the garlic clove to flavor.
  3. Add the drained artichokes and cook them for about 5 minutes.
  4. Pour in half a glass of water, cover and let cook for 15 minutes.
  5. In the meantime, cook the pasta in plenty of salted water.
  6. Remove some of the artichokes and blend them with a few tablespoons of the cooking water and parmesan.
  7. Pour the resulting cream into the pan and add the pasta, drained al dente.
  8. Toss with a little cooking water, add the parsley and serve with more parmesan.

Chef's tips

Tips and alternatives:

- Don't blend all the artichokes: leave some slices whole to add texture.

- You can add crispy pancetta or speck.

- For a fresher version, add lemon zest.

- You can substitute parmesan with pecorino.
