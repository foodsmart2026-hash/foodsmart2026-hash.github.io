Soft Pear and Spelt Flour Pancakes

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

These little pancakes use the natural sweetness of ripe pears for a soft and nutritious treat. Spelt flour gives a rustic note while remaining easy to swallow.

Ingredients

  • Ripe pears (Abate or Conference type)250 g
  • White spelt flour80 g
  • Medium egg1 pcs
  • Full-fat milk40 ml
  • Baking powder2 g
  • Sunflower oil10 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Peel the pears and grate them finely using a large-hole grater to obtain a sweet, watery pulp.
  2. In a large bowl, lightly whisk the egg with the milk using a fork until well combined.
  3. Add the grated pear pulp to the liquid mixture and stir well to distribute the flavours.
  4. Sift the spelt flour with the pinch of baking powder and gradually add it to the rest, stirring to avoid lumps.
  5. Heat a large non-stick frying pan and brush it with a tiny amount of sunflower oil, removing any excess with kitchen paper.
  6. Pour one tablespoon of batter at a time, spacing them well apart, to form small circles about 5 cm in diameter.
  7. Cook over a medium-low heat for about 2 minutes: when the first bubbles appear on the surface, it is time to flip them.
  8. Finish cooking on the other side for 1 minute until they are just golden and soft to the touch.
  9. Leave to cool slightly and serve, breaking the pancake into smaller pieces if necessary for the child.
