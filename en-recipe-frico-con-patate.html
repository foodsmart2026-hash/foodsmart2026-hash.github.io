Frico with Potatoes

Friuli-Venezia Giulia · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A crispy Friulian pancake made with potatoes, onion and Montasio cheese.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes600 g
  • Montasio cheese300 g
  • Onion100 g
  • Butter20 g
  • Fine salt3 g

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and coarsely grate them; thinly slice the onion.
  2. In a 26 cm non-stick frying pan, melt the butter and sauté the onion for 5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the potatoes and salt and cook for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften.
  4. Add the cubed Montasio cheese and stir until it has melted.
  5. Press the mixture down to compact it and cook for 6-7 minutes without stirring, until a crust forms.
  6. Flip the frico over with the help of a plate and cook for a further 6 minutes.
  7. Cut into wedges and serve hot.
