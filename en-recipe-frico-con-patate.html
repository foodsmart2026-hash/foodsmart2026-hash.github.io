Frico with Potatoes

Friuli-Venezia Giulia · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A crispy Friulian pancake made with potatoes, onion and Montasio cheese.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes600 g
  • Montasio cheese300 g
  • Onion100 g
  • Butter20 g
  • Fine salt3 g

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Start by preparing the base: peel the 600g of potatoes and grate them coarsely with a large-hole grater, then thinly slice the 100g of onion. Also cut the 300g of Montasio cheese into small cubes to facilitate its subsequent melting. This preliminary phase is essential to ensure that the ingredients cook evenly and simultaneously.
  2. Take a non-stick pan about 26 cm in diameter and melt the 20g of butter over low heat. Add the sliced onion and let it soften for about 5 minutes until it becomes translucent and releases its sweet aroma, being careful not to burn it. A well-stewed onion will give your frico a soft and irresistible base note.
  3. Slightly raise the heat to a medium level and add the 600g of grated potatoes to the onion, immediately adding the 3g of fine salt. Mix well to distribute the seasoning and cook for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally with a wooden spoon. The potatoes should soften and begin to compact, losing excess moisture without yet forming a crust.
  4. Once the potatoes are tender, it is time to incorporate the 300g of cubed Montasio cheese. Continue stirring vigorously until the cheese is completely melted and perfectly blended with the potatoes. You will notice the mixture becomes stringy and homogeneous: this is the secret to achieving a deliciously melting heart.
  5. Level the mixture in the pan with the back of a spoon, pressing lightly to create a compact and uniform disc. Let it cook without touching it for about 6-7 minutes over medium-high heat, until you see the edges turn a dark blond color. You will know it is ready to be flipped when, by shaking the pan, the frico detaches from the bottom and moves as a single block.
  6. Using a wide flat plate or a lid, flip the frico with a firm motion, just as you would an omelet. Slide the patty back into the pan to cook the second side. Pay attention during this step to the fat released by the cheese, which will be very hot and make the surface slippery.
  7. Continue cooking for about another 6 minutes until this side has also developed a golden and crispy crust. The ideal consistency should be a sharp contrast between the crunchy exterior and the soft, rich interior. The aroma of toasted cheese will signal that your Friulian masterpiece has reached perfection.
  8. Turn off the heat and let the frico rest in the pan for a minute to allow the structure to stabilize. Transfer it to a cutting board, cut it into regular wedges, and serve immediately while hot. This brief rest is a small chef's trick to obtain precise slices and bring out the savory flavor of the Montasio at its best.
