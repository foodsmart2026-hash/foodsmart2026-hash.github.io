Tom yum soup

Thailand · 4 servings · 30 min · Easy

Sour and spicy Thai soup with shrimp.

This Thai soup is a fragrance explosion thanks to fresh aromatics like lemongrass, galangal, and lime. The clear broth hosts tender shrimp and mushrooms, offering a citrusy and pleasantly spicy flavor.

Ingredients

  • Shrimp (peeled and deveined)300 g
  • Lemongrass (stalks)2 pieces
  • Lime (juice)60 ml
  • Button mushrooms (sliced)150 g
  • Fresh chili (Thai bird's eye)2 pieces
  • Fish sauce30 g
  • Galangal (freshly sliced)30 g
  • Water or shrimp stock1000 ml
  • Kaffir lime leaves4 pieces
  • Brown sugar10 g
  • Fresh cilantro10 g
  • Fine salt1 g

Allergens

Crustaceans, Fish
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Prepare the aromatics: lightly bruise the base of the lemongrass stalks and cut them into 5 cm segments; thinly slice the galangal.
  2. In a large pot, bring the water or stock to a boil over high heat.
  3. Add the lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaves, and lightly bruised chilies. Simmer for 5 minutes to infuse the broth with flavors.
  4. Add the button mushrooms and cook for about 3 minutes over medium heat.
  5. Stir in the brown sugar and fish sauce, mixing well to dissolve the sugar and balance the savory profile.
  6. Submerge the shrimp in the liquid and cook for about 2 minutes, or until they become opaque and turn pink.
  7. Turn off the heat and immediately stir in the fresh lime juice to preserve its acidity and aroma without letting it turn bitter from boiling.
  8. Taste and adjust salt if necessary. Serve the soup hot, garnishing each bowl with fresh cilantro leaves.

Tips

  • Lightly bruise the lemongrass before boiling to release its fragrant essential oils.
  • Add the lime juice only after turning off the heat to preserve its freshness and acidity.
  • Cook the shrimp for just a couple of minutes to prevent them from becoming rubbery.

Variations

  • Enrich the base with coconut milk for a creamier and more velvety version.
  • Replace the shrimp with cubes of white fish fillet for a milder flavor.
