Apple and oat cookies

15 servings · 25 min · Easy

Soft cookies for snack time.

🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Cookies with a pleasantly chewy texture, created from the combination of oats and fresh fruit. The natural moisture from the apple and banana makes them perfect enjoyed alone or dipped in a drink.

Ingredients

  • Whole grain rolled oats150 g
  • Golden Delicious apple (approx. 1 medium)150 g
  • Ripe banana (approx. 1 medium)120 g
  • Raisins30 g
  • Lemon juice5 ml
  • Ground cinnamon1 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Sulphites.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) conventional mode and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Soak the raisins in warm water for about 10 minutes to soften them, then drain and squeeze out excess water.
  3. In a large bowl, mash the banana with a fork until it becomes a smooth puree without large lumps.
  4. Peel the apple and grate it finely using a medium-hole grater; immediately add it to the banana with lemon juice to prevent browning.
  5. Add the rolled oats, drained raisins, and cinnamon to the fruit mixture.
  6. Mix thoroughly and let the dough rest for 5 minutes, allowing the oats to absorb the moisture from the fruit.
  7. Using a spoon, take about 25-30g of the mixture and form 15 small balls, placing them on the baking sheet spaced apart.
  8. Lightly flatten each ball with the back of the spoon to create a cookie shape (about 1 cm thick).
  9. Bake on the center rack for 15-18 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden but the center remains soft.
  10. Remove from the oven and let cool completely on a wire rack before serving to allow the texture to set.

Tips

  • Let the dough rest for a few minutes so the oats can absorb the fruit liquids and the cookies hold their shape.
  • Thoroughly squeeze the raisins after soaking to avoid introducing too much excess water into the mixture.
  • Use a Golden Delicious apple for its sweet flesh that blends perfectly with the banana puree.

Variations

  • Replace the raisins with dried cranberries for a slightly more tart flavor and a pop of color.
  • You can add sunflower or pumpkin seeds to increase the crunchiness and variety of textures.
