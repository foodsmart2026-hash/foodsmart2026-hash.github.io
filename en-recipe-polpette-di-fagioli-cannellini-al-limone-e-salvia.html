Cannellini Bean Bites with Lemon and Sage

💰 Save Euro · about €0.66 per person

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

Light, protein-rich bites made with humble larder ingredients. The lemon and sage provide a freshness that makes them perfect served both hot and cold.

Ingredients

  • Cooked cannellini beans500 g
  • Boiled potato150 g
  • Breadcrumbs60 g
  • Fresh sage6 foglie
  • Lemon (zest and juice)1 pcs
  • Nutritional yeast flakes15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Drain and rinse the cannellini beans very well if using tinned ones, to remove excess sodium.
  2. Mash the beans and the boiled potato with a fork in a large bowl until you get a rustic mixture.
  3. Add the finely chopped sage, lemon zest, and nutritional yeast (which provides a vegan 'cheesy' flavour).
  4. Add a tablespoon of lemon juice and start incorporating the breadcrumbs gradually until the mixture is mouldable.
  5. Form balls the size of a walnut and flatten them slightly to encourage even cooking.
  6. Place the bites on a tray lined with baking paper and brush them with a drizzle of olive oil.
  7. Bake in a conventional oven at 200°C for about 15-20 minutes, turning them halfway through for even browning.
  8. The bites are ready when the surface appears dry and slightly cracked.
  9. Leave them to rest for 5 minutes out of the oven before serving to allow them to firm up.
