Marinade for meat and vegetables

2 servings · Prep 160 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • lemon juice30 ml
  • garlic2 cloves
  • rosemary sprigs2
  • oregano1 tsp
  • mustard1 tsp
  • Black pepperto taste
  • Saltto taste

Allergens

Mustard
May contain traces of: Sulphites.

Method

  1. Pour the extra virgin olive oil into a large bowl.
  2. Add the lemon juice and mustard, mixing until you get a uniform base.
  3. Finely mince the garlic and the rosemary needles.
  4. Incorporate the aromatic herbs, oregano, and pepper.
  5. Submerge the meat or vegetables in the marinade, distributing the seasoning over all surfaces.
  6. Cover the container and transfer it to the refrigerator. For vegetables, 30 minutes is usually sufficient; for meat, you can go up to 1–2 hours, depending on the cut.
  7. Before cooking, drain the excess marinade. Do not reuse marinade that has come into contact with raw meat or fish, unless it has been brought to a boil and treated properly.
  8. Cook the food using the desired method, checking the safe internal temperature when necessary.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes, and alternatives:

- Do not leave meat or fish to marinate at room temperature.

- Overly acidic and prolonged marinades can alter the texture of the food.

- For fish, use less lemon and shorter times.

- For vegetables, you can add sweet paprika or balsamic vinegar.

- For an Oriental marinade, replace lemon and rosemary with soy sauce, ginger, and sesame, considering the salt content.
