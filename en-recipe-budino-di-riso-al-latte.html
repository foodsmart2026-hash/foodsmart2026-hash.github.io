Rice pudding

Kid-friendly

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 35 min · Easy

Rice slowly cooked in milk with vanilla and a dusting of cinnamon, creamy and delicate. Note: contains milk.

Ingredients

  • Pudding rice80 g
  • Whole milk600 ml
  • Caster sugar25 g
  • Vanilla pod1 g
  • Ground cinnamon0.5 g

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Pour 600 ml of whole milk into a thick-bottomed saucepan and add the 1 g vanilla bean after scoring it lengthwise. Heat over medium heat until you see the first bubbles rising to the surface, being very careful not to let it boil over. This step is essential to allow the heat to extract all the natural aroma and seeds from the vanilla.
  2. As soon as the milk reaches a boil, sprinkle in the 80 g of originario rice, a round-grain variety ideal for this preparation thanks to its ability to release starch. Immediately lower the heat to the minimum, as slow and gentle cooking is the secret to obtaining the creaminess typical of a true homemade pudding.
  3. Cook the rice for about 30-35 minutes in total, remembering to stir frequently with a wooden spoon to prevent the grains from sticking to the bottom of the pan. You will notice that, as the minutes pass, the milk will begin to reduce and coat the spoon, gradually becoming thicker and more fragrant.
  4. Continue cooking while maintaining a barely noticeable simmer, almost a quiver, until the rice is very tender but still intact in shape. The final consistency in the pot should appear similar to a very 'all'onda' (wavy) risotto, as the pudding will tend to firm up further during the cooling phase.
  5. When there are about 5 minutes of cooking time left (around the 30-minute mark), add the 25 g of sugar and stir carefully to let it dissolve completely. Adding the sugar only towards the end prevents the milk from browning excessively or the rice from having difficulty cooking properly due to the density of the syrup.
  6. Turn off the heat and gently remove the vanilla bean, making sure to scrape it well to leave all the small black seeds in the milk. The scent will now be intense and enveloping, while the color of the mixture will have taken on a beautiful and inviting ivory-white shade.
  7. Distribute the still hot mixture into 4 individual bowls, leveling the surface with the back of a spoon if necessary. Let the pudding rest at room temperature for a few minutes so that the heat stabilizes and the texture of the rice becomes perfectly velvety on the palate.
  8. Complete each portion by evenly sprinkling the 0.5 g of ground cinnamon over the surface, which will add a spicy and decorative note. You can choose to serve your rice pudding while still warm for a comfort food effect, or place it in the refrigerator if you prefer a firmer and more refreshing dessert.
