Rice pudding

Kid-friendly

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 35 min · Easy

Rice slowly cooked in milk with vanilla and a dusting of cinnamon, creamy and delicate. Note: contains milk.

Ingredients

  • Pudding rice80 g
  • Whole milk600 ml
  • Caster sugar25 g
  • Vanilla pod1 g
  • Ground cinnamon0.5 g

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Bring the milk to the boil with the split vanilla pod.
  2. Add the rice and cook over a very low heat for 30-35 minutes, stirring frequently.
  3. When creamy, stir in the sugar and remove the vanilla pod.
  4. Divide into 4 small bowls.
  5. Allow to cool slightly and finish with a dusting of cinnamon; serve warm or cold.
