Vegan peanut butter cookies

20 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

Soft cookies without butter or eggs, made with peanut butter and brown sugar.

These cookies offer a perfect balance between sweet and salty, with a rustic texture that delights from the first bite. Ideal for an energetic snack or to accompany a cup of tea, they are very simple to prepare without butter or eggs.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour180 g
  • Peanut butter120 g
  • Brown sugar80 g
  • Oat milk60 ml
  • Sunflower oil20 ml
  • Bicarbonate of soda3 g
  • Fine salt1 g

Allergens

Peanuts, Gluten
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Start by preheating the oven to 180°C (350°F) in static mode and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. It is essential for the oven to reach the correct temperature before baking to ensure uniform cooking and a fragrant texture right from the start.
  2. In a large bowl, pour 120g of peanut butter and add 80g of brown sugar. Mix vigorously with a spatula or a wooden spoon until you obtain a thick and homogeneous cream; this step allows the sugar to begin dissolving thanks to the fats in the butter.
  3. Gradually add 60ml of oat drink and 20ml of sunflower oil to the mixture. Continue stirring with a hand whisk until the liquids are completely emulsified, resulting in a shiny and fluid base with an inviting aroma.
  4. Sift 180g of all-purpose flour directly into the bowl along with 3g of baking soda and 1g of fine salt. Salt is a magical ingredient here because it balances the sweetness and enhances the roasted notes of the peanut butter, while the baking soda provides the right crumbliness.
  5. Work the dough initially with a spoon and then quickly with your hands until it forms a compact and non-sticky ball. Do not overwork it: stop as soon as the flour is fully absorbed to prevent the cookies from becoming chewy after baking.
  6. Take small portions of dough of about 20-25 grams each and shape 20 identical-sized balls between your palms. Place them on the baking sheet well-spaced from each other, as the heat will cause them to expand slightly during their time in the oven.
  7. Use the tines of a fork to gently press down on each ball, first in one direction and then the other, creating the typical crosshatch pattern. The ideal thickness should be about 1 centimeter; this flat shape will help the heat penetrate evenly into the core of the cookie.
  8. Bake on the middle rack for about 10-12 minutes at 180°C. Remove them from the oven as soon as the edges begin to brown slightly, even if they still feel very soft to the touch: this is the secret to keeping them tender on the inside.
  9. Let the cookies rest directly on the baking sheet for at least 10-15 minutes before moving them. This step is fundamental because the structure of a vegan cookie stabilizes and firms up only during cooling, reaching the perfect final consistency.

Tips

  • Do not overwork the dough with your hands to prevent heat from excessively melting the peanut butter fats.
  • Slightly flatten the dough balls with fork tines to create the classic crisscross pattern.
  • Store them in an airtight glass container to keep them fragrant for several days.

Variations

  • Add dark chocolate chips to the dough for an even more indulgent version.
  • Replace the brown sugar with muscovado sugar for a more intense molasses aftertaste.
