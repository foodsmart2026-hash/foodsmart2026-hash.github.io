Vegan peanut butter cookies

20 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

Soft cookies without butter or eggs, made with peanut butter and brown sugar.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour180 g
  • Peanut butter120 g
  • Brown sugar80 g
  • Oat milk60 ml
  • Sunflower oil20 ml
  • Bicarbonate of soda3 g
  • Fine salt1 g

Allergens

Peanuts, Gluten
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Mix the peanut butter, sugar, oat milk and oil.
  2. Add the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt and knead.
  3. Shape into 20 small balls and arrange them on a baking tray lined with baking parchment.
  4. Flatten them with the prongs of a fork.
  5. Bake at 180 °C for 10-12 minutes.
  6. Leave to cool on the tray: they will firm up as they cool.
