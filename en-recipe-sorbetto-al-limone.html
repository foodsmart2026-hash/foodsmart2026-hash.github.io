Lemon sorbet

6 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A fresh sorbet made with lemon juice and zest, water and sugar: dairy-free and egg-white-free.

Ingredients

  • Lemon juice200 ml
  • Water400 ml
  • Sugar150 g
  • Lemon zest3 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Heat the water and sugar until the sugar has dissolved; add the zest and leave to cool.
  2. Strain the syrup and stir in the lemon juice.
  3. Pour into a shallow container and place in the freezer.
  4. Every 30 minutes, for 3-4 hours, mix vigorously with a fork to break up the ice crystals.
  5. Before serving, blend briefly to make it creamy.
