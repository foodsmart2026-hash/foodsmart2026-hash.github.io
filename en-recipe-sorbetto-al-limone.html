Lemon sorbet

6 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A fresh sorbet made with lemon juice and zest, water and sugar: dairy-free and egg-white-free.

A refreshing and light palate cleanser, a pillar of Italian tradition, made with just a few essential ingredients. The infused lemon zest provides an aromatic intensity that perfectly balances the natural acidity of the juice.

Ingredients

  • Lemon juice200 ml
  • Water400 ml
  • Sugar150 g
  • Lemon zest3 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Start by pouring 400 ml of water into a saucepan along with 3 g of finely grated lemon zest. Heat over medium heat until you see the first bubbles, so the essential oils in the zest can release all their citrusy aroma.
  2. Add the 150 g of sugar to the hot water and stir well with a hand whisk. Continue heating for about 5 minutes over low heat until the liquid becomes clear and the sugar is completely dissolved, creating a perfect base syrup.
  3. Remove the pan from the heat and let the syrup rest at room temperature until it has cooled down completely. This resting time allows the zest to continue infusing, ensuring a more intense and persistent flavor in the final sorbet.
  4. Filter the now-cold syrup through a fine-mesh sieve to remove the zest residues and pour it into a large bowl. At this point, add the 200 ml of fresh lemon juice and stir vigorously to blend the flavors into a homogeneous mixture.
  5. Transfer the liquid into a shallow and wide container, preferably made of steel, to encourage quick and even thermal exchange. Place it in the freezer leveled, closing the container if possible to prevent it from absorbing odors from other foods.
  6. After the first 30 minutes, remove the container and use a fork to break the ice crystals that begin to form on the edges. This operation is essential to achieve a velvety texture and prevent a single block of hard ice from forming.
  7. Repeat the previous step every 30 minutes for a total of about 3-4 hours, until the mixture takes on a grainy but soft consistency. You will see the color turn from transparent to a bright, opaque white, a sign that air is being correctly incorporated.
  8. Just before serving, take the sorbet and blend it quickly with an immersion blender or a food processor for a few seconds. You will immediately notice the grain becoming very fine and creamy, just like a professional sorbet, ready to be enjoyed in your 6 servings.

Tips

  • Use organic lemons to safely grate the zest and achieve the maximum fragrance.
  • Break the ice crystals every thirty minutes to obtain a fine and pleasant texture on the palate.
  • For a creamier consistency, you can blend the frozen mixture just before serving.

Variations

  • Add some finely chopped fresh mint leaves to the syrup for an extra balsamic note.
  • Replace part of the water with pink grapefruit juice for a hint of color and a more bitter taste.
