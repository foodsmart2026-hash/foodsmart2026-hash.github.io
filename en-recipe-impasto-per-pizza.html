Pizza dough

4 servings · Prep 260 min · Cooking 8 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • type 0 flour600 g
  • water390 ml
  • extra virgin olive oil15 g
  • salt12 g
  • fresh yeast3 g
  • sugar (optional)5 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Mustard, Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Pour the water into a large bowl and dissolve the yeast inside. If using sugar, add it at this stage.
  2. Gradually incorporate the flour, stirring with a spoon until you get a coarse mixture.
  3. Add the salt and continue kneading for about 5 minutes, until it is absorbed.
  4. Incorporate the oil and work vigorously for another 8–10 minutes, until the dough is elastic and smooth.
  5. Form a ball, cover the bowl, and let it rise at room temperature until doubled, generally 2–3 hours.
  6. Divide the dough into 4 portions of about 250 g each, form balls, and let them rest covered for another 45–60 minutes.
  7. Gently stretch each dough ball with your hands, avoiding crushing the edge excessively.
  8. Top as desired and bake in a preheated oven at the maximum temperature possible, preferably 250–280 °C, until golden brown.

Chef's tips

Tips, errors, and alternatives:

- Do not add too much flour during handling: the dough must remain soft.

- If it is difficult to stretch, let it rest for another 15 minutes.

- For a longer maturation, you can reduce the yeast and transfer the dough to the refrigerator for 12–24 hours.

- You can replace 100 g of type 0 flour with whole wheat flour.
