Valtellina Pizzoccheri

Lombardia · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Buckwheat tagliatelle with savoy cabbage, potatoes, Valtellina Casera cheese and garlic butter.

Ingredients

  • Dried pizzoccheri320 g
  • Potatoes250 g
  • Savoy cabbage250 g
  • Valtellina Casera cheese180 g
  • Grated Grana Padano40 g
  • Butter70 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Sage3 g
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk
May contain traces of: Soy, Eggs.

Method

  1. Bring salted water to the boil and cook the diced potatoes and shredded cabbage for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the pizzoccheri and cook for a further 10-12 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, brown the butter with the garlic and sage.
  4. Drain the pasta and vegetables and layer them in a warm serving dish with thinly sliced Casera and the Grana Padano.
  5. Pour the melted butter over, mix gently and serve immediately.
