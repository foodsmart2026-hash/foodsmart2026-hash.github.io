Baked Swiss Chard Gratin with Turmeric Béchamel and Pecorino

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

A rich and comforting version of Swiss chard, where the leaves and stalks are coated in a spiced sauce and baked until golden brown.

Ingredients

  • Swiss chard1kg
  • Whole milk500ml
  • Butter40g
  • Plain flour40g
  • Turmeric powder1 tsp
  • Grated Pecorino Romano50g
  • Breadcrumbs2 tbsp
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Clean the Swiss chard, separating the leaves from the stalks; cut both into pieces of about 4-5 cm.
  2. Steam the stalks for 8 minutes, adding the leaves only for the final 3 minutes of cooking.
  3. Drain the vegetables well and squeeze them slightly to remove any excess water.
  4. Prepare the béchamel: melt the butter in a small saucepan, add the flour and cook for one minute while stirring continuously to create a roux.
  5. Pour in the milk gradually, whisking constantly to avoid lumps, and bring to the boil until the sauce thickens.
  6. Remove from the heat and stir in the turmeric, a pinch of salt, pepper and half of the Pecorino Romano.
  7. Arrange the Swiss chard in a previously buttered ovenproof dish.
  8. Cover the vegetables evenly with the turmeric béchamel.
  9. Sprinkle the surface with the remaining pecorino and the breadcrumbs to create a crust.
  10. Bake at 200°C (fan) for 15 minutes, finishing with 2 minutes under the grill until perfectly golden.
