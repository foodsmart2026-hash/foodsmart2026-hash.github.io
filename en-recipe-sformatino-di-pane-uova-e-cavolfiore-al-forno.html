Baked Bread, Egg and Cauliflower Savoury Pudding

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

An economical waste-reduction recipe that transforms stale bread and cauliflower into a soft flan similar to a savoury pudding. Ideal for helping little ones enjoy cruciferous vegetables thanks to the melt-in-the-mouth texture.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower (florets only)300 g
  • Stale breadcrumbs (soft inner part)100 g
  • Whole milk150 ml
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (soft Italian whey cheese)100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil for the moulds10 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Cut the cauliflower into very small florets and steam or boil them for 12-15 minutes, until they are tender enough to be mashed with a fork.
  2. Place the bread pieces in a bowl with the warm milk and let it rest for 10 minutes until it is completely soaked.
  3. Coarsely mash the cooked cauliflower with a fork, leaving a few soft pieces to encourage chewing without any risk.
  4. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs with the ricotta until you obtain a smooth and creamy mixture.
  5. Lightly squeeze the excess milk from the bread and add it to the egg mixture, finally adding the mashed cauliflower.
  6. Mix everything well to distribute the ingredients; the mixture should be moist and soft, not liquid.
  7. Grease 4 small moulds or a single baking dish with a drizzle of oil and pour in the mixture, levelling the surface with the back of a spoon.
  8. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C for about 20-25 minutes: the pudding is ready when it is golden on the surface and firm to the touch but elastic.
  9. Leave to rest out of the oven for 5 minutes before turning out; serve warm, checking that the centre is not too hot for the child.
