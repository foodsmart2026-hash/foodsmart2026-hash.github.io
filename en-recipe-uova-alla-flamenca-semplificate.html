Simplified Flamenco Eggs

💰 Save Euro · about €1.49 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A simple and humble traditional European dish, revisited to be prepared in a single pan. Eggs, tomatoes, and peas create a nutritious and colourful mix that invites you to mop up the sauce with bread.

Ingredients

  • Medium eggs8 pcs
  • Frozen peas200 g
  • Tomato pulp400 g
  • Yellow onion1 pcs
  • Sweet paprika1 cucchiaino
  • Diced prosciutto crudo (Italian dry-cured ham)50 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Eggs

Method

  1. Finely slice the onion and let it soften in a large frying pan with the oil and a tablespoon of water for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the diced prosciutto and let it sauté for a minute until it becomes crispy and flavoursome.
  3. Pour in the tomato pulp and the peas (still frozen), seasoning with the sweet paprika and a pinch of salt.
  4. Cover and cook over medium heat for 10-12 minutes, until the sauce has slightly thickened and the peas are tender.
  5. With the back of a spoon, create 8 small wells in the sauce, spaced apart from each other.
  6. Crack an egg into each well, being careful not to break the yolk.
  7. Cover the pan again and cook for another 4-5 minutes over low heat to allow the egg whites to set.
  8. The eggs are ready when the whites are opaque and firm, but the yolks remain creamy and runny.
  9. Serve directly from the pan with slices of toasted bread to accompany the sauce.
