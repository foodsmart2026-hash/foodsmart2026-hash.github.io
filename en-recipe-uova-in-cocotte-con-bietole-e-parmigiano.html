Eggs en Cocotte with Swiss Chard and Parmesan

💰 Save Euro · about €0.86 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A refined main dish in presentation but extremely economical. The eggs cook gently on a bed of flavourful vegetables.

Ingredients

  • fresh eggs4 pcs
  • Swiss chard500 g
  • Parmigiano Reggiano (hard Italian cheese)50 g
  • milk40 ml
  • butter15 g
  • dried chilli flakes1 pizzico

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Wash the chard and separate the green leaves from the stalks; cut the stalks into small pieces as they take longer to cook.
  2. Sauté the chard in a pan with a knob of butter, salt, and a pinch of chilli for 10 minutes until the vegetable water has evaporated.
  3. Butter 4 cocottes or small oven-proof ramekins and distribute the sautéed chard on the bottom, creating a hollow in the centre.
  4. Add a tablespoon of milk into each cocotte over the vegetables to maintain moisture during cooking.
  5. Crack an egg into each cocotte, being careful not to break the yolk.
  6. Sprinkle the egg white with grated Parmigiano, avoiding covering the yolk too much for a better visual effect.
  7. Place the cocottes in a baking tray with hot water (bain-marie) reaching halfway up the sides of the ramekins.
  8. Bake at 180°C for 10-12 minutes: the white should be set and opaque, while the yolk must remain liquid and creamy.
  9. Serve immediately accompanied by toasted wholemeal bread croutons.
