Vegan Vegetable Lasagne

6 servings · Prep 40 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

Lasagne with a vegetable ragù and a plant-based olive oil béchamel sauce, made without dairy or eggs.

Ingredients

  • Durum wheat lasagne sheets (egg-free)250 g
  • Courgettes300 g
  • Aubergines300 g
  • Peppers200 g
  • Tomato passata500 ml
  • Unsweetened soya milk800 ml
  • Plain flour60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil90 ml
  • Nutmeg0.5 g
  • Basil5 g
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

Gluten, Soy
May contain traces of: Eggs.

Method

  1. Dice the vegetables and sauté them in 40 ml of oil for 10 minutes; add the passata, basil and half of the salt and cook for a further 10 minutes.
  2. To make the béchamel, heat 50 ml of oil, stir in the flour and then gradually whisk in the hot soya milk.
  3. Cook while stirring until thickened; add the nutmeg and the remaining salt.
  4. In a baking dish, alternate layers of lasagne sheets, vegetable ragù and béchamel for 4 layers.
  5. Finish with a final layer of ragù and béchamel.
  6. Bake in the oven at 190 °C for 35-40 minutes and leave to rest for 10 minutes.
