Vegan Vegetable Lasagne

6 servings · Prep 40 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

Lasagne with a vegetable ragù and a plant-based olive oil béchamel sauce, made without dairy or eggs.

Ingredients

  • Durum wheat lasagne sheets (egg-free)250 g
  • Courgettes300 g
  • Aubergines300 g
  • Peppers200 g
  • Tomato passata500 ml
  • Unsweetened soya milk800 ml
  • Plain flour60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil90 ml
  • Nutmeg0.5 g
  • Basil5 g
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

Gluten, Soy
May contain traces of: Eggs.

Method

  1. Start by carefully washing and cleaning 300 g of zucchini, 300 g of eggplant, and 200 g of peppers, then cut them into small, uniform cubes of about one centimeter. A regular size is fundamental to ensure that all the vegetables cook in the same amount of time and blend well with the sauce in the final dish.
  2. Heat 40 ml of extra virgin olive oil in a large skillet over high heat and add the prepared vegetable cubes, sautéing them for about 10 minutes. You should see the vegetables turn slightly golden and release their characteristic aroma, a sign that the browning is proceeding correctly without burning.
  1. Slightly lower the heat, add 500 ml of tomato purée, the basil leaves (5 g), and half of the salt (4 g), letting it cook for another 10 minutes. The vegetable ragù will be ready when the tomato has thickened slightly and the flavors are well-balanced and harmonious with each other.
  2. For the plant-based bechamel, heat the remaining 50 ml of extra virgin olive oil in a small pot, pour in 60 g of 00 flour, and stir vigorously with a whisk to form a smooth mixture. This step creates the 'roux,' the thick base that will allow the sauce to be lump-free and velvety on the palate.
  3. Gradually pour 800 ml of very hot soy milk into the pot in a steady stream, continuing to whisk until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon. At this point, turn off the heat and add 0.5 g of nutmeg and the remaining 4 g of salt to provide that characteristic fragrant touch of classic bechamel.
  4. Take a baking dish and begin assembly by spreading a thin layer of bechamel on the bottom to prevent the 250 g of semolina lasagna sheets from sticking. Arrange the first layer of pasta and cover it with a generous spoonful of vegetable ragù and some bechamel, distributing the sauce well all the way to the edges to prevent them from drying out.
  5. Continue alternating the components to create a total of 4 layers, ending with a final generous layer of ragù and bechamel mixed well on the surface. This finish will create a delicious golden crust during baking while protecting the pasta underneath, which will remain soft and juicy.
  6. Bake the lasagna in a preheated oven at 190 °C for about 35-40 minutes, until you see bubbles on the sides and a well-browned surface. Once the tray is removed, let it rest for 10 minutes before serving: this step is essential so that the various layers settle and the slices come out perfect when cut.
