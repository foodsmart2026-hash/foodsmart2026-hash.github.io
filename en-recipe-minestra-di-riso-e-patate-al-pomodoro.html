Rice and Potato Soup with Tomato

💰 Save Euro · about €0.55 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

The quintessential comfort food, budget-friendly and filling, where the starch from the potato and rice melt into a creamy embrace. A traditional home-cooked one-pot dish that warms up cold days.

Ingredients

  • Originario rice (short-grain rice for soups)280 g
  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes400 g
  • Tomato passata (smooth sieved tomatoes)150 ml
  • Celery1 stalk
  • White onion1 pcs
  • Parmesan rind (optional, if available)1 pcs

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into small, even cubes (about 1 cm) to ensure uniform cooking.
  2. Prepare a light soffritto by finely chopping the onion and celery, then let them brown in a tall pot with a tablespoon of oil.
  3. Add the potatoes to the soffritto and let them gain flavour for 3 minutes until the edges become slightly translucent.
  4. Pour in the tomato passata and cover with about 1.5 litres of hot water or light broth.
  5. Bring to the boil and let the potatoes cook for 10 minutes before adding the rice.
  6. Add the rice and cook for about 15-18 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent it from sticking to the bottom.
  7. If the soup becomes too dry, add a little hot water: the final result should be 'creamy' but not liquid like a broth.
  8. Mash a few potato cubes against the side of the pot with the back of a spoon to increase the creaminess, adjust the salt and serve very hot.
