Jam (fruit preserve)

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 60 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Clean fresh fruit (plums, apricots, etc.)1 kg
  • Sugar500 gr
  • Lemon juice30 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Wash the fruit thoroughly, remove pits, stems, and damaged parts. Cut it into regular pieces to promote even cooking.
  2. Transfer the fruit to a large, thick-bottomed pot, add the sugar and lemon juice. Mix well to distribute the ingredients.
  3. Place over medium heat and stir until the sugar is completely dissolved. When the mixture starts to boil, lower the heat slightly.
  4. Cook for 40–60 minutes, stirring frequently and skimming if necessary. The fruit should soften and the liquid should concentrate.
  5. If you want a smooth consistency, blend the mixture partially or completely with an immersion blender.
  6. Check the consistency with the cold plate test: a small amount should run slowly and not be too liquid.
  7. Pour the still-hot preserve into suitable jars, previously washed and sterilized, following a verified preservation procedure to ensure heat treatment and a vacuum seal. Do not rely solely on hot-filling for pantry storage.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes, and alternatives:

- Do not use heat that is too high: the sugar could caramelize before the fruit is cooked.

- For a thicker consistency, you can use fruit naturally rich in pectin or food-grade pectin following the manufacturer's instructions.

- You can replace part of the sugar with honey, considering that consistency and shelf life will change.

- For an aromatic variation, add cinnamon, vanilla, or lemon zest.
