Eggs with Tomato and Basil

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A nutritious and balanced version of the classic 'Uova in Purgatorio' (Eggs in Purgatory), a pillar of Mediterranean peasant cooking. This recipe uses healthy fats and fresh herbs to transform simple supermarket ingredients into an excellent protein-rich main course.

Ingredients

  • Fresh medium eggs8 pcs
  • Rustic tomato passata800 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Copper onion (Cipolla ramata, a mild Italian variety)100 g
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Fresh basil20 g
  • Iodised salt5 g
  • Ground black pepper2 g

Allergens

Eggs

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion and lightly crush the unpeeled garlic cloves to help release the essential oils during cooking.
  2. In a large non-stick pan (30-32 cm), heat the extra virgin olive oil over a gentle heat and add the onion and garlic, leaving them to sweat for 5-6 minutes until the onion becomes translucent.
  3. Pour the tomato passata into the pan, season with salt and pepper, and stir well; cover partially and cook over a medium-low flame for about 15 minutes so the sauce reduces and loses its initial acidity.
  4. Remove the garlic cloves from the now flavourful sauce and add half of the basil leaves, tearing them by hand to prevent the edges from oxidising.
  5. With the back of a spoon, create 8 small wells in the tomato sauce, spaced well apart, which will hold the eggs.
  6. Crack each egg individually into a small cup and gently slide it into each well, ensuring the yolk remains intact in the centre of the hollow.
  7. Immediately cover the pan with a transparent lid and cook for about 3-5 minutes: the signal that they are ready is when the white is completely set and opaque while the yolk should still look glossy and remain soft.
  8. Turn off the heat, garnish with the remaining fresh basil leaves and a final drizzle of raw oil, leaving it to rest covered for 1 minute before carefully plating each egg with its portion of sauce.
