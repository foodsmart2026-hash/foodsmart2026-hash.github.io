Turmeric broccoli tempura with soy mayonnaise

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 10 min · Medium

An incredibly light and golden fry thanks to the turmeric, transforming the broccoli into a crispy, Oriental-inspired side dish.

Ingredients

  • Large broccoli head1
  • Rice flour150g
  • Iced sparkling water200ml
  • Turmeric powder1 tsp
  • Groundnut oil500ml
  • Vegan mayonnaise100g
  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Maldon saltto taste

Allergens

Peanuts, Soy
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Divide the broccoli into medium-sized florets, keeping a small piece of the stem attached.
  2. Rinse the florets and dry them thoroughly: any residual moisture will compromise the crunchiness.
  3. In a bowl placed over another bowl containing ice, sift the rice flour together with the turmeric.
  4. Slowly pour the iced sparkling water onto the flour, stirring very briefly with chopsticks; lumps are normal and expected.
  5. Heat the oil in a deep saucepan until it reaches 170-180°C.
  6. Dip a few florets at a time into the cold batter, shaking off any excess.
  7. Carefully drop the broccoli into the hot oil and fry for about 2-3 minutes, until they feel firm to the touch.
  8. Drain on kitchen paper and season immediately with the salt flakes.
  9. In a small bowl, mix the vegan mayonnaise with the soy sauce to create the dip.
  10. Serve the tempura piping hot to best enjoy the contrast between the crispy coating and the tender broccoli heart.
