Aubergine Rolls with Ricotta and Basil

💰 Save Euro · about €1.08 per person

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A Mediterranean main course that smells of summer. The aubergines become delicious parcels enclosing a creamy cheese heart.

Ingredients

  • long aubergines2 pcs
  • cow's milk ricotta300 g
  • tomato passata (sieved tomatoes)250 ml
  • Parmigiano Reggiano (aged Italian hard cheese)40 g
  • fresh basil1 mazzetto
  • extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Cut the aubergines into longitudinal slices about half a centimetre thick.
  2. Griddle the slices on a very hot grill pan for 2 minutes per side until grill marks appear and the flesh becomes flexible.
  3. In a bowl, mix the ricotta with half of the Parmigiano, pepper and plenty of hand-torn basil.
  4. Place a generous spoonful of filling on one end of each aubergine slice and roll up tightly.
  5. Spread a few tablespoons of tomato passata seasoned with oil and salt over the bottom of a baking dish.
  6. Place the rolls in the dish with the seam facing downwards to prevent them from opening during cooking.
  7. Cover with the remaining tomato passata and the rest of the Parmigiano.
  8. Bake at 190°C for 15-20 minutes until the sauce bubbles and the cheese has melted.
  9. Serve warm to best appreciate the sweetness of the grilled aubergine.
