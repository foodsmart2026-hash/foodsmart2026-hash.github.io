Japchae

South Korea · 4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 15 min · Medium

Korean sweet potato glass noodles stir-fried with vegetables, beef, soy sauce and sesame.

Ingredients

  • Sweet potato glass noodles (dangmyeon)250 g
  • Beef strips200 g
  • Spinach200 g
  • Carrot100 g
  • Onion100 g
  • Shiitake mushrooms100 g
  • Soy sauce50 ml
  • Sesame oil25 ml
  • Sugar15 g
  • Garlic8 g
  • Sesame seeds8 g
  • Vegetable oil20 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Sesame, Soy

Method

  1. Cook the noodles in boiling water for 6 minutes, drain, cut them with scissors and season with 10 ml of sesame oil.
  2. Marinate the beef with 15 ml of soy sauce, half of the garlic and 5 g of sugar.
  3. Blanch the spinach for 30 seconds and squeeze out the excess liquid.
  4. Stir-fry the sliced carrot, onion and mushrooms separately in vegetable oil, then the beef for 3 minutes.
  5. Combine everything with the noodles along with the remaining soy sauce, sugar, garlic and sesame oil.
  6. Mix well and finish with the sesame seeds.
