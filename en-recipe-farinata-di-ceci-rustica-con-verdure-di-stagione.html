Rustic Chickpea Farinata with Seasonal Vegetables

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A naturally gluten-free and vegan one-dish meal. Chickpea farinata (a traditional Italian savoury pancake) is cheap and high in protein, ideal for a nutritious and quick meal.

Ingredients

  • Chickpea flour (gram flour)300 g
  • Water900 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil50 ml
  • Fresh or frozen spinach200 g
  • Fresh rosemary1 rametto
  • Salt and pepperq.b.

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. In a bowl, gradually whisk the chickpea flour with the water, adding it in a thin stream to avoid lumps.
  2. Let the batter rest for at least 2 hours (or even overnight in the fridge); this step is crucial for digestibility and final texture.
  3. Skim any foam off the surface of the batter if necessary, then add the salt and oil, mixing well to incorporate them.
  4. If using fresh spinach, chop it roughly; if frozen, squeeze it very well after thawing so it doesn't release water into the farinata.
  5. Add the spinach and chopped rosemary needles to the chickpea batter, stirring gently.
  6. Generously grease a large baking tray (the farinata should be about 1 cm thick) and pour in the mixture.
  7. Cook in a very hot oven (220-250°C) for about 20-25 minutes: a dark crust should form on the surface while the inside remains soft.
  8. Remove from the oven, sprinkle with plenty of black pepper, and serve hot cut into irregular slices, ideally accompanied by a fresh salad.
