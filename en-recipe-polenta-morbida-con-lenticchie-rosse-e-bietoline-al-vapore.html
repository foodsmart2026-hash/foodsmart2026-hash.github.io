Soft Polenta with Red Lentils and Steamed Baby Chard

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.47 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A complete and creamy single-course dish, where split lentils blend with polenta for a safe and nutritious texture. The chard adds iron and minerals without weighing down digestion.

Ingredients

  • Instant polenta cornmeal (Italian boiled cornmeal)120 g
  • Red split lentils80 g
  • Fresh Swiss chard (leaves only)200 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Grated 30-month aged Parmigiano Reggiano20 g
  • Water800 ml

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Rinse the red split lentils thoroughly under running water to remove any impurities or dusty residue.
  2. Wash the chard leaves, remove the tough stalk and cut them into thin strips, then reduce them to 5 mm pieces to make swallowing easier.
  3. In a large pot, bring the water to the boil and add the lentils; cook over a medium heat for about 15 minutes until they start to break down.
  4. Add the chard to the same pot as the lentils and continue cooking for another 5 minutes until they are very tender.
  5. Slowly whisk in the cornmeal, stirring vigorously to prevent lumps from forming; cook for the time indicated on the package (usually 5-8 minutes).
  6. Check the consistency: it should be a thick but soft cream; if too dry, add a tablespoon of hot water.
  7. Turn off the heat and stir in the extra virgin olive oil and Parmigiano to make the dish naturally tasty without using salt.
  8. Leave to cool slightly before serving to children; the polenta will tend to firm up but will maintain a texture that is easy to chew.
  9. For adults, take out the children's portion and add salt and black pepper to the rest of the polenta directly on the plate.
