Rustic Apple and Raisin Tart with Oil Pastry

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A 'povera' shortcrust pastry without butter, crunchy and light, enclosing a juicy filling of seasonal apples. Ideal for those looking for a home-made dessert low in saturated fats.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Type 1 or wholemeal flour (semi-refined Italian flour)250 g
  • Sunflower oil70 ml
  • Brown sugar80 g
  • Medium egg1 pcs
  • Apples (Golden Delicious or Russet types)3 pcs
  • Raisins30 g
  • Ground cinnamon1 tsp

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Prepare the pastry by mixing the egg with the brown sugar and sunflower oil until well emulsified.
  2. Add the flour a little at a time, kneading quickly by hand until you get an elastic, non-sticky dough.
  3. Wrap the dough in cling film and let it rest in the fridge for 30 minutes; this will help the oil stabilise within the structure.
  4. Soak the raisins in lukewarm water for 10 minutes, then squeeze them well to remove excess moisture.
  5. Peel the apples and cut them into small cubes, then sauté them in a pan for 5 minutes with a tablespoon of sugar and the cinnamon.
  6. Roll out two-thirds of the pastry on a lined baking tray, covering the bottom and sides well to contain the filling.
  7. Distribute the apples and raisins over the base, levelling them well, and use the remaining pastry to create decorative strips.
  8. Bake in a static oven at 180°C for 30-35 minutes, until the pastry is well golden and the edges slightly pull away.
  9. Leave to cool slightly before cutting: the apple filling needs to stabilise so it doesn't leak when sliced.
