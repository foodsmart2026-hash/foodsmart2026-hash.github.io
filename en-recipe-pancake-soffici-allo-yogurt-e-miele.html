Fluffy Yogurt and Honey Pancakes

💰 Save Euro · about €0.49 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A light and protein-rich variation of classic pancakes, made with affordable plain yogurt. The texture is spongy, ideal for absorbing a drizzle of honey or jam.

Ingredients

  • Plain natural yogurt250 g
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Strong white flour (Type 0, an Italian flour with medium protein content)150 g
  • Wildflower honey (Millefiori, a common multi-flower Italian honey)30 g
  • Baking powder8 g
  • Vegetable oil20 ml
  • Semi-skimmed milk50 ml

Allergens

Eggs, Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. In a bowl, whisk the eggs with the yogurt and honey using a hand whisk until the mixture is smooth and lump-free.
  2. Sift the flour together with the baking powder directly into the bowl to avoid lumps that would compromise the fluffiness.
  3. Slowly pour in the vegetable oil and the milk, stirring gently from bottom to top until you get a thick and creamy batter.
  4. Heat a non-stick frying pan over a medium-low heat and lightly grease it with a paper towel soaked in oil to avoid excess fat.
  5. Pour a small ladle of batter for each pancake, trying to maintain a regular circular shape.
  6. Cook for about 2 minutes: when bubbles start to form on the surface, it is the signal that you can flip them.
  7. Turn the pancakes over with a spatula and cook the other side for 1 minute until they are well risen and golden.
  8. Stack the pancakes on top of each other to keep them warm using their own residual heat.
  9. Serve warm with an extra teaspoon of honey or fresh seasonal fruit as desired.
