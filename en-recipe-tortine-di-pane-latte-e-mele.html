Bread, Milk, and Apple Mini Cakes

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

An anti-waste recovery recipe that transforms stale bread into a delicious and nutritious breakfast. It is an economical, family-style version of the classic English bread pudding, revisited with Mediterranean ingredients.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Stale bread (Italian 'pane comune' style)200 g
  • Whole milk400 ml
  • Eggs2 pcs
  • Sugar70 g
  • Apples2 pcs
  • Lemon zest1 pcs
  • Butter10 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Cut the stale bread into small cubes and place them in a large bowl.
  2. Pour the warm milk over the bread and let it rest for about 15 minutes until it is completely softened.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs with the sugar and lemon zest until well combined.
  4. Mash the soaked bread with a fork to turn it into a pulp and add the egg mixture.
  5. Peel the apples, cut them into thin slices and add them to the batter, stirring gently.
  6. Grease 4 individual ramekins or a small baking dish with butter and pour in the mixture.
  7. Level the surface and, if you like, add a pinch of sugar on top to create a little crust.
  8. Bake in the oven at 180°C for 30-35 minutes until the cake is firm to the touch and golden on the surface.
  9. Serve warm: the consistency should be soft inside, similar to a sweet flan.
