Fish stock

6 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

Homemade fish stock with bones and heads: the base for seafood risottos, soups and sauces.

🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • White fish bones and heads800 g
  • Carrot1 pz
  • Onion1 pz
  • Celery1 costa
  • Parsley1 mazzetto
  • Dry white wine100 ml
  • Black peppercorns5 pz
  • Coarse saltq.b.
  • Cold water2 l

Allergens

Fish, Celery, Sulphites

Method

  1. Rinse the bones and heads well under cold water, removing the gills and any traces of blood.
  2. Put them in a pot with the chopped vegetables, parsley and pepper, then add the white wine.
  3. Cover with the cold water, bring to the boil and skim off the foam.
  4. Simmer for 30-40 minutes, no longer, or the stock turns bitter.
  5. Season lightly with salt and strain through a fine sieve lined with cheesecloth.

Chef's tips

Use only lean white fish (cod, sea bream, sea bass, gurnard): oily fish makes the stock heavy.

Always remove the gills, which give a bitter taste.

Do not cook it for more than 40 minutes.
