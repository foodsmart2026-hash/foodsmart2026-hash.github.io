Lemon and Parsley Marinated Carrots

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A fresh, crunchy and colourful side dish that enhances the natural sweetness of carrots. Ideal for accompanying structured or protein-rich main courses.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Fresh carrots600 g
  • Lemon (juice and zest)1 pcs
  • Fresh parsley1 bunch

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Wash and scrape the carrots with a peeler, removing the woody ends.
  2. Cut the carrots into thin rounds or matchsticks using a sharp knife or a mandolin for a more refined texture.
  3. Bring a pot of lightly salted water to the boil and blanch the carrots for just 3-4 minutes: they must remain al dente.
  4. Drain the carrots and plunge them immediately into iced water to stop the cooking and preserve the bright orange colour.
  5. In a small bowl, prepare the emulsion by mixing the lemon juice, grated zest, 2 tablespoons of oil and salt.
  6. Finely chop the fresh parsley, ensuring it is well dried to prevent it from turning into a mush.
  7. Arrange the carrots in an ovenproof dish and drizzle with the lemon emulsion, mixing well to distribute the dressing.
  8. Leave to marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes so that the flavours meld perfectly.
  9. Serve at room temperature, adding a final grating of black pepper and the fresh parsley just before serving.
