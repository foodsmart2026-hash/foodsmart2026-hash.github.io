Gnocchi alla Romana

Lazio · 4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Baked semolina discs gratinéed in the oven with butter and Parmesan.

Ingredients

  • Durum wheat semolina250 g
  • Whole milk1000 ml
  • Butter80 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano PDO100 g
  • Egg yolks40 g (2 yolks)
  • Nutmeg0.5 g
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Heat the milk in a saucepan with the salt, nutmeg and 30 g of butter.
  2. When the milk is just about to boil, pour in the semolina in a steady stream, whisking continuously to avoid lumps.
  3. Cook over a low heat for 8-10 minutes, stirring with a wooden spoon, until the mixture pulls away from the sides of the pan.
  4. Remove from the heat and leave to cool slightly for 2 minutes.
  5. Stir in the egg yolks and half the Parmesan, mixing quickly.
  6. Spread the mixture onto a sheet of damp baking parchment to a thickness of about 1 cm.
  7. Leave to cool completely for at least 30 minutes.
  8. Cut out discs using a 5-6 cm pastry cutter and arrange them slightly overlapping in a buttered ovenproof dish.
  9. Top with dots of the remaining butter and the rest of the Parmesan.
  10. Gratinate in the oven at 200°C for 20-25 minutes until the surface is golden brown, and serve hot.
