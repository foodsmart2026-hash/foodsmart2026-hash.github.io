Cooked Ham and Pear Mousse

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

A small snack or autumn appetizer. Try it spread on beautiful slices of freshly toasted semolina bread.

A refined and quick appetizer that plays on the original contrast between the sweetness of fruit and the savoriness of cured meat. The softness of cheese and ham pairs beautifully with fresh pear and the crunchy touch of walnuts. Ideal for spreading on crostini for an elegant start.

Ingredients

  • Cooked ham100 g
  • Ripe pears8 pcs
  • Creamy cheese wedges4 pcs
  • Walnut kernels4 pcs

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel and core the pears
  2. Cut one half into thin slices and mash the rest with a fork
  3. Mix everything together with the creamy cheese wedges and the chopped ham
  4. Serve it decorated with the previously sliced pears and the walnuts

Tips

  • Use very ripe pears to make it easier to create a smooth, sweet, and velvety pulp.
  • Mince the cooked ham very finely before mixing it with the cream cheese for a more uniform texture.
  • Serve the mousse well-chilled from the refrigerator to best enhance the contrast between the flavors.

Variations

  • Replace the walnuts with toasted chopped pistachios for a different color and aroma.
  • Add a pinch of freshly ground black pepper to give the cream an extra touch of character.
