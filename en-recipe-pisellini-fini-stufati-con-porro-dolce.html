Stewed Fine Peas with Sweet Leek

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.37 per person

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

Very tender peas cooked with the white part of the leek which becomes almost a cream. A very economical source of fibre and iron, well-liked by little ones.

Ingredients

  • Frozen fine peas300 g
  • Leek (white part only)50 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 g
  • Water or salt-free vegetable stock150 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Slice the leek very thinly and then chop it further with a knife to reduce it to tiny fragments.
  2. In a small saucepan, heat the oil with a tablespoon of water and add the chopped leek.
  3. Allow the leek to soften over a very low heat for 5 minutes, adding water if necessary, until it becomes transparent.
  4. Add the peas (still frozen) and stir well to coat them with the leek base.
  5. Add the remaining water and cover, leaving to cook for about 12-15 minutes over a gentle heat.
  6. The peas are ready when they are very tender to the bite and the liquid has almost entirely reduced.
  7. For younger children (12-18 months), lightly mash the peas with the back of a spoon to break the skin.
  8. Leave to cool slightly before serving, ensuring the consistency is moist and not dry.
