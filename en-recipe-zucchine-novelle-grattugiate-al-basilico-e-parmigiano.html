Grated Baby Courgettes with Basil and Parmesan

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.37 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A quick preparation that transforms courgettes into an almost velvety texture, perfect for children who struggle to chew large pieces. Fresh basil stimulates the sense of smell without being heavy.

Ingredients

  • Medium courgettes400 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano (a hard, aged Italian PDO cheese)20 g
  • Fresh basil4 leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 g
  • Water20 ml

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Wash the courgettes, trim the ends and grate them using the large holes of a kitchen grater.
  2. Heat a large non-stick pan with the water and extra virgin olive oil over a low heat.
  3. Pour the grated courgettes into the pan and stir well to distribute them evenly.
  4. Cover with a lid and leave to stew for about 8-10 minutes; the courgettes should become translucent and soft.
  5. Wash the basil, pat it dry and finely chop it with a knife into very small pieces.
  6. Turn off the heat when the water has completely evaporated and the courgettes begin to soften.
  7. Add the chopped basil and the Parmigiano Reggiano, which will provide the right boost of natural flavour.
  8. Stir vigorously to create a sort of creamy bond between the cheese and the moisture of the courgette.
  9. Serve warm, ensuring there are no long strands for the child's safety.
