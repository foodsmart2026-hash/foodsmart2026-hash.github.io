Baked Chickpea and Vegetable Patties

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A nutritious and tasty starter that makes the most of plant-based proteins and seasonal vegetables, perfect for the whole family. These patties, crispy on the outside and soft inside, are an excellent budget-friendly alternative to ready-made supermarket products.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Canned chickpeas (drained and rinsed)480 g
  • Medium courgettes200 g
  • Carrots150 g
  • Wholemeal breadcrumbs70 g
  • Egg1 pz
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Chopped fresh parsley10 g
  • Fine salt3 g
  • Black pepper1 g
  • Untreated lemon zest5 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Drain the chickpeas from their liquid, rinse thoroughly under running water and dry them well with a tea towel to prevent excess moisture from making the mixture fall apart.
  2. Grate the courgettes and carrots using a coarse grater; then squeeze them firmly between your hands or in a clean cloth to remove the excess water.
  3. Pulse the chickpeas in a food processor: the aim is to achieve a coarse crumb rather than a smooth puree, to ensure a rustic texture.
  4. In a large bowl, combine the blended chickpeas, the squeezed vegetables, egg, parsley, lemon zest, salt, and pepper, mixing with a spoon.
  5. Add the wholemeal breadcrumbs a little at a time, incorporating them with your hands until the mixture can be shaped and no longer sticks excessively to your fingers.
  6. Preheat the oven to 200°C (conventional) and line a baking tray with baking parchment, lightly greasing the base with a tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil.
  7. Take about 30g of the mixture at a time and form walnut-sized balls, compacting them well between your palms to avoid cracking during cooking.
  8. Arrange the patties on the tray spaced apart, drizzle with the remaining oil and cook for about 15-18 minutes, turning halfway through for even browning.
  9. Remove from the oven when the patties are golden and firm to the touch; let them rest for 5 minutes before serving so the structure can set correctly.
