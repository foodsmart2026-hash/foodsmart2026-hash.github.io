Turkey Strips with Peppers and Sweet Paprika

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A quick recipe that uses the sweetness of peppers to balance the neutral flavour of turkey breast. Paprika provides a vibrant colour and deep aroma without making the dish heavy.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Turkey breast slices600 g
  • Mixed peppers (yellow and red)2 pcs
  • Red onion1 pcs
  • Sweet paprika1 cucchiaio
  • Dry white wine50 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Plain flour (Type 0 flour)20 g

Allergens

Gluten, Sulphites

Method

  1. Cut the turkey breast into thin strips (straccetti) and lightly coat them in flour, shaking off the excess.
  2. Wash the peppers, remove the seeds and internal membranes, and cut them into strips of the same size as the meat.
  3. In a large pan, heat the oil and sauté the finely sliced red onion until it becomes translucent.
  4. Add the peppers and toss over high heat for 5 minutes so they stay crunchy but start to brown.
  5. Add the turkey to the peppers and sauté for 3-4 minutes, stirring often with a wooden spoon.
  6. Deglaze with the white wine and let the alcohol evaporate over high heat for about 1 minute.
  7. Dust with the sweet paprika, add half a glass of hot water, and cook with the lid on for another 5 minutes.
  8. The dish is ready when the peppers are soft and the turkey is coated in a thick, colourful sauce.
  9. Season with salt and pepper and serve with a side of white rice or boiled potatoes.
