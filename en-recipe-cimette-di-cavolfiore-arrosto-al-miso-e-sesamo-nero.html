Roasted Miso and Black Sesame Cauliflower Florets

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

A crunchy umami-rich side dish where the saltiness of miso paste perfectly complements the charred sweetness of roasted cauliflower.

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower (white or coloured)800g
  • White miso paste2 tablespoons
  • Soy sauce1 tablespoon
  • Maple syrup1 tablespoon
  • Sunflower oil2 tablespoons
  • Freshly grated ginger1 teaspoon
  • Black sesame seeds1 tablespoon
  • Spring onion (green part only)1

Allergens

Sesame, Soy

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (fan).
  2. Break the cauliflower into relatively small and uniform florets.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together the miso paste, soy sauce, maple syrup, ginger and sunflower oil until smooth.
  4. Add the cauliflower florets to the bowl and mix well by hand to ensure they are completely coated in the glaze.
  5. Line a baking tray with baking parchment and arrange the cauliflower in a single layer, ensuring they do not overlap.
  6. Roast for approximately 20-25 minutes, turning the florets halfway through the cooking time.
  7. The cauliflower should be tender on the inside but well-caramelised and slightly charred around the edges.
  8. Remove from the oven and transfer the cauliflower to a serving platter.
  9. Sprinkle with the black sesame seeds and the spring onion greens, sliced into very thin rounds.
  10. Serve warm to highlight the contrast between the sweet and salty flavours.
