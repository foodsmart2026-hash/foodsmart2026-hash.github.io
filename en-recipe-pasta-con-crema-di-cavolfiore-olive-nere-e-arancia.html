Pasta with Cauliflower Cream, Black Olives and Orange

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A refined yet extremely budget-friendly first course that makes the most of the natural creaminess of blended cauliflower. The contrast between the savouriness of the olives and the citrus aroma makes it a surprising hit for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • Short semolina pasta (Durum wheat pasta common in Italy)320 g
  • Cauliflower600 g
  • Pitted black olives50 g
  • Organic orange (zest)1 pcs
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Salt and Pepperq.b.

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Trim the cauliflower, breaking it into small florets and dicing the stem thinly so nothing goes to waste.
  2. Boil the cauliflower in salted boiling water for about 8-10 minutes, until it is very fork-tender.
  3. Drain the florets (reserving the cooking water) and blend two-thirds of them with a ladle of the water and the oil until you achieve a smooth cream.
  4. In the same cauliflower cooking water, cook the pasta for the time indicated on the packet.
  5. In a large pan, sauté the garlic with a drizzle of oil and sear the remaining whole florets to make them crispy on the surface.
  6. Add the roughly chopped olives and the grated zest of half the orange to release the essential oils.
  7. Drain the pasta while al dente and toss it in the pan with the cauliflower cream, adding cooking water if necessary to adjust the consistency.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the remaining fresh orange zest and a drizzle of raw oil before serving.
  9. The dish is ready when the pasta is perfectly coated in a velvety, not dry, sauce.
