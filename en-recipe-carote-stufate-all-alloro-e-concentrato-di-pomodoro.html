Carrots Braised with Bay Leaves and Tomato Purée

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A simple preparation that transforms common carrots into a tasty side dish thanks to the slow braising technique. The bay leaf releases a balsamic aroma that pairs perfectly with the sweetness of the vegetable.

Ingredients

  • Carrots600 g
  • Tomato purée (highly concentrated tomato paste)15 g
  • Garlic clove1 pcs
  • Bay leaves2 foglie
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the carrots and cut them into even rounds about half a centimetre thick to ensure they all cook in the same amount of time.
  2. In a saucepan, heat the oil with the crushed garlic clove and bay leaves until the garlic begins to smell fragrant.
  3. Add the carrots to the saucepan and brown them over high heat for 2 minutes, stirring to coat them well in the oil.
  4. Dissolve the tomato purée in half a glass of hot water and pour it over the carrots to create a flavourful cooking base.
  5. Add a pinch of salt and pepper, lower the heat to minimum, and cover tightly with the lid.
  6. Leave to braise for about 15-20 minutes, shaking the pot occasionally to prevent the carrots from sticking to the bottom.
  7. Check the texture with a fork: the carrots should be soft on the outside but offer a slight resistance in the centre.
  8. Remove the lid for the last 2 minutes if there is too much water, letting the sauce thicken and glaze the carrots.
