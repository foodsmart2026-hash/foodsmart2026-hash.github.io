Baked Banana and Oat Bites

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

These 'mock biscuits' consist only of fruit and cereals, resulting in a soft and chewy texture just right for toddlers to handle. They are extremely budget-friendly and quick to prepare.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🌍 International

Ingredients

  • Very ripe bananas (with dark spots)2 pcs
  • Fine porridge oats100 g
  • Vanilla powder1 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Peel the ripe bananas and mash them thoroughly with a fork in a shallow dish until reduced to a puree.
  2. Add the oats to the banana pulp and mix well so that the oats hydrate with the moisture from the fruit.
  3. Stir in the vanilla and let the mixture rest for about 10 minutes: this step makes the biscuits more tender.
  4. Line a baking sheet with baking parchment and form small balls using a spoon.
  5. Flatten the balls with the back of the spoon to form discs about 1 cm thick.
  6. Bake at 180°C for 12-15 minutes: they should be firm but stay pale, without becoming crunchy or hard.
  7. Remove from the oven and allow to cool completely so the structure stabilises.
  8. Check that the edges have not become sharp; if so, remove the drier outer part.
  9. They keep well for two days in an airtight container, maintaining their softness.
