Raw broccoli tartare with lime and toasted almonds

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A fresh and surprising starter where raw broccoli reveals an unexpected crunch, enhanced by lime and toasted almonds.

Ingredients

  • Fresh broccoli500g
  • Flaked almonds40g
  • Lime (juice and zest)1
  • Extra virgin olive oil3 tablespoons
  • Dijon mustard1 teaspoon
  • Fine saltto taste
  • Ground black pepperto taste
  • Fresh chives1 bunch

Allergens

Tree nuts, Mustard
May contain traces of: Sesame.

Method

  1. Wash the broccoli thoroughly and dry them perfectly with a tea towel.
  2. Separate the florets from the main stalk; peel the latter to remove the fibrous outer layer.
  3. Finely chop the florets with a knife until they reach a consistency similar to coarse couscous.
  4. Cut the core of the stalks into tiny cubes (brunoise) of about 2 millimetres.
  5. In a small non-stick frying pan, toast the almonds over a medium heat for 3 minutes until golden and fragrant.
  6. In a small bowl, prepare the dressing by mixing the lime juice, grated zest, oil, mustard, salt and pepper.
  7. Combine the chopped broccoli and finely snipped chives in a large bowl.
  8. Dress the mixture with the emulsion and leave in the fridge for about 10 minutes to allow the flavours to develop.
  9. Add the toasted almonds only at the last moment to preserve their crunch.
  10. Use a plating ring to serve the tartare, pressing down lightly with a spoon before serving.
