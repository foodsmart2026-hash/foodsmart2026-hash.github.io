Spaghetti and Crunchy Savoy Cabbage Yakisoba

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

An affordable Asian-style reinterpretation using Italian spaghetti instead of noodles. An excellent way to get children to enjoy savoy cabbage thanks to the savoury glaze.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🌍 International

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti n.5 (a standard thin Italian dried pasta)320 g
  • Savoy cabbage300 g
  • Carrots2 pcs
  • Spring onions2 pcs
  • Soy sauce40 ml
  • Brown sugar10 g
  • Sunflower oil30 ml
  • Fresh ginger10 g

Allergens

Gluten, Soy

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti, draining them 2 minutes before the time indicated on the packet to keep them very 'al dente'.
  2. While the pasta cooks, cut the savoy cabbage into thin strips and the carrots into matchsticks to achieve a texture similar to noodles.
  3. In a wok or large non-stick frying pan, heat the oil and sauté the grated ginger with the white part of the spring onions for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the cabbage and carrots, stir-frying over a high heat for 5 minutes: the vegetables should soften but remain crunchy to the bite.
  5. In a small cup, mix the soy sauce with the sugar until dissolved, creating a base for the umami glaze.
  6. Add the spaghetti to the vegetables, pour in the soy mixture and toss for another 3 minutes until the pasta takes on a uniform brown colour.
  7. Serve garnished with the green part of the spring onions cut into rounds for a final touch of freshness.
