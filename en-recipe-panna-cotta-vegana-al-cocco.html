Vegan coconut panna cotta

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A plant-based panna cotta made with coconut milk and agar agar, served with red berries.

Ingredients

  • Coconut milk400 ml
  • Soya milk100 ml
  • Brown sugar40 g
  • Agar agar powder2 g
  • Vanilla extract5 ml
  • Mixed berries150 g

Allergens

Soy

Method

  1. Dissolve the agar agar in the cold soya milk.
  2. Heat the coconut milk with the sugar and vanilla.
  3. Add the soya milk and agar agar mixture and boil for 2 minutes, stirring continuously.
  4. Pour into 4 ramekins and leave to cool.
  5. Place in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  6. Turn out and serve with the mixed berries.
