Vegan coconut panna cotta

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A plant-based panna cotta made with coconut milk and agar agar, served with red berries.

Ingredients

  • Coconut milk400 ml
  • Soya milk100 ml
  • Brown sugar40 g
  • Agar agar powder2 g
  • Vanilla extract5 ml
  • Mixed berries150 g

Allergens

Soy

Method

  1. Start by pouring 100 ml of very cold soy drink into a small saucepan. Add 2 g of agar agar powder and whisk vigorously by hand until there are no visible lumps left. This step is crucial because agar agar only dissolves correctly in cold liquids before being activated by heat.
  2. In another larger saucepan, combine 400 ml of coconut milk and 40 g of brown sugar. Mix well while cold to begin dissolving the sugar crystals into the fatty part of the coconut. You will notice that coconut milk has a naturally creamy consistency and a tropical scent that will serve as the structural base for our dessert.
  3. Add 5 ml of vanilla extract to the coconut milk and sugar mixture. Stir again to evenly distribute the aroma, which will lend a sweet and refined note to the panna cotta. You will immediately smell a delicious fragrance that perfectly balances the bold, rich note of the coconut.
  4. Place the saucepan with the coconut milk over medium-low heat for about 2 minutes. Heat the liquid while stirring constantly with a spoon, strictly avoiding reaching a boil at this stage. The sugar must be completely dissolved, making the liquid look smooth, shiny, and homogeneous.
  5. Now pour the soy drink, in which you dissolved the agar agar, into the warm saucepan with the coconut milk. Slightly increase the heat and continue stirring with the whisk to perfectly blend the two mixtures. It is important to keep a constant motion to prevent the thickener from settling at the bottom of the pan.
  6. Bring the mixture to a simmer and let it cook for exactly 2 minutes, maintaining a gentle and constant flame. Agar agar needs intense heat to activate its gelling power, so do not rush during this final minute. You will see the liquid start to slightly coat the back of the spoon, a sign that the structure is stabilizing.
  7. Turn off the heat and immediately pour the mixture, dividing it equally into 4 single-portion molds. Agar agar starts to solidify at higher temperatures than classic gelatin, so work quickly before the dessert starts to set in the pan. Let the molds rest at room temperature for about 15-20 minutes.
  8. Once the molds are no longer hot, transfer them to the refrigerator for at least 2 hours. This prolonged resting time is essential to allow the panna cotta to stabilize and achieve that firm yet velvety consistency on the palate. The longer the dessert rests in the cold, the better it will hold its shape when you decide to unmold it.
  9. When ready to serve, gently run the tip of a small knife along the inner edge of each mold and flip the dessert onto the serving plate. Garnish the top with 150 g of fresh berries, which will balance the richness of the coconut with their natural acidity. The result will be an elegant, fresh, and visually inviting dessert.
