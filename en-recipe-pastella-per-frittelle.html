Fritter batter

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • all-purpose flour
  • eggs
  • milk
  • instant baking powder for savory recipes
  • salt
  • pepper
  • grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Mustard, Soy.

Method

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them with the milk.
  2. Add the sifted flour a little at a time, stirring to avoid lumps.
  3. Fold in the baking powder, salt, pepper, and Parmesan cheese, if desired.
  4. Whisk until you get a thick, smooth, and homogeneous batter.
  5. Cover and let rest for about 15 minutes.
  6. Stir in your chosen ingredients, such as grated and well-squeezed zucchini, corn, or aromatic herbs.
  7. Take small amounts with a spoon and fry them in oil at 170–175 °C, turning them for even browning.
  8. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not use ingredients that are too watery.

- Keep the oil temperature stable.

- For a sweet version, omit salt and pepper and add sugar and lemon zest.

- You can also bake the fritters in the oven at 190 °C, knowing that the result will be less crispy.
