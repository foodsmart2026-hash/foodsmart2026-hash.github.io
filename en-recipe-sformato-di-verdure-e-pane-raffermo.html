Vegetable and Stale Bread Bake

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A traditional peasant-style starter that elevates leftover bread and seasonal vegetables into a soft and nutritious savoury cake. This recipe balances complex carbohydrates, egg proteins, and fibre, offering an economical and sustainable alternative to classic flans.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Stale wholemeal bread200 g
  • Medium courgettes300 g
  • Carrots150 g
  • Pre-cooked chickpeas (well rinsed)120 g
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Semi-skimmed milk150 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Fine salt4 g
  • Pinch of nutmeg1 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Start by cutting the stale wholemeal bread into small 1 cm cubes and place them in a large bowl, drizzling them with the semi-skimmed milk to soften for about 15 minutes.
  2. Wash and coarsely grate the courgettes and carrots using a large-hole grater; this will allow for quick and even cooking in the oven.
  3. In a non-stick pan, heat 15 ml of extra virgin olive oil with the unpeeled garlic clove; add the grated vegetables and sauté over a high heat for 5-7 minutes until the vegetable water has evaporated and the vegetables are tender.
  4. Mash the chickpeas with a fork or a food mill until you get a coarse pulp, which will act as a natural binder and enrich the bake with plant-based protein.
  5. Lightly squeeze any excess milk from the bread if necessary and add it to a bowl with the sautéed vegetables (removing the garlic) and the chickpea pulp.
  6. In a separate dish, lightly beat the 2 eggs with the salt and nutmeg, then incorporate them into the bread and vegetable mixture, stirring carefully to homogenise the flavours.
  7. Grease an ovenproof dish (or 4 single-portion moulds) with the remaining extra virgin olive oil and pour in the mixture, smoothing the surface with the back of a spoon.
  8. Bake in a preheated static oven at 180°C for about 25-30 minutes; it is ready when the surface appears golden and the edges start to pull away slightly from the sides of the dish.
  9. Remove from the oven and allow to cool slightly for 5-10 minutes before serving: resting allows the structure of the bake to stabilise, making it easier to cut and the flavours more defined.
