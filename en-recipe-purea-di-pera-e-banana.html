Pear and banana purée

Kid-friendly

1 servings · 10 min · Easy

Naturally sweet snack with no added sugar.

A sweet and fragrant combination that combines the juiciness of Williams pears with the creaminess of ripe bananas. This puree is perfect for a natural flavor-filled afternoon break.

Ingredients

  • Pear (Williams or Abate variety)1 whole (approx. 160g gross weight)
  • Ripe banana1/2 (approx. 60g net weight)

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the pear under running water, peel it, and remove the stem, core, and all internal seeds.
  2. Cut the pear flesh into even cubes of about 1.5 cm to ensure uniform cooking.
  3. Prepare for steaming: place the pear cubes in the steamer basket and cook for about 6-8 minutes, until very tender when pressed with a fork.
  4. In the meantime, peel the banana, take the specified half, and cut it into thin slices.
  5. Remove the pear from the steamer and let it cool for 2 minutes to release excess heat.
  6. Transfer the cooked pear cubes and raw banana slices into a tall, narrow container suitable for an immersion blender.
  7. Blend the mixture at high speed until a perfectly smooth, velvety puree without lumps or fibers is achieved.
  8. Check the consistency: if it is too thick for the baby's weaning stage, add a teaspoon of the pear cooking water.
  9. Check the temperature of the puree on the back of your hand before serving.
  10. Serve immediately to prevent enzymatic oxidation which would darken the fruit.

Tips

  • Choose bananas with slightly spotted skins to ensure they are easy to mash.
  • Let the pear cool down after steaming to prevent excess heat from altering the taste of the raw banana.
  • Consume the puree immediately after preparation to avoid the fruit's natural oxidation.

Variations

  • Replace the Williams pear with an Abate pear if you want a slightly grainier texture.
  • Add a few drops of orange juice for a citrus note that also prevents browning.
