Egg-free Dark Chocolate and Hazelnut Salami

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

A family holiday classic, updated to be safe and easy to store without raw eggs. Uses common dry biscuits and cocoa for a very low cost.

Ingredients

  • Rich Tea or Petit Beurre biscuits (plain dry biscuits)200 g
  • Butter80 g
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder40 g
  • Caster sugar60 g
  • Whole milk50 ml
  • Chopped toasted hazelnuts30 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Tree nuts

Method

  1. Crush the biscuits roughly by hand in a bowl: visible pieces must remain to simulate the fat in a real salami.
  2. In a small saucepan, melt the butter over very low heat without letting it sizzle, then let it cool slightly.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix the cocoa powder with the sugar and gradually add the milk, creating a thick, dark paste.
  4. Add the melted butter to the cocoa paste and mix vigorously until you get a glossy, smooth mixture.
  5. Pour the resulting cream over the crushed biscuits and add the chopped hazelnuts, mixing well to coat every piece of biscuit.
  6. Transfer the mixture onto a sheet of parchment paper and shape it with your hands into the classic cylindrical salami shape.
  7. Wrap tightly in the parchment paper and then in a sheet of foil, pressing well to compact the mixture and eliminate air pockets.
  8. Place in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, or better overnight, until it is hard and easy to slice.
  9. Before serving, you can roll it in icing sugar to simulate the white bloom of real salami and cut it into thick slices.
