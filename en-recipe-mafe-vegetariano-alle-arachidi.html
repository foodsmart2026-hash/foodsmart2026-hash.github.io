Vegetarian Peanut Mafe

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A typical West African stew that uses peanut butter to create a velvety, protein-rich sauce. A surprising recipe that transforms common vegetables into an exotic dish.

Ingredients

  • Peanut butter (100% peanuts)100 g
  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes400 g
  • Carrots3 pcs
  • Tomato passata (smooth sieved tomatoes)200 g
  • Brown onion1 pcs
  • Vegetable stock600 ml
  • Vegetable oil20 ml
  • Basmati rice (for serving)200 g

Allergens

Peanuts

Method

  1. Start by cooking the basmati rice in salted water using the absorption method and keep it covered to stay warm.
  2. Chop the onion and cut the potatoes and carrots into chunky pieces, ideal for long cooking to maintain their structure.
  3. Sauté the onion in a casserole with the oil, then add the vegetables and tomato passata, browning for 5 minutes.
  4. Pour in the hot vegetable stock until the vegetables are completely covered and bring to a gentle boil.
  5. Take a ladle of hot stock and dissolve the peanut butter in a separate bowl until it becomes a smooth liquid.
  6. Pour the peanut cream into the pot, stir well and cook over low heat for 25 minutes with the lid on.
  7. The dish is ready when the sauce has become thick and oily on the surface and the vegetables break apart easily with a fork.
  8. Serve the stew piping hot over the bed of previously prepared basmati rice.
