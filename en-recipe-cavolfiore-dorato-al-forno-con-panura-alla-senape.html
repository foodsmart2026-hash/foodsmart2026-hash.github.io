Golden Roasted Cauliflower with Mustard Breadcrumbs

💰 Save Euro · about €0.45 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A crunchy transformation of classic boiled cauliflower, enriched with a savoury and slightly spicy breadcrumb coating. Budget-friendly, quick, and loved even by those who don't usually enjoy this vegetable.

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower800 g
  • Breadcrumbs50 g
  • Mild mustard1 tbsp
  • Fresh parsley1 bunch
  • Garlic powder1 pinch

Allergens

Gluten, Mustard

Method

  1. Trim the cauliflower by removing the green leaves and the central core, then cut it into similar-sized florets.
  2. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil and parboil the florets for just 4 minutes: they must remain very firm in the centre.
  3. Drain the cauliflower well and let it cool slightly on a clean tea towel to remove excess moisture, which is essential for crunchiness.
  4. In a large bowl, create an emulsion by mixing the mustard, three tablespoons of oil, garlic powder, pepper, and chopped parsley.
  5. Add the cauliflower florets to the bowl and toss gently with your hands or a spoon to coat them evenly.
  6. Sprinkle with breadcrumbs and shake the bowl so that the coating adheres well to the damp mustard surface.
  7. Arrange the florets on a baking tray lined with baking parchment, avoiding overlapping them to allow the hot air to circulate.
  8. Bake in a preheated conventional oven at 200°C for about 15-20 minutes, turning them halfway through if necessary.
  9. The dish is ready when the surface looks a beautiful deep golden colour and you can smell an intense toasted aroma.
