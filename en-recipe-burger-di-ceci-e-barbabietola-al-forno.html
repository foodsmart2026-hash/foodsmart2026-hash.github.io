Baked Chickpea and Beetroot Burgers

💰 Save Euro · about €0.48 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

Homemade veggie burgers, nutritious and eye-catching thanks to the beetroot's colour. Perfect for a quick meal or inside a classic bun.

Ingredients

  • cooked chickpeas (canned)400 g
  • pre-cooked beetroot150 g
  • egg1 pcs
  • breadcrumbs80 g
  • ground cumin1 cucchiaino
  • fresh parsley1 mazzetto

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Drain the chickpeas and rinse them well; dry them with paper towels as excess moisture would ruin the burger's texture.
  2. Cut the beetroot into cubes and place in a food processor along with the chickpeas and parsley.
  3. Pulse until combined: you don't want a liquid purée, but a grainy mixture where small pieces are still visible.
  4. Transfer to a bowl, add the egg, cumin and the breadcrumbs a little at a time.
  5. Knead with your hands; the mixture is ready when it no longer sticks to your fingers but remains compact.
  6. Form 4 large burgers (or 8 mini ones) using a pastry ring or simply by pressing them between your hands.
  7. Place them on a baking tray lined with baking paper, brush with a thin layer of oil and bake at 200°C for 15 minutes per side.
  8. They are ready when a dark outer crust forms while the inside remains soft and fuchsia.
