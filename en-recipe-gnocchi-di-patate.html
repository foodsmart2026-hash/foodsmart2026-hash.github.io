Potato Gnocchi

4 servings · Prep 35 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • starchy potatoes1 kg
  • all-purpose flour, approx.300 g
  • medium egg (optional)1
  • salt8 g
  • Flour for dustingas needed

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs
May contain traces of: Soy.

Method

  1. Wash the potatoes and boil them with their skins on in cold salted water for about 30 minutes, checking for doneness with a fork.
  2. Drain them, peel them while still hot, and mash them with a potato masher onto a work surface.
  3. Let them cool slightly, then add the salt, the egg if using, and about 250 g of flour.
  4. Knead quickly, incorporating more flour only if necessary, until you get a soft mass that doesn't stick excessively to your hands.
  5. Divide the dough into portions and form small logs about 2 cm thick.
  6. Cut the logs into small pieces and pass them gently over the tines of a fork or use a gnocchi board.
  7. Arrange the gnocchi on a floured tray, without overlapping them.
  8. Cook in boiling salted water: drain them with a slotted spoon when they rise to the surface and season them immediately.

Chef's tips

Tips, errors and alternatives:

- Prefer starchy potatoes and not new potatoes.

- Do not add too much flour: the gnocchi would become hard.

- Work the dough when the potatoes are still lukewarm.

- You can prepare variations with pumpkin, spinach or beetroot.
