Piadina Romagnola

4 servings · Prep 50 min · Cooking 12 min · Medium

🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • 00 flour500 gr
  • water250 ml
  • lard80 gr
  • salt10 gr
  • instant baking powder for savory bakes (optional)5 gr

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Pour the flour into a bowl and add the salt.
  2. Incorporate the lard at room temperature and work it with the flour until evenly distributed.
  3. Gradually add the water and knead until you obtain a smooth and compact mass.
  4. Form a dough ball, cover it and let it rest for about 30 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into 4 equal portions and form small balls.
  6. Roll out each ball with a rolling pin until you obtain a disc about 3 mm thick.
  7. Heat a non-stick pan or a piadina griddle and cook each disc for about 1–2 minutes per side, turning it when the characteristic bubbles appear.
  8. Fill as desired and serve hot.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not cook at too low a temperature: the piadina would become dry.

- If bubbles that are too large form, prick them gently.

- You can replace the lard with 70 ml of extra virgin olive oil.

- For a thinner version, roll out the dough to about 2 mm.
