Bread Gnocchi with Tomato and Mozzarella

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 15 min · Medium

A rescue recipe that transforms old bread into soft gnocchi. A complete meal thanks to the presence of carbohydrates, dairy protein, and lycopene from the tomato.

Ingredients

  • Stale bread (crumb only)300 g
  • Milk200 ml
  • Egg1 pcs
  • Grated Parmesan or Grana Padano (hard Italian aged cheeses)50 g
  • Plain flour100 g
  • Tomato passata500 ml
  • Firm mozzarella [low moisture pizza style]125 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Cut the bread crumb into small pieces and soak in the milk for about 15-20 minutes until completely saturated.
  2. Squeeze the bread well with your hands to remove excess milk and place it in a bowl with the egg, grated cheese, and a pinch of salt.
  3. Gradually add the flour while kneading with your hands until you obtain a firm but workable dough; the amount of flour may vary based on the bread's moisture.
  4. Form small logs and cut them into pieces of about 2 cm, then roll them over the tines of a fork to create ridges that will better hold the sauce.
  5. In a pan, heat the tomato passata with a drizzle of oil and salt for 10 minutes, creating a tasty and thick base.
  6. Cook the gnocchi in boiling salted water: they will be ready as soon as they rise to the surface (about 2-3 minutes), indicating the starch is cooked and the egg has set.
  7. Drain them with a slotted spoon directly into the pan with the tomato and add the mozzarella cut into small cubes.
  8. Stir with the heat off until the mozzarella starts to melt and stretch, then serve immediately to enjoy the soft texture of the gnocchi.
