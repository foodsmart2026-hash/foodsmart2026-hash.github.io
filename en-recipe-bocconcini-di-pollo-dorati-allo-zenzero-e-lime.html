Golden Ginger and Lime Chicken Bites

💰 Save Euro · about €2.05 per person

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A light main course with an exotic touch, perfect for brightening up chicken breast with affordable ingredients. The quick marinade ensures juicy meat and a fresh aroma, ideal for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • Whole chicken breast600 g
  • Limes (or lemons)2 pcs
  • Fresh ginger30 g
  • Plain flour (Farina tipo 0)50 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Fine saltq.b.
  • Fresh parsley1 mazzetto

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Cut the chicken into roughly 2-3 cm cubes, trying to keep them uniform for even cooking.
  2. Grate the fresh ginger and squeeze the lime juice into a large bowl.
  3. Submerge the chicken in the juice and ginger, leaving it to marinate in the fridge for 15 minutes to tenderise the fibres.
  4. Drain the bites from the marinade and lightly coat them in flour, shaking off the excess to prevent lumps in the pan.
  5. Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan and sear the chicken over a high heat for 5 minutes until a golden crust appears.
  6. Deglaze with the remaining marinade and cook for a further 4 minutes over a medium heat until a thick, creamy sauce forms.
  7. The dish is ready when the chicken is opaque in the centre and the sauce coats every piece well without being liquid.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the chopped parsley and a pinch of salt before serving hot.
