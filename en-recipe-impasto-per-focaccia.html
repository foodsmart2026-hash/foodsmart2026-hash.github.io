Focaccia dough

4 servings · Prep 200 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • type 0 flour500 g
  • water350 ml
  • fresh yeast10 g
  • salt10 g
  • extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • sugar5 g
  • Rosemaryto taste
  • For the topping
  • water30 ml
  • extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Coarse saltto taste

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Mustard, Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast and sugar in lukewarm water.
  2. Add the flour a little at a time and mix until you obtain a very soft dough.
  3. Incorporate the salt and then the oil, working it until they are evenly distributed.
  4. Cover and leave to rise for about 2 hours, performing two folds during the first hour to give structure to the dough.
  5. Grease the baking pan generously and transfer the dough, gently spreading it with your hands.
  6. Leave to rise for another 45–60 minutes, until the surface appears puffy.
  7. Emulsify water and oil and distribute over the focaccia. Press with your fingertips to create the characteristic dimples.
  8. Add rosemary and coarse salt, then bake at 220 °C for about 20–25 minutes.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not use a dough that is too dry: the focaccia should be soft and airy.

- If the dough shrinks while you are spreading it, let it rest for 10 minutes.

- You can add olives, cherry tomatoes, onions or thin potatoes.

- For a thicker focaccia, use a slightly smaller baking pan.
