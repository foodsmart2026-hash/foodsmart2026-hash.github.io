Seitan stew with potatoes and peas

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Easy

A plant-based seitan stew with potatoes and peas in a rich tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Plain seitan400 g
  • Potatoes400 g
  • Frozen peas200 g
  • Tomato passata300 ml
  • Onion80 g
  • Carrot80 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Rosemary2 g
  • Water250 ml
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Celery, Soy.

Method

  1. Cut the seitan into cubes and the potatoes into chunks.
  2. Sauté the chopped onion and carrot in the oil for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the seitan and brown for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the potatoes, passata, water, rosemary and salt and cook, covered, for 20 minutes.
  5. Add the peas and cook for a further 10 minutes.
  6. Serve hot.
