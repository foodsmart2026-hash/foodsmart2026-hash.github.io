Seitan stew with potatoes and peas

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Easy

A plant-based seitan stew with potatoes and peas in a rich tomato sauce.

A hearty main course that recalls traditional home-cooked flavors in a plant-based way. The firm texture of seitan pairs with the creaminess of potatoes and the sweetness of peas, wrapped in a rosemary-scented tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Plain seitan400 g
  • Potatoes400 g
  • Frozen peas200 g
  • Tomato passata300 ml
  • Onion80 g
  • Carrot80 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Rosemary2 g
  • Water250 ml
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Celery, Soy.

Method

  1. Begin the preparation by peeling and finely chopping 80g of onion and 80g of carrot. Then, cut the 400g of natural seitan and 400g of potatoes into cubes of about 2-3 centimeters, trying to keep them uniform in size so they cook evenly.
  2. In a large saucepan, heat 40ml of extra virgin olive oil over medium-low heat. Add the chopped onion and carrot and sauté for about 5 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the onion has become translucent and aromatic.
  3. Slightly increase the heat and add the 400g of diced seitan. Brown it evenly for 5 minutes, stirring often, until a light golden crust forms on the surface, which will improve its final texture.
  4. Add the 400g of potatoes and 2g of rosemary to the pot, letting them flavor with the seitan for a couple of minutes. This step allows the potatoes to absorb the flavors of the cooking base before adding the liquids.
  5. Pour 300ml of tomato puree and 250ml of water into the saucepan, then add 5g of fine salt. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat, cover with a lid, and let it simmer gently for 15 minutes, allowing the potatoes to soften without falling apart.
  6. After this time, add the 200g of frozen peas to the rest of the ingredients. Continue cooking for another 10 minutes without a lid if you prefer a thicker sauce; the peas should be tender and the potatoes should be easily pierced with a fork.
  7. Once cooking is complete, turn off the heat and let the stew rest for a few minutes before serving. This short rest is the chef's secret to allowing the flavors to blend perfectly and the sauce to reach the ideal density, coating every cube of seitan.

Tips

  • Brown the seitan cubes well before adding liquids to achieve a tastier outer crust.
  • Cut the potatoes into pieces of similar size to the seitan to ensure even cooking of all ingredients.
  • If the sauce thickens too much during the slow cooking, add a ladle of hot water to maintain the right creaminess.

Variations

  • Add a sprinkle of freshly ground black pepper to give the dish a slightly spicy note.
  • Replace the rosemary with bay leaves for a more balsamic and lingering aroma.
