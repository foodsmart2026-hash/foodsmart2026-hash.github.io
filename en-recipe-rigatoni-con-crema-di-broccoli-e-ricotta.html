Rigatoni with Cream of Broccoli and Ricotta

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A thick cream made by blending boiled broccoli with ricotta. A clever way to get children to eat their vegetables.

💰 Save Euro🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Rigatoni (large ridged pasta tubes)320 g
  • Broccoli500 g
  • Cow's milk ricotta (a creamy Italian whey cheese)150 g
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Chilli flakes (optional)1 pizzico
  • Grated Parmesan (an aged Italian hard cheese)20 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Divide the broccoli into florets and wash well; do not throw away the stalk, peel it and cut it into cubes.
  2. Boil the broccoli in plenty of salted water for about 8-10 minutes, until very tender.
  3. Drain them with a slotted spoon (reserving the water to cook the pasta) and place them in a blender jar.
  4. Add the ricotta, Parmesan, and a ladle of the cooking water to the broccoli, then blend until a smooth cream is formed.
  5. In a pan, heat the oil with the garlic and chilli, then pour in the broccoli cream and heat briefly.
  6. Cook the rigatoni in the same water used for the broccoli to absorb even more flavour.
  7. Drain the pasta al dente and transfer it to the pan with the cream.
  8. Toss well, adding more cooking water if needed to make it silky, and serve immediately.
