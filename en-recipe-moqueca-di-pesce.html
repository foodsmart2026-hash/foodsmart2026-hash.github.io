Fish Moqueca

Brazil · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A Brazilian white fish stew with coconut milk, peppers, tomato and lime.

Ingredients

  • Cod fillets600 g
  • Coconut milk400 ml
  • Red pepper150 g
  • Yellow pepper150 g
  • Tomatoes250 g
  • Onion120 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Lime juice30 ml
  • Fresh coriander10 g
  • Sweet paprika3 g
  • Vegetable oil30 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Fish
May contain traces of: Crustaceans, Molluscs.

Method

  1. Start by cutting the 600 g of cod fillets into cubes of about 4 cm, ensuring they are uniform in size. Place them in a bowl and season with 30 ml of lime juice, 6 g of finely minced garlic, and half of the salt (3 g). Let it rest for 15 minutes: this step is essential to firm up the fish flesh and infuse it with the acidic aromas typical of Brazilian tradition.
  2. While the fish is marinating, take a wide casserole, preferably clay, and heat 30 ml of seed oil over medium heat. Add the 120 g of onion cut into thin rounds and let it soften for about 3 minutes until it becomes translucent. The onion should not fry violently but only soften to release its natural sweetness.
  3. Now add the 150 g of red bell pepper and 150 g of yellow bell pepper, previously cut into thin strips. Cook for another 5 minutes over medium-high heat, stirring often until they begin to release their characteristic scent. This colorful sauté will form the aromatic and structural base of your stew.
  4. Arrange the 250 g of tomatoes cut into thick slices over the bed of peppers and sprinkle evenly with 3 g of sweet paprika. From this point on, avoid stirring the ingredients: the original technique requires precise layering that allows flavors to blend without breaking down the vegetables.
  5. Gently lay the cod pieces over the tomato layer, making sure to pour all the marinating liquid (lime and garlic) into the pot as well. Try to create a compact layer so that the steam and juices from the vegetables underneath cook the fish evenly and gently.
  6. Pour the 400 ml of coconut milk over the fish and vegetables, trying to cover the ingredients almost entirely. Coconut milk is the key element that will give the Moqueca its velvety creaminess and exotic flavor, perfectly balancing the savoriness of the fish.
  7. Cover the casserole with a lid and let it cook over medium-low heat for about 15 minutes without ever stirring. To prevent the fish from breaking, limit movements and just shake the pot slightly from time to time; you will know it is ready when the fish appears opaque white and the peppers are very tender.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the remaining 3 g of salt to balance the flavor, and complete with 10 g of coarsely chopped fresh cilantro. Let the dish rest for 2 minutes before serving: this short rest allows the aromas to stabilize and the coconut milk to set slightly, creating a perfect harmony between land and sea.
