Mediterranean Spelt and Chickpea Salad

💰 Save Euro · about €1.40 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A balanced one-dish meal that combines spelt fibre with plant-based chickpea protein, enriched with crisp fresh vegetables. Ideal for a quick and affordable meal, perfect for preparing in advance for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • Pearl spelt280 g
  • Precooked chickpeas (drained weight)480 g
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Cucumber1 pz
  • Tropea red onion (a sweet red onion variety from Calabria)60 g
  • Pitted black olives40 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Lemon (juice and zest)1 pz
  • Fresh parsley20 g
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Rinse the spelt under cold running water using a fine-mesh sieve to remove impurities and excess starch until the water runs clear.
  2. Bring a pot with about 2 litres of lightly salted water to the boil and cook the spelt for about 20 minutes, testing the texture which should be tender but still pleasantly al dente.
  3. Drain the chickpeas from the canning liquid and rinse them thoroughly under water to remove the preserving salt, then let them drain well.
  4. Wash the cherry tomatoes and cut them into quarters; peel the cucumber, remove the central seeds if necessary and cut into small, uniform cubes.
  5. Slice the red onion very finely and soak it in cold water for 10 minutes to make it crunchier and mellow its pungent flavour, improving digestibility.
  6. Once cooked, drain the spelt and run it quickly under a jet of cold water to stop the cooking process and keep the grains separate and firm.
  7. In a small bowl, prepare an emulsion by mixing the extra virgin olive oil with the lemon juice, grated zest and a pinch of black pepper, whisking with a fork.
  8. In a large bowl, combine the well-dried spelt, chickpeas, tomatoes, cucumber, drained onion and sliced olives, mixing gently from bottom to top.
  9. Add the freshly chopped parsley and pour the oil and lemon emulsion over the salad, ensuring every grain is evenly seasoned.
  10. Let the dish rest in the fridge or at room temperature for at least 15 minutes before serving, allowing the Mediterranean flavours to blend perfectly.
