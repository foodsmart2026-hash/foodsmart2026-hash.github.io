Citronette and Vinaigrette

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • lemon juice30 ml
  • white wine vinegar15 ml
  • mustard5 g
  • honey (optional)5 g
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Mustard
May contain traces of: Sulphites.

Method

  1. Squeeze the lemon and filter the juice to remove seeds and excess pulp.
  2. Pour the juice into a bowl and add the vinegar.
  3. Incorporate the mustard and honey, mixing until you obtain a uniform base.
  4. Pour in the oil in a thin stream while whisking vigorously.
  5. Continue until you obtain a slightly creamy emulsion.
  6. Add salt and pepper and taste to check the balance between acidity and savoriness.
  7. Transfer to a container with a lid and shake again before use.
  8. Dress salads, cooked vegetables, fish, or white meats.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes, and alternatives:

- If it is too acidic, slightly increase the oil.

- If it is too fatty, add a little lemon juice.

- For a lemon-free version, use only vinegar.

- You can add parsley, chives, or oregano.
