Black bean and sweetcorn soup

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

A thick and spicy black bean, sweetcorn and red pepper soup, finished with lime and coriander.

Ingredients

  • Cooked black beans500 g
  • Sweetcorn200 g
  • Red pepper150 g
  • Onion100 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Peeled plum tomatoes250 g
  • Ground cumin3 g
  • Vegetable stock700 ml
  • Lime juice20 ml
  • Fresh coriander8 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Sauté the onion, garlic and diced red pepper in the oil for 6 minutes.
  2. Add the cumin, peeled tomatoes and stock.
  3. Stir in the beans and cook for 20 minutes.
  4. Blitz half of the soup until creamy and return it to the pan.
  5. Add the sweetcorn and cook for 5 minutes; season with salt.
  6. Serve with lime juice and coriander.
