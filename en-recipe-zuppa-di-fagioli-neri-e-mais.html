Black bean and sweetcorn soup

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

A thick and spicy black bean, sweetcorn and red pepper soup, finished with lime and coriander.

Ingredients

  • Cooked black beans500 g
  • Sweetcorn200 g
  • Red pepper150 g
  • Onion100 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Peeled plum tomatoes250 g
  • Ground cumin3 g
  • Vegetable stock700 ml
  • Lime juice20 ml
  • Fresh coriander8 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Start by finely chopping 100 g of onion and 6 g of garlic. Heat 30 ml of extra virgin olive oil in a large pot over medium heat and sauté the mixture for about 3 minutes, until the onion becomes translucent and releases its sweet aroma.
  2. Now add 150 g of red pepper cut into small, uniform cubes. Continue cooking for another 3 minutes, stirring frequently: the pepper should soften slightly without burning, intensifying the color and fragrance of the cooking base.
  3. Add 3 g of cumin powder and 250 g of peeled tomatoes, crushing them coarsely with a wooden spoon directly in the pot. Let the flavors meld for a couple of minutes so the heat releases the spice's essential oils, creating a warm and earthy aromatic base.
  4. Pour 700 ml of vegetable broth into the pot and add the 500 g of pre-cooked black beans. Bring everything to a boil, then lower the heat and let it simmer gently for about 15 minutes; this time is essential to allow the beans to absorb the flavors of the spiced base.
  5. Remove about half of the soup with a ladle and blend it with an immersion blender until smooth, then pour it back into the pot. This technical step serves to give the soup a thick and velvety consistency, typical of the best rustic preparations, without adding extra fats.
  6. Stir 200 g of sweet corn into the pot and season with 5 g of fine salt. Continue cooking for the final 5 minutes over low heat, allowing the corn to warm through and integrate perfectly into the creamy structure of the soup.
  7. Turn off the heat and complete the dish by adding 20 ml of fresh lime juice and 8 g of coarsely chopped coriander. Mix well to distribute the lime's acidity, which masterfully balances the sweetness of the legumes, and serve your soup piping hot.
