Marinated chicory, orange segments and bottarga mille-feuille

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Sardegna · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

A fresh and refined starter that plays on the contrast between the bitterness of raw chicory, the sweetness of the orange and the saltiness of the bottarga.

Ingredients

  • Tender Catalogna chicory400g
  • Blonde oranges2
  • Whole or grated mullet bottarga40g
  • Extra virgin olive oil4 tablespoons
  • Lemon juice1 tablespoon
  • Cider vinegar1 tablespoon
  • Fine saltto taste
  • Black peppercornsto taste

Allergens

Fish, Sulphites

Method

  1. Select the tenderest hearts of the Catalogna chicory, removing any tough or fibrous parts.
  2. Wash the leaves thoroughly in cold water and dry them perfectly using a salad spinner.
  3. Cut the chicory into very thin strips lengthways, so they almost form curls.
  4. In a small bowl, prepare an emulsion by whisking the extra virgin olive oil with the cider vinegar, lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  5. Transfer the chicory to a large bowl and dress it with the emulsion, massaging it lightly to let the flavours penetrate; leave to marinate for 10 minutes.
  6. Meanwhile, segment the oranges by removing all the pith and skin to obtain only the clean flesh.
  7. Slice the mullet bottarga into very thin shavings using a truffle slicer or a very sharp knife.
  8. Arrange a first layer of marinated chicory, spread out well, in the centre of the plate.
  9. Place some orange segments and bottarga shavings on top of the chicory, alternating them to create volume.
  10. Finish with a generous grinding of fresh black pepper and serve immediately.
