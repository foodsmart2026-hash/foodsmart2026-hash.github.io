Baked Cauliflower and Turmeric Fritters

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

An original and tasty way to cook cauliflower, avoiding the strong smell of prolonged boiling. Turmeric provides a golden colour and antioxidant properties.

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower florets600 g
  • Eggs2 pcs
  • Chickpea flour or plain flour100 g
  • Grated Pecorino or Grana Padano (hard Italian cheeses)40 g
  • Turmeric powder1 cucchiaino
  • Instant savoury baking powder5 g
  • Parsleyq.b.

Allergens

Eggs, Milk, Gluten

Method

  1. Grate the raw cauliflower with a coarse grater to obtain a sort of 'couscous' texture.
  2. In a bowl, whisk the eggs with the cheese, turmeric, salt and chopped parsley.
  3. Add the flour and baking powder to the egg mixture, stirring until all lumps are removed.
  4. Incorporate the grated cauliflower and mix well: you will get a thick and sticky mixture.
  5. Line a baking tray with parchment paper and, using a spoon, form well-spaced portions.
  6. Lightly flatten each portion to create a flat fritter shape (about 1 cm thick).
  7. Bake at 190°C for 20-25 minutes, turning them halfway through until completely golden.
  8. The fritters are ready when they are firm to the touch and crispy on the edges.
