Baked Cauliflower and Turmeric Fritters

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

An original and tasty way to cook cauliflower, avoiding the strong smell of prolonged boiling. Turmeric provides a golden colour and antioxidant properties.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro⬇️ Big saving🧒 Kid-friendly

Tasty and colorful, these baked fritters are a light alternative to traditional frying, perfect as an appetizer or vegetarian main course. Turmeric adds an exotic aroma and a vibrant yellow hue that complements the cauliflower. The texture is soft inside with a pleasant outer crust.

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower florets600 g
  • Eggs2 pcs
  • Chickpea flour or plain flour100 g
  • Grated Pecorino or Grana Padano (hard Italian cheeses)40 g
  • Turmeric powder1 cucchiaino
  • Instant savoury baking powder5 g
  • Parsleyq.b.

Allergens

Eggs, Milk, Gluten

Method

  1. Start by preparing the cauliflower (600g): separate the florets, wash them thoroughly, and dry them perfectly with a clean kitchen towel. Using a coarse grater, grate the raw florets directly into a large bowl to achieve a texture similar to couscous. This process takes about 10 minutes and should be done gently to avoid mashing the vegetable. The final result should be light, dry, and uniform cauliflower grains. Chef's tip: do not grate the tough part of the stalk as it releases too much water, which would compromise the final crunchiness.
  2. In a second, smaller bowl, crack the 2 eggs and add 40g of grated cheese (Pecorino or Grana), a pinch of salt, and finely chopped fresh parsley. Whisk everything vigorously with a hand whisk or a fork for about 2 minutes until you have a smooth and slightly frothy mixture. Ensure the cheese is well distributed and there are no unmixed egg lumps. The result should be an aromatic, pale yellow batter. Chef's tip: use room-temperature eggs to promote better emulsification with the fats in the cheese.
  3. Add one teaspoon of turmeric powder to the egg mixture, stirring well to spread the colour and aroma. Next, sift 100g of flour (chickpea or plain) along with 5g of instant baking powder directly over the liquid mixture. Work the mixture with a spatula or wooden spoon using circular motions from the centre outwards. You should obtain a very thick, smooth batter with an intense ochre-yellow colour thanks to the turmeric. Chef's tip: sifting the flour is essential to avoid small lumps that would remain raw during the short baking time.
  4. Now combine the previously grated cauliflower with the egg and flour batter. Mix carefully from bottom to top for about 3 minutes, ensuring every grain of cauliflower is perfectly coated by the binder. The final mass will be thick, sticky, and very rich in vegetables, with a spicy and fresh scent. If the mixture seems too dry, let it rest for 2 minutes; the cauliflower will release the necessary moisture. Chef's tip: do not overwork the dough after adding the cauliflower to prevent the vegetable from losing its 'grainy' texture.
  5. Prepare the baking tray by lining it with a sheet of high-quality baking paper and preheat the oven to 190°C (static mode). Take a generous spoonful of dough at a time and place it on the tray, spacing each portion about 3-4 centimetres apart. Repeat until all the mixture is used, aiming for about 12-16 portions in total (3-4 per person). You will have small mounds of golden dough ready to be shaped. Chef's tip: if the dough sticks too much to the spoon, dampen it slightly with water between portions.
  6. Use the back of the spoon to gently flatten each mound of dough, giving them the classic disc shape of fritters. Try to maintain a uniform thickness of about 1 cm to ensure even cooking both in the centre and at the edges. The fritters should be about 6-8 cm in diameter and appear compact on the surface. This step is crucial for achieving the right consistency: neither too dry nor too soggy. Chef's tip: do not make them too thin, otherwise they will become excessively hard and lose the softness of the cauliflower core.
  7. Place the tray on the middle shelf of the preheated oven at 190°C and set the timer for the first 12-13 minutes of cooking. During this time, the egg will set and the baking powder will provide a slight lift, making the fritters lighter. Towards the end of this interval, the edges will start to turn a golden colour and the surface will appear matte. Do not open the oven prematurely to avoid losing the heat necessary for the instant leavening. Chef's tip: if using a fan oven, lower the temperature to 175°C to prevent the outside from burning before the inside is cooked.
  8. Temporarily remove the tray from the oven and, using a thin spatula, gently flip each fritter. Return to the oven for another 10-12 minutes, or until both sides are well browned and the edges are slightly crispy. The scent of toasted turmeric will fill the kitchen, a sign that the Maillard reaction is completing its work. To the touch, the fritters should feel firm but with a slight elasticity in the centre. Chef's tip: in the last 2 minutes, you can activate the grill function to achieve an even more inviting outer crust.
  9. Remove the fritters from the oven and let them rest on the tray for about 3-5 minutes before serving. This short rest allows the internal structure to stabilise and the residual moisture to distribute evenly, ensuring the perfect texture. Arrange them on a serving platter, perhaps accompanied by a yoghurt dip or a fresh salad. They will appear vibrant in colour, inviting in shape, and with an irresistible crunch on the outside. Chef's tip: serve them warm to fully enhance the aroma of the turmeric and pecorino, which tend to fade when cold.

Tips

  • Dry the cauliflower florets thoroughly after washing to prevent the batter from becoming too watery.
  • Use high-quality parchment paper to ensure the fritters don't stick while baking.
  • Let the fritters cool for a few minutes before serving so their structure can set properly.

Variations

  • Replace the pecorino with a well-aged hard cheese for a bolder, sharper flavor profile.
  • Add a pinch of dried chili flakes for a spicy kick that enhances the turmeric aroma.
