Smooth Cannellini Bean Cream with Sage and Good Olive Oil

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A budget-friendly and quick alternative to meat, rich in plant-based proteins and iron. The smooth and velvety texture makes it suitable even for the fussiest children.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Cooked cannellini beans (well rinsed)300 g
  • Potatoes100 g
  • Sage1 leaf
  • Water or unsalted vegetable stock150 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the potato, cut it into small cubes and cook it in the water or stock for about 12 minutes until very soft.
  2. Rinse the cannellini beans under running water for several minutes to completely remove the canning liquid and added industrial salt.
  3. Add the beans to the pot with the potatoes and the whole sage leaf (to add aroma without leaving pieces).
  4. Allow the flavours to infuse over a gentle heat for 5-7 minutes, stirring so that the beans heat through well.
  5. Remove the sage leaf before proceeding to blend it into a cream.
  6. Blend everything with a hand blender until you obtain a velouté without lumps or obvious skins.
  7. If the cream is too thick, add a splash of hot water until the desired consistency is reached.
  8. Plate up and finish with the raw extra virgin olive oil, stirring well before serving.
