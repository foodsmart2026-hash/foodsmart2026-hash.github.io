Bread and Milk Soup with Strawberry Puree

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.45 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 3 min · Easy

A reimagining of a classic old-fashioned snack, made creamy and colourful thanks to fresh fruit. The bread crumb soaks completely, becoming a cream that is easy to swallow even for the little ones.

Ingredients

  • Soft white sandwich bread or white bread crumb100 g
  • Whole milk350 ml
  • Fresh strawberries150 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Carefully remove the crust from the bread, using only the inner crumb which should be soft to the touch.
  2. Wash the strawberries under running water, remove the green hulls, and dry them carefully.
  3. Blend the strawberries or mash them with a fork until you get a fine pulp; if the strawberries have prominent seeds, you can pass the pulp through a fine-mesh sieve.
  4. In a small saucepan, heat the milk without bringing it to a boil: it should be just warm to facilitate absorption.
  5. Tear the bread crumb into small pieces directly into a large bowl.
  6. Pour the warm milk over the bread and let it rest for 2-3 minutes so the bread soaks completely and becomes almost a mush.
  7. Add the strawberry pulp to the milk soup and stir gently to distribute the colour and fruity flavour.
  8. Mix well until a smooth consistency is obtained; if it is too thick, add an extra tablespoon of milk.
