Sautéed Green Beans with Cherry Tomatoes and Capers

💰 Save Euro · about €1.07 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A classic Mediterranean summer side dish, where the freshness of the tomato meets the character of the capers. Ideal for those looking for a light but flavourful dish.

Ingredients

  • Fresh green beans600 g
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Salted capers (capers preserved in salt, common in Italian pantries)15 g
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Fresh basil5 leaves

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Trim the green beans by removing the ends and wash them well; if very long, cut them in half to make them easier to eat.
  2. Boil the green beans in salted boiling water for about 8 minutes: they should be tender but still retain some bite.
  3. Meanwhile, rinse the salt off the capers under running water and cut the cherry tomatoes into halves or quarters.
  4. In a pan, sauté the whole garlic clove in the oil, then add the cherry tomatoes and the rinsed capers.
  5. Cook the cherry tomatoes over medium heat for 5 minutes until they begin to soften and release their juices.
  6. Drain the green beans and toss them directly into the pan with the dressing, turning up the heat for 2-3 minutes.
  7. Mix well so that the beans absorb the flavour of the capers and tomatoes, adjusting the salt if necessary.
  8. Turn off the heat, remove the garlic, and add the basil, torn by hand to prevent it from turning black.
