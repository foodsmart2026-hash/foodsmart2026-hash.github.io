Chicken, Curry Carrot and Raisin Couscous

💰 Save Euro · about €1.68 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A one-dish meal that plays on the sweet-savoury contrast typical of Southern Mediterranean cuisines. Very quick to prepare using basic supermarket ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Semolina couscous250 g
  • Chicken breast400 g
  • Carrots400 g
  • Raisins30 g
  • Curry powder1 cucchiaio
  • Boiling water or stock250 ml

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Soak the raisins in a little lukewarm water for about 10 minutes until they rehydrate and become soft.
  2. Cut the chicken into small bite-sized pieces and the carrots into thin rounds, so they have similar cooking times in the pan.
  3. In a large frying pan, brown the chicken with a drizzle of oil until it changes colour, then add the carrots and curry powder.
  4. Deglaze with a splash of water, cover, and cook for 12-15 minutes; the carrots should be tender and the chicken well-cooked inside.
  5. Meanwhile, pour the couscous into a bowl, add a tablespoon of oil and pour over the boiling salted water; cover and leave to rest for 5 minutes.
  6. Fluff the couscous with a fork to separate the grains, a fundamental step to prevent it from becoming a compact mass.
  7. Add the squeezed raisins to the chicken and carrots, then add the fluffed couscous to the pan and mix over a high heat for one minute.
  8. Serve the dish hot or warm, optionally garnishing with fresh parsley if available.
