Baked Anchovies with Potatoes and Cherry Tomatoes

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Oily fish is the most affordable and healthy choice (rich in Omega-3) in supermarkets. This one-pan meal is simple, quick, and typically Mediterranean.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Fresh cleaned anchovies500 g
  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes500 g
  • Cherry tomatoes200 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Fresh parsley1 ciuffo
  • Breadcrumbs20 g

Allergens

Fish, Gluten

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into very thin slices (about 2 mm) with a mandoline to ensure they cook at the same time as the fish.
  2. Wash the cherry tomatoes and cut them in half; finely chop the garlic and parsley.
  3. In an oiled baking dish, create an even layer of potatoes, season them with salt, a drizzle of oil and some of the chopped herbs.
  4. Bake only the potatoes at 200°C for 10 minutes: this will ensure they are tender before adding the anchovies.
  5. Remove the tray from the oven and place the butterflied anchovies on top of the potatoes, skin side up.
  6. Scatter the cherry tomatoes over the fish and sprinkle with breadcrumbs and the remaining chopped herbs.
  7. Bake again for about 10-12 minutes; anchovies cook very quickly and must not dry out.
  8. Remove from the oven when the breadcrumbs are golden and the cherry tomatoes have slightly softened.
