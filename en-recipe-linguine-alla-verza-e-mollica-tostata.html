Linguine with Savoy Cabbage and Toasted Breadcrumbs

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A humble peasant dish from the rural tradition that transforms simple ingredients into an explosion of flavours. The sweetness of the braised cabbage pairs perfectly with the crunch of the seasoned bread.

Ingredients

  • Linguine320 g
  • Savoy cabbage (Verza)400 g
  • Coarse breadcrumbs or 'mollica' (the soft inner part of a loaf of bread)50 g
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Dried chilli1 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Start by cleaning the Savoy cabbage: remove the tougher outer leaves, cut it into thin strips and wash it thoroughly under running water.
  2. In a large frying pan, heat a drizzle of oil with the breadcrumbs, stirring until they become golden and crunchy for about 3 minutes.
  3. Remove the breadcrumbs from the pan and set them aside; in the same pan, add more oil, the crushed garlic and the chilli.
  4. Add the cabbage with a pinch of salt and a ladle of water, cover with a lid and leave to braise over a medium heat for 12-15 minutes until tender.
  5. Bring a pot of water to the boil for the pasta and, once boiling, add salt and cook the linguine according to the pack instructions.
  6. Drain the pasta when it is 'al dente', keeping a little of the cooking water aside, and tip it into the pan with the braised cabbage.
  7. Toss everything over a high heat for a minute, adding enough cooking water to create a silky emulsion that binds the ingredients together.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the previously toasted breadcrumbs for a final crunchy touch and serve immediately.
