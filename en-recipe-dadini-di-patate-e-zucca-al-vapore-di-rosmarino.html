Steamed Potato and Squash Cubes with Rosemary

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 18 min · Medium

An autumn classic with soft pieces that melt in the mouth, steamed to maintain nutrients and natural sweetness. The rosemary delicately scents the cooking water.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Mantovana or Butternut squash (Mantovana is a sweet, floury Italian pumpkin)250 g
  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes250 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 g
  • Fresh rosemary1 sprig

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the squash, removing the skin, seeds and internal fibres, then cut it into 1 cm cubes.
  2. Peel the potatoes and cut them to the same size as the squash to ensure they cook at the same time.
  3. Fill a pot with two fingers of water and place the rosemary sprig in the water (not in contact with the vegetables).
  4. Place the steamer basket over the pot and pour in the squash and potato cubes.
  5. Turn on the heat, cover tightly and time for about 15-18 minutes from the moment it boils.
  6. Check it's cooked by piercing a potato: it should be soft without offering resistance.
  7. Remove the basket and transfer the vegetables to a large bowl.
  8. Lightly mash only some of the cubes with a fork to create a thicker base that is easier to scoop up with a teaspoon.
  9. Season with the extra virgin olive oil and stir gently so as not to break everything apart.
