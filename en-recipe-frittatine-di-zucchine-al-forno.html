Baked zucchini mini frittatas

4 servings · 25 min · Easy

Soft mini frittatas without the frying.

🧒 Kid-friendly

Small oven-baked frittatas, featuring a soft texture and a delicate flavor enriched by Parmigiano Reggiano. They are ideal as an appetizer or a light main course, perfect for those seeking clean and even cooking.

Ingredients

  • Medium eggs3 pieces (165 g)
  • Zucchini150 g
  • Parmesan Cheese DOP30 g
  • Whole milk30 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil5 g
  • Fine breadcrumbs10 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the zucchini, trim the ends, and grate finely using a medium-hole grater.
  2. Slightly squeeze the zucchini pulp between your hands to remove excess vegetation water.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the eggs with the whole milk using a hand whisk until well combined.
  4. Add the grated Parmesan cheese and breadcrumbs, stirring well to prevent lumps.
  5. Fold the grated zucchini into the egg mixture and blend everything with a spatula.
  6. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) in static mode and lightly grease 8 holes of a mini muffin tin.
  7. Pour the mixture into the molds, filling them up to about 3/4 of their height.
  8. Bake for 15-18 minutes until the mini frittatas are puffed and lightly golden on top.
  9. Let them cool for 5 minutes before removing from the molds to maintain a soft and moist texture.

Tips

  • Squeeze the grated zucchini very well to prevent them from releasing too much water during baking.
  • Use a silicone muffin tin to unmold the frittatas easily without breaking them.
  • If there are leftovers, store them in the fridge and reheat briefly in the oven to restore the original texture.

Variations

  • You can replace zucchini with finely grated pumpkin for an autumnal version.
  • Add a pinch of freshly ground black pepper to enhance the flavor of the cheese.
