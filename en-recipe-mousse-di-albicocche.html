Apricot mousse

Kid-friendly

2 servings · 15 min · Easy

Summer fruit in a soft mousse, from 8 months onwards.

A velvety mousse that enhances the flavor of ripe apricots, balancing them with the creaminess of plain yogurt. It is a fresh and versatile preparation, ideal as a snack or a light dessert.

Ingredients

  • Ripe apricots4 pieces (approx. 160 g)
  • Whole plain yogurt60 g

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the apricots under running water, dry them, and remove the stems.
  2. Cut the apricots in half, remove the stone, and chop them into small, uniform pieces.
  3. Place the pieces in a steamer basket and cook for 6-8 minutes until they are very tender to the touch.
  4. Transfer the cooked apricots to a tall container and blend with an immersion blender until a very smooth puree is formed.
  5. Pass the puree through a fine-mesh sieve to remove any skin fragments or fibers, ensuring a safe consistency for the infant.
  6. Allow the fruit puree to cool completely to room temperature (approx. 20°C).
  7. In a small bowl, combine the whole yogurt with the cooled apricot puree.
  8. Gently stir with a spoon until a smooth and velvety mousse is achieved.
  9. Serve immediately or store in the refrigerator for a maximum of 12 hours in an airtight container.

Tips

  • Always pass the puree through a fine-mesh sieve to achieve a smooth texture free of any fibrous residue.
  • Only fold in the yogurt once the fruit is completely cold to prevent the mousse from becoming too runny.
  • Use very ripe apricots to fully utilize their natural sugar content and intense fragrance.

Variations

  • Replace full-fat yogurt with Greek yogurt to achieve a thicker and firmer mousse consistency.
  • You can flavor the preparation by adding a few finely chopped fresh mint leaves just before serving.
