Fresh egg pasta

4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • 00 Flour400 g
  • Medium eggs4
  • Salt1 pinch
  • Semolina flour for dustingas needed

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs
May contain traces of: Mustard, Soy.

Method

  1. Arrange the flour in a mound on a work surface and create a central well large enough to hold the eggs.
  2. Break the eggs into the center, add the salt, and beat them lightly with a fork, gradually incorporating the flour from the edges.
  3. When the mixture becomes consistent, work it with your hands until all the flour is gathered.
  4. Knead vigorously for about 10–12 minutes, folding and rotating the dough until it becomes a smooth, compact, and elastic mass.
  5. Form a ball, wrap it in plastic wrap, and let it rest for at least 30 minutes at room temperature.
  6. Divide the dough into portions and roll them out with a rolling pin or a pasta machine, starting from the thickest setting and progressively decreasing it.
  7. Cut the sheets into the desired shape: tagliatelle, fettuccine, lasagne, or maltagliati. Dust lightly with semolina to prevent sticking.
  8. Cook in plenty of salted water for about 2–4 minutes, checking the doneness based on the thickness and shape.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes, and alternatives:

- If the dough is too dry, add a tiny bit of water, one teaspoon at a time.

- If it's too sticky, add a little flour.

- Don't skip the rest: it allows the gluten to relax and makes rolling easier.

- For a more rustic pasta, replace 100 g of 00 flour with re-milled semolina.

- For ravioli and tortellini, roll the sheet thinner than for tagliatelle.
