Peppers stuffed with rice and lentils

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 45 min · Easy

Baked peppers stuffed with rice, lentils, tomato and herbs.

Ingredients

  • Peppers4 pieces (800 g)
  • Rice150 g
  • Cooked lentils200 g
  • Chopped tomatoes200 g
  • Onion80 g
  • Parsley10 g
  • Oregano1 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Start by bringing a pot of water to a boil with part of the 6g of salt and cook 150g of rice for just 10 minutes. Drain it while it is still very 'al dente', as it will finish cooking directly in the oven by absorbing the juices from the seasoning.
  2. Carefully wash the 4 peppers (800g) and cut off the top cap, keeping it aside like a lid. Remove the seeds and white internal filaments with a small knife, taking care not to pierce the bottom to create perfect containers for your filling.
  3. Finely chop 80g of onion and let it soften in a large pan with 20ml of extra virgin olive oil. When the onion becomes translucent and releases its sweet aroma without burning, you will have created the ideal aromatic base for the sauce.
  4. Pour 200g of tomato pulp and 200g of boiled lentils into the pan, letting them flavor for about 5 minutes over medium heat. The mixture should be well-combined and slightly reduced, ensuring a rich and full-bodied consistency.
  5. In a large bowl, combine the parboiled rice with the lentil and tomato sauce, adding 10g of chopped fresh parsley, 1g of oregano, and the remaining salt. Mix carefully so that every grain is evenly seasoned, ensuring a balanced flavor in every single bite.
  6. Using a spoon, generously fill the peppers with the rice and lentil mixture up to the rim. Press lightly to avoid the formation of air pockets, but without compressing too much because the rice will tend to swell further during the final baking phase.
  7. Place the peppers in a high-sided baking dish and close them with their original caps. Drizzle the surface with the remaining 20ml of oil and pour a finger of water into the bottom of the dish to generate the moisture needed to keep the pepper flesh tender and juicy.
  8. Bake at 190°C for about 40-45 minutes in a static oven. You will know they are perfectly cooked when the skin of the peppers appears slightly wrinkled and softened, while an inviting scent of roasted vegetables and herbs fills your kitchen.
