Roasted cauliflower with aromatic herbs

💰 Save Euro · about €0.63 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A light and fragrant side dish that transforms cauliflower into a crispy treat thanks to its aromatic coating. Perfect to accompany fish or legume dishes, showcasing an affordable and versatile seasonal vegetable.

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower (trimmed weight)800 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Wholemeal breadcrumbs40 g
  • Fine salt5 g
  • Black pepper1 g
  • Fresh rosemary2 sprigs
  • Fresh thyme3 sprigs
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Organic lemon zest5 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C in fan mode to ensure rapid and even browning of the vegetables.
  2. Trim the cauliflower by removing the green leaves and the base of the core, then cut it into similar-sized florets (about 3 cm).
  3. Wash the florets under running water and dry them perfectly with a clean tea towel; this step is essential to achieve a crispy rather than boiled texture.
  4. Finely chop the rosemary needles, thyme leaves and the garlic, having removed its central germ to make it more digestible.
  5. In a large bowl, mix the wholemeal breadcrumbs with the chopped herbs, garlic, grated lemon zest, salt and pepper.
  6. Add the cauliflower florets to the bowl and pour in the extra virgin olive oil, mixing with your hands so that every piece is evenly coated with oil and breadcrumbs.
  7. Distribute the cauliflower on a baking tray lined with greaseproof paper, spacing the pieces well apart to prevent steam from stopping a crust from forming.
  8. Bake for about 20-25 minutes, making sure to turn the florets halfway through: the dish is ready when the edges are golden and the inside is tender to the bite.
  9. Remove from the oven and serve warm, checking that the fragrance of the lemon and herbs has been fully released, making the side dish fresh and appealing.
