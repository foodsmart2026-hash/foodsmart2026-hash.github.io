Stuffed Paccheri Gratin with Pumpkin, Sausage and Provola

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Campania · 4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A stunning baked pasta dish where the pumpkin becomes a creamy filling that perfectly complements the bold flavour of the sausage.

Ingredients

  • Gragnano paccheri320g
  • Prepared Mantovana pumpkin600g
  • Pork sausage200g
  • Smoked provola cheese150g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano60g
  • Brown onion1/2
  • Fresh rosemary1 sprig
  • Dry white wine50ml
  • Extra virgin olive oilas needed
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Dice the pumpkin into small cubes and finely chop the onion.
  2. In a large frying pan, sauté the onion in the oil, add the pumpkin and rosemary, and cook with a ladleful of water until tender.
  3. Crumble the sausage into another pan and brown until golden, deglazing with the white wine.
  4. Mash the cooked pumpkin with a fork until creamy (remove the rosemary) and add it to the browned sausage.
  5. Allow the mixture to cool slightly, then stir in the very finely diced provola and half of the Parmigiano.
  6. Boil the paccheri in plenty of salted water, draining them halfway through the cooking time indicated on the packet.
  7. Rinse the paccheri under cold water to stop the cooking process and coat with a drizzle of oil to prevent them from sticking.
  8. Transfer the pumpkin and sausage filling into a piping bag and generously stuff each pacchero.
  9. Arrange the paccheri vertically in a greased baking dish, then sprinkle with the remaining Parmigiano and a drizzle of oil.
  10. Bake at 200°C for about 15 minutes until a golden crust has formed on the surface.
