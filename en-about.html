About us

Food Smart is an independent digital brand, born in Italy with a European outlook. It is not related to companies, nutrition platforms or projects with similar names.

What Food Smart is

Food Smart is a digital platform that brings together cooking, recipes, grocery planning and savings. It is available as a website, www.food-smart.it, and as a mobile app.

How the website and the app work

On the website you can search and read recipes, explore categories, discover new dishes in “Recipes to discover” and “Food Smart combinations” and read our articles. With your account you can save favourite recipes, publish your own recipes and browse the community recipes.

The app uses the same account and the same recipes as the website and adds the shopping tools: digital pantry, shopping list, product search and estimated prices. From every recipe on the website you can open the same recipe in the app.

Services and features

Available now

  • Recipe search: Italian, international, Asian, vegetarian and vegan, kids' recipes and desserts
  • Ingredient guides with nutrition information, properties, benefits, contraindications and allergens
  • Allergen information and recipes suitable for a low-nickel diet
  • Favourite recipes and personal recipes with photos, on the website and in the app
  • Community recipes, published by users after review
  • Adjustable servings with automatic recalculation of quantities
  • Digital pantry with the expiry dates of the food you have at home (app)
  • Shopping list created from recipes, with the packs to buy (app)
  • Product search and comparison of estimated supermarket prices, with nearby stores (app)

Coming soon

  • Android app on Google Play and iPhone app on the App Store.

How it helps you plan your shopping

  • Choose the recipes you want to cook and the number of servings: ingredient quantities are recalculated automatically.
  • Food Smart removes from the list what you already have in your pantry.
  • For each ingredient it shows the packs to buy, so you only buy what you need.
  • The shopping list gathers the ingredients of all the chosen recipes in a single list.

How it helps you save

Comparing estimated prices, managing the food you have at home with expiry dates and keeping a tidy shopping list help you avoid unnecessary purchases, reduce waste and keep your food budget under control. Prices shown are indicative estimates, not official store prices.

Articles and guides

In “Good to know” and “Kitchen secrets and techniques” we publish articles on food storage, food safety, labels and expiry dates, allergens, cooking techniques, doughs, sauces and tips to save time, energy and money.

Where we operate

Food Smart is aimed at the European market, with content in Italian and English, and may expand to other countries.
