Valle d'Aosta style cheesy polenta

Valle d'Aosta · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 45 min · Easy

Soft polenta enriched with Fontina cheese and melted butter.

Ingredients

  • Polenta flour300 g
  • Water1200 ml
  • Fontina DOP cheese200 g
  • Butter60 g
  • Coarse sea salt10 g

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Bring the salted water to the boil.
  2. Gradually whisk in the polenta flour to avoid any lumps.
  3. Cook for 40 minutes over a low heat, stirring frequently.
  4. Stir in the diced Fontina until fully melted.
  5. Melt the butter until lightly browned and pour it over the polenta once served.
