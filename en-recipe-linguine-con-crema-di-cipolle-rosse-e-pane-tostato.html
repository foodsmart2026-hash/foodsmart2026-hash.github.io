Linguine with Red Onion Cream and Toasted Breadcrumbs

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Red onions, slow-cooked for a long time, become sweet and creamy, creating a perfect base for the pasta. Toasted bread adds the necessary texture for a balanced dish.

Ingredients

  • Linguine320 g
  • Tropea red onions (sweet red onions from Calabria)400 g
  • Grated Pecorino Romano (sharp, salty sheep's milk cheese)40 g
  • Stale bread (breadcrumbs)50 g
  • Apple cider vinegar1 cucchiaio
  • Sugar1 cucchiaino
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Salt and pepperq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Slice the red onions very thinly and put them in a pan with the oil and a pinch of salt.
  2. Add the sugar and apple cider vinegar, cover and leave to stew over a very low heat for 20 minutes, until they have reduced to a pulp.
  3. While the onions are cooking, crumble the stale bread and toast it in a small non-stick pan with a drizzle of oil until it becomes brown and crunchy.
  4. Take half of the onions and blend them with a little pasta cooking water to obtain a cream.
  5. Cook the linguine in plenty of salted water, draining them al dente.
  6. Combine the pasta with the onions in the pan and also add the blended part.
  7. Mix vigorously, adding the Pecorino to bind the dressing well.
  8. Plate up and sprinkle generously with the toasted breadcrumbs and a grind of pepper.
