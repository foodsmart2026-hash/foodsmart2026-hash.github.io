Chickpea and Spinach Stew with Poached Eggs

💰 Save Euro · about €1.16 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 18 min · Easy

A humble but complete dish, typical of European rural cuisine, combining pulses and leafy greens. The egg cooked directly in the sauce creates a creamy and very economical meal.

Ingredients

  • Pre-cooked chickpeas480 g
  • Fresh (or frozen) spinach400 g
  • Tomato passata (smooth sieved tomatoes)200 ml
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Eggs4 pcs
  • Rustic bread4 fette
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Eggs, Gluten

Method

  1. In a saucepan, sauté the whole garlic clove in oil, then remove it once it starts to brown.
  2. Add the cleaned spinach and let it wilt for 3-4 minutes until it has reduced in volume.
  3. Add the rinsed chickpeas and the tomato passata, along with a pinch of salt and half a glass of water.
  4. Let it simmer over low heat for 10 minutes so the flavours meld and the sauce becomes thick.
  5. Create four spaces in the chickpea and spinach mixture with a spoon.
  6. Crack an egg into each space, cover the saucepan, and cook for 3-4 minutes over a very low flame.
  7. The egg is ready when the white turns milky white but the yolk remains soft (poached style).
  8. Toast the bread slices and place them in individual bowls before covering them with the stew and the egg.
  9. Finish with a drizzle of raw olive oil and a grating of black pepper.
