Imam bayildi

Turkey · 4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

Turkish braised aubergines stuffed with onions, tomato and parsley, served lukewarm.

Ingredients

  • Long aubergines800 g
  • Onions300 g
  • Tomatoes350 g
  • Garlic10 g
  • Parsley15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil90 ml
  • Sugar5 g
  • Water150 ml
  • Fine salt7 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the aubergines in alternating strips, make a lengthwise slit in each and salt them; leave for 20 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Brown them in 50 ml of oil on all sides for 8 minutes.
  3. Soften the sliced onions with the garlic in the remaining oil for 10 minutes.
  4. Add the diced tomatoes, sugar, salt and parsley and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Open the aubergines, fill them with the stuffing and arrange them in a baking dish with the water.
  6. Bake in the oven at 180 °C for 35 minutes; serve lukewarm.
