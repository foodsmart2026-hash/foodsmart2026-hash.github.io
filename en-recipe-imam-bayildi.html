Imam bayildi

Turkey · 4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

Turkish braised aubergines stuffed with onions, tomato and parsley, served lukewarm.

Ingredients

  • Long aubergines800 g
  • Onions300 g
  • Tomatoes350 g
  • Garlic10 g
  • Parsley15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil90 ml
  • Sugar5 g
  • Water150 ml
  • Fine salt7 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Start by washing 800 g of eggplants and partially peeling them in a 'zebra' pattern with alternating stripes, then cut a slit lengthwise without piercing the bottom. Sprinkle the inside with about half of the 7 g of salt and let them rest for 20 minutes; this helps drain the bitter juices and soften the fibers.
  2. After the resting time, quickly rinse the eggplants and dry them extremely thoroughly to prevent dangerous hot oil splatters. Heat 50 ml of extra virgin olive oil in a large skillet and sear the eggplants on all sides over medium heat for about 8 minutes. You will know they are ready when the skin is evenly golden and the flesh begins to soften slightly under pressure.
  3. In the same pan, add the remaining 40 ml of oil along with 300 g of thinly sliced onions and 10 g of minced garlic. Let them soften over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring often until they become translucent and release a sweet aroma; this slow cooking is essential to remove the onions' acidity and make them digestible.
  4. Now add 350 g of small diced tomatoes, 5 g of sugar, the remaining salt, and 15 g of chopped fresh parsley. Continue cooking for another 5 minutes over medium-high heat, allowing the flavors to meld and the tomatoes to soften without completely breaking down. The sugar will perfectly balance the tomatoes' natural acidity, bringing harmony to the filling.
  5. Take the seared eggplants and gently widen the central slit with a spoon to create the necessary space for the filling. Stuff each eggplant generously with the onion and tomato mixture, pressing down lightly to pack it in. Ensure each eggplant receives an equal share of this flavorful stuffing.
  6. Place the eggplants in a high-sided baking dish, arranging them close together but without overlapping. Pour 150 ml of water into the bottom of the dish, being careful not to wet the filling directly to avoid diluting its flavor. Bake in a preheated static oven at 180°C for about 35 minutes, until the eggplants are very tender and the liquid at the bottom is almost completely absorbed.
  7. Once out of the oven, let the Imam bayildi rest until it reaches a lukewarm or even room temperature. This step is essential for the flavors to settle and for the oil to fully infuse into the eggplant flesh, making it irresistibly velvety. Serve them with a drizzle of their fragrant cooking juices for an authentic experience.
