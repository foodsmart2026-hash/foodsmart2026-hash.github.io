Baked Borlotti Bean and Potato Burgers

4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A great vegetarian and budget-friendly alternative to classic meat burgers, rich in fibre and very filling. The texture is crispy on the outside and soft inside, perfect for Saturday night burger buns.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Pre-cooked borlotti beans480 g
  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes300 g
  • Breadcrumbs60 g
  • Grated Pecorino (a sharp, aged Italian sheep's milk cheese)30 g
  • Medium egg1 pcs
  • Rosemary1 rametto
  • Salt and Pepperq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins in salted water for about 25 minutes until tender when pierced with a fork.
  2. Drain the borlotti beans, rinse them under running water and mash them with a fork in a bowl, leaving a few pieces whole for texture.
  3. Peel the potatoes while still hot, mash them with a potato ricer and add them to the beans in the bowl.
  4. Add the egg, grated pecorino, finely chopped rosemary and season with salt and pepper, mixing with your hands.
  5. Add the breadcrumbs a little at a time: the mixture should be mouldable and not sticky.
  6. Shape into 8 compact burgers and place them on a baking tray lined with parchment paper greased with a drizzle of oil.
  7. Bake at 200°C (fan oven) for 10 minutes per side, turning them gently halfway through cooking.
  8. The burgers are ready when the surface is well browned and they feel firm rather than soft to the touch.
  9. Leave to cool for 2 minutes before serving to allow the structure to stabilise.
