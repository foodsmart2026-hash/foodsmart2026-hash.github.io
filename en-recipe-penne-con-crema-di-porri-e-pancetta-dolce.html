Penne with Leek Cream and Mild Pancetta

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

Leeks turn into a velvety cream that coats the pasta, contrasted by the crunchiness of the pancetta. A gourmet first course made with everyday ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Penne rigate320 g
  • Leeks (white part only)300 g
  • Mild pancetta cubes (Italian salt-cured pork belly)80 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano (aged hard cheese)30 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Pasta cooking water100 ml
  • Salt and pepperq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Clean the leeks by removing the dark green part and slice them into thin rounds, then wash them well.
  2. In a large pan, heat the oil and add the leeks with a pinch of salt; let them stew over a medium heat for 10 minutes until they become translucent and tender.
  3. Transfer the cooked leeks to a blender and process them with a ladle of hot water until you obtain a smooth and thick cream.
  4. In the same pan used for the leeks, brown the pancetta without adding any fat until it becomes golden and crispy.
  5. Cook the penne in plenty of salted water, draining them about 2 minutes before the end of the cooking time.
  6. Pour the leek cream into the pan with the pancetta and add the drained penne, keeping a bit of the cooking water.
  7. Toss the pasta, adding the Parmigiano and enough cooking water to create a creamy emulsion.
  8. Serve immediately, finishing with a dusting of freshly ground black pepper.
