Cinnamon and Apple Sweet Bread

💰 Save Euro · about €0.56 per person

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A soft and fragrant leavened loaf, perfect for starting the day with the aroma of baked apples. The ingredients are simple and budget-friendly, typical of an average Italian pantry.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour (Type 00, a finely milled Italian soft wheat flour)300 g
  • Golden Delicious apples2 pcs
  • Light brown sugar80 g
  • Whole milk120 ml
  • Butter50 g
  • Dried yeast4 g
  • Ground cinnamon5 g
  • Salt1 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm milk (not hot) with a teaspoon of sugar to activate it until you see a light foam on the surface.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the flour with the remaining sugar, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt to distribute the aromas well.
  3. Add the milk with the yeast and the lukewarm melted butter, kneading vigorously for about 10 minutes until you get a smooth and elastic dough.
  4. Peel the apples and cut them into small cubes (about 1 cm), gently folding them into the dough so as not to break them too much.
  5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a damp cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for 2 hours, or until doubled in size.
  6. Gently deflate the dough and form a loaf or place it in a loaf tin lined with baking parchment for the second rising of 30 minutes.
  7. Preheat the conventional oven to 180°C and brush the surface with a little milk to achieve a golden and shiny crust.
  8. Bake for 35-40 minutes: the bread is ready when it sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom and the surface is brown.
  9. Leave to cool completely on a wire rack before slicing, so the moisture from the apples distributes evenly.
