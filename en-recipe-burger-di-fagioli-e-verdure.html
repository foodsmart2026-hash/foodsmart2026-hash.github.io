Bean and Vegetable Burgers

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 15 min · Medium

A tasty and economical plant-based alternative to classic burgers, ideal for integrating legumes into the family diet. The combination of cannellini beans and fresh vegetables ensures an excellent protein and fibre profile with a texture that is crispy outside and soft inside.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Cooked cannellini beans (drained)480 g
  • Carrots150 g
  • Courgettes150 g
  • Wholemeal breadcrumbs80 g
  • Egg1 pc
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Brown onion50 g
  • Fresh parsley10 g
  • Unrefined sea salt4 g
  • Black pepper1 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Carefully drain the cannellini beans from their canning liquid, rinse them under running water, and pat them dry with kitchen paper to remove excess moisture.
  2. Wash and trim the carrots and courgettes, then grate them using a coarse grater; firmly squeeze the grated vegetables between your hands or in a clean tea towel to remove the vegetable water.
  3. Finely chop the onion and sweat it in a non-stick pan with a tablespoon of oil (10 ml) for about 5 minutes over a low heat, until soft and translucent.
  4. In a large bowl, mash the beans with a fork until you get a coarse purée (do not use a blender to prevent the mixture from becoming too liquid).
  5. Add the grated vegetables, softened onion, chopped parsley, egg, salt, and pepper to the bean purée, mixing well to combine the flavours.
  6. Gradually incorporate the wholemeal breadcrumbs, mixing by hand: the final mixture should be workable, not sticky but still slightly moist.
  7. Divide the mixture into 4 portions of about 180g each, form into balls and gently flatten them to create burgers about 2 cm thick, compacting the edges well with your fingers.
  8. Heat the remaining extra virgin olive oil (20 ml) in a large pan and cook the burgers over a medium heat for about 6 minutes per side, turning them only once a well-browned and solid crust has formed.
