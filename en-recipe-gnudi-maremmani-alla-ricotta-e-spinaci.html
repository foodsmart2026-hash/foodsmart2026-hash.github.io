Spinach and Ricotta Gnudi

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 15 min · Medium

A Tuscan classic 'gnudi' (naked), which is essentially the ravioli filling served without the pasta shell. Light, budget-friendly and very quick to prepare using fridge staples.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • frozen spinach600 g
  • cow's milk ricotta (soft Italian whey cheese)250 g
  • large eggs1 pcs
  • grated Parmigiano Reggiano (hard aged Italian cheese)60 g
  • 00 flour (finely milled Italian flour)50 g
  • butter40 g
  • fresh sage4 foglie
  • nutmeg1 pizzico

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Cook the spinach in a pan with a splash of water for 8-10 minutes until completely wilted.
  2. Squeeze the spinach extremely hard using a potato ricer or a clean tea towel; not a single drop of water should remain to prevent the gnudi from falling apart during cooking.
  3. Finely chop the spinach with a knife and combine in a bowl with the well-drained ricotta, egg, Parmigiano, and nutmeg.
  4. Mix the mixture with a fork until smooth; the consistency should be firm but malleable.
  5. Form balls the size of a walnut (about 20-25g each) by lightly rolling them between floured palms.
  6. Toss each ball in a light dusting of flour, shaking off the excess; they should look slightly powdery but not heavy.
  7. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil and gently drop the gnudi in; they are ready when they float to the surface, like gnocchi (about 2-3 minutes).
  8. Drain them with a slotted spoon and toss in a pan with melted butter and sage until glossy and fragrant.
