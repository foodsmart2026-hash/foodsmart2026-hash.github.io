Calamarata Pasta with Squid and Chickpea Puree

2 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

An autumnal dish, tasty and fragrant!

🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Calamarata pasta160 g
  • Squid200 g
  • Cooked chickpeas300 g (cooked weight)
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Chili pepperto taste
  • Rosemary1 sprig
  • Extra virgin olive oilto taste
  • Lemon juice15 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Molluscs
May contain traces of: Crustaceans, Fish, Mustard, Soy.

Method

  1. In a large skillet, add oil and sauté a garlic clove, the broken fresh chili pepper, and the rosemary sprig.
  2. Clean and wash the squid, cut them into pieces that are not too small, cook them in the skillet with the previously prepared sauté, and season with salt. Once cooked, remove the garlic, chili pepper, and rosemary from the pan.
  3. In the meantime, using an immersion blender, blend the chickpeas with a bit of extra virgin olive oil, a pinch of salt, and the lemon juice until a cream is obtained.
  4. Once the squid is cooked, add the chickpea puree to the still-hot skillet and let the flavors meld for a few minutes.
  5. Cook the pasta in plenty of salted water. Drain the pasta while still al dente and toss it in the pan with the sauce.
  6. Serve hot.
