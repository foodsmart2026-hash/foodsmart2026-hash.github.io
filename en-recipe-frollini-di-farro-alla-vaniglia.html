Vanilla spelt biscuits

Kid-friendly

25 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

Crumbly spelt flour biscuits with low sugar, easy to hold. Warning: contains gluten, eggs and milk.

Ingredients

  • Spelt flour200 g
  • Butter80 g
  • Egg50 g
  • Sugar40 g
  • Vanilla extract3 ml
  • Baking powder3 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Cream the softened butter with the sugar.
  2. Add the egg and vanilla.
  3. Combine the flour and baking powder and knead briefly; leave to rest in the fridge for 30 minutes.
  4. Roll out to 5 mm and cut out the biscuits with simple cutters.
  5. Bake at 175 °C for 10-12 minutes, until just golden.
  6. Allow to cool completely: they will become crumbly.
