Vanilla spelt biscuits

Kid-friendly

25 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

Crumbly spelt flour biscuits with low sugar, easy to hold. Warning: contains gluten, eggs and milk.

These rustic shortbread cookies combine the nutty notes of spelt flour with the sweet aroma of vanilla. Perfect for breakfast or a snack, they offer a crumbly texture and an authentic flavor.

Ingredients

  • Spelt flour200 g
  • Butter80 g
  • Egg50 g
  • Sugar40 g
  • Vanilla extract3 ml
  • Baking powder3 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Start by working the softened butter (80 g) with the sugar (40 g) using a spatula or a hand whisk. You need to achieve a pale, silky cream: this step is essential to ensure the sugar dissolves properly and the cookies have a uniform texture.
  2. Add the whole egg (50 g) and the vanilla extract (3 ml) to the butter and sugar mixture. Mix vigorously until the egg is completely absorbed; you will immediately smell the sweet aroma of vanilla beginning to fragrance your dough.
  3. Carefully sift the spelt flour (200 g) together with the baking powder (3 g) directly over the bowl. Sifting is a chef's secret to avoid lumps and to evenly distribute the leavening agent, ensuring a consistent rise in the oven.
  4. Incorporate the dry ingredients into the cream by working the dough quickly with your fingertips or a fork. It is important not to overheat the butter or overwork the spelt gluten: stop as soon as the dough is homogeneous to preserve its crumbliness.
  5. Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and let it rest in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes. This rest allows the butter to solidify again and the flour to stabilize, making the pastry much easier to roll out without breaking.
  6. Preheat the oven to 175 °C and, on a lightly floured work surface, roll out the pastry with a rolling pin until it reaches a thickness of 5 mm. Try to be precise with the thickness: an even surface ensures that all cookies bake in the same amount of time.
  7. Cut out the shortbread cookies using simple molds to get about 25 portions. Arrange them neatly on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, spacing them a couple of centimeters apart to encourage heat circulation during baking.
  8. Bake at 175 °C for 10-12 minutes, making sure the surface remains pale and only the edges just begin to turn golden. The toasted scent of spelt will indicate they are almost ready; do not overcook them or they will become hard instead of crumbly.
  9. Remove the tray and let the cookies rest for 5 minutes before moving them to a wire rack. Only once completely cold will they reach their final consistency, becoming perfect to hold and deliciously crumbly to the bite.

Tips

  • Ensure the butter is softened but not melted to achieve a pliable dough.
  • Let the dough rest in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to make rolling easier.
  • Store the cookies in a tin box to keep them crunchy for several days.

Variations

  • You can replace the vanilla extract with grated lemon zest for a citrusy note.
  • Add a pinch of cinnamon to the dough for a spicier and more comforting aroma.
