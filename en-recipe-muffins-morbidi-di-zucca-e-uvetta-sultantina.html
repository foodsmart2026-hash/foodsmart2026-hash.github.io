Soft Pumpkin and Sultana Muffins

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 18 min · Medium

Mantuan pumpkin (a sweet, dense Italian variety) gives a vibrant colour and natural sweetness to these small muffins. They are spongy and melt in the mouth, ideal for a snack for the whole family.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly

Ingredients

  • Steamed pumpkin pulp150 g
  • Type 0 flour (Italian medium-strength wheat flour)100 g
  • Sunflower oil30 ml
  • Sultanas30 g
  • Baking powder4 g
  • Whole milk20 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Soak the sultanas in a little hot water for 10 minutes, then drain and chop them finely with a knife.
  2. Blend the cooked pumpkin pulp together with the sunflower oil and milk until you get a smooth, thick cream.
  3. In a bowl, sift the flour with the baking powder to aerate the dry ingredients.
  4. Combine the pumpkin cream with the flour and fold gently from bottom to top using a spatula.
  5. Fold in the chopped sultanas, which will provide small bursts of natural sweetness in every bite.
  6. Pour the mixture into paper cases or silicone muffin moulds, filling them about two-thirds full.
  7. Bake at 180°C for about 15-18 minutes: check they are cooked by inserting a skewer, which should come out dry.
  8. Cool the muffins on a wire rack to prevent moisture from making them too spongy at the base.
  9. Serve the muffins halved or in small pieces to make it easier for a child to grip.
