Spaghetti with garlic, oil and toasted breadcrumbs

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

Garlic, oil and chilli spaghetti with crunchy breadcrumbs instead of cheese.

Ingredients

  • Durum wheat spaghetti360 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil70 ml
  • Garlic10 g
  • Chilli1 g
  • Breadcrumbs50 g
  • Parsley10 g
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Soy, Eggs.

Method

  1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water.
  2. Toast the breadcrumbs in a pan with 10 ml of the oil until golden; set aside.
  3. Heat the remaining oil with the sliced garlic and the chilli over a low heat, without burning the garlic.
  4. Drain the pasta al dente and toss it in the oil with a small ladle of the cooking water.
  5. Serve with chopped parsley and the toasted breadcrumbs.
