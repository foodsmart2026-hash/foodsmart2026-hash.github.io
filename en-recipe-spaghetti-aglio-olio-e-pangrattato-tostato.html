Spaghetti with garlic, oil and toasted breadcrumbs

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

Garlic, oil and chilli spaghetti with crunchy breadcrumbs instead of cheese.

🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

A traditional Italian pasta dish that elevates simple ingredients with an irresistible crunchy touch. The intense flavors of garlic and chili flakes blend perfectly with the texture of golden breadcrumbs. It is the ideal solution for a quick yet characterful dinner.

Ingredients

  • Durum wheat spaghetti360 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil70 ml
  • Garlic10 g
  • Chilli1 g
  • Breadcrumbs50 g
  • Parsley10 g
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Soy, Eggs.

Method

  1. Bring a large pot with about 4 liters of water to a boil and, as soon as it reaches a rolling boil, add the 8 g of fine salt. This will allow the spaghetti to be properly seasoned from the very heart of the pasta during cooking.
  2. In a small non-stick pan, pour 10 ml of extra virgin olive oil and add the 50 g of breadcrumbs. Toast over medium-low heat for about 3 minutes, stirring often until it turns a beautiful golden brown and releases a toasted bread aroma; then set it aside in a small bowl to keep it crunchy.
  3. Prepare the aromatic base by thinly slicing the 10 g of garlic and mincing the 1 gram of chili pepper. Slicing the garlic thinly, rather than chopping it, allows the essential oils to be released without the risk of it burning too quickly in the next step.
  4. In the main pan, heat the remaining 60 ml of extra virgin olive oil together with the garlic and chili over very low heat. Let it sauté for about 2-3 minutes until the garlic becomes slightly blond: it must not darken too much, otherwise it would become bitter and ruin the sweetness of the oil.
  5. Drop the 360 g of spaghetti into the boiling water and cook them following the package instructions, but drain them 2 minutes before the end of the indicated time. Remember to save a ladle of the starch-rich cooking water, which will be essential for creating a silky emulsion.
  6. Transfer the spaghetti directly into the pan with the flavored oil, slightly increasing the heat to enliven the cooking. Add the reserved ladle of cooking water and toss the pasta vigorously for about 2 minutes, so that the oil and water form a delicious cream that coats every strand.
  7. While the pasta finishes absorbing the flavors, finely chop the 10 g of fresh parsley and add it to the pan only after turning off the heat. Mix well so that the residual heat releases the aroma of the herb without overcooking it, thus maintaining a bright green color and a fresh taste.
  8. Plate the hot spaghetti immediately and complete each serving by generously distributing the previously prepared crispy toasted breadcrumbs. This 'mollica' acts as an excellent cheese substitute, providing an irresistible contrast of textures between the softness of the pasta and the crunchiness of the bread.

Tips

  • Toast the breadcrumbs over low heat to achieve an even golden color without burning them.
  • Slice the garlic very thinly to allow it to release its full aroma into the warm oil without browning too much.
  • Save a cup of pasta water to toss with the spaghetti for a silkier finish before serving.

Variations

  • Replace fresh parsley with dried oregano for a more rustic, Mediterranean fragrance.
  • Add a handful of chopped walnuts along with the breadcrumbs to enhance the dish's crunchiness and complexity.
