Creamy Star Pasta with Broccoli Velouté and Fresh Ricotta

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A delicate first course that transforms broccoli into a velvety cream, perfect for helping little ones enjoy green vegetables. The ricotta adds a soft protein note and an enveloping texture without being heavy.

Ingredients

  • Star-shaped pasta (Stelline)160 g
  • Broccoli (florets)300 g
  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (a soft, mild Italian whey cheese)100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Water or unsalted vegetable stock500 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the broccoli and use only the tenderest florets, removing the tough parts of the stalk.
  2. Bring the water or vegetable stock to a boil in a medium-sized pot.
  3. Add the broccoli to the liquid and cook for about 15 minutes, until very tender when pierced with a fork.
  4. Drain the broccoli, reserving the cooking liquid, and blend with a hand blender, adding a tablespoon of the cooking water until a smooth cream is obtained.
  5. Pour the star pasta into the remaining cooking liquid and cook for the time indicated on the packet (usually 5-7 minutes).
  6. While the pasta is cooking, mix the ricotta with the extra virgin olive oil in a small bowl until it becomes a smooth cream.
  7. Drain the star pasta (leaving it slightly moist) and combine it with the broccoli velouté.
  8. Add the ricotta cream with the heat off and stir well to blend all the textures.
  9. Check that the temperature is lukewarm before serving to children; the rest of the family can add salt and pepper directly to their own plates.
