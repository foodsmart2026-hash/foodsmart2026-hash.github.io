Mediterranean Rice with Seasonal Vegetables

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A balanced main course combining the complex carbohydrates of brown rice with plant-based protein from chickpeas. An economical and versatile recipe, perfect for the family and rich in fibre thanks to fresh vegetables.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Brown rice320 g
  • Pre-cooked chickpeas (jarred)240 g
  • Medium courgettes300 g
  • Cherry tomatoes200 g
  • Yellow onion60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Vegetable stock900 ml
  • Fresh parsley10 g
  • Lemon (zest)1 pc
  • Salt2 g

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the courgettes and cut them into approximately 1 cm cubes; cut the cherry tomatoes into quarters. Uniformity in cutting ensures even cooking and a pleasant texture.
  2. Finely chop the onion and sweat it in a large pan or pot with half of the extra virgin olive oil and two tablespoons of stock. The onion should become translucent and soft in about 5 minutes without browning, acting as a sweet base.
  3. Pour the brown rice into the pot and toast it over a high heat for 2 minutes, stirring constantly. The rice is ready for cooking when the grains appear glossy and hot to the touch; this is essential to seal them.
  4. Begin adding the boiling vegetable stock one ladle at a time, just covering the rice. Cook over a gentle heat following the times on the packet (usually 35-40 minutes for brown rice), maintaining a light simmer.
  5. Halfway through the rice cooking (after about 18-20 minutes), add the cubed courgettes. This delayed addition allows the vegetables to cook while remaining firm and bright green, preserving their vitamins.
  6. Add the pre-cooked chickpeas, previously rinsed very well under running water to remove the canning liquid and excess sodium. Add them to the rice 5 minutes before the end of cooking to warm and flavour them.
  7. Stir in the cherry tomatoes for the last 3 minutes of cooking. They should only soften slightly to release their natural acidity, which balances the sweetness of the chickpeas and courgettes.
  8. Turn off the heat when the rice is al dente and the liquid has been almost completely absorbed. Add the rest of the extra virgin olive oil raw and the grated lemon zest to enhance the Mediterranean aromas.
  9. Add the finely chopped fresh parsley and let the rice rest, covered, for 2 minutes before serving. This final rest allows the flavours to blend perfectly and the grains to settle into their ideal consistency.
