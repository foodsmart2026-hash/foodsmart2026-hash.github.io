Seasonal Vegetable Mini-Frittatas

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A nutritious and versatile starter that combines the freshness of courgettes with the savouriness of chickpeas. These small golden frittatas are ideal for introducing pulses and fibre into the family diet in a tasty and economical way.

Ingredients

  • Large eggs4 pz
  • Courgettes200 g
  • Carrots100 g
  • Canned chickpeas150 g
  • Wholemeal wheat flour40 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano (a hard, aged Italian PDO cheese)30 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil25 ml
  • Unrefined sea salt5 g
  • Black pepper2 g
  • Fresh parsley10 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the courgettes and carrots, then grate them coarsely with a large-hole grater to maintain a good texture after cooking.
  2. Place the grated vegetables in a colander, salt them lightly and let them rest for 10 minutes to release their water, then squeeze them vigorously with your hands.
  3. Drain the chickpeas, rinse them under running water and coarsely mash half of them with a fork to help bind the mixture.
  4. In a large bowl, crack the 4 eggs and whisk them with a hand whisk along with the Parmigiano, pepper, and chopped parsley until the mixture is smooth.
  5. Sift the wholemeal flour directly over the egg mixture, stirring carefully to avoid lumps that would compromise the fluffiness.
  6. Add the squeezed vegetables and all the chickpeas (whole and mashed) to the egg batter, mixing everything with a spoon until you have a thick, rich mixture.
  7. Heat 15 ml of extra virgin olive oil in a large non-stick frying pan over medium heat; the oil should be shimmering but not smoking.
  8. Take spoonfuls of the mixture and pour them into the pan to form small discs, spacing them apart to allow the edges to cook evenly.
  9. Cook for 4 minutes per side: only flip the mini-frittatas when the base is golden brown and releases easily from the bottom of the pan.
  10. Drain the mini-frittatas on kitchen paper to remove excess oil and serve warm, when the fragrance of the vegetables and the internal softness are at their best.
