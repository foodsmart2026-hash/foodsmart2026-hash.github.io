Crispy cavolo nero and pecorino fritters

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Toscana · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Delicious golden fritters where cavolo nero meets the saltiness of pecorino in a light and crispy batter.

Ingredients

  • Cavolo nero (trimmed)250 g
  • Plain flour150 g
  • Cold sparkling water200 ml
  • Grated Pecorino Romano50 g
  • Egg1
  • Baking powder5 g
  • Groundnut oil for frying500 ml
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Peanuts.

Method

  1. Wash the cavolo nero, remove the tough central ribs and cut the leaves into thin strips.
  2. Blanch the strips in salted water for just 2 minutes, drain and squeeze them very well to remove all the water.
  3. Sift the flour and baking powder into a bowl, then add the lightly beaten egg.
  4. Slowly pour in the cold sparkling water, whisking to avoid lumps until you have a smooth batter.
  5. Add the grated pecorino, a pinch of pepper and the well-dried cavolo nero to the batter.
  6. Heat the groundnut oil in a deep pan until it reaches 170-180°C.
  7. Take spoonfuls of the mixture and gently drop them into the hot oil.
  8. Fry a few fritters at a time for about 3 minutes, turning them until they are puffed up and golden.
  9. Drain the fritters on kitchen paper to remove any excess oil.
  10. Serve immediately while hot with a sprinkle of salt on top.
