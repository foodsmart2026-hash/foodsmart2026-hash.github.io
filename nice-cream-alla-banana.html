Banana Nice Cream

2 servings · 5 min · Easy

Instant fruit-only ice cream.

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Banana nice cream is an instant spoon dessert, loved for its traditional ice cream-like texture but made entirely from fruit. The combination of banana, peanut butter, and cocoa creates an intense and enveloping flavor.

Ingredients

  • Ripe bananas (peeled weight)330 g
  • Natural peanut butter16 g
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder7 g

Allergens

Peanuts

Method

  1. Peel the ripe bananas and slice them into even rounds approximately 1 cm thick.
  2. Arrange the slices on a tray lined with parchment paper, ensuring they do not overlap, and freeze at -18°C for at least 4-6 hours.
  3. Remove the fruit from the freezer and let it sit at room temperature for 3-5 minutes to allow ice crystals to soften slightly, protecting the blender motor.
  4. Transfer the frozen bananas into a food processor or high-speed blender.
  5. Process using the pulse function initially until the fruit reaches a crumbly, snow-like consistency.
  6. Add the peanut butter and sifted cocoa powder to prevent any clumping.
  7. Blend on medium speed, scraping down the sides as needed, until the mixture becomes smooth, creamy, and glossy (soft-serve texture).
  8. Serve immediately in chilled bowls to maintain the thermal structure of the cream.
  9. Optional: garnish with a pinch of Maldon salt to enhance the cocoa and peanut flavor profiles.

Tips

  • Use very ripe, spotted bananas to achieve maximum natural sweetness without adding sugars.
  • Blend the frozen fruit in pulses to avoid overheating the mixture and compromising its creaminess.
  • Serve immediately after preparation to enjoy the soft and velvety texture.

Variations

  • Garnish your nice cream with toasted peanut crumbles to create a pleasant contrast of textures.
  • Add a pinch of sea salt over the peanut butter to enhance the aromatic notes of the cocoa.
