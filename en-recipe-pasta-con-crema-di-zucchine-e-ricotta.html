Pasta with courgette cream and ricotta

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A creamy and balanced main course that makes the most of the sweetness of seasonal courgettes and the lightness of fresh ricotta. An affordable and quick solution for a complete family meal, enriched by the crunchy touch of walnuts.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Wholewheat durum wheat pasta (e.g. fusilli or penne)320 g
  • Fresh seasonal courgettes500 g
  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (Italian whey cheese)250 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 g
  • Shallot1 pz
  • Walnut halves20 g
  • Fresh basil leaves10 foglie
  • Wholegrain sea salt5 g
  • Ground black pepper1 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Tree nuts

Method

  1. Fill a large pot with about 3 litres of water, bring to the boil and add salt to prepare the pasta cooking base.
  2. Thoroughly wash the courgettes, trim the ends and cut into small, even cubes (about 1 cm) to ensure quick and uniform cooking in the pan.
  3. In a large non-stick pan, pour 20g of extra virgin olive oil and add the finely chopped shallot; let it soften over low heat for about 3 minutes until translucent.
  4. Add the courgettes to the pan, increase the heat slightly and cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring often, until tender but still a vibrant green.
  5. Submerge the pasta in the boiling water and cook according to the packet instructions, taking care to drain it 'al dente' to maintain an optimal glycaemic index.
  6. Transfer about three-quarters of the cooked courgettes to a jug for a hand blender, adding the ricotta, basil leaves and a ladle of pasta cooking water.
  7. Blend the mixture for about 30-45 seconds until you obtain a smooth and velvety cream, adding another tablespoon of water if the consistency is too thick.
  8. Pour the ricotta and courgette cream into the pan with the remaining whole courgettes, creating a rich sauce with alternating textures.
  9. Drain the pasta and toss it in the pan with the sauce over medium heat for 1 minute, finishing with the remaining 10g of raw oil and the roughly chopped walnuts for a final touch of Omega-3.
