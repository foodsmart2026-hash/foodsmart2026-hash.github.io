Spaghetti with sardines and breadcrumbs

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A healthy reimagining of Sicilian tradition, affordable and rich in precious Omega-3 thanks to the fresh oily fish. The use of wholewheat pasta and toasted breadcrumbs ensures a controlled glycaemic index and an irresistible crunchy texture.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • wholewheat spaghetti320 g
  • fresh sardines (cleaned and butterflied)300 g
  • wholewheat breadcrumbs60 g
  • extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • cherry tomatoes200 g
  • garlic2 spicchi
  • organic lemon (zest)1 pz
  • fresh parsley20 g
  • fresh chilli1 pz

Allergens

Gluten, Fish

Method

  1. Begin by preparing the aromatics: finely chop the fresh parsley, slice the chilli and wash the cherry tomatoes, cutting them into quarters.
  2. Toast the breadcrumbs in a non-stick pan with a teaspoon of oil taken from the total amount, stirring continuously for 3-4 minutes until golden and fragrant.
  3. Bring a large pot of water to the boil and salt moderately (keeping in mind that sardines are naturally salty).
  4. In a large pan, heat the remaining extra virgin olive oil with the crushed garlic cloves and the chilli over low heat for 2 minutes, avoiding burning them.
  5. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook over medium heat for about 5 minutes, mashing them slightly with a fork so they release their juices to create a moist base.
  6. Add the fresh sardines to the tomato sauce and cook for just 2-3 minutes; oily fish cooks quickly and should remain tender without breaking apart too much.
  7. Drop the wholewheat spaghetti into the water and drain 2 minutes before the time indicated on the packet, reserving a ladle of the starch-rich cooking water.
  8. Transfer the pasta to the pan with the sauce and toss over high heat, adding the reserved cooking water to create a creamy emulsion between the oil and starch.
  9. Turn off the heat and complete the dish by adding the grated lemon zest, chopped parsley and half of the toasted breadcrumbs.
  10. Plate immediately, distributing the remaining crunchy breadcrumbs over each portion to preserve their texture during consumption.
