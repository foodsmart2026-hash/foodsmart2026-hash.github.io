Polpettine al sugo

Kid-friendly

4 servings · Prep 0 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

Tender meat patties in a light tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef (lean cut)250 g
  • White bread (crumb)40 g
  • Whole milk50 ml
  • Parmigiano Reggiano DOP (aged 24 months)20 g
  • Tomato puree300 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 g
  • Small carrot20 g
  • Celery10 g
  • Yellow onion10 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Celery

Method

  1. Finely mince the carrot, celery, and onion until they form a very fine texture.
  2. In a bowl, soak the breadcrumbs in milk for about 5 minutes until fully absorbed.
  3. Lightly squeeze the bread and combine it in a bowl with the ground beef and grated Parmigiano Reggiano.
  4. Mix the ingredients by hand until you achieve a smooth and soft consistency.
  5. Form small meatballs about 2 cm in diameter (approximately 15-18g each).
  6. In a non-stick pan, sauté the minced vegetables with olive oil and two tablespoons of water over low heat for 5 minutes.
  7. Add the tomato puree and bring to a gentle simmer.
  8. Carefully place the meatballs into the sauce, cover with a lid, and cook over low heat for 20 minutes.
  9. Turn the meatballs halfway through cooking and add a tablespoon of hot water if the sauce becomes too thick.
  10. Ensure the center is fully cooked and let cool slightly before serving.
