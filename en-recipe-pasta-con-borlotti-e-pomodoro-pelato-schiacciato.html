Pasta with Borlotti Beans and Crushed Peeled Tomatoes

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

The classic Italian 'pasta e fagioli' revisited for babies: the beans have their skins removed for optimal digestibility and the sauce is sweet and velvety.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Short pasta such as tubetti (small tube-shaped pasta)120 g
  • Pre-cooked borlotti beans (organic, salt-free)200 g
  • Peeled plum tomatoes150 g
  • Small carrot1 pcs
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Pass the borlotti beans through a food mill with small holes: this is essential to remove the skins and obtain a safe purée.
  2. Finely chop the carrot and stew it in a pan with a little water until it softens completely.
  3. Add the peeled tomatoes, crushing them well with a fork to eliminate any large pieces.
  4. Add the bean purée to the tomato sauce and let the flavours blend over a low heat for 10 minutes.
  5. Cook the pasta in plenty of unsalted water and drain it when it is well cooked.
  6. Combine the pasta with the legume and tomato cream, stirring well so the sauce fills the tubetti.
  7. If the dish is too dry, loosen it with a tablespoon of cooking water to make it creamy.
  8. Season with raw extra virgin olive oil and, if liked, a pinch of very finely chopped dried oregano.
  9. Serve warm, ensuring the consistency is 'spoonable', perfect for young children.
