Roasted Carrots with Paprika and Oregano

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

Carrots, one of the most affordable ingredients in the supermarket, take centre stage thanks to bold seasoning. Roasting them in the oven concentrates their natural sugars, creating a lovely bittersweet contrast.

Ingredients

  • Carrots800 g
  • Sweet paprika1 cucchiaino
  • Dried oregano1 cucchiaino
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • White wine vinegar10 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the carrots and cut them into chip-style batons, trying to make them all the same size.
  2. Blanch the carrot sticks in salted water for just 3 minutes; this trick ensures a soft interior.
  3. Drain the carrots well and let them cool slightly on a clean tea towel to remove excess moisture.
  4. In a small bowl, prepare an emulsion with oil, vinegar, paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  5. Season the carrots with the emulsion, tossing them so that every baton is well coated.
  6. Arrange them on a baking tray lined with baking parchment, spacing them out slightly.
  7. Bake at 210°C for about 15-18 minutes, turning on the grill function for the final 3 minutes.
  8. The carrots will be ready when they appear slightly shrivelled and with browned tips.
  9. Serve them hot or at room temperature as a healthy snack or a vibrant side dish.
