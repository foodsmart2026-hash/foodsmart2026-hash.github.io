Potato, spinach and ricotta bake

💰 Save Euro · about €1.79 per person

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 45 min · Hard

A complete and balanced main course that combines the creaminess of cow's milk ricotta with the fibre of fresh spinach. An affordable solution, perfect for the family, using simple yet nutrient-rich essential ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes800 g
  • Fresh spinach (or frozen)500 g
  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (a soft Italian whey cheese)250 g
  • Medium egg1 pz
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano (a hard, aged Italian cheese)40 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Wholemeal breadcrumbs30 g
  • Unrefined sea salt5 g
  • Nutmeg1 g

Allergens

Milk, Eggs, Gluten

Method

  1. Wash the potatoes and boil them in their skins in plenty of water for about 25-30 minutes; they are ready when the prongs of a fork easily penetrate the centre.
  2. While the potatoes are cooking, wash the spinach and wilt it in a large pan with a tablespoon of water and a pinch of salt for 5-7 minutes until completely reduced in volume.
  3. Carefully drain the spinach and squeeze it firmly between your hands or in a potato ricer to remove all excess water, preventing the bake from becoming soggy.
  4. Peel the potatoes while still hot and pass them through a potato ricer into a large bowl; leave to cool for a few minutes to avoid cooking the egg prematurely.
  5. Add the well-drained ricotta, the egg, 30g of Parmigiano and the nutmeg to the mashed potatoes, stirring with a wooden spoon until the mixture is smooth.
  6. Roughly chop the squeezed spinach and gently fold it into the potato and ricotta mixture, distributing it evenly to create a marbled effect.
  7. Grease an ovenproof dish with a tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil and dust with half of the breadcrumbs, shaking off any excess to create a non-stick base.
  8. Transfer the mixture to the dish, level it with a spatula and finish the surface with the remaining breadcrumbs, the rest of the Parmigiano and a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil to help it brown.
  9. Bake in a preheated conventional oven at 200°C for about 25-30 minutes, turning on the grill function for the last 5 minutes until the surface is golden and crispy.
  10. Remove from the oven and let the bake rest for at least 10 minutes before slicing; this waiting time allows the fibres to set, ensuring a perfect slice.
