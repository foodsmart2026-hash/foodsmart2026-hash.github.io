Semolina Pudding with Mandarin Juice

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.30 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A creamy dessert with a citrus flavour, perfect for winter when mandarins are in peak season. Semolina creates a thick and comforting texture that little ones love.

Ingredients

  • Whole milk300 ml
  • Wheat semolina40 g
  • Strained mandarin juice100 ml
  • Grated mandarin zest1 pcs
  • Egg yolk1 pcs

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Wash a mandarin well, finely grate the zest and squeeze the juice until you reach the indicated amount.
  2. In a small saucepan, heat the milk together with the mandarin zest without bringing it to a boil.
  3. Rain the semolina into the hot milk, whisking continuously to avoid lumps.
  4. Cook over a very low heat for about 5 minutes, continuing to stir until the mixture thickens considerably.
  5. Remove from the heat and stir in the mandarin juice, mixing well to combine the liquids.
  6. Allow the cream to cool slightly, then add the egg yolk, stirring quickly so it doesn't scramble.
  7. Return to the heat for just one minute, continuing to stir to pasteurise the egg without letting it boil.
  8. Pour the pudding into four small bowls and cover with cling film touching the surface to prevent a skin from forming.
  9. Serve at room temperature to best appreciate the sweetness and creaminess of the dessert.
