Cooked apple with biscuit

1 servings · 15 min · Easy

Soft snack from 6 months onwards.

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly

A delicate and velvety dessert that highlights the natural sweetness of steamed Golden or Fuji apples. The biscuit crumble topping adds a pleasant crunchy note to every spoonful.

Ingredients

  • Apple (Golden or Fuji variety)150 g
  • Granulated baby biscuit8 g

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Milk, Soy, Eggs.

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the apple under running water, peel it, and remove the core.
  2. Cut the apple flesh into uniform 1.5 cm cubes to ensure even cooking.
  3. Place the cubes in a steamer basket and cook for approximately 8-10 minutes.
  4. Check for doneness: the apple should be very tender when pressed with a fork.
  5. Transfer the apple to a blender or use a hand blender to achieve a smooth puree.
  6. If the consistency is too thick, add a teaspoon of the remaining cooking water.
  7. Allow the puree to cool down to a temperature of approximately 37°C (98°F).
  8. Add the granulated biscuit and stir vigorously to prevent lumps from forming.
  9. Check the final temperature before serving the snack to the infant.

Tips

  • Cut the apple cubes uniformly to ensure even steaming during preparation.
  • Use the leftover cooking water to adjust the smoothness of the purée according to your preference.
  • Add the biscuit crumbs only just before serving to preserve their crunchy texture.

Variations

  • Try cooking the apple with a pinch of cinnamon to intensely fragrance the purée.
  • Replace the biscuit crumbs with puffed cereals for a less sweet but equally crunchy alternative.
