Baked breaded cod

Kid-friendly

2 servings · 25 min · Easy

Homemade fish sticks.

Homemade fish sticks, crispy on the outside and tender inside thanks to the lemon-scented breading. A simple oven preparation that enhances the clean flavor of cod without being heavy on the palate.

Ingredients

  • Fresh or thawed cod fillet250 g
  • Salt-free breadcrumbs50 g
  • Medium egg1 piece
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 g
  • Organic lemon zest2 g
  • All-purpose flour15 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish, Eggs

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (392°F) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Thoroughly dry the cod fillets with paper towels and carefully check for any remaining bones by touch.
  3. Cut the fish into even strips (sticks) about 2 cm wide to ensure uniform cooking.
  4. Prepare three bowls: one with flour, one with the beaten egg, and one with breadcrumbs mixed with lemon zest.
  5. Coat each stick first in flour (shaking off excess), then in the egg, and finally in the breadcrumbs, pressing firmly.
  6. Place the sticks on the baking sheet, spacing them out to allow heat circulation.
  7. Drizzle or brush evenly with extra virgin olive oil using a pastry brush or oil mister.
  8. Bake for 12-15 minutes, turning halfway through, until the coating is lightly golden and crisp.
  9. Let cool for 2-3 minutes before serving to stabilize texture and reach a safe internal temperature.

Tips

  • Dry the cod perfectly before flouring to ensure the egg and breadcrumbs adhere better.
  • Do not overlap the sticks on the tray to allow hot air to circulate and make them uniformly crispy.
  • Grate only the yellow part of the lemon peel to avoid the bitter taste of the white pith.

Variations

  • Add finely chopped rosemary to the breadcrumbs for a Mediterranean aromatic note.
  • You can use plaice fillets instead of cod following the same breading procedure.
