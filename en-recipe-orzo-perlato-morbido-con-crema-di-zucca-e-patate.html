Soft Pearl Barley with Pumpkin and Potato Cream

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

When cooked for a long time, barley becomes tender and pleasant. Paired with pumpkin, it creates a sweet and comforting first course, perfect for autumn.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Pearl barley140 g
  • Cleaned pumpkin (Mantovana type - a dense, sweet Italian squash)300 g
  • Potato100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Sage1 leaf

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Cut the pumpkin and potato into cubes of approximately 1 cm.
  2. Rinse the pearl barley under cold water to remove excess starch.
  3. Place the pumpkin, potato, and barley in a large pot with the sage leaf.
  4. Cover with about a litre of water and bring to the boil.
  5. Simmer on a low heat for about 35-40 minutes: the barley should be very swollen and the vegetables broken down.
  6. Remove the sage leaf and mash any remaining chunks of pumpkin and potato directly in the pot using a fork.
  7. Stir vigorously to achieve a sort of creamy risotto consistency.
  8. Add the raw olive oil and stir one last time.
  9. Serve warm, checking that the consistency is not too dry (add a splash of water if necessary).
