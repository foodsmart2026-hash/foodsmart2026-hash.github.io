Scrippelle 'mbusse

Abruzzo · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Thin rolled crepes with pecorino cheese, served in a hot hen broth.

Ingredients

  • Eggs200 g
  • Plain flour80 g
  • Water150 ml
  • Parsley5 g
  • Grated pecorino80 g
  • Hen broth1200 ml
  • Vegetable oil10 ml
  • Fine salt2 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs with the flour, water, chopped parsley and salt until you obtain a smooth batter.
  2. Leave the batter to rest for 15 minutes.
  3. Lightly grease a 20 cm frying pan and cook a thin crepe for about 1 minute per side; repeat until the batter is finished.
  4. Sprinkle each crepe with pecorino and roll it up.
  5. Arrange 3-4 rolls per plate and pour over the boiling broth.
  6. Leave to rest for 1 minute and serve.
