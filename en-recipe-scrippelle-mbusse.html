Scrippelle 'mbusse

Abruzzo · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Thin rolled crepes with pecorino cheese, served in a hot hen broth.

Ingredients

  • Eggs200 g
  • Plain flour80 g
  • Water150 ml
  • Parsley5 g
  • Grated pecorino80 g
  • Hen broth1200 ml
  • Vegetable oil10 ml
  • Fine salt2 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. In a large bowl, crack 200 g of eggs and whisk them lightly, then gradually stir in 80 g of sifted 00 flour to prevent lumps. Mix vigorously until the mixture is smooth and velvety; this step is essential to ensure the lightness of your scrippelle.
  2. Pour in 150 ml of water gradually along with 2 g of fine salt, continuing to stir to achieve a very fluid, almost liquid consistency. Finally, add 5 g of finely chopped fresh parsley, which will provide an herbal aroma and a touch of color typical of Abruzzo tradition.
  3. Let the batter rest at room temperature for about 15 minutes, covering it with a clean cloth; this time allows the gluten to relax and air bubbles to disappear. Meanwhile, heat 1200 ml of chicken broth in a pot over medium heat, bringing it almost to a boil.
  4. Lightly grease a 20 cm diameter non-stick pan with a thin layer of seed oil (using about 10 ml total for all cooking) and heat it over medium-low heat. When the pan is hot but not smoking, pour in a small ladle of batter and quickly rotate the handle to distribute it in a very thin, almost transparent layer.
  5. Cook the scrippella for about 1 minute until the edges begin to pull away and the surface looks dry and matte. Flip it gently using a spatula or your fingers and cook the other side for about 30 seconds until you see light golden speckles.
  6. As they are ready, stack the crepes on a flat plate to keep them moist and flexible thanks to the residual heat. Repeat until all the batter is used, aiming to get about 12-16 scrippelle in total to serve 3-4 pieces per guest.
  7. Take each still-warm scrippella and sprinkle the entire surface with a generous handful of grated pecorino cheese (use 80 g total for all portions). Roll the crepe tightly onto itself, forming a uniform cylinder that securely encloses the flavorful cheese inside.
  8. Arrange 3 or 4 rolls in each soup bowl, placing them neatly side by side. Pour the boiling chicken broth over the scrippelle, making sure they are almost completely submerged; this step transforms the crepes into 'mbusse, which literally means wet.
  9. Let the dish rest for 1 minute before serving, allowing the heat of the broth to melt the pecorino inside and the scrippelle to absorb the flavor. You will feel an enveloping aroma being released, recalling Sunday lunches and warm home atmospheres.
