'Risottata' Ditalini with White Beans and Rosemary

💰 Save Euro · about €0.54 per person

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A 'pasta e fagioli' in a 'risottata' version where the pasta cooks directly with the pulses for a dense and comforting consistency. A complete and very filling vegan dish.

Ingredients

  • Smooth ditalini (small, short tube-shaped pasta)320 g
  • Canned Cannellini beans400 g
  • Fresh rosemary1 rametto
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Tomato passata50 ml
  • Hot water or stock800 ml

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Drain the beans from their liquid and rinse them well under running water.
  2. In a saucepan, sauté the unpeeled garlic clove and the rosemary sprig with extra virgin olive oil.
  3. Take a third of the beans and blend them with a little water to obtain a cream that will help bind the dish.
  4. Add the whole beans and the tomato passata to the saucepan, letting them flavour for 3 minutes.
  5. Pour the ditalini directly into the pot and cover just to the level of the pasta with boiling salted water or stock.
  6. Cook the pasta stirring often like a risotto, adding liquid as it is absorbed.
  7. Halfway through cooking, add the previously prepared bean cream to increase the density of the base.
  8. Once cooked, remove the garlic and rosemary and finish with a drizzle of raw oil and plenty of black pepper.
