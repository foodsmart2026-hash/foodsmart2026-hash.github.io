Stewed Castelluccio Lentils

Umbria · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

Small Umbrian lentils stewed with soffritto and tomato; vegan.

Ingredients

  • Castelluccio lentils300 g
  • Carrot80 g
  • Celery60 g
  • Onion80 g
  • Tomato purée20 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Bay leaf1 g
  • Water900 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils (no soaking required).
  2. Finely chop the carrot, celery and onion and sauté them in the oil for 6 minutes.
  3. Add the lentils, tomato purée and bay leaf and stir.
  4. Pour in the hot water and simmer over a low heat for 35-40 minutes.
  5. Season with salt at the end of cooking and remove the bay leaf.
  6. Serve with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.
