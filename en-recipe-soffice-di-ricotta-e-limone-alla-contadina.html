Farmhouse Ricotta and Lemon Sponge

💰 Save Euro · about €0.98 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A moist, fragrant cake that melts in your mouth, typical of Italian rural snacks. Ricotta replaces most of the fats, making the cake light and high in protein.

Ingredients

  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (a soft Italian whey cheese)250 g
  • Caster sugar120 g
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Plain flour (extra-fine Italian '00' flour)180 g
  • Baking powder16 g
  • Organic lemon (zest)1 pcs
  • Semi-skimmed milk30 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. In a large bowl, work the ricotta with the sugar using a hand whisk until you obtain a smooth cream free of lumps.
  2. Add the eggs one at a time, incorporating them well into the ricotta mixture before moving on to the next to ensure stability.
  3. Grate the lemon zest directly into the batter to release the essential oils that will provide the characteristic aroma.
  4. Sift the flour together with the baking powder and add them gradually, alternating with the milk to keep the batter soft but not liquid.
  5. Mix with upward movements so as not to deflate the eggs until the mixture is homogeneous and glossy.
  6. Line a 20 cm cake tin with wet and squeezed parchment paper, then pour in the batter, levelling the surface with a spatula.
  7. Bake in a preheated static oven at 180°C for about 35-40 minutes: the cake should be golden on the surface.
  8. Check the cooking with a skewer: it must come out dry, but the cake should remain slightly springy to the touch in the centre.
  9. Leave to cool completely in the tin before turning out, as the ricotta makes the cake very delicate when hot.
