Warm Pearl Barley, Tuna and Courgette Salad

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

Pearl barley is an affordable grain with a low glycaemic index. Combined with canned tuna and courgettes, it creates a balanced one-dish meal, perfect for taking to work.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Pearl barley280 g
  • Tuna in olive oil (drained weight)160 g
  • Medium courgettes3 pcs
  • Sun-dried tomatoes in oil30 g
  • Lemon (juice)0.5 pcs
  • Fresh or dried mintq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Fish

Method

  1. Rinse the pearl barley under running water and cook it in plenty of salted water for about 25-30 minutes, checking that it remains firm to the bite.
  2. While the barley is cooking, cut the courgettes into small cubes (mirepoix) to ensure quick and even cooking.
  3. In a non-stick frying pan with a tablespoon of oil, sauté the courgettes over a high heat for 5-6 minutes: they should be golden on the outside but still crunchy inside.
  4. Finely chop the sun-dried tomatoes, which will add a savoury umami note to the dish without the need for too much extra salt.
  5. Drain the barley and rinse it quickly under lukewarm water to stop the cooking process, then transfer it to a large bowl.
  6. Add the flaked tuna, sautéed courgettes, sun-dried tomatoes, and mint leaves to the barley for a touch of freshness.
  7. Season with lemon juice and a drizzle of raw olive oil, mixing well so that all the flavours are distributed among the grains.
  8. Serve the salad warm or at room temperature; letting it rest for 10 minutes helps the barley better absorb the aromas of the dressing.
