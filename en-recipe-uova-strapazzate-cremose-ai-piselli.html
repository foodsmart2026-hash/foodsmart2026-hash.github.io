Creamy Scrambled Eggs with Peas

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A time-saving and budget-friendly recipe. Peas are blended into a cream to avoid choking hazards (round, hard legumes) and to colour the egg in a fun way for the child.

Ingredients

  • Medium eggs3 pcs
  • Fine frozen peas150 g
  • Cream cheese30 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil5 g
  • Waterq.b.

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Boil the peas in boiling water for 10 minutes until they are very soft.
  2. Drain the peas and blend them with a splash of water until you get a fine purée, then pass it through a sieve to remove the skins.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the eggs together with the cream cheese until the mixture is smooth.
  4. Add the pea cream to the eggs and mix well: you will get a bright green mixture.
  5. Heat a non-stick frying pan with a thin layer of oil over a very low heat.
  6. Pour in the mixture and stir continuously with a wooden spoon or a silicone spatula.
  7. Continue stirring for 3-4 minutes: the egg should set into small, soft flakes and never become dry or brown.
  8. Turn off the heat when the egg is still glossy and has an almost creamy consistency.
  9. Leave to rest for a minute before serving so that the residual heat finishes the cooking gently.
