Fusilli with White Turkey Ragù and Black Olives

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A light and budget-friendly alternative to traditional meat ragù. Minced turkey is affordable and pairs perfectly with the aroma of the olives.

Ingredients

  • Fusilli (corkscrew-shaped pasta)320 g
  • Minced turkey300 g
  • Pitted black olives50 g
  • Dry white wine50 ml
  • Onion1/2 pcs
  • Rosemary1 rametto
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Salt and pepperq.b.

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion and sauté it in a large pan with the oil and chopped rosemary.
  2. Add the minced turkey and break it up well with a fork while browning it over a high heat.
  3. Once the meat has changed colour and is well browned, deglaze with the white wine and let the alcohol evaporate.
  4. Slice the black olives into rings and add them to the white ragù; season with salt and pepper.
  5. Lower the heat and cook for another 10 minutes, adding a tablespoon of water if necessary.
  6. Boil the fusilli in salted water, drain them while setting aside a little of the cooking water.
  7. Toss the fusilli in the pan with the sauce, mixing well to distribute the meat into the pasta spirals.
  8. Remove any woody parts of the rosemary and serve with a drizzle of raw olive oil.
