Sweet Potato and Black Bean Chilli

💰 Save Euro · about €1.29 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A revisited Mexican one-pot dish, naturally meat-free and very budget-friendly. The sweetness of the potato pairs perfectly with the savouriness of the black beans.

Ingredients

  • Sweet potatoes (orange flesh)600 g
  • Pre-cooked black beans480 g
  • Tomato passata (smooth sieved tomatoes)400 g
  • Canned sweetcorn150 g
  • Red onion1 pcs
  • Tomato purée30 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Coriander or parsleyq.b.

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the sweet potatoes and cut them into regular cubes of about 2 cm so they cook in the same amount of time.
  2. In a large pot, sauté the chopped red onion in oil until it becomes translucent, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato purée and sweet potatoes, allowing them to gain flavour for 3 minutes while stirring often to prevent sticking.
  4. Pour in the tomato passata and a glass of water, cover and cook over medium heat for 20 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
  5. Add the rinsed black beans and sweetcorn, continuing to cook for another 5-10 minutes without a lid to let the sauce reduce.
  6. Check the consistency: the chilli is ready when the spoon meets slight resistance and the sauce has become a thick cream.
  7. Turn off the heat and finish with chopped fresh coriander or parsley to balance the sweetness of the potatoes.
