Buckwheat Fusilli with Broccoli Cream and Lemon Zest

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A rustic and flavourful first course that uses the natural savouriness of nutritional yeast and the freshness of lemon to enhance the buckwheat. This recipe is naturally gluten-free and free from main allergens, ensuring great palatability in under 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • Buckwheat pasta (fusilli shape)320 g
  • Fresh broccoli (florets)600 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Nutritional yeast flakes20 g
  • Fresh garlic2 cloves
  • Untreated lemon (zest and juice)1 pcs
  • Unrefined sea salt10 g
  • Ground black pepper2 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Bring 4 litres of water to the boil in a large pot and add the salt; a vigorous boil is essential for cooking buckwheat pasta to prevent it from releasing too much starch and becoming sticky.
  2. Wash the broccoli florets and cut them into small pieces of about 1-2 cm; cutting the vegetables into uniform sizes ensures quick cooking and a balanced texture between the creamy and whole parts.
  3. Drop the broccoli into the boiling water and cook for about 4-5 minutes; the broccoli is ready when tender when pierced with a fork but still bright green.
  4. Remove about half of the cooked broccoli florets with a slotted spoon and transfer them to a stick blender beaker, keeping the other half in the pot with the water.
  5. Add the nutritional yeast, the juice of half a lemon and a ladle of cooking water to the beaker, then blend until a smooth cream is formed; the yeast provides an umami flavour similar to aged cheese.
  6. Add the buckwheat pasta to the same water where the remaining broccoli is cooking; follow the cooking times on the packet (usually 8-10 minutes) to achieve an 'al dente' texture.
  7. In a wide pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil with the two crushed garlic cloves for 2 minutes over a low heat, until the garlic becomes golden without burning, releasing its essential oils.
  8. Drain the pasta and the remaining broccoli directly into the pan, add the blended cream and sauté everything for 60 seconds over a high heat to allow the sauce to coat the rough surface of the pasta.
  9. Turn off the heat and finish the dish by finely grating the lemon zest over it and adding the black pepper; the oils from the zest provide an aromatic note that balances the earthy taste of the buckwheat.
