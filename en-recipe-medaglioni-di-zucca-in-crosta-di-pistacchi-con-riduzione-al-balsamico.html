Pistachio-crusted squash medallions with a balsamic reduction

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Sicilia · 4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

Slices of firm squash, breaded and pan-fried until golden, served with an elegant sweet and sour touch.

Ingredients

  • Delica squash (cut into 1.5cm thick round slices)8 slices
  • Chopped pistachios100g
  • Breadcrumbs50g
  • Medium eggs2
  • Plain flouras required
  • Balsamic vinegar of Modena100ml
  • Acacia honey1 tsp
  • Groundnut oilfor frying
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Tree nuts, Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Clean the squash, keeping the skin on (if edible as with Delica), and cut into 8 circular slices about 1.5cm thick.
  2. In a small saucepan, combine the balsamic vinegar and honey; simmer over a low heat until reduced and syrupy.
  3. Prepare three dishes: one with the flour, one with the eggs beaten with salt and pepper, and one with the mixture of breadcrumbs and chopped pistachios.
  4. Dredge each squash slice first in the flour, shaking off any excess, then dip into the egg, and finally coat in the pistachio mix, pressing down well.
  5. Heat the groundnut oil in a large frying pan until it reaches 170°C.
  6. Fry the squash medallions for about 4-5 minutes per side over a medium-low heat to ensure the inside cooks through without burning the crust.
  7. Drain the medallions on kitchen paper and season with salt.
  8. Assemble the dish by plating two medallions per serving.
  9. Drizzle with the balsamic reduction and garnish with a few whole pistachios if you have any left.
