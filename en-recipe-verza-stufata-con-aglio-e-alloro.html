Braised Savoy Cabbage with Garlic and Bay Leaves

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

Savoy cabbage is one of the cheapest winter vegetables and, if braised slowly, loses its bitter note and becomes very sweet. Bay leaves provide an ancient and comforting scent.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Savoy cabbage (Verza)800 g
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Dried bay leaves2 leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Red wine vinegar15 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Remove the tougher outer leaves of the cabbage and cut the rest into thin strips.
  2. Wash the cabbage thoroughly under running water and drain well, leaving it slightly damp.
  3. In a large pot, heat the oil with the whole, crushed garlic cloves and the bay leaves.
  4. Add the cabbage a little at a time, letting it wilt as you add it to make room in the pot.
  5. Adjust with salt and pepper and deglaze with the red wine vinegar to balance the natural sweetness of the vegetable.
  6. Cover the pot and cook over a very low heat for about 25-30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. If the cabbage becomes too dry, add a couple of tablespoons of water.
  8. The cabbage is ready when it is completely tender and has turned a dark olive green colour.
  9. Remove the garlic and bay leaves before serving the side dish hot, ideal with pork dishes or legumes.
