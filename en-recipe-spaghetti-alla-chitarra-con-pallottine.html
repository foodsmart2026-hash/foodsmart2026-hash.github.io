Spaghetti alla chitarra with tiny meatballs

Abruzzo · 4 servings · Prep 40 min · Cooking 50 min · Medium

Egg pasta from Teramo with tomato sauce and tiny meatballs.

Ingredients

  • Egg spaghetti alla chitarra360 g
  • Minced veal250 g
  • Egg50 g
  • Grated Abruzzese Pecorino40 g
  • Tomato passata700 ml
  • Onion60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Celery, Soy.

Method

  1. Mix the meat with the egg, half of the pecorino and a pinch of salt.
  2. Form tiny meatballs the size of a hazelnut (about 3 g each).
  3. In a saucepan, sauté the chopped onion in the oil for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the passata, season with salt and cook over a low heat for 20 minutes.
  5. Add the meatballs to the sauce and cook for another 20 minutes without stirring too much.
  6. Boil the pasta in salted water for 3-4 minutes, drain and toss with the sauce.
  7. Serve with the remaining pecorino.
