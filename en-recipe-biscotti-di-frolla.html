Shortcrust Pastry Cookies

3 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • all-purpose flour300 g
  • cold butter150 g
  • powdered sugar120 g
  • medium egg1
  • egg yolk1
  • salt1 pinch
  • Grated lemon zestto taste
  • baking powder (optional)5 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Pour the flour into a bowl and add the cold cubed butter.
  2. Work quickly with your fingers until you obtain a sandy and uniform mixture.
  3. Stir in the powdered sugar, salt, and lemon zest.
  4. Add the egg and the yolk, working briefly until you get a compact dough.
  5. Form a ball, wrap it in plastic wrap and let it rest in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
  6. Roll out the pastry to a thickness of about 5 mm and cut out the cookies with the desired cookie cutters.
  7. Place them on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, leaving a few centimeters between each cookie.
  8. Bake in a preheated static oven at 175 °C for about 12–15 minutes, until the edges just begin to brown. Let them cool before moving them.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes, and alternatives:

- Don't wait for them to become completely golden brown: they firm up while cooling.

- If the dough is too soft, put it back in the refrigerator before cutting.

- For cocoa cookies, replace 25 g of flour with unsweetened cocoa powder.

- You can add chocolate chips, cinnamon, or hazelnut grains.

- For crispier cookies, roll out the pastry slightly thinner.
