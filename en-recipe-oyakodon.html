Oyakodon

Japan · 2 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A Japanese rice bowl with chicken and egg cooked in a sweet and savoury dashi broth.

Ingredients

  • Cooked Japanese rice400 g
  • Boneless chicken thighs250 g
  • Eggs150 g
  • Onion100 g
  • Dashi stock150 ml
  • Soy sauce30 ml
  • Mirin30 ml
  • Sugar5 g
  • Spring onion10 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish, Soy, Eggs

Method

  1. Start by preparing the base ingredients: cut the 250g of deboned chicken thighs into small bite-sized pieces of about 2-3 cm and thinly slice the 100g of onion. Cutting the chicken into uniform pieces ensures even cooking, while the finely sliced onion will soften until it almost turns into a cream in the broth.
  2. Take a small flat-bottomed skillet and pour in the 150ml of dashi broth, 30ml of soy sauce, 30ml of mirin, and 5g of sugar. Bring everything to a light boil over medium heat, stirring gently until the sugar has completely dissolved and the aroma of the dashi begins to release.
  3. Distribute the 100g of sliced onion evenly into the boiling liquid and cook for about 2-3 minutes. The onion should become slightly translucent and begin to absorb the amber color of the soy sauce, losing its pungent note in favor of an aromatic sweetness.
  4. Now add the 250g of chicken pieces, spreading them over the onion layer without overlapping too much. Cover the pan with a lid and continue cooking for 4-5 minutes over medium-low heat; you will know the chicken is ready when it appears white on the outside and tender to the touch, keeping its juices inside.
  5. While the chicken is cooking, crack the 150g of eggs into a small bowl and beat them lightly with chopsticks or a fork, being careful not to over-mix the yolk and white. This technique allows you to achieve that typical marbled appearance and a fluffier, more varied texture in the final dish.
  6. Pour the beaten eggs in a thin stream over the chicken and onions, starting from the center and moving towards the edges in a circular motion. Immediately cover with the lid again and cook for about 60-90 seconds over low heat: the egg should just set, remaining glossy and very creamy on the surface.
  7. Divide the 400g of warm, pre-cooked Japanese rice into two large serving bowls (donburi). Gently slide half of the chicken and egg mixture onto each bowl, tilting the pan so that the delicious cooking liquid flows down to soak the rice grains below.
  8. Complete your Oyakodon by sprinkling the 10g of thinly sliced green onion over the surface for a touch of freshness. Serve the steaming dish immediately; the creaminess of the egg and the saltiness of the dashi broth will create a perfect harmony with the neutral texture of the rice.
