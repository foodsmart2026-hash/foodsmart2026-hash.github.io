Oyakodon

Japan · 2 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A Japanese rice bowl with chicken and egg cooked in a sweet and savoury dashi broth.

Ingredients

  • Cooked Japanese rice400 g
  • Boneless chicken thighs250 g
  • Eggs150 g
  • Onion100 g
  • Dashi stock150 ml
  • Soy sauce30 ml
  • Mirin30 ml
  • Sugar5 g
  • Spring onion10 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish, Soy, Eggs

Method

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and slice the onion.
  2. In a small frying pan, bring the dashi, soy sauce, mirin and sugar to the boil.
  3. Add the onion and chicken and cook for 6-7 minutes.
  4. Drizzle in the lightly beaten eggs, cover and cook for 1 minute: they should remain soft.
  5. Slide the mixture onto the hot rice in the bowls.
  6. Finish with the sliced spring onion.
