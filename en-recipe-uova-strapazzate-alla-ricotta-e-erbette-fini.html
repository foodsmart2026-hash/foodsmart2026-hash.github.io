Scrambled Eggs with Ricotta and Fine Herbs

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A creamy version of classic eggs, where ricotta replaces salt and provides a velvety texture. Fresh herbs add micronutrients and fragrance.

Ingredients

  • Fresh eggs2 pc
  • Cow's milk ricotta (a soft, mild Italian whey cheese)100 g
  • Chives or parsley1 tsp
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 ml

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them lightly with a fork just to break the yolks.
  2. Add the ricotta and mix roughly; it doesn't need to be perfectly smooth, as the small flakes of ricotta are pleasant.
  3. Wash and very finely chop the chives or parsley and add them to the mixture.
  4. Heat a small non-stick pan with the oil over a very low heat.
  5. Pour the egg and ricotta mixture into the pan and let it sit for 30 seconds without touching it.
  6. Start moving the mixture with a wooden spoon, bringing it from the edges towards the centre with slow movements.
  7. Cook for no more than 3 minutes; the eggs should remain 'moist' and creamy, not dry or browned.
  8. Remove immediately from the hot pan to stop the cooking and serve at room temperature.
