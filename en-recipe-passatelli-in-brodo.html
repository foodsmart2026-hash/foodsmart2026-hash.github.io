Passatelli in broth

Emilia-Romagna · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

Small cylinders of breadcrumbs, eggs and Parmesan cooked in broth, typical of the Romagna region.

Ingredients

  • Breadcrumbs120 g
  • Grated Parmesan Reggiano120 g
  • Eggs150 g
  • Lemon zest2 g
  • Nutmeg0.5 g
  • Meat broth1500 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Mix the breadcrumbs, Parmesan, lemon zest and nutmeg.
  2. Add the eggs and knead until you have a firm dough; leave it to rest in the fridge for 20 minutes.
  3. Bring the broth to the boil.
  4. Pass the dough through a dedicated passatelli iron or a potato ricer with large holes directly over the broth.
  5. Cook for 2 minutes, until the passatelli rise to the surface, and serve.
