Tender Chopped Green Beans with Butter and Parmesan

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.54 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

Green beans cooked until very tender and cut into small segments to be handled safely. Butter and Parmesan offer a comforting, rounded flavour.

Ingredients

  • Fresh or frozen green beans400 g
  • High-quality butter10 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano (hard aged Italian cheese)20 g

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Trim the green beans by removing the ends (if fresh) and wash them thoroughly.
  2. Bring a pot of unsalted water to the boil and add the green beans.
  3. Cook the beans for 15-20 minutes, longer than usual to ensure maximum tenderness.
  4. Drain the beans and let them cool for a minute on a chopping board.
  5. With a sharp knife, cut the beans into small segments about 1 centimetre long.
  6. In a small pan or the same cooking pot (empty), melt the butter over very low heat without letting it sizzle.
  7. Add the chopped beans and stir gently for a minute to coat them.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the grated Parmigiano, and stir until a tasty coating forms.
  9. Serve the side dish lukewarm, checking that the pieces are easy to pick up with fingers or a small fork.
