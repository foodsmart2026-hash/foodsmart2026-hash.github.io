Penne with White Cannellini and Fennel Sauce

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

An unusual and refined pasta dish that plays on the sweetness of stewed fennel and the creaminess of cannellini beans. An original way to enjoy pulses even in dry pasta dishes.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Penne pasta320 g
  • Canned cannellini beans240 g
  • Large fennel bulb1 pcs
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Black peppercornsq.b.

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Clean the fennel by removing the green fronds (keep them aside) and cut it into very thin slices or small cubes.
  2. In a pan, sauté the garlic clove with oil, then add the fennel and let it stew with a little water for 10 minutes.
  3. Drain and rinse the cannellini beans, then add them to the fennel and continue cooking for another 5 minutes until the fennel is tender.
  4. Take a portion of the mixture and blend it with a bit of the pasta cooking water to create a creamy binding base.
  5. Cook the penne in plenty of salted water and drain when al dente, always reserving a ladle of the cooking water.
  6. Pour the pasta into the pan with the cannellini and fennel, add the blended cream, and stir vigorously.
  7. Add a generous grind of fresh black pepper, which contrasts pleasantly with the sweetness of the vegetables.
  8. Garnish with the finely chopped fennel fronds to provide freshness and an anise-like aroma before serving.
