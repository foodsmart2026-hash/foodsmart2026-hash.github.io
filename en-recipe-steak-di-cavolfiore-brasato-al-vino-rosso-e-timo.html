Braised Cauliflower Steaks with Red Wine and Thyme

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A thick and flavourful vegetable 'steak', slowly braised in a red wine base that gives it an intense colour and a bold taste.

Ingredients

  • Large, firm cauliflower1 piece
  • Still red wine (such as Merlot or Sangiovese)300ml
  • Vegetable stock200ml
  • Tomato purée1 tablespoon
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Fresh thyme4 sprigs
  • Rice flour2 tablespoons
  • Extra virgin olive oil4 tablespoons
  • Salt and black pepperto taste

Allergens

Celery, Sulphites

Method

  1. Remove the outer leaves from the cauliflower but leave the central core intact.
  2. Cut the cauliflower vertically into 4 slices about 2.5 - 3 cm thick, keeping the core to hold the florets together.
  3. Lightly dust the 'steaks' with rice flour, shaking off any excess.
  4. In a large non-stick frying pan, heat the oil with the crushed garlic cloves and the thyme.
  5. Place the cauliflower slices in the pan and sear for 4 minutes per side over a medium-high heat until well browned.
  6. Deglaze with the red wine and allow the alcohol to evaporate for a couple of minutes.
  7. Add the vegetable stock with the tomato purée dissolved in it.
  8. Cover with a lid and leave to braise over a low heat for about 20 minutes, turning the slices very gently halfway through cooking.
  9. Once the cauliflower is tender, remove the lid and let the cooking liquid reduce until it becomes a glossy sauce.
  10. Serve the steaks hot, drizzling them generously with the red wine sauce.
