Complete Legume and Grain Soup

💰 Save Euro · about €1.80 per person

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 55 min · Hard

A thick and comforting preparation that perfectly balances plant proteins and complex carbohydrates. The addition of anchovies, a typical affordable oily fish, provides a unique depth of flavour and precious essential fats without increasing the cost of the dish.

Ingredients

  • Pearl spelt200 g
  • Small dried lentils (no soaking required)200 g
  • Anchovy fillets in oil20 g
  • Carrots150 g
  • Celery (stalks)100 g
  • Golden onion100 g
  • Cavolo nero (Tuscan kale, or chard in season)250 g
  • Tomato purée (concentrate)30 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil50 ml
  • Hot water or vegetable stock1600 ml
  • Whole sea salt5 g
  • Ground black pepper2 g

Allergens

Gluten, Fish, Celery

Method

  1. Begin by preparing the soffritto: finely chop the carrot, celery and onion with a knife or a mixer to obtain small, uniform pieces that will melt during cooking.
  2. Rinse the spelt and lentils under cold running water using a fine-mesh sieve until the water runs clear, removing any dust or residue.
  3. In a large pot (preferably terracotta or thick-bottomed steel), heat 30 ml of extra virgin olive oil and add the anchovy fillets, mashing them with a wooden spoon until they are completely dissolved.
  4. Add the chopped vegetables and soften over a medium-low heat for 8 minutes; the vegetables should become tender and the onion translucent without burning.
  5. Add the tomato purée and toast it for 2 minutes, stirring well: this step removes the acidity of the tomato and intensifies the colour of the soup.
  6. Pour the spelt and lentils into the pot and let them season for 3 minutes, stirring constantly, allowing the grains to absorb the flavours of the base.
  7. Add the hot water (or stock) and bring to the boil. Then lower the heat and let it cook with the lid slightly ajar for about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. In the meantime, wash the cavolo nero, remove the tough central rib and cut the leaves into strips of about 1 cm.
  9. After 30 minutes, add the cavolo nero to the soup and continue cooking for another 10-15 minutes: the soup is ready when the lentils are tender but intact and the spelt has a pleasantly chewy texture.
  10. Turn off the heat, adjust the salt and pepper, and let the soup rest covered for 5 minutes; finally, serve each portion with a generous tablespoon of raw extra virgin olive oil to enhance the aromas.
