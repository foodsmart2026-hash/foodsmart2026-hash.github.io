Creamy Mixed Berry Porridge

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A warm and comforting breakfast made with budget-friendly oats and frozen mixed berries. It is a quick, fiber-rich solution for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • Porridge oats160 g
  • Semi-skimmed milk600 ml
  • Water200 ml
  • Frozen forest fruits150 g
  • Honey40 g
  • Chia seeds (optional)10 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. In a saucepan, combine the oats, milk, and water, bringing the mixture to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
  2. Lower the heat and cook for about 5-8 minutes, stirring continuously to prevent the oats from sticking to the bottom.
  3. The porridge is ready when it has absorbed most of the liquid and has a consistency similar to a thick cream.
  4. In another small saucepan, heat the frozen berries with a tablespoon of water for 3 minutes until they release their juices.
  5. Turn off the heat for the porridge and stir in the honey, mixing vigorously to distribute the sweetness.
  6. Add the chia seeds if you want an even thicker consistency and an extra boost of omega-3.
  7. Divide the porridge into 4 individual bowls while it is still nice and hot.
  8. Pour the hot berry sauce over each bowl, creating an inviting colour contrast.
  9. Serve immediately, perhaps adding a pinch of cinnamon if you enjoy a spicy flavour.
