Orange jelly with agar agar

Kid-friendly

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A natural orange juice jelly thickened with agar agar, containing no artificial colours. Free from major allergens.

Ingredients

  • Freshly squeezed orange juice450 ml
  • Agar agar powder2 g
  • Sugar10 g
  • Water50 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Dissolve the agar agar in the cold water.
  2. Heat the orange juice with the sugar in a small saucepan.
  3. Add the agar agar and boil for 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
  4. Pour into 4 small moulds.
  5. Leave to cool, then refrigerate for 1 hour.
  6. Turn out and serve in cubes.
