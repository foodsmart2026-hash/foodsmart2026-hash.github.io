Spanakopita

Greece · 6 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A crispy Greek filo pastry pie with spinach, feta and herbs.

Ingredients

  • Filo pastry250 g
  • Fresh spinach800 g
  • Feta cheese250 g
  • Eggs100 g
  • Spring onions100 g
  • Fresh dill10 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil80 ml
  • Black pepper1 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Sesame.

Method

  1. Start by washing the 800g of fresh spinach; wilt them in a large pan over medium heat for about 5-7 minutes until they reduce in volume. Once ready, let them cool and squeeze them very firmly to remove all residual water, then chop them coarsely. This step is crucial to prevent the phyllo pastry from becoming soggy during baking.
  2. Thinly slice the 100g of spring onions and sauté them in a pan with 20ml of extra virgin olive oil over low heat for about 3 minutes. Wait until they become translucent and release their sweet aroma without burning them. Their softness will provide a perfect aromatic base for your filling.
  3. In a large bowl, combine the well-dried spinach, the sautéed spring onions, the 250g of feta crumbled coarsely by hand, and the 100g of lightly beaten eggs. Add the 10g of chopped fresh dill and 1g of black pepper, mixing well to blend the flavors. You will get a dense and fragrant mixture, ready to be enclosed in the pastry.
  4. Take a 25x30 cm baking tin and start lining it with half of the 250g of phyllo pastry, laying one sheet at a time. Gently brush each single sheet with a portion of the remaining 60ml of extra virgin olive oil, making sure to cover the edges well. This technique creates air pockets that will make the pie incredibly crispy and light.
  5. Pour the spinach and feta filling into the pan, leveling it with the back of a spoon to distribute it evenly. Try not to press too hard to maintain the fluffiness of the internal mixture. The green layer must be uniform to ensure consistent cooking throughout.
  6. Cover the filling with the remaining sheets of phyllo pastry, proceeding exactly as before: overlap each sheet while brushing it with extra virgin olive oil. Make sure the top layer is well oiled to achieve a shiny and inviting golden brown color. Any excess edges can be tucked inwards to seal the heart of the pie well.
  7. With a sharp knife, score the surface of the phyllo pastry into squares or diamonds, reaching down to touch the filling but without going too deep. This operation allows steam to escape during baking and makes it easier to cut the portions after serving.
  8. Bake the Spanakopita in a static oven preheated to 180 °C for about 45 minutes. The pie will be ready when the surface appears a beautiful dark blond color and feels very crunchy to the touch. Let it rest for 10 minutes outside the oven before serving, so the flavors can settle and the slice will be perfect.
