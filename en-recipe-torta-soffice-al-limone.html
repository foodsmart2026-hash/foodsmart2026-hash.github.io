Soft lemon cake

8 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

A tall and fragrant cake made with lemon juice and zest.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour250 g
  • Caster sugar180 g
  • Medium eggs165 g (3 eggs)
  • Butter100 g
  • Whole milk100 ml
  • Lemon juice60 ml
  • Grated lemon zest6 g
  • Baking powder16 g
  • Icing sugar10 g
  • Fine salt1 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 170°C (conventional) and grease and flour a 24 cm cake tin.
  2. Melt the butter and allow it to cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the sugar and lemon zest for 8-10 minutes, until the mixture is light and fluffy.
  4. Gradually pour in the melted butter and the milk, continuing to mix at a low speed.
  5. Stir in the lemon juice.
  6. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt together.
  7. Fold in the dry ingredients a little at a time using a spatula, with an upwards motion from the bottom.
  8. Pour the batter into the tin and level it.
  9. Bake for 38-40 minutes: a skewer inserted into the centre should come out clean.
  10. Allow to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out and dust with icing sugar.
