Soft lemon cake

8 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

A tall and fragrant cake made with lemon juice and zest.

A soft and fragrant cake, ideal for breakfast or tea time thanks to its natural aroma. The light and airy texture pairs perfectly with the citric freshness of lemon juice and zest.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour250 g
  • Caster sugar180 g
  • Medium eggs165 g (3 eggs)
  • Butter100 g
  • Whole milk100 ml
  • Lemon juice60 ml
  • Grated lemon zest6 g
  • Baking powder16 g
  • Icing sugar10 g
  • Fine salt1 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 170°C (conventional) and grease and flour a 24 cm cake tin.
  2. Melt the butter and allow it to cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the sugar and lemon zest for 8-10 minutes, until the mixture is light and fluffy.
  4. Gradually pour in the melted butter and the milk, continuing to mix at a low speed.
  5. Stir in the lemon juice.
  6. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt together.
  7. Fold in the dry ingredients a little at a time using a spatula, with an upwards motion from the bottom.
  8. Pour the batter into the tin and level it.
  9. Bake for 38-40 minutes: a skewer inserted into the centre should come out clean.
  10. Allow to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out and dust with icing sugar.

Tips

  • Ensure the eggs are at room temperature to help the mixture whip up more stably and with more volume.
  • Grate only the yellow part of the lemon zest, avoiding the white pith which would give the cake a bitter taste.
  • Let the cake cool completely on a wire rack before dusting it with powdered sugar to prevent it from getting damp.

Variations

  • Add poppy seeds to the batter for a slightly crunchy texture and an original visual appearance.
  • Replace the lemon with lime for a more exotic aromatic profile and a sharper citrus note.
