Butter-free buckwheat cookies

2 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

Crumbly cookies, excellent for breakfast or afternoon tea.

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Buckwheat flour230 g
  • Cornstarch (maizena)60 g
  • Brown sugar110 g
  • Sunflower oil90 ml
  • Egg1 medium
  • Baking powder4 g
  • Lemon zestto taste
  • A pinch of saltto taste

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. In a bowl, place the egg, sugar, and salt, and mix with a hand whisk.
  2. Then add the sunflower oil and lemon zest and mix again.
  3. In another bowl, mix the buckwheat flour, cornstarch, and baking powder.
  4. Add the flours and baking powder one tablespoon at a time to the bowl with the egg, sugar, and salt, and mix.
  5. Transfer the dough to a work surface and knead until it becomes a smooth and homogeneous dough ball. Wrap the ball in plastic wrap and let it rest in the fridge for 20 minutes.
  6. Once this time has passed, roll out the dough on the work surface, cut out the cookies with a shape of your choice, and bake in a preheated static oven at 170°C for 35/40 minutes.
