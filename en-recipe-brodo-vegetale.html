Vegetable broth

8 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 60 min · Easy

Light, fragrant homemade vegetable broth: perfect for risottos, cream soups and soups.

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Carrots2 pz
  • Onion1 pz
  • Celery2 coste
  • Potato1 pz
  • Tomato1 pz
  • Parsley1 mazzetto
  • Coarse saltq.b.
  • Cold water2,5 l

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Wash and trim the vegetables, then cut them into large pieces.
  2. Put all the vegetables in a large pot with the cold water and the parsley.
  3. Bring to the boil, then lower the heat and simmer gently for about 1 hour with the lid ajar.
  4. Season with salt at the end and strain through a fine sieve.

Chef's tips

Use seasonal vegetables: add a piece of pumpkin in autumn, a courgette in summer.

Avoid cabbage and broccoli, which make the flavour too strong.

Freeze it in portions or ice-cube trays so it is always ready.
