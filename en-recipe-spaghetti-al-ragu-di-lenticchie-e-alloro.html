Spaghetti with Lentil and Bay Leaf Ragù

💰 Save Euro · about €0.79 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A vegetarian twist on the classic ragù, thick and flavourful thanks to brown lentils. It is a nutritious and very affordable dish, perfect for family meals.

Ingredients

  • Wholemeal spaghetti320 g
  • Small dried lentils200 g
  • Tomato passata (smooth sieved tomatoes)400 g
  • Yellow onion1 pcs
  • Carrot1 pcs
  • Celery stalk1 pcs
  • Bay leaves2 foglie
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Salt and pepperq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Celery

Method

  1. Start by thoroughly rinsing the lentils under running water to remove any impurities and leave them to drain.
  2. Prepare a soffritto by finely chopping the onion, carrot, and celery, then place them in a saucepan with the oil and a tablespoon of water to let them soften gently for 5-7 minutes.
  3. Add the lentils to the soffritto and toast them for 2 minutes, stirring often to flavour them well.
  4. Pour in the tomato passata and add the bay leaves; cover with a lid and leave to cook on a low heat for about 30-40 minutes.
  5. If the sauce dries out too much, add a ladle of hot water: the ragù is ready when the lentils are tender but not mushy.
  6. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti following the times indicated on the packet for an 'al dente' texture.
  7. Drain the pasta and transfer it directly into the saucepan with the lentil ragù, removing the bay leaves first.
  8. Sauté everything over a high heat for a minute, adding a drizzle of raw oil and a grind of black pepper before serving.
