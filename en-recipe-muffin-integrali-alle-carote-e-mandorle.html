Wholemeal Carrot and Almond Muffins

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 22 min · Medium

Moist and nutritious muffins that hide a good dose of vegetables, perfect for children's snacks. Wholemeal flour and almonds guarantee slow-release energy.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Peeled carrots250 g
  • Wholemeal flour200 g
  • Light brown sugar90 g
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Sunflower oil70 ml
  • Ground almonds50 g
  • Baking powder12 g

Allergens

Eggs, Gluten, Tree nuts

Method

  1. Grate the carrots very finely and squeeze them slightly with a tea towel to remove excess water.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs with the brown sugar until you get a frothy mixture.
  3. Add the sunflower oil in a thin stream, mixing gently so as not to deflate the egg mixture.
  4. Incorporate the grated carrots into the liquid batter, distributing them evenly with a spatula.
  5. Add the wholemeal flour, ground almonds, and baking powder, mixing only as much as necessary to combine.
  6. Do not overwork the batter after adding the flour to prevent the muffins from becoming rubbery.
  7. Fill the muffin cases to about 3/4 of their height to allow for even rising in the oven.
  8. Bake at 180°C for about 20-22 minutes; perform the skewer test: it must come out dry and clean.
  9. Leave the muffins to cool in the tin for 5 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack.
