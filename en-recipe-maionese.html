Mayonnaise

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • pasteurized whole egg1 unit
  • seed oil200 ml
  • lemon juice15 ml
  • vinegar5 ml
  • salt3 g

Allergens

Eggs
May contain traces of: Sulphites.

Method

  1. Pour the pasteurized egg into a tall and narrow container, suitable for an immersion blender.
  2. Add the salt, lemon juice, and vinegar.
  3. Pour the oil slowly over the ingredients, without stirring.
  4. Insert the blender all the way to the bottom of the container and operate it without moving it for a few seconds.
  5. When the bottom part starts to thicken, slowly lift the blender to incorporate all the oil.
  6. Continue until you obtain a thick and smooth sauce.
  7. Taste and adjust the acidity with a few drops of lemon.
  8. Transfer to a clean container and store immediately in the refrigerator.

Chef's tips

Tips, errors, and alternatives:

- Use pasteurized eggs to reduce microbiological risk.

- If the mayonnaise breaks, try to emulsify it again by adding a pasteurized yolk little by little in a clean container.

- You can replace part of the seed oil with mild extra virgin olive oil.

- Add mustard for a more aromatic variation.
