Fluffy Carrot and Ricotta Mini-Frittata

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 8 min · Easy

A version of the omelette that is much softer than the original thanks to the ricotta, which prevents the egg from becoming rubbery. The grated carrots add a natural sweetness highly appreciated by little ones.

Ingredients

  • Medium eggs3 pcs
  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (a soft, mild Italian whey cheese)100 g
  • Medium carrots2 pcs
  • 24-month aged Parmigiano Reggiano (a hard, aged Italian cheese)15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil5 g

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Clean the carrots and grate them very finely so that they cook quickly and blend with the egg.
  2. In a bowl, work the ricotta with a fork until it becomes a velvety cream without lumps.
  3. Add the eggs to the ricotta and whisk vigorously to incorporate air, making the frittata spongy.
  4. Join the grated carrots and the Parmigiano, stirring gently to distribute the ingredients.
  5. Brush a small non-stick pan with the oil and heat it over a medium-low heat.
  6. Pour in the mixture and cover immediately with a lid: the steam will help the frittata to puff up and cook evenly.
  7. After 4 minutes, when the surface appears set, flip the frittata with the help of the lid or a plate.
  8. Cook the other side for just 2 minutes: the inside must remain moist and not dry.
  9. Cut into strips or small cubes easy for the child to hold (safe finger food).
