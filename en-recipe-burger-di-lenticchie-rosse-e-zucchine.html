Red Lentil and Courgette Burgers

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

Red split lentils are the most digestible legumes for young children as they are hull-free. These burgers have a melting texture that requires no excessive chewing.

Ingredients

  • Dried red split lentils120 g
  • Courgettes150 g
  • Fine cornmeal (fioretto) [A very finely ground corn flour common in Italy for a smooth texture]30 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 g
  • Water300 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils well and place them in a pan with cold water, without adding salt.
  2. Bring to a boil and cook for 15 minutes until the lentils have absorbed the water and turned into a purée.
  3. While the lentils are cooking, grate the courgette and squeeze it with your hands to remove excess water.
  4. Combine the squeezed courgette with the still-warm lentil purée and mix well.
  5. Add the cornmeal to the mixture to make it workable, letting it rest for 5 minutes so the corn can hydrate.
  6. With slightly damp hands, form small flattened medallions about 4 cm in diameter.
  7. Place the burgers on a baking tray lined with greaseproof paper and brush them with a drop of olive oil.
  8. Bake at 180°C for about 15 minutes, turning them halfway through until they are slightly golden but still soft.
  9. Leave to cool slightly: the structure of the burger will stabilise as it cools.
