Shortcrust Pastry (Pasta Frolla)

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 60 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • 00 Flour300 gr
  • Cold butter150 gr
  • Icing sugar120 gr
  • Eggs1 number
  • Egg yolk1 number
  • Salt1 pinch
  • Grated lemon zest0.5 number
  • Baking powder (optional)2 gr

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Pour the sifted flour into a bowl or, if available, into a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment and add the cold butter cut into cubes.
  2. Work quickly with your fingertips until you get a sandy mixture, without overheating the butter too much.
  3. Add the icing sugar, salt, and lemon zest, distributing them evenly.
  4. Incorporate the whole egg and the egg yolk. Work briefly until the ingredients begin to come together.
  5. Transfer the mixture onto the work surface and compact it without kneading it too much. Form a flattened disk.
  6. Wrap in plastic wrap and let it rest in the refrigerator for at least 60 minutes.
  7. Roll out the pastry on a lightly floured surface to a thickness of about 4 mm, then use it for tarts or cookies.
  8. For a tart, transfer to a buttered and floured mold, prick the bottom lightly, and bake at 170–180 °C for about 25–35 minutes, depending on the filling and thickness.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not overwork the dough: it would become elastic and less crumbly.

- If it breaks during rolling, let it rest for a few minutes at room temperature.

- For a cocoa pastry, replace 25 g of flour with 25 g of unsweetened cocoa powder.

- For a crumblier texture you can use only icing sugar.

- Baking powder is optional: traditional shortcrust pastry doesn't necessarily require its use.
