Buckwheat Soup with Mushrooms and Spinach

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A restorative and naturally gluten-free soup. The intensity of button mushrooms meets the earthiness of buckwheat for a comforting evening meal.

Ingredients

  • Buckwheat groats200 g
  • Button mushrooms400 g
  • Fresh or frozen spinach200 g
  • Leek1 pcs
  • Vegetable stock1.5 l
  • Dried thyme1 cucchiaino
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the buckwheat under cold running water until the water runs clear.
  2. Slice the leek into thin rounds and sauté gently in a large pot with the oil.
  3. Clean the mushrooms and slice them, then add them to the leek and sauté over high heat until they release their water.
  4. Add the buckwheat and toast it for a minute with the vegetables to seal the grains.
  5. Cover with boiling vegetable stock, add the thyme, and simmer over low heat for about 15-18 minutes.
  6. Five minutes before the end of cooking, add the spinach (if fresh, it will wilt instantly).
  7. Season with salt and pepper and turn off the heat when the buckwheat is tender but still intact (it should not become mushy).
  8. Let the soup rest, covered, for 2 minutes before serving to harmonise the flavours.
  9. Complete with a drizzle of raw oil and, if liked, a pinch of chilli flakes.
