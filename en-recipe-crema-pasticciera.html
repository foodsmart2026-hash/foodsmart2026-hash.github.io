Pastry cream

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Whole milk500 ml
  • Sugar120 g
  • Egg yolks80 g
  • Cornstarch40 g
  • Vanilla bean1
  • Salt1 pinch

Allergens

Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Pour the milk into a saucepan and add the split vanilla bean. Heat until just before boiling, without letting it evaporate excessively.
  2. In a bowl, work the egg yolks with the sugar and a pinch of salt. Mix with a whisk until you obtain a smooth mixture.
  3. Incorporate the sifted cornstarch, a little at a time, avoiding the formation of lumps.
  4. Gradually pour the hot milk over the yolk mixture, stirring continuously to temper it without cooking it suddenly.
  5. Transfer everything back into the saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly with a whisk and reaching a temperature of about 82–85 °C.
  6. When the cream thickens and the first bubbles appear, continue cooking for about a minute, still stirring.
  7. Immediately transfer the cream to a shallow and wide container, cover with plastic wrap in contact with the surface and cool quickly.
  8. Once cold, whisk vigorously to make it smooth again before use.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Never stop stirring during cooking: the cream might stick to the bottom.

- If lumps appear, you can use an immersion blender.

- For a richer cream, replace 100 ml of milk with fresh heavy cream.

- You can flavor it with coffee, cinnamon, lemon or orange.

- Store in the refrigerator at 4 °C or below and consume preferably within 48 hours.
