Tofu in a Potato and Rosemary Crust

💰 Save Euro · about €1.21 per person

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 12 min · Medium

A recipe that transforms tofu into a delicious and crispy main course using potatoes as a 'breading'. An original way to enjoy plant-based proteins with Mediterranean flavours.

Ingredients

  • Plain tofu400 g
  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes300 g
  • Cornflour20 g
  • Fresh rosemary2 rametti
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Salt and Pepperq.b.

Allergens

Soy

Method

  1. Wrap the tofu in kitchen paper and press it with a weight for 15 minutes to remove excess water.
  2. Cut the tofu into 8 equal rectangular or triangular slices to ensure even cooking.
  3. Grate the raw potatoes using a coarse grater and squeeze them very hard with a tea towel to remove the liquid starch.
  4. In a bowl, mix the grated potatoes with the cornflour, chopped rosemary, salt, and pepper.
  5. Coat each slice of tofu in the potato mix, pressing firmly so the potatoes adhere well to both sides.
  6. Heat plenty of oil in a non-stick frying pan; it must be very hot before adding the tofu.
  7. Cook the tofu for 5-6 minutes per side over medium-high heat without moving it too much at the start.
  8. The dish is ready when the potatoes form a golden, compact, and very crispy crust, similar to a rosti.
  9. Serve immediately so the crust does not lose its typical texture.
