Ginger and Apple Cider Vinegar Caramelised Carrots

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A budget-friendly and vibrant side dish that transforms simple carrots into a gourmet accompaniment. The spicy note of fresh ginger pairs perfectly with the delicate acidity of apple cider vinegar.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Medium carrots800 g
  • Fresh ginger20 g
  • Apple cider vinegar40 ml
  • Brown sugar15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Fine salt5 g
  • Fresh parsley10 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the carrots, peel them, and cut them into diagonal rounds of about 1 cm to ensure even cooking and an inviting appearance.
  2. Grate the peeled fresh ginger and set it aside; this will be used to infuse flavour without leaving fibrous pieces.
  3. In a large frying pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil and add the carrots, browning them over a high heat for 5 minutes until the edges start to turn golden.
  4. Add salt and the grated ginger, stirring well to distribute the flavours evenly over all the rounds.
  5. Pour in the apple cider vinegar and sprinkle with brown sugar, allowing the acidic and sharp notes to evaporate for about 2 minutes.
  6. Lower the heat, cover with a lid, and cook for another 10 minutes; the carrots should be tender but still firm to the bite (al dente).
  7. Remove the lid and turn up the heat for the final 2 minutes to reduce the cooking juices until you get a glossy glaze coating the vegetables.
  8. Turn off the heat and garnish with finely chopped fresh parsley for a final touch of colour and freshness.
