Turkey Stew with Apples and Rosemary

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A light and budget-friendly main course that uses fruit to create a creamy sauce without cream. The apple melts during cooking, creating a delicious sweet and sour contrast.

Ingredients

  • Diced turkey breast600 g
  • Apples (Golden Delicious or Reinette types)2 pcs
  • White onion1 pcs
  • Plain flour (Type 00)30 g
  • Dry white wine50 ml
  • Vegetable stock200 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Sulphites

Method

  1. Toss the turkey cubes in the flour, shaking off any excess.
  2. In a large frying pan, heat the oil and brown the turkey over a high heat for 5 minutes until sealed on all sides.
  3. Remove the meat from the pan and, in the same pan, add the chopped onion with a tablespoon of stock, letting it soften.
  4. Peel the apples, cut them into small cubes and add them to the onion, cooking for 3 minutes.
  5. Return the turkey to the pan, deglaze with the white wine and allow the alcohol to evaporate over a high heat.
  6. Add the stock and rosemary, lower the heat, cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes.
  7. At the end of the cooking time, mash a few pieces of apple with a fork to further thicken the sauce.
  8. Season with salt and pepper and serve the stew very hot, perhaps accompanied by some pilau rice.
