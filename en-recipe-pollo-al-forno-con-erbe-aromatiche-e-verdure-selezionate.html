Roast chicken with herbs and selected vegetables

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 50 min · Easy

Roast chicken thighs with rosemary, thyme, and oregano, served with potatoes, courgettes, and aubergines.

🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

An Italian Sunday classic combining juicy chicken thighs with the freshness of seasonal vegetables. The blend of fresh and dried herbs provides an intense aroma and a rustic flavor profile.

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs1000 g
  • Potatoes600 g
  • Courgettes400 g
  • Aubergines300 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil50 g
  • Fresh rosemary4 g
  • Dried thyme1 g
  • Dried oregano1 g
  • Fine salt9 g
  • Ground black pepper1 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Preheat the fan oven to 200 °C.
  2. Finely chop the rosemary and mix it with the thyme, oregano, 5 g of salt, pepper, and 30 g of oil.
  3. Massage the chicken thighs with the herb seasoning.
  4. Peel the potatoes and cut them into wedges.
  5. Cut the courgettes and aubergines into pieces of approximately 3 cm.
  6. Season the vegetables with the remaining oil and salt.
  7. Arrange the potatoes and aubergines in a large roasting tin and place the chicken thighs on top, skin-side up.
  8. Cook for 25 minutes, then add the courgettes and stir the vegetables.
  9. Continue cooking for a further 20-25 minutes, until the skin is golden and the meat pulls away from the bone.
  10. Allow to rest for 5 minutes and serve the chicken with the vegetables.

Tips

  • Pat the chicken skin dry before rubbing with oil to achieve a crispier, golden crust.
  • Cut vegetables into uniform 3 cm pieces to ensure they cook evenly alongside the meat.
  • Let the dish rest for a few minutes after baking to allow the juices to redistribute before serving.

Variations

  • You can replace eggplants with colorful bell peppers for an extra touch of sweetness and a different texture.
  • Add unpeeled garlic cloves to the roasting tin for an even more pronounced Mediterranean aroma.
