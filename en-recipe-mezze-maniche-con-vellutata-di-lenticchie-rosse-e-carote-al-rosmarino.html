Mezze Maniche with Red Lentil Velouté and Rosemary Carrots

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A nutritious and velvety first course that transforms red lentils into an enveloping cream for the pasta. The sweetness of the carrots pairs beautifully with the aroma of rosemary for a complete vegan meal at a very low cost.

Ingredients

  • Mezze maniche rigate (short, ridged tube-shaped pasta)320 g
  • Red split lentils (husked red lentils)150 g
  • Carrots200 g
  • Yellow onion1 pcs
  • Fresh rosemary1 rametto

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Start by prepping the onion and carrots, cutting them into very small cubes to speed up the cooking process and help release their natural sugars.
  2. In a large pot, heat two tablespoons of oil and sauté the vegetables with the rosemary sprig for about 5 minutes over medium heat until the onion becomes translucent.
  3. Add the red lentils, previously rinsed under running water to remove any impurities, and toast them for 1 minute along with the vegetables.
  4. Cover everything with about 800 ml of hot water or light vegetable stock, bring to the boil, and cook for 15-20 minutes until the lentils begin to break down naturally.
  5. Remove the rosemary sprig and, using an immersion blender, blend part of the mixture into a cream directly in the pot, leaving some lentils whole to provide texture.
  6. Meanwhile, cook the mezze maniche in plenty of salted water, draining them about 2 minutes before the time indicated on the packet to finish cooking in the cream.
  7. Transfer the pasta to the pot with the lentil velouté, add a ladle of the cooking water, and stir vigorously for 2 minutes so the starch perfectly binds the sauce.
  8. Turn off the heat when the pasta is al dente and the dressing is creamy but not dry; let it rest for a minute before serving with a drizzle of raw olive oil.
  9. The dish is ready when the cream coats the pasta evenly, creating a velvety effect similar to a creamy white ragù.
