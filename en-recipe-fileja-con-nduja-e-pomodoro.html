Fileja with 'nduja and tomato

Calabria · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

Traditional Calabrian hand-rolled pasta with a spicy 'nduja tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Semolina fileja pasta360 g
  • 'Nduja di Spilinga60 g
  • Tomato passata400 ml
  • Tropea red onion60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Grated Crotonese pecorino30 g
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk
May contain traces of: Celery, Mustard.

Method

  1. Finely slice the onion and soften it in the oil for 4 minutes.
  2. Add the 'nduja and allow it to melt over a low heat.
  3. Pour in the passata, season with salt and cook for 15 minutes.
  4. Boil the fileja in salted water for the time indicated on the packaging.
  5. Drain the pasta and toss it through the sauce for 1 minute.
  6. Serve with the pecorino.
