Fileja with 'nduja and tomato

Calabria · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

Traditional Calabrian hand-rolled pasta with a spicy 'nduja tomato sauce.

🇮🇹 Italian

A robust Calabrian first course featuring fileja pasta coated in a spicy, creamy sauce. The sweetness of Tropea onions balances the bold 'nduja, creating a deeply satisfying and enveloping flavor.

Ingredients

  • Semolina fileja pasta360 g
  • 'Nduja di Spilinga60 g
  • Tomato passata400 ml
  • Tropea red onion60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Grated Crotonese pecorino30 g
  • Fine salt5 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk
May contain traces of: Celery, Mustard.

Method

  1. Start by peeling and thinly slicing the 60 g of Tropea red onion on a clean cutting board. The Tropea onion is essential for this recipe because its natural sweetness perfectly balances the heat of the 'nduja.
  2. In a large skillet, pour the 30 ml of extra virgin olive oil and heat it over medium-low heat. Add the sliced onion and let it soften gently for about 4 minutes until it becomes tender and almost transparent, avoiding too much browning.
  3. Take the 60 g of 'nduja di Spilinga, remove the casing, and add it to the pan with the onion. Mash it with a wooden spoon over very low heat until it has completely melted, releasing its red oil and an intense, spicy aroma.
  4. Pour the 400 ml of tomato purée into the pan and stir carefully to blend with the spicy sauté base. Add the 5 g of fine salt, keeping in mind that 'nduja is already savory, so do not overdo it.
  5. Partially cover the pan and let the sauce cook over low heat for about 15 minutes. You will know it is ready when the consistency has become thick and velvety, with a very inviting dark red color.
  6. While the sauce finishes cooking, bring a large pot of water to a boil. Once boiling, drop in the 360 g of durum wheat fileja; this typical pasta needs space to cook evenly without sticking.
  7. Cook the fileja according to the package instructions, usually about 10-12 minutes, until al dente. Their rolled shape is specifically designed to trap the sauce within its coils.
  8. Drain the pasta and immediately transfer it to the skillet with the tomato and 'nduja sauce. Toss everything over high heat for 1 minute, stirring vigorously so that every fileja is perfectly coated in the creamy sauce.
  9. Turn off the heat and serve the hot fileja in shallow bowls or large plates. Complete each dish by evenly distributing the 30 g of grated Crotonese pecorino, which will provide a savory note and extra creaminess thanks to the residual heat of the pasta.

Tips

  • Melt the 'nduja thoroughly in hot oil for a smooth sauce.
  • Drain the pasta al dente and toss it directly into the pan.
  • Store any leftovers in the refrigerator for up to one day.

Variations

  • You can replace Pecorino crotonese with grated ricotta salata.
  • Add pitted black olives for an extra savory touch.
