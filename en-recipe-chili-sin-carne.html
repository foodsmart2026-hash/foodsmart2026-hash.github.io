Chili sin carne

United States · 4 servings · 45 min · Easy

Vegan chili with beans and corn.

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🌍 International

A rich and spicy vegetarian take on the famous Tex-Mex stew, perfect for sharing during a casual night with friends. The blend of red beans and corn provides a thick texture and comforting flavor.

Ingredients

  • Red kidney beans (cooked, drained)480 g
  • Sweet corn (drained)150 g
  • Canned peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Red bell pepper200 g
  • Yellow onion100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 g
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Ground cumin1 tsp
  • Fresh chili pepper5 g
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Fine salt6 g
  • Vegetable broth200 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Clean the onion and bell pepper, dicing them into uniform 1 cm cubes.
  2. In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, heat the extra virgin olive oil and sauté the onion and crushed garlic for 5 minutes over medium heat (approx. 140°C).
  3. Add the bell pepper and sauté for 7 minutes, stirring frequently for even cooking.
  4. Add the cumin, smoked paprika, and chopped chili (remove seeds for less heat) and toast for 1 minute.
  5. Add the canned peeled tomatoes, crushing them coarsely with a spoon, and season with salt.
  6. Pour the red kidney beans, corn, and hot vegetable broth into the saucepan.
  7. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for about 25 minutes with a half-closed lid.
  8. Remove the lid during the last 5 minutes of cooking to let the sauce reduce until thick and hearty.
  9. Let the chili rest for 5 minutes before serving to allow the flavors to meld together.

Tips

  • Sauté the spices with the onion to bloom their essential oils and enhance the overall depth of the dish.
  • Crush the peeled tomatoes roughly with a spoon to achieve a rustic and hearty consistency for the sauce.
  • Simmer over low heat until the liquid reduces significantly, creating a thick and glossy stew.

Variations

  • Serve the chili alongside steamed white basmati rice for a complete and satisfying single-course meal.
  • Stir in a small square of dark chocolate at the end of cooking for added color and flavor complexity.
