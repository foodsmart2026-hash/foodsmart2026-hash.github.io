Yogurt and Chocolate Chip Loaf Cake

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

The classic 'yogurt pot' recipe that requires no scales, perfect for involving children. The yogurt ensures a softness that lasts for days without the use of butter.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Natural plain yogurt125 g
  • Plain flour (Type 00)250 g
  • Caster sugar150 g
  • Vegetable oil100 ml
  • Medium eggs3 pcs
  • Chocolate chips50 g
  • Baking powder (Italian 'lievito per dolci')16 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Start by whisking the eggs with the sugar until the mixture is pale and very fluffy.
  2. Add the room-temperature plain yogurt and the vegetable oil in a thin stream, continuing to mix gently.
  3. Sift the flour together with the baking powder and add them to the mixture gradually, folding with a spatula from bottom to top.
  4. Lightly flour the chocolate chips before adding them: this trick will prevent them from all falling to the bottom during baking.
  5. Pour the batter into a loaf tin (about 25 cm) previously greased or lined with parchment paper.
  6. Level the surface well and, if you like, add a few extra chocolate chips on top for appearance.
  7. Bake in a static oven at 180°C for 40-45 minutes without opening the door for the first half hour.
  8. Check if it's cooked using a skewer: it must come out clean. The surface should have the typical central crack of a loaf cake.
  9. Leave to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack until completely cool.
