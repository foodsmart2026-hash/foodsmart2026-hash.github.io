Semolina Milk Cream with Dried Apricots

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 5 min · Easy

A warm and velvety breakfast that uses the natural sweetness of dried apricots rehydrated in milk. The semolina ensures a smooth texture perfect for the transition to solid foods.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly

Ingredients

  • Wheat semolina (finely milled durum wheat)60 g
  • Whole milk500 ml
  • Dried apricots (no added sugar)6 pcs

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Soak the dried apricots in a glass of hot water or milk for at least 15 minutes to rehydrate and soften them.
  2. Drain the apricots and chop them finely with a knife or blend them with a splash of milk until you get a smooth paste.
  3. Pour the milk into a small saucepan and bring it almost to the boil over a moderate heat.
  4. Rain the semolina into the hot milk, whisking continuously to prevent lumps from forming.
  5. Cook the semolina for about 4-5 minutes, continuing to stir until it thickens into a creamy consistency.
  6. Turn off the heat and stir in the previously prepared apricot paste, mixing well to standardise the flavour.
  7. If the cream is too firm, add another splash of warm milk to make it smoother and easier to swallow.
  8. Distribute into small bowls and let it rest for a few minutes until it reaches the ideal temperature for eating.
