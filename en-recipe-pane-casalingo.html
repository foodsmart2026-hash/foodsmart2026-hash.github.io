Homemade Bread

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • type 0 flour500 gr
  • water330 ml
  • fresh yeast7 gr
  • salt10 gr
  • extra virgin olive oil10 ml

Allergens

Gluten
May contain traces of: Mustard, Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in about 100 ml of water taken from the total. Pour the flour into a bowl and gradually add the rest of the water.
  2. Incorporate the dissolved yeast and start kneading until you get a homogeneous mixture.
  3. Add the salt and the oil, then continue working for about 10 minutes, until you obtain an elastic and uniform dough.
  4. Form a ball, transfer it into a lightly greased bowl and cover with plastic wrap. Let rise for about 2 hours or until doubled.
  5. Gently turn the dough out onto the work surface, deflate it slightly and form a loaf, creating a well-tensioned surface.
  6. Place it on a baking tray with parchment paper, cover and let rise for another 60–90 minutes.
  7. Make a shallow cut on the top and bake in a preheated oven at 220 °C for 15 minutes, then lower to 190 °C and continue for another 25 minutes.
  8. Remove from the oven and let cool completely on a wire rack before slicing.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not add too much flour during the process: the dough should remain slightly soft.

- The bread is ready when the crust is golden and the base produces a hollow sound when tapped.

- For a crispier crust, create steam in the oven at the start of baking, using a safe method for your oven.

- You can replace 100 g of type 0 flour with whole wheat flour.

- You can add sesame, flax or sunflower seeds.
