Golden Polenta Medallions with Sautéed Mushrooms and Sunflower Seeds

💰 Save Euro · about €0.99 per person

4 servings · Prep 5 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A crunchy and savoury starter that uses the convenience of ready-made polenta to create a perfect gluten-free base. The contrast between the softness of the mushrooms and the toasted note of the sunflower seeds makes it ideal for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • Ready-to-eat polenta block (precooked cornmeal block, gluten-free)500 g
  • Fresh button mushrooms300 g
  • Fresh garlic2 spicchi
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Hulled sunflower seeds30 g
  • Fresh parsley10 g
  • Fine salt4 g
  • Ground black pepper2 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Slice the polenta block into 8 equal slices about 1.5 cm thick, using a smooth-bladed knife to prevent them from crumbling.
  2. Heat a large non-stick pan with 15 ml of oil and place the polenta slices inside; cook them over medium-high heat for 4 minutes per side until a golden and crunchy crust forms.
  3. While the polenta browns, clean the mushrooms by removing the soil with a damp cloth and slice them thinly (about 3 mm) to ensure quick and even cooking.
  4. In a second pan, heat 15 ml of oil with the crushed garlic cloves for 2 minutes to flavour the oil without burning the garlic.
  5. Pour the mushrooms into the pan with the garlic and sauté them over a high heat for 6 minutes; do not add salt immediately to prevent them from releasing too much water and becoming rubbery.
  6. Toast the sunflower seeds in a separate small pan for 2 minutes over low heat until they release a nutty aroma, moving them continuously so they don't burn.
  7. Turn off the heat for the mushrooms, remove the garlic, and season them with the salt, pepper, and finely chopped parsley, stirring well to distribute the flavours.
  8. Assemble the starter by placing two polenta medallions on each plate and topping them with the still-warm sautéed mushrooms.
  9. Finish the dish by scattering over the toasted sunflower seeds and a final drizzle of raw oil (the remaining 10 ml) to enhance the shine and flavour.
