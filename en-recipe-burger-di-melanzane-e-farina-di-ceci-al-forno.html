Baked Aubergine and Chickpea Flour Burgers

4 servings · Prep 45 min · Cooking 25 min · Hard

A plant-based alternative to classic burgers, very economical as it uses seasonal aubergines and legume flour as a natural binder.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Aubergines800 g
  • Chickpea flour (Gram flour)120 g
  • Garlic1 spicchio
  • Fresh parsley1 mazzetto
  • Dried oregano5 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Salt and Pepperq.b.

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Cut the aubergines into small cubes and cook them in a pan with a drizzle of oil and garlic until they are completely soft.
  2. Mash the cooked aubergines with a fork or chop them roughly with a knife to obtain a homogenous pulp.
  3. In a bowl, combine the aubergine pulp, chopped parsley, oregano, and sifted chickpea flour.
  4. Knead well with your hands: the chickpea flour will absorb the moisture from the aubergine, creating a compact mass.
  5. Let the mixture rest in the fridge for 30 minutes; this step is essential to make the burgers less sticky.
  6. Form 4 or 8 burgers (depending on the desired size) and lightly coat them in a thin layer of extra chickpea flour.
  7. Place on a baking tray and brush with oil. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes, turning them gently halfway through.
  8. They will be ready when a dark outer crust has formed and the centre feels firm to the touch.
  9. Serve hot in a bun or on a plate with a fresh tomato sauce.
