Potato and mortadella croquettes

6 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A classic Italian appetizer but with an extra kick

🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Mortadella, 1 slice200 g
  • Potatoes200 g
  • Egg yolks4
  • Eggs2
  • BreadcrumbsTo taste
  • Parmesan cheese100 g
  • Fine saltTo taste
  • Ground pepperTo taste

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and place them in a pot with cold water. Bring to a boil and check occasionally if the potatoes are cooked.
  2. The potatoes are ready when you pierce them with a fork and feel no resistance.
  3. Drain the potatoes and wait for them to cool. Mash the potatoes with a potato masher or a fork until you get a smooth mixture.
  4. Chop the mortadella with a knife, or if you prefer, with a mixer.
  5. Combine the mortadella and parmesan with the potatoes and knead together.
  6. Beat the eggs in a bowl.
  7. Form small balls with the mixture, dip them in the egg and then in the breadcrumbs.
  8. Fry them in hot oil. Serve immediately.
