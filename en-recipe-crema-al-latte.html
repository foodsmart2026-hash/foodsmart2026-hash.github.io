Milk Cream

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 8 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • whole milk500 ml
  • sugar100 g
  • cornstarch40 g
  • vanilla extract1 teaspoon
  • fresh whipping cream (optional, for a fluffier version)100 ml

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Gluten, Sulphites.

Method

  1. Pour the milk into a small saucepan and add the vanilla. Heat over medium heat without bringing it to a boil.
  2. In a bowl, mix the sugar with the sifted cornstarch.
  3. Gradually pour about 100 ml of hot milk over the dry ingredients, whisking to obtain a smooth batter.
  4. Combine the batter with the remaining milk in the saucepan and return to the heat.
  5. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the cream becomes thick and velvety.
  6. Transfer to a bowl, cover with plastic wrap in contact with the surface and let cool quickly.
  7. If you want a lighter consistency, whip the cold cream and gently fold it into the completely cooled cream.
  8. Store in the refrigerator until ready to use.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not fold in the cream while the custard is still hot: it would lose volume.

- For a more fragrant version, add lemon zest.

- You can replace 100 ml of milk with a plant-based drink, making sure it is suitable for the desired consistency.

- For a firmer cream, increase the starch by 5–10 g.
