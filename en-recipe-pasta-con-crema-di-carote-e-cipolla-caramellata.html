Pasta with Carrot Cream and Caramelised Onion

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A vibrantly coloured first course with a gently balanced flavour, perfect for getting little ones to eat their vegetables. The cream is achieved simply by blending boiled carrots, creating a velvety sauce with no added fats.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Short pasta (e.g. Penne)320 g
  • Carrots500 g
  • Golden onions2 pcs
  • Fresh ginger (or powder)10 g
  • Brown sugar5 g
  • White wine vinegar10 ml

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Peel the carrots, cut them into thin rounds and cook in lightly salted boiling water for about 15 minutes until they are very tender.
  2. While the carrots are cooking, finely slice the onions and put them in a pan with a drizzle of oil, the sugar and a pinch of salt.
  3. Allow the onions to soften over a very low heat for 10 minutes, deglazing with the vinegar at the end to create a sweet and sour note.
  4. Drain the carrots (keeping the cooking water for the pasta) and blend them with a stick blender, adding the grated ginger and a drizzle of oil until a very smooth cream is obtained.
  5. Cook the pasta in the carrot water to recover all the nutrients and residual colour.
  6. Drain the pasta al dente and pour it into the pan with the caramelised onions, immediately adding the carrot cream.
  7. Toss everything together with a ladle of cooking water to combine the sauce well with the pasta.
  8. Serve with a generous grinding of black pepper which contrasts perfectly with the sweetness of the carrot.
