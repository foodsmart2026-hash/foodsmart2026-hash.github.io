Stewed Castelluccio Lentils

Umbria · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

Small Umbrian lentils stewed with soffritto and tomato; vegan.

🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

A classic Italian rustic dish highlighting the prized Castelluccio lentils, known for their thin skin and firm texture. This recipe offers a comforting consistency and earthy flavors enriched by an aromatic vegetable base and bay leaf.

Ingredients

  • Castelluccio lentils300 g
  • Carrot80 g
  • Celery60 g
  • Onion80 g
  • Tomato purée20 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Bay leaf1 g
  • Water900 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Begin by thoroughly rinsing the 300g of Castelluccio lentils under cold running water using a fine-mesh strainer. This prized Umbrian variety does not require soaking, but washing is essential to remove any impurities or dust residues from among the grains.
  2. Prepare the aromatic base by finely mincing 80g of carrot, 60g of celery, and 80g of onion until you obtain a uniform mixture. Try to keep the size of the pieces consistent so they can soften evenly, releasing all their natural aromas during the cooking process.
  3. Pour about 30ml of extra virgin olive oil into a large saucepan and heat over medium heat, then add the chopped vegetables. Let them sauté gently for about 6 minutes until the onion becomes translucent and the carrots have softened, taking care not to burn them to avoid bitter notes.
  4. Add the 300g of well-drained lentils and 20g of tomato paste to the aromatic base, stirring with a wooden spoon. Sauté for a couple of minutes over medium-high heat: this step allows the lentils to 'toast' slightly and the tomato to develop a deeper brownish-red color.
  5. Add 1g of bay leaves and pour in 900ml of water, preferably already hot so as not to cause thermal shock to the legumes. Using hot water is a small professional secret to keeping the lentil skins intact and ensuring a final velvety texture.
  6. Cover the pot and let it simmer over low heat for about 35-40 minutes, maintaining a very gentle boil. You will know they are ready when the lentils are tender to the bite but still perfectly whole, immersed in a thick, fragrant sauce with a typical brownish hue.
  7. Only at this point, turn off the heat and add the 6g of fine salt, stirring gently to distribute it perfectly throughout the dish. Salting only at the end of cooking is essential because salt tends to harden the skin of legumes if added during boiling.
  8. Remove the bay leaf and let your stew rest for a couple of minutes so the flavors can settle. Serve the lentils warm in deep bowls, finishing each portion with the remaining 10ml of raw extra virgin olive oil to enhance their herbaceous notes.

Tips

  • Rinse the lentils thoroughly under running water to remove any debris before you start cooking.
  • Use pre-heated water to keep the simmer steady and ensure the legumes cook evenly.
  • Prepare the dish in advance, as letting it rest allows the flavors to meld and the consistency to thicken.

Variations

  • For a savory touch, add sautéed pancetta cubes to the aromatic base of the stir-fry.
  • Replace the water with a light vegetable broth to achieve an even more intense flavor profile.
