Pastina in vegetable broth

Kid-friendly

1 servings · 30 min · Easy

Small pasta and Parmigiano, from 8 months.

A cornerstone of Italian home cooking, perfect for early introductions to grains. This dish offers a soft texture and a delicate flavor thanks to the fresh vegetable broth. The final touch of 30-month aged Parmigiano Reggiano adds a savory and distinctive character.

Ingredients

  • Baby pasta (small grains)20 g
  • Carrot50 g
  • Celery20 g
  • Potato60 g
  • Parmigiano Reggiano PDO aged 30 months5 g
  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil5 g
  • Mineral water350 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Celery

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the vegetables, peel the carrot and potato, and remove the outer strings from the celery.
  2. Roughly chop the vegetables and place them in a small pot with 350 ml of cold water.
  3. Bring to a boil and simmer for about 25 minutes until the water volume is reduced to approximately 200 ml.
  4. Strain the broth through a fine-mesh sieve, lightly pressing the vegetables to extract their juice (without pureeing them entirely).
  5. Bring 150-180 ml of the filtered broth back to a boil in a clean saucepan.
  6. Add the baby pasta and cook for the time indicated on the packaging (usually 2-4 minutes), stirring with a small whisk to prevent clumping.
  7. Remove from heat and stir in the Parmigiano Reggiano and extra virgin olive oil, mixing well to emulsify.
  8. Allow to cool slightly and check the temperature before serving to the infant.

Tips

  • Cut vegetables into large pieces to easily strain them after boiling.
  • Lightly press the vegetables in the sieve to extract all the flavor into the filtered broth.
  • Cook the pasta directly in the broth so it better absorbs the taste of the preparation.

Variations

  • You can replace the micron pasta with a rice-grain shape to vary the texture.
  • Use only carrot and potato if you prefer to obtain a broth with a sweeter flavor.
