Homemade Vanilla Pudding

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A creamy dessert made with milk, egg yolks and flour, without using ready-made mixes.

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

A simple recipe that evokes childhood flavors, featuring a thick and velvety consistency. The use of egg yolks and butter provides an enveloping richness, perfect for an authentic homemade snack.

Ingredients

  • Whole milk500 ml
  • Egg yolks60 g (3 yolks)
  • Caster sugar90 g
  • Plain flour35 g
  • Butter15 g
  • Vanilla extract5 g
  • Fine salt0.5 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs

Method

  1. Heat 400 ml of milk with the vanilla extract without letting it come to a boil.
  2. In a saucepan, whisk the egg yolks with the sugar.
  3. Add the sifted flour and salt and mix until you have a smooth cream.
  4. Loosen the mixture with the remaining cold milk to avoid lumps.
  5. Pour in the hot milk in a steady stream, stirring continuously.
  6. Place over a medium-low heat and cook, whisking constantly.
  7. When the pudding thickens and the first bubbles appear, cook for a further minute.
  8. Remove from the heat and stir in the butter.
  9. Pour into moulds or small bowls and cover with cling film directly on the surface.
  10. Allow to cool and refrigerate for at least 3 hours before serving.

Tips

  • Sift the flour carefully before adding it to the yolks to prevent annoying lumps from forming in the pudding.
  • Cook over low heat while stirring constantly with a whisk to achieve a perfectly smooth cream.
  • Cover the surface of the pudding with cling film in direct contact while cooling to prevent a skin from forming.

Variations

  • You can garnish the finished pudding with fresh berries to add a touch of freshness and acidity.
  • Place crushed dry biscuits at the bottom of the bowl before pouring the cream to create a crunchy base.
