Lentil and Button Mushroom Meatballs

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A plant-based alternative to classic meatballs, these are affordable and rich in 'umami' flavour thanks to the mushrooms. Perfect served with a simple tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Tinned cooked lentils400 g
  • Button mushrooms300 g
  • Breadcrumbs80 g
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Egg1 pcs
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Dried thyme1 tsp

Allergens

Eggs, Gluten

Method

  1. Clean the mushrooms and cut them into small pieces, then sauté them in a pan with garlic and a drizzle of oil for 10 minutes until they release their water.
  2. Drain the lentils well and rinse them under running water, letting them drip for a few minutes.
  3. In a food processor, roughly blend half of the lentils and the cooked mushrooms to create a binding base that isn't too smooth.
  4. Transfer the mixture to a bowl, add the remaining whole lentils, the egg, thyme, and the breadcrumbs a little at a time.
  5. Knead by hand until you reach a mouldable consistency; if it's too wet, add more breadcrumbs.
  6. Form balls the size of a walnut (about 20-24 pieces) and roll them lightly in the remaining breadcrumbs.
  7. Place the meatballs on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, brush them with a little oil and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  8. Turn the meatballs halfway through cooking to ensure even browning on all sides.
