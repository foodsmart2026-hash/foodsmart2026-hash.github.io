Fluffy Soufflé Omelette with Mild Cheese and Courgette

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

An incredibly soft omelette enriched with ricotta which makes it similar to a soufflé, perfect for children from 1 to 3 years old. The grated courgettes melt into the egg for a texture that is easy to manage.

Ingredients

  • Medium eggs3 pcs
  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta [A mild, creamy Italian whey cheese]80 g
  • Courgettes150 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 ml

Allergens

Eggs, Milk

Method

  1. Wash the courgette and grate it finely; squeeze it slightly between your hands to remove excess water.
  2. In a bowl, whisk the eggs vigorously with a hand whisk until they become slightly frothy.
  3. Add the ricotta to the eggs and continue to mix until there are no large lumps of cheese left.
  4. Fold the grated courgettes into the egg and ricotta mixture, distributing them evenly.
  5. Heat a non-stick frying pan with a drizzle of oil, then pour in the mixture, levelling it with a spoon.
  6. Immediately cover with a lid and cook over a very low heat for 5 minutes; the steam will help the omelette puff up.
  7. Carefully flip the omelette when the surface appears set and cook for another 2 minutes on the other side.
  8. Cut the omelette into cubes of about 2 cm, ideal for the child to pick up with their hands or a small fork.
