Rice with Vegetables, Eggs and Legumes

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

A balanced and complete one-dish meal that combines wholemeal rice fibre with high-quality egg protein and plant-based chickpea protein. An affordable, colourful preparation perfect for managing the family's weekly menu.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro👍 Low price🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

A hearty dish that evokes home-cooked flavors with a modern twist. The brown rice provides a pleasantly firm texture, enriched by the softness of eggs and the sweetness of sautéed vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Wholemeal rice280 g
  • Cooked chickpeas (drained)240 g
  • Medium eggs4 pz
  • Fresh courgettes300 g
  • Carrots200 g
  • Golden onion80 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Fine salt5 g
  • Black pepper2 g

Allergens

Eggs

Method

  1. Begin by thoroughly rinsing 280g of brown rice under cold running water to remove excess starch. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and cook the rice for approximately 30-35 minutes over medium heat, ensuring it remains 'al dente'—firm to the bite but cooked through. The result should be distinct grains that are tender yet resilient. Chef's tip: drain the rice two minutes before the package instructions suggest, as it will continue to cook slightly during the final stir-fry stage.
  2. While the rice is boiling, focus on preparing the vegetables to ensure a professional finish. Wash 300g of courgettes (zucchini) and 200g of carrots, then cut them into tiny, uniform cubes of about 5mm, a technique known as 'brunoise'. This precision cut ensures even cooking and keeps the vegetables vibrant and crisp. Chef's tip: use a sharp chef's knife to slice rather than crush the vegetables, which preserves their structure and prevents them from becoming soggy.
  3. Select a large non-stick frying pan or, ideally, a wok to allow for easy movement of the ingredients. Heat 20ml of extra virgin olive oil (half the total amount) and add 80g of finely chopped onion. Sweat the onion over very low heat for about 4 minutes until it becomes translucent and sweet, being careful not to let it brown. Chef's tip: if the onion starts to dry out, add a tablespoon of the rice cooking water to soften it without burning.
  4. Slightly increase the heat to medium and add the carrot cubes to the pan. Cook for 3 minutes, stirring frequently with a wooden spoon, allowing the heat to penetrate the core of the carrots, which are denser than the courgettes. You are looking for a carrot that is slightly softened but still has a 'snap'. Chef's tip: do not cover the pan with a lid during this stage; this prevents steaming and encourages a subtle caramelisation of the natural sugars.
  5. Now add the 300g of diced courgettes and continue cooking for a further 5 minutes, sautéing over high heat. The courgettes should look bright and just barely golden on the outside while maintaining a firm texture. The goal is a colourful and fragrant vegetable base. Chef's tip: keep the pan moving constantly to ensure the courgettes cook evenly and don't stick or release too much water.
  6. Drain 240g of chickpeas, rinse them under cold water to remove any canning liquid or foam, and add them to the vegetables in the pan. Let them warm through for about 2 minutes with a pinch of the 5g of salt provided, allowing the flavours to meld perfectly. The chickpeas should be hot and lightly coated in the onion-infused oil. Chef's tip: if you prefer a deeper flavour, toast the chickpeas in a clear corner of the pan for a minute before mixing them with the rest.
  7. In a small bowl, lightly whisk the 4 medium eggs with 2g of black pepper and the remaining salt just until the yolks break. Push the vegetable and chickpea mixture to the edges of the pan to create a space in the centre, then pour in the egg mixture. Stir vigorously with a spatula in the centre until the eggs begin to set into soft, irregular ribbons. Chef's tip: avoid overcooking the eggs at this stage; they should remain slightly moist as they will finish firming up with the residual heat of the rice.
  8. Drain the cooked brown rice and add it directly into the pan over the vegetables, chickpeas, and eggs. Raise the heat to high and drizzle over the remaining 20ml of extra virgin olive oil. Sauté everything for 2-3 minutes, tossing or stirring from the bottom up to evenly distribute the colours and flavours among the separated rice grains. Chef's tip: this high-heat 'finishing' step is crucial for achieving a glossy appearance and a satisfying, non-sticky texture.
  9. Turn off the heat and allow the dish to rest for a minute before serving, performing a final taste check for seasoning. Serve the rice warm or at room temperature in wide bowls, ensuring each portion has a balanced mix of eggs and vegetables. The final result should be a harmonious dish where every grain of rice is well-coated and distinct. Chef's tip: for a gourmet touch, use a food ring or ramekin to mould the rice into a neat shape in the centre of the plate.

Tips

  • Use a wok or a large non-stick skillet to sauté the ingredients quickly over high heat.
  • Rinse the brown rice thoroughly before cooking to remove impurities and improve the grain texture.
  • You can prepare the vegetables ahead of time and combine them with the rice and eggs just before serving.

Variations

  • Add a pinch of turmeric to the rice cooking water for a spiced note and a golden color.
  • Swap the chickpeas for sweet peas for a softer and more delicate version.
