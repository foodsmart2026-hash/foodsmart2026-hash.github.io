Vegan Apple Cake

8 servings · 60 min · Easy

A traditional apple cake, made without eggs.

This vegan apple cake is a home-baking classic, soft and fragrant thanks to the presence of cinnamon and lemon. This version without animal products maintains all the typical softness of the traditional dessert, ideal for a snack.

Ingredients

  • Apples (Golden Delicious or Reinette)600 g
  • All-purpose flour250 g
  • Brown sugar150 g
  • Unsweetened soy milk200 ml
  • Sunflower oil80 ml
  • Baking powder16 g
  • Organic lemon (zest and juice)1 piece
  • Fine salt2 g
  • Ground cinnamon2 g

Allergens

Gluten, Soy

Method

  1. Peel the apples, dice two of them and slice the third one thinly, tossing them with lemon juice to prevent browning.
  2. In a large bowl, sift the flour and baking powder together, then stir in the brown sugar, salt, and grated lemon zest.
  3. In a separate container, create an emulsion by whisking the soy milk and sunflower oil together.
  4. Gradually pour the liquid mixture into the dry ingredients, whisking by hand until the batter is smooth and lump-free.
  5. Fold the diced apples and cinnamon into the batter using a spatula with gentle bottom-to-top movements.
  6. Grease and flour a 22-24 cm cake pan and pour the mixture in, leveling it evenly.
  7. Decorate the surface by arranging the apple slices in a radial pattern, pressing them slightly into the batter.
  8. Bake in a preheated static oven at 180°C (350°F) for about 45-50 minutes, checking doneness with a toothpick in the center.
  9. Remove from the oven and let the cake cool on a wire rack for at least 20 minutes before removing from the pan.

Tips

  • Choose Golden or Renetta apples because their texture holds up well during baking and releases the right moisture.
  • Sift the flour and baking powder well to prevent lumps and achieve an even rise.
  • Allow the cake to cool completely before removing it from the mold to prevent it from breaking due to its extreme softness.

Variations

  • Add chopped walnuts to the dough to introduce a crunchy note that contrasts with the tenderness of the apples.
  • You can brush the surface with a thin layer of maple syrup right out of the oven for a shiny finish.
