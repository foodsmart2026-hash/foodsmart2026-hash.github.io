Ditalini in Potato Broth with Silky Eggs

💰 Save Euro · about €0.51 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A thick and comforting soup, typical of winter evenings, where eggs are added at the end of cooking to create a rich texture. It is very affordable and a favourite with children.

Ingredients

  • Ditalini pasta (small, short tube-shaped pasta)250 g
  • Potatoes400 g
  • Eggs2 pcs
  • White onion1 pc
  • Fresh parsley1 bunch

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into very small cubes (about 1 cm) so they cook quickly and release their starch.
  2. Chop the onion and let it sauté gently in a pot with a drizzle of oil until softened without browning.
  3. Add the potatoes to the onion, cover with about 1.5 litres of hot water and bring to the boil, cooking for 15 minutes until the potatoes start to break apart.
  4. Roughly mash some of the potatoes with a fork against the side of the pot to make the broth thicker and creamier.
  5. Add the ditalini directly into the potato broth and cook for the time indicated on the pasta packaging.
  6. In a small bowl, lightly whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt, pepper and the finely chopped parsley.
  7. When the pasta is cooked, turn off the heat and pour the eggs in a thin stream into the boiling soup, stirring quickly with a whisk or a fork.
  8. The egg will create small soft flakes; let it rest for a minute before serving this thick, steaming soup.
