Creamy Ditalini with Baby Peas and Fresh Ricotta

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A delicate pasta dish where the peas are blended for a velvety consistency suitable for little ones. The ricotta adds creaminess without the need for heavy fats.

Ingredients

  • Ditalini pasta (small tube-shaped pasta)160 g
  • Frozen baby peas200 g
  • Cow's milk ricotta100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano (aged Italian hard cheese)20 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Bring a pot of water to the boil without adding salt for the children's version.
  2. Cook the peas in the boiling water for about 8-10 minutes until they are very tender to the touch.
  3. Drain the peas, keeping the cooking water, and blend them with a hand blender, adding a tablespoon of water until you get a smooth cream.
  4. In the same water, cook the ditalini for the time indicated on the packet, preferring a soft texture to make chewing easier.
  5. In a small bowl, loosen the ricotta with a tablespoon of the pasta cooking water until it becomes a smooth cream.
  6. Drain the pasta and return it to the pot with the heat off.
  7. Combine the pea cream and the ricotta with the pasta, stirring vigorously to distribute the sauce.
  8. Add the raw extra virgin olive oil and the grated Parmigiano to season naturally.
  9. Serve warm, ensuring the consistency is moist and easy to swallow.
