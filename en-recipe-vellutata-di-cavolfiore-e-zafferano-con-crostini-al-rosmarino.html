Creamy Cauliflower and Saffron Soup with Rosemary Croutons

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Easy

A comforting and refined starter that enhances the sweetness of cauliflower with a silky texture and the fragrance of saffron.

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower800g
  • Waxy potatoes200g
  • Saffron strands or powder1 sachet
  • Shallot1
  • Vegetable stock1 litre
  • Rustic bread3 slices
  • Fresh rosemary1 sprig
  • Extra virgin olive oil3 tbsp
  • Salt and black pepperto taste

Allergens

Gluten, Celery

Method

  1. Clean the cauliflower and cut it into small florets; peel the potatoes and cut them into roughly 2cm cubes.
  2. Finely slice the shallot and soften it in a large pot with two tablespoons of oil over a low heat.
  3. Add the cauliflower and potatoes, allowing them to sauté for 3-4 minutes while stirring frequently to absorb the flavours.
  4. Cover the vegetables with the boiling vegetable stock and bring to a boil.
  5. Lower the heat and simmer for about 20-25 minutes, until the vegetables are very tender.
  6. Meanwhile, cut the bread into cubes and sauté in a pan with a drizzle of oil and the chopped rosemary until golden and crunchy.
  7. Dissolve the saffron in a ladleful of hot stock taken from the pot and stir it into the vegetables.
  8. Use a stick blender to process the mixture into a smooth, silky cream.
  9. Adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper and, if necessary, add a little more stock to achieve your desired consistency.
  10. Serve the soup piping hot in shallow bowls, finishing with the rosemary croutons and a final drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.
