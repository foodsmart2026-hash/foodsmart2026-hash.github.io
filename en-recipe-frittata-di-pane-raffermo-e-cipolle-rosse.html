Stale Bread and Red Onion Frittata

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A traditional European peasant 'recovery' recipe that avoids food waste. The bread absorbs the egg, becoming soft, almost like a savoury cake.

Ingredients

  • Stale bread200 g
  • Eggs6 pcs
  • Red onions2 pcs
  • Milk100 ml
  • Fresh parsley1 bunch
  • Sunflower oil30 ml
  • Salt and pepperto taste

Allergens

Eggs, Milk, Gluten

Method

  1. Cut the bread into small cubes and soak it in the milk for about 15 minutes until completely softened.
  2. Slice the onions into thin rings and sauté them in a non-stick frying pan with half the oil and a tablespoon of water for 10 minutes over a gentle heat.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs with salt, pepper, and the finely chopped parsley.
  4. Lightly squeeze the excess milk from the bread and add it to the eggs, mixing well to incorporate.
  5. Add the softened onions to the egg and bread mixture and stir everything together.
  6. Heat the remaining oil in the pan, pour in the mixture and spread it evenly with a spatula.
  7. Cook with the lid on for about 8 minutes over a medium-low heat; the frittata is ready to flip when the edges are firm and the base is golden.
  8. Flip the frittata using a plate and cook the other side for another 4-5 minutes without a lid to make it crispy.
