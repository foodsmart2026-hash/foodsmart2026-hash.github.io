'Sandy' Roast Potatoes with Sweet Paprika

💰 Save Euro · about €0.53 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A rustic and tempting version of roast potatoes, made crunchy by a quick coating that creates a savoury 'sand'. The paprika gives an amber colour and an enveloping scent.

Ingredients

  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes800 g
  • Breadcrumbs40 g
  • Sweet paprika10 g
  • Fresh rosemary2 sprigs
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Salt and pepperq.b.

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes, wash them, and cut them into regular 2 cm cubes to ensure they all cook at the same time.
  2. Blanch the cubes in boiling salted water for just 5 minutes: this step allows for a soft interior and a perfect outer crust.
  3. Drain the potatoes and let them cool slightly in a large bowl to remove excess moisture which would prevent crunchiness.
  4. In a small bowl, mix the breadcrumbs with the sweet paprika, pepper, and finely chopped rosemary needles.
  5. Drizzle the potatoes with the extra virgin olive oil, stirring well to coat every piece, then add the breadcrumb and spice mix.
  6. Shake the bowl vigorously so that the 'sand' adheres perfectly to all sides of the potato cubes.
  7. Distribute the potatoes on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, avoiding overlapping to allow the hot air to circulate.
  8. Bake at 200°C (fan oven) for about 20-25 minutes, turning them halfway through, until they are golden and visibly crunchy.
