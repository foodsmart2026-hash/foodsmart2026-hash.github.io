crispy fried artichokes

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

🌿 Seasonal recipe🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • artichokes8 count
  • 00 flour100 g
  • eggs2 count
  • breadcrumbs100 g
  • lemon1 count
  • Oil for fryingas needed
  • Saltas needed

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs
May contain traces of: Milk, Mustard, Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Clean the artichokes by removing the tough outer leaves and the tips.
  2. Cut them into wedges that are not too thick and soak them in lemon water.
  3. Dry them thoroughly with a clean kitchen towel.
  4. Prepare three plates: one with flour, one with beaten eggs, and one with breadcrumbs.
  5. Dredge each wedge first in the flour, then in the egg, and finally in the breadcrumbs, pressing lightly to make the breading adhere.
  6. Heat plenty of oil in a pan and bring it to about 170 °C.
  7. Fry a few artichokes at a time, turning them until they reach an even golden brown.
  8. Drain them on paper towels and add salt only just before serving them.

Chef's tips

Tips and alternatives:

- Dry the artichokes well to avoid oil splatters.

- Do not overcrowd the pan: the oil temperature must remain stable.

- You can add parmesan and parsley to the breadcrumbs.

- For a lighter version, bake them in the oven at 200 °C with a drizzle of oil.
