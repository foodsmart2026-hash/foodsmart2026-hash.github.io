Choux pastry

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 35 min · Medium

🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • water125 ml
  • milk125 ml
  • butter100 g
  • all-purpose flour150 g
  • medium eggs4
  • salt3 g
  • sugar5 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Mustard, Soy.

Method

  1. Pour water, milk, butter, salt, and sugar into a saucepan and bring to a boil.
  2. Momentarily remove from heat and pour in all the flour at once.
  3. Mix vigorously with a wooden spoon until you obtain a compact mixture that pulls away from the sides.
  4. Return to low heat and cook for about 1–2 minutes, stirring continuously to slightly dry the dough.
  5. Transfer to a bowl and let cool slightly for a few minutes.
  6. Incorporate the eggs one at a time, waiting for each to be absorbed before adding the next. The final consistency should be smooth, shiny, and capable of maintaining its shape.
  7. Transfer to a piping bag and form small mounds on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  8. Bake in a preheated static oven at 200 °C for 10 minutes, then lower to 180 °C and continue for about 20–25 minutes. Do not open the oven during baking.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes, and alternatives:

- Do not add all the eggs at once: they could make the dough too runny.

- Do not open the oven too early: the cream puffs could deflate.

- For a regular surface, gently flatten any peaks with a damp finger.

- Choux pastry is suitable for cream puffs, éclairs, and zeppole.
