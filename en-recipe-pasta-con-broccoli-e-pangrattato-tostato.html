Pasta with Broccoli and Toasted Breadcrumbs

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A balanced main course that revisits Italian 'cucina povera' (peasant cooking) by adding a protein base of blended legumes for an irresistible creaminess. It is an economical and nutritious recipe, perfect for getting the whole family to eat vegetables thanks to the contrast between the cream and the crunchy crumbs.

Ingredients

  • Wholemeal durum wheat pasta (such as fusilli or orecchiette)320 g
  • Fresh broccoli600 g
  • Pre-cooked cannellini beans (rinsed)200 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 g
  • Coarse breadcrumbs40 g
  • Fresh garlic1 clove
  • Dried chilli flakes1 g
  • Unrefined sea salt5 g
  • Black pepper1 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Begin by cleaning the broccoli: separate the florets and peel the central stalk, cutting it into small cubes as it is rich in fibre and flavour. Wash thoroughly under running water.
  2. Bring a large pot with about 3 litres of water to the boil and add the salt. Drop in the broccoli and cook for about 5-6 minutes: they should be tender when pierced with a fork but still bright green.
  3. While the broccoli is cooking, heat a small non-stick pan with 5g of oil and add the breadcrumbs. Toast over a medium heat, stirring constantly for 3 minutes until golden and fragrant, taking care not to burn them.
  4. Remove about half of the broccoli from the pot with a slotted spoon and place in a blender with the rinsed cannellini beans and a ladle of the cooking water. Blend until you get a smooth, thick cream to toss with the pasta.
  5. In the same water where the remaining broccoli is boiling, add the wholemeal pasta. This step allows the pasta to absorb the nutrients and essential oils of the vegetables left in the water.
  6. In a large pan, heat the rest of the extra virgin olive oil (35g) with the crushed garlic clove and the chilli. Sauté over a very low heat for 2 minutes to flavour the oil without exceeding the smoke point.
  7. Add the remaining whole broccoli florets to the pan and sauté for 2-3 minutes to season them. Remove the garlic once it is lightly golden.
  8. Drain the pasta al dente (about 2 minutes before the time indicated on the packet), reserving some of the water. Pour it into the pan with the whole broccoli and add the previously prepared cannellini and broccoli cream.
  9. Toss everything over a high heat for 1-2 minutes, adding a splash of cooking water if necessary, until the cream perfectly coats the pasta. Turn off the heat, plate up, and top with the crunchy toasted breadcrumbs and a grind of fresh pepper.
