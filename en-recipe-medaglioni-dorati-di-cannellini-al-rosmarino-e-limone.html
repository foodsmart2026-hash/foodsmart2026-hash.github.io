Golden Cannellini Bean Medallions with Rosemary and Lemon

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A nutritious and crunchy main course that transforms simple beans into aromatic burgers. Ideal for a quick dinner, it utilises the natural creaminess of pulses without the need for eggs or soy products.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Canned cannellini beans (small white beans, drained)480 g
  • Fresh spinach leaves250 g
  • Cornflour30 g
  • Gluten-free breadcrumbs60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Unwaxed lemon zest1 pcs
  • Fresh rosemary1 rametto
  • Fine salt4 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Rinse the cannellini beans under running water to remove the canning liquid and excess sodium, then leave them to drain well for at least 2 minutes so the mixture isn't too wet.
  2. Roughly chop the fresh spinach and sauté them in a very hot non-stick pan without oil for about 3 minutes; this step allows the moisture to evaporate while keeping the green colour vibrant.
  3. Squeeze the cooked spinach firmly in a sieve or between your hands to remove any remaining liquid, a critical step to prevent the medallions from falling apart during cooking.
  4. In a large bowl, mash the cannellini beans with a fork until you have a coarse paste; leave a few beans whole to give a more rustic and pleasant texture.
  5. Add the squeezed spinach, grated lemon zest, finely chopped rosemary, and cornflour to the bean mixture, which will act as a natural binder, perfectly replacing the egg.
  6. Mix the mixture with your hands and form 8 medallions about 2 cm thick, compacting them well between your palms to remove any internal air bubbles.
  7. Coat each medallion in the gluten-free breadcrumbs, pressing lightly on the surface so the breading adheres evenly to both sides and along the edges.
  8. Heat the extra virgin olive oil in a large pan and place the medallions inside, cooking them over medium heat for 4 minutes per side until the surface is brown and crunchy to the touch of the spatula.
  9. Drain briefly on kitchen paper to remove excess grease and serve immediately to enjoy the contrast between the soft heart and the crunchy crust.
