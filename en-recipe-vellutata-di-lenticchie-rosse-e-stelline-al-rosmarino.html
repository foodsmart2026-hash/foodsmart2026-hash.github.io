Red Lentil Velouté and Stelline Pasta with Rosemary

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.31 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Red split lentils melt during cooking, eliminating the risk of annoying skins and providing iron and protein. A nutritious and colourful one-pot meal.

Ingredients

  • Split red lentils120 g
  • Stelline pasta (small star-shaped pasta for soup)100 g
  • Carrot1 pc
  • Small potato1 pc
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Fresh rosemary1 sprig

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Peel the carrot and potato and cut them into very small cubes.
  2. Rinse the red lentils well under running water to remove any impurities.
  3. Place the lentils, carrot, and potato in a pot with 600 ml of water and the whole sprig of rosemary.
  4. Bring to the boil and cook for about 20 minutes; the lentils should begin to fall apart.
  5. Remove the rosemary and blend everything with a mixer until you get a smooth, thick cream.
  6. Bring the velouté back to the boil and pour the stelline pasta directly into it.
  7. Cook the small pasta directly in the lentil cream for the required time, adding water if it becomes too thick.
  8. When the pasta is tender, turn off the heat and add the raw olive oil.
  9. Serve in small bowls, making sure the temperature is ideal for the child.
