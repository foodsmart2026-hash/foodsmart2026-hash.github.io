Courgette Risotto

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

Creamy risotto with courgettes in two textures, scented with basil.

A creamy and light first course that highlights the delicate flavor of seasonal zucchini. The double texture of cubed and grated vegetables coats each grain of Carnaroli rice for a velvety finish. Fresh basil added at the end provides an unmistakable aromatic note.

Ingredients

  • Carnaroli rice320 g
  • Courgettes500 g
  • Butter50 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano PDO60 g
  • Dry white wine80 ml
  • Hot water1300 ml
  • Fresh basil6 g
  • Fine salt8 g
  • Ground black pepper0.5 g

Allergens

Milk, Sulphites

Method

  1. Bring the water to a gentle boil with 6 g of salt and keep it hot.
  2. Wash the courgettes: cut half into small cubes and grate the other half.
  3. Heat the oil in a saucepan and cook the diced courgettes for 5 minutes, then set aside half for garnish.
  4. Add the grated courgettes and let them soften for 2 minutes.
  5. Add the rice and toast for 2 minutes while stirring.
  6. Deglaze with the white wine and allow it to evaporate.
  7. Add the hot water a ladleful at a time, stirring often, for about 17 minutes.
  8. Season with salt and pepper at the end of cooking.
  9. Turn off the heat and stir in the butter, Parmesan, and torn basil.
  10. Serve, garnishing with the reserved diced courgettes.

Tips

  • Stir in the butter while it is very cold and the heat is off for a superior creamy texture.
  • Toast the rice thoroughly until the grains are translucent to ensure it stays perfectly al dente.
  • Use hot, lightly salted water for cooking if you want to preserve the vibrant green color of the zucchini.

Variations

  • Add some grated lemon zest during the final mixing stage for a fresh, citrusy touch.
  • Mix in some shredded zucchini flowers during the last few minutes of cooking to enhance the flavor.
