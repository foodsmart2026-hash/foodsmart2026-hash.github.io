Mini Yogurt and Apple Loaves

Kid-friendly

12 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 22 min · Easy

Soft little cakes made with yogurt and grated apple, low in sugar. Note: contains eggs, milk and gluten; serve warm.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour180 g
  • Full-fat plain yogurt125 g
  • Eggs100 g
  • Apple150 g
  • Sugar40 g
  • Sunflower oil50 ml
  • Baking powder8 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and prepare 12 mini loaf cases.
  2. Whisk the eggs with the sugar for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the yogurt and oil and stir to combine.
  4. Fold in the sifted flour and baking powder, followed by the finely grated apple.
  5. Fill the cases two-thirds full.
  6. Bake for 20-22 minutes until a skewer comes out clean; allow to cool before serving.
