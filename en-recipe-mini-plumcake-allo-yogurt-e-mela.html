Mini Yogurt and Apple Loaves

Kid-friendly

12 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 22 min · Easy

Soft little cakes made with yogurt and grated apple, low in sugar. Note: contains eggs, milk and gluten; serve warm.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour180 g
  • Full-fat plain yogurt125 g
  • Eggs100 g
  • Apple150 g
  • Sugar40 g
  • Sunflower oil50 ml
  • Baking powder8 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Soy.

Method

  1. Preheat your static oven to 180°C (350°F) and prepare a 12-slot mini plumcake tin by lining it with paper liners or greasing them lightly. This step is essential so that the batter can go straight into the oven once ready, keeping its leavening power intact.
  2. In a large bowl, whip 100g of whole eggs with 40g of sugar using an electric whisk for about 3 minutes at high speed. You should achieve a pale, voluminous, and almost frothy mixture; this step incorporates air to make the cakes fluffy despite the low sugar content.
  1. Slowly pour in 50ml of sunflower oil and 125g of plain whole yogurt while continuing to mix with a hand whisk or at low speed. The oil ensures long-lasting softness, while the whole yogurt provides that creamy and fragrant note typical of homemade cakes.
  2. Take 150g of apple, peel it, and grate it finely using a medium-hole grater directly into the liquid mixture. Gently fold in the pulp with a spoon: the grated apple will almost completely melt during baking, providing incredible natural moisture to every single bite.
  3. Carefully sift 180g of all-purpose flour together with 8g of baking powder and add them to the mixture in two or three batches. Mix with folding motions from bottom to top using a spatula until no traces of flour remain; you will get a thick, fragrant, and lump-free batter.
  4. Distribute the batter into the 12 previously prepared liners using a spoon, filling them about two-thirds of the way. Do not overfill, otherwise the batter might overflow during baking, losing its elegant shape.
  5. Bake the mini plumcakes for exactly 22 minutes on the middle rack, monitoring the color until it turns a beautiful, warm, and inviting golden brown. Before removing from the oven, insert a toothpick into the center of a cake: if it comes out dry and clean, the internal cooking is perfectly done.
  6. Remove the cakes from the oven and let them rest for 5 minutes in the tin, then carefully transfer them to a wire rack to prevent moisture from building up at the bottom. Serve them while still warm to best appreciate the sweet apple fragrance and the cloud-like texture of the yogurt.
