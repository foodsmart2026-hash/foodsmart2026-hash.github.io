Coconut and Turmeric Chicken

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A main course inspired by Southeast Asian flavours, creamy and delicate. Using canned coconut milk ensures a velvety consistency and an enveloping taste.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Canned coconut milk400 ml
  • Courgettes2 pcs
  • Turmeric powder15 g
  • Fresh ginger20 g
  • Vegetable oil20 ml
  • Saltq.b.

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Cut the chicken breast into 3 cm chunks and lightly dust them with flour (optional) to help the sauce thicken.
  2. Slice the courgettes into thin half-moons and grate the fresh ginger after peeling it.
  3. In a non-stick frying pan, heat the oil and brown the chicken over high heat for 5 minutes until a golden crust forms.
  4. Add the courgettes and ginger, stir-frying everything for another 3 minutes to keep the vegetables bright.
  5. Dust with turmeric and pour in the coconut milk, stirring well to dissolve the intense yellow colour throughout the liquid.
  6. Reduce the heat and let it simmer for 10 minutes, until the coconut milk has reduced by half and become creamy.
  7. The dish is ready when the chicken is tender and the sauce coats the back of a spoon; adjust the salt only at the end.
