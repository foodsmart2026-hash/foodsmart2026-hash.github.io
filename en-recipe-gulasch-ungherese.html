Hungarian Goulash

Hungary · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 120 min · Medium

A slow-cooked beef stew with onions and plenty of sweet paprika.

Ingredients

  • Beef chuck800 g
  • Onions400 g
  • Hungarian sweet paprika20 g
  • Tomato purée30 g
  • Red pepper150 g
  • Caraway seeds2 g
  • Garlic6 g
  • Lard30 g
  • Water700 ml
  • Fine salt8 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Celery, Mustard.

Method

  1. Cut the meat into 3 cm cubes and thinly slice the onions.
  2. Soften the onions in the lard over a low heat for 15 minutes.
  3. Remove from the heat, add the paprika and stir (it must not burn).
  4. Add the meat, garlic, caraway seeds and tomato purée, and brown for 5 minutes.
  5. Add the chopped pepper and the hot water, season with salt and cook, covered, over a low heat for 1 hour and 45 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with boiled potatoes or bread.
