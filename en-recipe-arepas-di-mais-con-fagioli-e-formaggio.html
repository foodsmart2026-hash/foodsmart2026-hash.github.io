Corn Arepas with Beans and Cheese

💰 Save Euro · about €0.65 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

Typical Venezuelan and Colombian corn flatbreads, naturally gluten-free and versatile. Here they are served with a budget-friendly filling of beans and melty cheese.

Ingredients

  • Pre-cooked cornmeal (type P.A.N.)300 g
  • Warm water450 ml
  • Pre-cooked black or kidney beans240 g
  • Edam-style or Mozzarella cheese150 g
  • Salt5 g
  • Vegetable oil20 ml

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. In a bowl, combine the warm water and salt; gradually add the cornmeal while mixing with your hands to avoid lumps.
  2. Knead for 2 minutes until the mixture is smooth and no longer sticks to your hands; let it rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Divide the dough into 8 balls and flatten them to form discs about 1.5 cm thick.
  4. Heat a lightly greased griddle or frying pan and cook the arepas for 5-7 minutes per side until a golden crust forms.
  5. While the arepas are cooking, heat the beans in a small saucepan, mashing some of them with a fork to create a paste.
  6. Slice the arepas in half horizontally (without separating them completely) and fill them with the bean paste and sliced cheese.
  7. Place the filled arepas back on the hot griddle for 2 minutes to melt the cheese and serve immediately.
