Malloreddus alla campidanese

Sardegna · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 40 min · Easy

Sardinian gnocchetti with saffron, sausage ragù and Sardinian pecorino cheese.

🇮🇹 Italian

Sardinian gnocchetti are elegantly dressed in a rich sausage ragù flavored with aromatic saffron. This savory first course, typical of the Campidano area, features a contrast between firm pasta and an enveloping sauce.

Ingredients

  • Malloreddus (Sardinian gnocchetti)360 g
  • Fresh sausage250 g
  • Tomato passata500 ml
  • Onion50 g
  • Ground saffron0.2 g
  • Grated Sardinian pecorino50 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Fine salt4 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk
May contain traces of: Celery, Mustard.

Method

  1. Start by finely chopping 50g of onion. In a large pan, heat 20ml of extra virgin olive oil over low heat and let the onion wither for about 4 minutes until it becomes translucent and tender, without burning it. This step is essential to create a sweet aromatic base that will balance the strong flavor of the sausage.
  2. Take 250g of fresh sausage, remove the casing, and crumble it coarsely with your hands or using a fork. Add it to the pan with the onion and slightly increase the heat to medium intensity to begin browning.
  3. Let the sausage cook for about 6 minutes, breaking it up well with a wooden spoon until it has changed color, becoming uniformly golden. You will notice the fat starting to render, releasing an intense and inviting aroma, a sign that the meat is ready for the tomato.
  4. Pour 500ml of tomato puree into the pan and stir well to blend the flavors. Dissolve 0.2g of saffron powder in a tablespoon of warm water and add it to the sauce; this precious spice will provide the characteristic golden color and an unmistakable aroma typical of Sardinian tradition.
  5. Add a portion of the 4g of fine salt, cover with a lid, and let the sauce simmer over very low heat for about 30 minutes. The sauce should reduce slowly, becoming thick and full-bodied, with a deep red color enriched by the yellow reflections of the saffron.
  6. While the sauce finishes cooking, bring a large pot of water to a boil and add the remaining salt. Pour in 360g of malloreddus and cook them according to the times indicated on the package, remembering to taste them often to drain them perfectly al dente.
  7. Before draining the gnocchetti, set aside a ladle of their starch-rich cooking water. Drain the malloreddus and pour them directly into the pan with the sausage sauce, sautéing them over high heat for a couple of minutes so the pasta perfectly absorbs the dressing.
  8. Turn off the heat and add 50g of grated Sardinian pecorino cheese, stirring vigorously to combine everything. The residual heat will melt the cheese, creating a delicious cream that will coat every single malloreddus, making the dish balanced and flavorful.

Tips

  • Carefully break apart the sausage with a wooden spoon while cooking to prevent large chunks from forming.
  • Dissolve the saffron in a tiny bit of hot water before adding it to the tomato sauce for even color distribution.
  • Let the sauce rest for one minute off the heat before mixing the pasta with the Sardinian pecorino cheese.

Variations

  • For a milder flavor, you can replace the Sardinian pecorino with highly aged Parmigiano Reggiano.
  • Add a final touch of freshness to the ragù by stirring in a few hand-torn fresh basil leaves.
