Pasta with Rocket and Almond Pesto

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A very quick main course with a bold and slightly peppery flavour. Rocket is cheap and available all year round, a great alternative to basil.

Ingredients

  • Mezze maniche (short, wide pasta tubes)320 g
  • Fresh rocket (a peppery salad leaf)100 g
  • Whole almonds (with or without skin)30 g
  • Grated Grana Padano (a savoury, crumbly hard cheese)40 g
  • Garlic1/2 spicchio
  • Extra virgin olive oil50 ml
  • Saltq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Tree nuts

Method

  1. Put the pasta water on to boil and add salt once boiling.
  2. Wash the rocket and dry it well using a salad spinner.
  3. In a mixer or mortar, place the almonds and garlic and begin to grind finely.
  4. Add the rocket in handfuls, the oil, and the grated cheese, blending in pulses so as not to heat the pesto.
  5. Adjust the salt and add a tablespoon of cold water if the pesto is too thick or lumpy.
  6. Cook the mezze maniche and, before draining, set aside a small cup of the cooking water.
  7. In a large bowl, mix the pesto with the cooking water to make it fluid.
  8. Pour the drained pasta into the bowl, mix well, and serve with some coarsely chopped almonds on top.
