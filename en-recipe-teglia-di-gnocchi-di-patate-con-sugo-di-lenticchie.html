Baked Potato Gnocchi with Lentil Ragù

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A hearty twist where lentils replace meat in the sauce, providing iron and fibre at a very low cost. A perfect comfort food for the cold months.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Potato gnocchi (fresh Italian potato dumplings)800 g
  • Dried lentils (small)150 g
  • Tomato passata (smooth sieved tomatoes)400 ml
  • Onion1 pcs
  • Carrot1 pcs
  • Scamorza or Provola cheese (semi-soft Italian cow's milk cheese)100 g

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils and boil them in unsalted water for about 20 minutes or until tender but still intact; drain and set aside.
  2. Prepare a classic 'soffritto' by finely chopping the onion and carrot, then let them soften with oil in a saucepan for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato passata and the cooked lentils to the pan, season with salt, and cook for 15 minutes to create a thick vegetable ragù.
  4. Drop the gnocchi into boiling salted water and drain them as soon as they rise to the surface, using a slotted spoon.
  5. Mix the gnocchi directly into the pot with the lentil ragù, ensuring each dumpling is well coated in the sauce.
  6. Transfer everything to an ovenproof baking dish and distribute the thinly sliced or grated scamorza over the surface.
  7. Place the dish under the oven grill at maximum power for 5 minutes, or until the cheese has melted and created an inviting crust.
  8. Serve immediately: the combination of the soft gnocchi and the texture of the lentils makes this a very satisfying dish.
