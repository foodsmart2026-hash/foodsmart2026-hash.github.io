Roasted Pumpkin Cubes with Rosemary and Honey

💰 Save Euro · about €0.56 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Roasted pumpkin gains a unique depth of flavour thanks to a touch of honey that enhances its natural sweetness. A simple yet visually striking autumnal side dish.

Ingredients

  • Pumpkin (Delica or Mantovana variety - typical Italian firm-fleshed pumpkins)900 g
  • Acacia honey15 g
  • Fresh rosemary3 sprigs
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Coarse sea salt5 g
  • Black pepperto taste

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the pumpkin (if the skin is very tough), remove the seeds and internal fibres, then cut it into regular 3 cm cubes.
  2. In a bowl, mix the olive oil with the slightly warmed honey to make it fluid and easy to distribute.
  3. Pour the dressing over the pumpkin and add the whole rosemary needles and a generous grind of black pepper.
  4. Arrange the pumpkin cubes on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, spacing them out so they don't steam against each other.
  5. Sprinkle with a pinch of coarse sea salt, which will draw moisture to the surface and help form a tasty crust.
  6. Bake at 200°C for 20-25 minutes; the pumpkin is ready when it is tender when pierced with a fork and the edges are caramelised.
  7. Check that the honey doesn't burn: if it darkens too quickly, lower the temperature by 10 degrees for the final few minutes.
  8. Serve warm, perfect as an accompaniment to goat's cheese or roasted white meats.
