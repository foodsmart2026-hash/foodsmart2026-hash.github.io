Hazelnut paste

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • shelled and peeled hazelnuts300 g

Allergens

Tree nuts
May contain traces of: Peanuts, Milk, Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Spread the hazelnuts on a baking tray lined with parchment paper, avoiding overlapping them.
  2. Toast them in a preheated static oven at 160 °C for about 10–12 minutes, checking that they do not darken excessively.
  3. Remove them from the oven and let them cool slightly.
  4. Transfer them to a powerful food processor and start blending with short pulses.
  5. During processing, the hazelnuts will go from a grainy texture to a compact paste, until they release their natural oil.
  6. Periodically stop the food processor to scrape the sides of the container and avoid overheating.
  7. Continue until you obtain a smooth and fluid cream, depending on the power of the appliance.
  8. Transfer to a clean jar and store in the refrigerator.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Do not add water: it would compromise the shelf life.

- Do not blend continuously without pauses: the food processor could overheat.

- You can prepare the same paste using almonds, pistachios or peanuts.

- This recipe produces pure paste, not a sweetened spread.

- Store in the refrigerator and use clean, dry utensils.
