Palak Paneer

India · 4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Creamy spiced spinach with cubes of Indian paneer cheese.

One of the most beloved vegetarian dishes in Indian cuisine, featuring paneer cheese cubes in a velvety spinach gravy. It is a spiced and comforting dish, perfect to be served with naan bread or basmati rice.

Ingredients

  • Fresh spinach600 g
  • Paneer250 g
  • Onion120 g
  • Tomato150 g
  • Fresh ginger10 g
  • Garlic8 g
  • Garam masala4 g
  • Cumin seeds2 g
  • Double cream50 ml
  • Vegetable oil30 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Milk
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Mustard.

Method

  1. Start by bringing water to a boil and blanch the 600 g of fresh spinach for just 2 minutes. Drain them immediately and plunge them into very cold water: this thermal shock is the secret to maintaining a brilliant green and preventing the leaves from darkening.
  2. Once cooled, lightly squeeze the spinach to remove excess water and blend them with a mixer until you get a smooth and velvety cream. Set aside this base which will provide the iconic consistency to your dish.
  3. In a large pan, heat the 30 ml of seed oil over medium heat and add the 2 g of cumin seeds. Let them sizzle for about a minute until they release their toasted aroma, being careful not to let them brown too much.
  4. Add the 120 g of finely chopped onion, the 10 g of grated fresh ginger, and the 8 g of minced garlic. Cook for 6 minutes over low heat, stirring often, until the onion becomes golden and translucent, creating an intense aromatic base.
  5. Add the 150 g of small diced tomatoes and the 4 g of garam masala, the spice blend that will give depth to the dish. Continue cooking for 5 minutes until the tomatoes have softened, blending perfectly with the sauté.
  6. Pour the spinach cream into the pan and add the 6 g of fine salt to balance the flavors. Let it cook for 5 minutes over medium-low heat, allowing the aromas to meld with the sweetness of the spinach until you get a homogeneous mixture.
  7. Cut the 250 g of paneer into cubes of about 2 centimeters and gently add them to the sauce. Stir carefully so as not to break the cheese, letting the cubes warm up and absorb the flavors of the base for about 2 minutes.
  8. Finally, pour in the 50 ml of heavy cream and heat everything for the last minute over very low heat. You will see the deep green become creamier and more inviting; serve your palak paneer immediately while hot to appreciate all its softness.

Tips

  • Dip the spinach in ice water after blanching to preserve a bright green color.
  • Toast the cumin seeds in hot oil to release their full pungent aroma.
  • Blend the onion and tomato base finely if you prefer an even smoother sauce.

Variations

  • You can lightly brown the paneer cubes in a pan before adding them to the cream.
  • If you can't find original paneer, you can try using firm tofu.
