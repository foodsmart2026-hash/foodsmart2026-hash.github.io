Red Onions Braised in Apple Cider Vinegar

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

An affordable side dish with an irresistible sweet and sour flavour. Perfect to accompany legumes, veggie burgers or white meats.

Ingredients

  • Large red onions600 g
  • Apple cider vinegar50 ml
  • Brown or white sugar1 tsp

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the red onions and cut them into slices about half a centimetre thick (not too thin to prevent them from falling apart).
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a non-stick frying pan and add the onions, spreading them out well.
  3. Soften them over a gentle heat for about 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally until they become translucent.
  4. Add the sugar and stir well for a minute to begin the natural caramelisation process.
  5. Deglaze with the apple cider vinegar, turning up the heat slightly to allow the acidity to mellow.
  6. Lower the heat again, add a pinch of salt and cover with a lid.
  7. Continue cooking for another 10-15 minutes, adding a tablespoon of water if the onions dry out too much.
  8. The onions are ready when they are very soft and coated in a shiny, fragrant glaze.
  9. Serve hot or cold; the flavour improves significantly after a few hours of resting.
