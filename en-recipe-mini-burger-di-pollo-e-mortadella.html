Mini chicken and mortadella burgers

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast4 pcs
  • Mortadella, 1 slice200 g
  • Eggs3 pcs
  • Breadcrumbs100 g
  • Parmesan cheese150 g
  • NutmegTo taste
  • Fine saltTo taste
  • Ground black pepperTo taste

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Dice the chicken, also cut the mortadella and place both ingredients in a mixer and blend.
  2. Place the mixture in a bowl and mix it with the parmesan cheese, nutmeg, salt, and pepper.
  3. Beat the eggs. Form the mixture into slightly flattened balls.
  4. Dip them in the egg, then in the breadcrumbs and fry in very hot oil. Serve immediately
