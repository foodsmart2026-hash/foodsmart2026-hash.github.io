Pasta with Lentils and Tomato

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A reimagining of peasant tradition that combines the creaminess of legumes with the freshness of Mediterranean tomatoes. This one-pot dish ensures a complete protein profile thanks to the pairing of whole grains and lentils.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Wholemeal pasta (ditalini or mixed shapes)320 g
  • Dried lentils (tiny or green variety)200 g
  • High-quality peeled plum tomatoes400 g
  • Yellow onion80 g
  • Carrot80 g
  • Celery (stalk)50 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Bay leaf1 leaf
  • Unrefined sea salt5 g
  • Hot water or vegetable stock1000 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Celery

Method

  1. Thoroughly rinse the dried lentils under cold running water to remove any debris or dust, draining them well in a fine-mesh sieve.
  2. Prepare a fine dice of onion, carrot, and celery, which will serve as the fundamental aromatic base to give depth of flavour without using animal fats.
  3. Heat 20 ml of extra virgin olive oil in a large, heavy-based pot and sauté the aromatic vegetables over a gentle heat for about 6-7 minutes until they are tender and translucent.
  4. Add the lentils and the bay leaf, letting them toast for 2 minutes with the vegetables to intensify their nutty flavour.
  5. Add the peeled tomatoes, previously crushed with a fork, and cover with about 800 ml of hot water. Bring to the boil and cook over a medium heat for 25-30 minutes until the lentils are tender to the bite.
  6. Pour the wholemeal pasta directly into the pot with the lentils, adding the remaining hot water if necessary so that the liquid is two fingers above the pasta.
  7. Cook the pasta for the time indicated on the packet, stirring frequently to activate the starches and achieve a naturally thick and creamy consistency.
  8. Adjust the salt only at the end of cooking to prevent the lentil skins from toughening, turning off the heat when the pasta is still slightly al dente.
  9. Allow the dish to rest for 2 minutes off the heat, adding the remaining extra virgin olive oil raw to emulsify the flavours, and serve hot and comforting.
