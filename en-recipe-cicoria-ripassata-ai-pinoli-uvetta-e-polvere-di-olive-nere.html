Sautéed chicory with pine nuts, sultanas and black olive powder

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Lazio · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

An autumn side dish that balances the bitterness of the chicory with the sweetness of the sultanas and the savoury note of the black olive powder.

Ingredients

  • Wild chicory800 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Sultanas30 g
  • Pine nuts25 g
  • Pitted black olives50 g
  • Fresh chilli1
  • Fine saltto taste

Allergens

Tree nuts

Method

  1. Trim the chicory by removing the tougher end of the stalk and wash thoroughly in cold water.
  2. Boil the chicory in plenty of salted boiling water for about 6 minutes, keeping it slightly al dente.
  3. Drain the vegetable and immediately plunge it into ice water to preserve its bright green colour and stop the cooking process.
  4. Soak the sultanas in lukewarm water for about 15 minutes, then squeeze them out and dry carefully.
  5. Prepare the olive powder: very finely chop the black olives with a knife and leave them to dry on kitchen paper.
  6. In a small non-stick frying pan, toast the pine nuts over a low heat without adding fat until golden.
  7. In a large frying pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil with the two crushed garlic cloves and the sliced chilli.
  8. Drain the chicory well, squeeze with your hands to remove excess water and add it to the pan.
  9. Sauté the chicory over a high heat for 5-7 minutes, adding the squeezed sultanas and toasted pine nuts halfway through.
  10. Remove the garlic, arrange on serving plates and finish each portion with a generous sprinkle of black olive powder.
