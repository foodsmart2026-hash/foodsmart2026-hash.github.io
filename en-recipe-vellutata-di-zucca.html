Creamy pumpkin soup

4 servings · 35 min · Easy

Sweet and comforting ginger-infused pumpkin soup.

A silky and comforting cream, perfect for autumn evenings thanks to the pungent aroma of fresh ginger. The sweetness of pumpkin and potatoes creates a balanced flavor, enhanced by the crunch of toasted seeds.

Ingredients

  • Pumpkin (Delica or Butternut, peeled weight)800 g
  • Potatoes150 g (1 medium)
  • Yellow onion100 g (1 medium)
  • Fresh ginger root15 g (1 piece)
  • Vegetable broth (containing celery)800 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 g
  • Hulled pumpkin seeds20 g (2 tbsp)
  • Fine salt4 g
  • Black pepperto taste

Allergens

Celery

Method

  1. Peel and dice the pumpkin and potato into even 2 cm cubes to ensure uniform cooking.
  2. Finely mince the onion and grate the peeled fresh ginger.
  3. In a large saucepan, heat 20 g of extra virgin olive oil and sauté the onion over low heat for 5 minutes until translucent.
  4. Add the grated ginger, pumpkin, and potato; season with salt and sauté over medium heat for 3-4 minutes, stirring frequently.
  5. Cover the vegetables with boiling vegetable broth and bring to a boil.
  6. Reduce the heat and simmer with a partially closed lid for about 20-25 minutes, or until the pumpkin and potato are very tender.
  7. Meanwhile, toast the pumpkin seeds in a non-stick pan without fat for 2 minutes until crunchy.
  8. Turn off the heat and blend the mixture with an immersion blender until it reaches a smooth and velvety texture.
  9. Emulsify the soup by whisking in the remaining 10 g of oil and adjust salt and pepper if necessary.
  10. Pour into bowls and garnish with the toasted pumpkin seeds before serving.

Tips

  • Blend the soup thoroughly with an immersion blender to achieve a perfectly smooth and velvety texture.
  • Toast the pumpkin seeds in a pan for a few minutes to release their full aroma and fragrance.
  • Leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator for 3 days or frozen in single portions for a quick meal.

Variations

  • Add a pinch of ground cinnamon during cooking to highlight the pumpkin's natural sweet notes.
  • You can replace fresh ginger with turmeric for a more earthy and spicy aromatic profile.
