Sautéed Swiss Chard with Sultanas and Toasted Pine Nuts

💰 Save Euro · about €1.18 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

A recipe that elevates Swiss chard, often considered less than exciting. The addition of sultanas and pine nuts recalls Italian Renaissance traditions, creating a bittersweet balance.

Ingredients

  • Swiss chard (large-ribbed variety)1 kg
  • Sultanas30 g
  • Pine nuts20 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • Dried chilli flakes1 pinch
  • Saltto taste

Allergens

Tree nuts

Method

  1. Separate the green leaves of the chard from the white ribs; cut the ribs into 2 cm pieces and roughly chop the leaves.
  2. Soak the sultanas in a glass of warm water for about 10 minutes to rehydrate and soften them.
  3. Boil the ribs first in salted water for 5 minutes, then add the leaves and cook everything together for another 3-4 minutes.
  4. Drain the chard well and squeeze it slightly to remove excess water, which would make the dish watery.
  5. In a pan, dry-toast the pine nuts for a minute until they are golden and fragrant, then set them aside.
  6. In the same pan, heat the oil with the chilli and add the boiled chard, the squeezed sultanas, and the toasted pine nuts.
  7. Sauté over a high heat for 5 minutes, allowing the vegetables to absorb the flavours and dry out well.
  8. Serve as a warm side dish, perfect for accompanying fried eggs or lean meat main courses.
