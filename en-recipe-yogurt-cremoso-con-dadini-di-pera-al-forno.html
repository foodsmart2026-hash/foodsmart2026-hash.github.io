Creamy Yogurt with Oven-Baked Pear Cubes

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

A classic pairing made safe by cooking the fruit until it is incredibly tender. Full-fat yogurt provides the necessary fats for children's growth in this age group.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free

Ingredients

  • Full-fat natural white yogurt400 g
  • Ripe Abate or Conference pears2 pcs
  • Water20 ml

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Wash the pears, peel them carefully, and remove the core and fibrous central parts.
  2. Cut the pears into small cubes of about half a centimetre, ensuring they are a safe size for the child.
  3. Place the pear cubes in a small saucepan with two tablespoons of water, cover, and cook over a low heat for about 10 minutes.
  4. The pear will be ready when it looks translucent and squashes easily with light pressure from a fork.
  5. Drain any remaining water and let the pear cubes cool completely at room temperature.
  6. In a bowl, stir the white yogurt to make it smooth and consistent.
  7. Add the cooked pear cubes to the yogurt and gently fold them in with a spoon.
  8. Serve the preparation in small cups, ensuring the fruit is well distributed in each portion.
