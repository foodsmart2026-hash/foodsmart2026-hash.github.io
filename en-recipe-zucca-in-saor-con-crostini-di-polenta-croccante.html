Pumpkin in saor with crispy polenta crostini

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Veneto · 4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

An autumnal twist on the classic Venetian 'saor': fried pumpkin marinated in a sweet and sour sauce with onions, pine nuts, and raisins, served on crispy polenta crostini.

Ingredients

  • Cleaned Mantua or Delica pumpkin600g
  • White onions400g
  • White wine vinegar100ml
  • Raisins40g
  • Pine nuts30g
  • Plain flour50g
  • Caster sugar10g
  • Cooked, firm polenta400g
  • Groundnut oil500ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil30ml
  • Salt and black pepperto taste

Allergens

Peanuts, Tree nuts, Gluten, Sulphites
May contain traces of: Sesame.

Method

  1. Prepare the pumpkin by removing the outer skin and seeds, then cut it into uniform slices about half a centimetre thick.
  2. Dredge the pumpkin slices in the plain flour, pressing lightly to ensure it adheres and shaking off any excess to achieve a very thin coating.
  3. Heat the groundnut oil in a deep pan and fry the pumpkin slices a few at a time until they are golden brown and tender.
  4. Drain the pumpkin on kitchen paper to remove excess oil and season immediately with salt.
  5. Thinly slice the onions into rounds and let them soften in a large pan with the extra virgin olive oil over a very low heat for about 15 minutes.
  6. Add the raisins (previously soaked in warm water and squeezed dry) and the toasted pine nuts to the now translucent onions.
  7. Pour in the white wine vinegar and the sugar, increase the heat slightly and leave to bubble for 2-3 minutes until the liquid has reduced slightly.
  8. In a ceramic or glass dish, arrange a layer of fried pumpkin, cover with a portion of the onion mixture, and continue layering until all the ingredients are used.
  9. Cover the dish and leave to marinate in a cool place or in the fridge for at least 12 hours (the saor requires resting time to balance the flavours).
  10. Before serving, cut the polenta into rectangles, grill them on a smoking hot griddle pan until crispy, and top with the pumpkin in saor at room temperature.
