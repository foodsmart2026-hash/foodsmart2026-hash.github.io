Baked Chickpea and Spinach Soft Bites

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Small vegetable bakes rich in iron and plant-based protein. The combination of chickpeas and spinach is a classic of 'cucina povera' (traditional Italian peasant cooking), here reimagined in a safe and attractive form for early childhood.

Ingredients

  • Pre-cooked chickpeas (rinsed)240 g
  • Fresh or frozen spinach100 g
  • Egg1 pcs
  • Breadcrumbs15 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil5 g

Allergens

Eggs, Gluten

Method

  1. Rinse the chickpeas carefully and remove the outer skins if possible (by rubbing them between your hands) for better digestibility.
  2. Cook the spinach in a pan with a tablespoon of water for a few minutes until wilted, then squeeze them very hard to remove liquid.
  3. Finely chop the spinach with a knife to avoid long fibres that are difficult for the child to manage.
  4. Blend the chickpeas until you get a thick paste, adding a tablespoon of warm water if necessary.
  5. In a bowl, combine the chickpea paste, chopped spinach, egg, and breadcrumbs.
  6. Mix everything together until you have a workable but soft dough.
  7. Create small flattened patties or fill muffin tins greased with a little oil.
  8. Cook in a preheated oven at 180°C for 12-15 minutes: they only need to firm up without creating a hard crust.
  9. Serve warm, checking that the inside is soft and easy to break apart with fingers.
