Soffritto

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • onion80 g
  • carrot80 g
  • celery60 g
  • extra virgin olive oil30 ml
  • salt1 pinch

Allergens

Celery
May contain traces of: Sulphites.

Method

  1. Peel the onion and carrot and wash the celery thoroughly.
  2. Remove the ends and any stringy parts from the celery.
  3. Finely chop all the ingredients, trying to achieve pieces of similar size.
  4. Pour the extra virgin olive oil into a saucepan and heat it over low heat.
  5. Add the onion, carrot, and celery, distributing them evenly on the bottom.
  6. Cook slowly for about 8–10 minutes, stirring periodically.
  7. When the vegetables are soft and the onion is translucent, add the pinch of salt.
  8. Use the soffritto as a base for meat sauce (ragù), risottos, legumes, soups, and numerous savory preparations.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes, and alternatives:

- Do not burn the onion: the soffritto must cook slowly.

- Cut the vegetables evenly to achieve uniform cooking.

- You can replace the onion with shallot or leek.

- For a more delicate version, reduce the amount of onion.

- You can prepare more soffritto and freeze it in small portions, using suitable containers.
