Pork Loin Scallopini with Milk and Green Peppercorns

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 12 min · Easy

Pork loin is one of the most affordable and versatile cuts, which in this recipe becomes incredibly creamy thanks to the milk. The green peppercorns provide a spicy yet delicate aroma, making the dish refined at a low cost.

Ingredients

  • Sliced pork loin500 g
  • Whole milk200 ml
  • Plain flour (Farina tipo 0)40 g
  • Butter20 g
  • Green peppercorns1 cucchiaio
  • Fine saltq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Lightly pound the pork loin slices with a meat tenderiser to make them thin and uniform, so they cook in just a few moments.
  2. Flour the slices one by one, pressing well, and shake off the excess flour to prevent the pan juices from burning.
  3. Melt the butter in a large non-stick frying pan and lay the meat down without overlapping the slices.
  4. Brown the meat over a medium-high heat for 2 minutes per side until lightly golden.
  5. Pour in the warm milk and add the green peppercorns, previously crushed to release their aroma.
  6. Cook over a low heat for 5-6 minutes until the milk transforms into a thick, velvety sauce.
  7. Check that the meat is tender and the sauce has the consistency of a light béchamel.
  8. Season with salt only at the end to avoid toughening the meat fibres and serve coated with the pan sauce.
