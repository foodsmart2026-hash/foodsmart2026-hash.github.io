Fluffy Carrot and Orange Cake

💰 Save Euro · about €0.51 per person

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 45 min · Hard

A vitamin-rich, colourful cake prepared with freshly grated carrots and orange juice. The lack of butter makes it ideal for a healthy breakfast or a light snack.

Ingredients

  • Peeled carrots300 g
  • Plain flour (extra-fine Italian '00' flour)200 g
  • Caster sugar100 g
  • Medium eggs2 pcs
  • Sunflower oil80 ml
  • Orange (juice and zest)1 pcs
  • Baking powder12 g

Allergens

Gluten, Eggs

Method

  1. Grate the carrots finely with a small-hole grater and dab them lightly with kitchen paper to remove excess water.
  2. Whisk the eggs with the sugar using an electric whisk for at least 5 minutes, until the mixture doubles in volume and becomes pale.
  3. Pour in the sunflower oil gradually and the filtered orange juice while continuing to whisk at low speed.
  4. Add the grated carrots and the orange zest, mixing gently with a spatula.
  5. Incorporate the sifted flour and baking powder, adding them in two stages so as not to deflate the air mass created with the eggs.
  6. Mix until you obtain a bright orange batter free of flour pockets.
  7. Pour the mixture into a previously oiled and floured 22 cm bundt tin or cake tin.
  8. Bake at 175°C for about 45 minutes; do not open the oven before 30 minutes to prevent the cake from sinking.
  9. Cool completely on a wire rack to allow the moisture from the carrots to distribute evenly.
