Sweet Little Risotto with Carrots and Red Split Lentils

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A complete meal disguised as a first course, where the red lentils melt away completely to create a thick, nutritious cream. The bright orange colour and the naturally sweet flavour of the carrot make it a firm favourite with children.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Baby rice or Originario rice (a small, round-grain Italian rice)160 g
  • Medium carrots200 g
  • Red split lentils60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml
  • Water600 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the carrots and grate them very finely with a fine grater to obtain an almost liquid pulp.
  2. Rinse the red split lentils thoroughly under running water until the water runs clear.
  3. In a saucepan, place the grated carrots and lentils with half the water and cook for 10 minutes over a low heat.
  4. When the lentils start to break down, add the rice and the rest of the hot water.
  5. Cook the rice, stirring often, like a traditional risotto, for about 15-18 minutes (baby rice should be very soft).
  6. If the liquid dries out too quickly, add more hot water a little at a time.
  7. The dish is ready when the lentils are no longer visible and the rice is well-cooked and creamy.
  8. Turn off the heat and stir in the raw extra virgin olive oil.
  9. Leave to cool slightly before serving; adults can add a pinch of salt and turmeric to their own plates.
