Potato Gnocchi with Carrot and Ginger Sauce

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 20 min · Medium

A velvety and slightly spicy sauce that transforms classic gnocchi into a modern and vibrant dish. The cost is negligible and the flavour is surprising.

Ingredients

  • Fresh potato gnocchi (Italian potato dumplings)800 g
  • Carrots400 g
  • Fresh ginger15 g
  • Whole milk100 ml
  • White onion1/2 pcs
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Sesame seeds1 cucchiaio
  • Saltq.b.

Allergens

Gluten, Milk, Sesame

Method

  1. Peel the carrots and cut them into thin rounds; roughly chop the onion.
  2. In a small saucepan, heat the oil and sauté the onion and carrots for a few minutes.
  3. Cover the carrots with water and cook for 15 minutes until they are very tender.
  4. Drain the carrots (keeping the water) and blend them with the milk and grated ginger until you get a smooth cream; if it's too thick, add some cooking water.
  5. Transfer the sauce to a large pan and season with salt.
  6. Cook the gnocchi in boiling salted water and drain them as soon as they rise to the surface (a sign they are ready).
  7. Place the gnocchi directly into the pan with the carrot cream and mix gently over a low heat.
  8. Serve in bowls, decorating with a pinch of sesame seeds to add a crunchy note.
