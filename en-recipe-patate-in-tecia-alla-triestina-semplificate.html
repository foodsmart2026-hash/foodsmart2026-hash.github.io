Simplified Trieste-Style 'Patate in Tecia' (Pan-Fried Potatoes)

💰 Save Euro · about €0.53 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 45 min · Medium

A budget-friendly version of the classic recipe from Trieste where potatoes are crushed and sautéed for a long time with plenty of onion until a delicious crust forms.

Ingredients

  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes800 g
  • Large golden onions2 pcs
  • Vegetable stock (or water)100 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil30 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Wash the potatoes and boil them whole with their skins in salted water for about 25 minutes or until they are tender but still firm.
  2. Drain them, let them cool enough to peel, and then cut them into thick slices or irregular chunks.
  3. In a non-stick frying pan (the 'tecia'), heat the oil and add the thinly sliced onions; braise them over low heat until they become dark and sweet.
  4. Add the potatoes to the pan and lightly crush them with a fork or wooden spoon while mixing them with the onions.
  5. Pour in a little stock or water to moisten and continue cooking over medium-high heat without stirring too often.
  6. Press the potatoes down firmly onto the bottom of the pan to brown them: a golden and tasty crust must form.
  7. Turn the potatoes in blocks to brown the other side as well, adding more liquid if you see they are tending to burn too quickly.
  8. The side dish is ready when the potatoes have almost become a single mass, creamy on the inside and crispy on the outside.
