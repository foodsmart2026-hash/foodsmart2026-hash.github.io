Saffron Risotto with Grated Courgettes

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 18 min · Medium

A creamy and colourful risotto where the grated courgettes melt into the rice during cooking. Economical and impressive thanks to powdered saffron.

Ingredients

  • Carnaroli or Arborio rice (typical Italian risotto rice)320 g
  • Medium courgettes2 pcs
  • Saffron1 bustina
  • Vegetable stock1.2 L
  • Shallot1 pcs
  • Butter20 g
  • Parmigiano Reggiano (aged hard cheese)40 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Prepare the vegetable stock and keep it boiling; dissolve the saffron in a small cup of stock taken from the total amount.
  2. Wash the courgettes and grate them with a large-hole grater, then set them aside.
  3. In a saucepan, sauté the finely chopped shallot with the oil until it becomes translucent.
  4. Add the rice and toast it for 2-3 minutes over a medium flame until the grains are hot and almost transparent at the edges.
  5. Add the grated courgettes and start pouring in the boiling stock one ladle at a time, stirring often to release the starch.
  6. Halfway through cooking, pour in the stock with the saffron to give the risotto its typical bright yellow colour.
  7. Continue cooking by adding stock every time the previous ladle is absorbed, until the rice is al dente.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the cold butter and the Parmigiano, then cover for 2 minutes before stirring vigorously to achieve the 'all'onda' (wavy) effect.
