Chickpea Farinata

Liguria · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

A thin, savoury chickpea flour flatbread with olive oil and rosemary, baked in the oven: vegan.

Ingredients

  • Chickpea flour250 g
  • Water750 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Fine salt6 g
  • Rosemary2 g
  • Black pepper0.5 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. In a large bowl, pour 250 g of chickpea flour and gradually add 750 ml of room temperature water, stirring vigorously with a hand whisk. It is essential to pour the water slowly to prevent annoying lumps from forming and to obtain a perfectly smooth and liquid batter. You will smell a typical nutty aroma of legumes starting to release even while raw.
  2. Let the mixture rest covered for at least 4 hours at room temperature, making sure to stir it occasionally if you can. During this phase, the flour will absorb the moisture and a light foam will form on the surface, which you must remove with a skimmer before proceeding. This resting time is the true secret to making the farinata digestible and giving it a silky texture.
  3. Once the resting time has passed, add 6 g of fine salt to the batter and give it a final firm stir to distribute it well. Ensure that the salt dissolves completely in the liquid to guarantee uniform saltiness in every slice of your preparation. You will notice that the mixture has become slightly denser and more homogeneous than at the beginning of the process.
  4. Preheat the oven in fan mode to a temperature of 250 °C, ensuring it is very hot before placing the pan inside. Position the rack in the highest part of the oven, as intense and immediate heat is necessary to form the iconic golden crust on the surface. A high temperature allows the water to evaporate quickly while keeping the interior soft.
  5. Pour the 60 ml of extra virgin olive oil into a round baking pan with a diameter of about 32 cm, preferably made of aluminum or copper for optimal heat conduction. Move the pan so that the oil uniformly covers the entire bottom and slightly climbs the sides, creating a protective layer. The oil serves not only to prevent sticking but also to lightly fry the base, making it irresistibly crunchy.
  6. Pour the chickpea batter into the center of the pan and, with a spoon, gently move the oil from the edges toward the center using circular motions. You should try to partially emulsify the oil with the batter without fully incorporating it until golden streaks appear on the surface. The ideal thickness of the liquid should be about 5 mm to ensure fast and even cooking.
  7. Immediately place the pan in the top part of the oven and bake for about 20-25 minutes at 250 °C without ever opening the door. The farinata will be ready when the surface appears a beautiful amber color, almost charred at the edges, and feels firm to the touch but still flexible. An intense, toasted aroma will spread through the kitchen, signaling that the crust has become perfect.
  8. Remove the farinata from the oven and immediately sprinkle it with 2 g of rosemary and 0.5 g of freshly ground black pepper to enhance its flavor. The residual heat will instantly release the essential oils of the herbs, enriching the dish with fresh and pungent aromatic notes. Cut it into irregular slices and serve very hot to best appreciate the contrast between the tender heart and the crunchy surface.
