Chickpea Farinata

Liguria · 4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Easy

A thin, savoury chickpea flour flatbread with olive oil and rosemary, baked in the oven: vegan.

Ingredients

  • Chickpea flour250 g
  • Water750 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Fine salt6 g
  • Rosemary2 g
  • Black pepper0.5 g

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Whisk the chickpea flour into the water until smooth and lump-free.
  2. Leave to rest for at least 4 hours, skimming off any foam from the surface.
  3. Add the salt and stir.
  4. Pour the oil into a large 32 cm baking tray and then the batter (about 5 mm deep), mixing it with the oil.
  5. Bake in a fan oven at 250 °C for 20-25 minutes, until the surface is golden brown.
  6. Finish with rosemary and pepper and serve hot.
