Carrot and Fennel Velouté with a Hint of Citrus

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.27 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 20 min · Easy

A very sweet and creamy side dish that makes the most of the natural sweetness of carrots and the freshness of fennel. Ideal for introducing little ones to delicate vegetable flavours without using salt.

Ingredients

  • Fresh carrots300 g
  • Fennel200 g
  • Strained orange juice30 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 g
  • Water250 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the carrots and fennel under running water to remove any soil residue.
  2. Peel the carrots with a vegetable peeler and remove the tougher outer parts of the fennel.
  3. Cut the vegetables into very small cubes, about half a centimetre, to facilitate even and rapid cooking.
  4. Place the vegetables in a small saucepan with the water and bring to the boil over medium heat.
  5. Cover with a lid and leave to cook for about 20 minutes until the carrots are very tender when pressed with a fork.
  6. Drain half of the cooking water and set aside, then blend the rest with a stick blender until a smooth cream is obtained.
  7. Add the strained orange juice to provide an aromatic note that replaces the savouriness of salt.
  8. If the consistency is too thick, add a tablespoon of the reserved cooking water.
  9. Finish with the raw extra virgin olive oil before serving warm to the family.
