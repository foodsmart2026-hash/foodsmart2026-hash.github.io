Chocolate cream

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 10 min · Easy

🥕 Vegetarian🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • whole milk500 ml
  • sugar100 g
  • egg yolks3
  • cornstarch35 g
  • dark chocolate100 g
  • unsweetened cocoa powder15 g
  • salt1 pinch

Allergens

Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Tree nuts, Gluten, Soy.

Method

  1. Pour the milk into a saucepan and heat it until it almost reaches a boil.
  2. In a bowl, whisk the egg yolks with the sugar and salt until you get a uniform mixture.
  3. Add the cornstarch and the sifted cocoa, stirring with a whisk to remove any lumps.
  4. Gradually pour the hot milk into the mixture, stirring constantly.
  5. Transfer everything back into the saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, stirring until the cream starts to thicken.
  6. Turn off the heat and add the chopped dark chocolate. Stir until completely melted.
  7. Transfer to a bowl, cover with plastic wrap in direct contact with the surface and cool quickly.
  8. Before using, whisk briefly to achieve a smooth and creamy consistency.

Chef's tips

Tips, mistakes and alternatives:

- Use high-quality chocolate: it directly influences the final taste.

- If the cream is too thick, add a little hot milk.

- For a more intense version, use 70% dark chocolate.

- For a milder cream, use milk chocolate and reduce the sugar.

- It is excellent for filling cakes, cream puffs, tarts and rolls.
