Creamy Potato and Leek Gratin

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 45 min · Hard

A rich side dish or a substantial main course that transforms humble vegetables into elegant comfort food. The golden crust and soft heart make it irresistible for the whole family.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌾 Gluten-free

Ingredients

  • yellow-fleshed potatoes800 g
  • leeks (white part only)2 pcs
  • cooking cream200 ml
  • whole milk100 ml
  • grated Gruyère or Emmental cheese100 g
  • garlic1 spicchio
  • butter20 g

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and slice them into very thin rounds (about 2-3 mm) using a mandoline for even cooking.
  2. Clean the leeks and cut into thin rounds, sweating them in a pan with a knob of butter and a tablespoon of water for 5 minutes until translucent.
  3. Rub a baking dish with the garlic clove cut in half and grease it generously with the remaining butter.
  4. Arrange a layer of potatoes on the bottom, salt lightly, and add some of the sautéed leeks.
  5. In a bowl, mix the cream with the milk, salt, and pepper, then pour a third of the mixture over the first layer.
  6. Continue layering potatoes and leeks until all ingredients are used, finishing with the liquid mixture.
  7. Cover the dish with foil and bake at 180°C for 30 minutes; the cover allows the potatoes to steam-cook without burning.
  8. Remove the foil, sprinkle with the grated cheese, and continue cooking for another 15 minutes until the surface is brown and crispy.
  9. Let the gratin rest out of the oven for 5 minutes before serving so the cream sets correctly.
