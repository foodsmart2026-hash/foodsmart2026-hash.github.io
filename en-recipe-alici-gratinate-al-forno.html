Baked gratin anchovies

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 12 min · Medium

A humble main dish very rich in Omega-3, typical of the Italian maritime tradition, made crispy with a wholemeal aromatic panure. The combination of oily fish and cherry tomatoes creates a perfect balance between savouriness and freshness, ideal for a healthy and affordable dinner.

Ingredients

  • Fresh anchovies (cleaned and butterflied)600 g
  • Wholemeal breadcrumbs100 g
  • Cherry tomatoes200 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Garlic clove1 pz
  • Fresh parsley20 g
  • Dried oregano2 g
  • Fine salt3 g
  • Lemon (grated zest)1 pz

Allergens

Fish, Gluten

Method

  1. Start by gently rinsing the cleaned anchovies under cold running water and pat them dry with kitchen paper to remove excess moisture, thus ensuring a crispy coating.
  2. Prepare the aromatic panure: in a large bowl combine the wholemeal breadcrumbs, finely chopped garlic (with the germ removed), chopped fresh parsley and grated lemon zest.
  3. Add 20 ml of extra virgin olive oil and the oregano to the panure, mixing with your hands or a fork until the mixture resembles wet sand, which is essential to prevent the bread from burning in the oven.
  4. Wash the cherry tomatoes and cut them into quarters; season them lightly with a pinch of salt and a drizzle of oil so they release some juice during cooking, creating a moist base.
  5. Line a large baking tray with greaseproof paper and grease the base with a tablespoon of oil; arrange the butterflied anchovies side by side, without overlapping them too much to allow even cooking.
  6. Distribute the cherry tomatoes over and between the anchovies, so that their flavour penetrates the flesh of the oily fish during the gratin process.
  7. Cover the fish uniformly with the previously prepared aromatic panure, pressing lightly with the back of a spoon so that it adheres well to the surface of the anchovies.
  8. Bake in a preheated conventional oven at 200°C for about 10-12 minutes; you will know they are ready when the top crust is golden and the tomatoes begin to soften.
  9. Remove from the oven and let rest for 2 minutes before serving, to stabilise the juices and allow the panure to become perfectly crispy to the bite.
