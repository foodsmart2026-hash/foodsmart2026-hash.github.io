Open lasagna with Swiss chard, walnuts and cannellini bean cream

🌿 Seasonal recipe

Toscana · 4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A comforting autumn first course where Swiss chard is the star, used both as a cream and sautéed, paired with the sweetness of pulses and the crunchiness of walnuts.

Ingredients

  • Fresh Swiss chard800g
  • Egg lasagna sheets250g
  • Pre-cooked cannellini beans300g
  • Walnut halves60g
  • Grated Pecorino Toscano PDO100g
  • Whole milk200ml
  • Shallot1
  • Extra virgin olive oilas required
  • Saltas required
  • Black pepperas required
  • Nutmeg1 pinch

Allergens

Tree nuts, Gluten, Milk, Eggs
May contain traces of: Celery.

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the Swiss chard, using a knife to separate the white stalks from the green leaves.
  2. Boil the white stalks in plenty of salted water for about 10 minutes until tender.
  3. Drain the stalks and blend them with the cannellini beans, a drizzle of oil, salt and a pinch of nutmeg until you have a smooth, thick cream.
  4. Roughly chop the green leaves and sauté them in a large frying pan with the finely chopped shallot and a drizzle of oil for about 6-8 minutes.
  5. In a small saucepan, heat the milk without bringing it to the boil, add the grated pecorino and whisk until it forms a smooth fondue.
  6. Toast the walnut halves in a non-stick frying pan for 2 minutes, then roughly chop them with a knife.
  7. Boil the pasta sheets in salted boiling water for the time indicated on the packet, drain them and lay them out on a clean tea towel without overlapping.
  8. Assemble the lasagna directly on individual plates or in a baking dish: spread a thin layer of the chard stalk and cannellini cream on the bottom.
  9. Place a first sheet of pasta, cover with more cream, some of the sautéed chard leaves, the chopped walnuts and a little pecorino fondue.
  10. Repeat the layers 3 times and finish with a drizzle of oil, black pepper and the remaining walnuts before placing in the oven at 200°C for 5 minutes to harmonise the flavours.
