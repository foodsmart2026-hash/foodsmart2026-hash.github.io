Soft Cauliflower Hearts with Cows' Milk Ricotta

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

White cauliflower becomes creamy and delicate when paired with fresh ricotta. An excellent way to introduce cruciferous vegetables with a safe texture.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🌾 Gluten-free🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • White cauliflower400 g
  • Fresh cows' milk ricotta (soft Italian whey cheese)80 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil10 g
  • Nutmeg1 pinch

Allergens

Milk

Method

  1. Divide the cauliflower into small florets, removing the tougher, fibrous stems.
  2. Wash the florets under running water and place them in a pot with boiling (unsalted) water.
  3. Boil the cauliflower for about 15 minutes; it must be much softer than 'al dente'.
  4. Drain the cauliflower and transfer it to a shallow bowl or dish.
  5. Mash the florets with a fork until reduced to small soft pieces or almost a pulp.
  6. Add the well-drained cows' milk ricotta and a pinch of nutmeg for aroma.
  7. Mix well until the ricotta is completely blended with the warm vegetables.
  8. Drizzle in the extra virgin olive oil and stir one last time.
  9. Check that the temperature is lukewarm and the consistency is uniform before serving.
