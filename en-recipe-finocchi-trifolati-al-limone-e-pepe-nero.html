Sautéed Fennel with Lemon and Black Pepper

💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 18 min · Easy

When cooked in a pan, fennel loses its overpowering aniseed note and becomes sweet and buttery. The final addition of black pepper and lemon makes it a refined side dish at zero cost.

Ingredients

  • Fennel800 g
  • Lemon (juice)20 ml
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Parsley5 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Clean the fennel by removing the green fronds (keep them for garnish) and the tougher, fibrous outer layers.
  2. Cut the fennel into quarters and then into slices about 1 cm thick, trying to keep the piece of the core that holds the leaves together.
  3. Sauté the unpeeled garlic clove (in its skin) in a pan with the oil until it releases its aroma, then remove it if you prefer a milder taste.
  4. Arrange the fennel in a single layer if possible and brown over a high heat for 3-4 minutes until they appear golden.
  5. Add half a glass of water, season with salt, cover, and cook for about 10-12 minutes over a medium heat.
  6. When the water has evaporated and the fennel is tender, remove the lid and deglaze with the lemon juice.
  7. Turn up the heat for a minute to glaze the fennel with the remaining lemon juice at the bottom of the pan.
  8. Turn off the heat and add plenty of freshly ground black pepper and the chopped parsley before serving.
