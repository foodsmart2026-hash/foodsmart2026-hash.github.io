Roasted potatoes and onions

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A classic Mediterranean diet side dish that enhances the sweetness of red onions and the crispness of yellow-fleshed potatoes. Thanks to a brief initial parboil and the use of wholemeal breadcrumbs, a tasty dish with optimal plant-based fat distribution is achieved.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🌱 Vegan🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

Ingredients

  • Yellow-fleshed potatoes800 g
  • Red onions400 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Wholemeal breadcrumbs30 g
  • Fresh rosemary1 sprig
  • Dried oregano1 tsp
  • Iodised salt5 g
  • Ground black pepper2 g

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Carefully wash the potatoes to remove any soil residue, peel them and cut them into regular slices about 5 millimetres thick to ensure even cooking.
  2. Bring a pot of lightly salted water to the boil and parboil the potato slices for just 4 minutes; this step softens the fibres and allows for impeccable external browning in the oven.
  3. Gently drain the potatoes and let them dry on a clean tea towel to remove moisture, an essential factor to avoid a boiled effect during subsequent cooking.
  4. While the potatoes rest, clean the red onions by removing the tough outer layer and slice them into rounds about 1 centimetre thick.
  5. In a large bowl, combine the warm potatoes and onions, seasoning them with extra virgin olive oil, salt and pepper, massaging them gently so that every slice is evenly oiled.
  6. Add the finely chopped rosemary, oregano and wholemeal breadcrumbs, shaking the bowl so the panure adheres to the vegetables, creating a protective barrier against excessive heat.
  7. Line a large baking sheet with greaseproof paper and distribute the vegetables, trying not to overlap them, thus ensuring the circulation of hot air necessary for crispiness.
  8. Bake in a preheated conventional oven at 200°C for 30 minutes; the signal for the halfway point is the intense aroma of the onions starting to soften and release their sugars.
  9. Activate the grill function for the last 5-7 minutes until the surface is well browned and the edges of the potatoes are visibly crispy and golden.
