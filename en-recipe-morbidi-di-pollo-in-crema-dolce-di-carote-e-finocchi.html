Tender Chicken in Sweet Carrot and Fennel Cream

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

A nutritious and velvety main course where the chicken is finely chopped and folded into a naturally sweet vegetable cream. The texture is ideal for young children, ensuring hydration and ease of chewing without the use of salt.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast250 g
  • Carrots150 g
  • Fennel (tender heart)150 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 g
  • Water100 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the carrots and fennel, peeling the carrots with a vegetable peeler and removing the tougher, fibrous outer leaves of the fennel.
  2. Cut the vegetables into very small cubes (about 1 cm) to speed up the cooking process and ensure they cook evenly.
  3. Steam the vegetables or cook them in a saucepan with the specified amount of water for about 18-20 minutes, until they are tender enough to fall apart when pressed with a spoon.
  4. Use a stick blender to purée the cooked vegetables into a smooth, thick cream; if it's too dry, add a tablespoon of hot water.
  5. Prepare the chicken by finely hand-chopping it into tiny pieces, similar to shreds or cubes of no more than 5 millimetres, suitable for a child's mouth.
  6. In a non-stick pan, combine the vegetable cream and extra virgin olive oil, bringing it to a light simmer over a very low heat.
  7. Add the chicken to the cream, cover with a lid and cook for 10 minutes: the meat should cook through indirect heat, remaining moist and very white.
  8. Check it is cooked by mashing a piece of chicken with a fork: if it breaks apart without resistance, the dish is ready to be served warm.
