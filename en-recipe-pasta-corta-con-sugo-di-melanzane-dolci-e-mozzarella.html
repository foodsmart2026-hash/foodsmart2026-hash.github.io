Short Pasta with Sweet Aubergine Sauce and Mozzarella

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

By using only the flesh of the aubergine and peeling it carefully, you obtain a soft sauce free from bitter parts or pieces that are difficult to chew.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro🥕 Vegetarian🧒 Kid-friendly🇮🇹 Italian

Ingredients

  • Mezze penne or small fusilli160 g
  • Medium aubergine1 pc
  • Sweet tomato passata (sieved tomatoes)150 ml
  • Pizza mozzarella (low-moisture variety)60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml

Allergens

Gluten, Milk

Method

  1. Completely peel the aubergine, removing all the dark skin.
  2. Cut the flesh into tiny cubes (about 5 mm).
  3. In a non-stick pan, stew the aubergine with the tomato passata and half a glass of water for 15 minutes over a low heat.
  4. The aubergines should become a pulp almost indistinguishable from the tomato.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in plenty of unsalted water.
  6. Cut the mozzarella into very small cubes or grate it.
  7. Drain the pasta and toss it in the pan with the aubergine sauce for one minute.
  8. Turn off the heat, add the mozzarella, and cover for 30 seconds until it strings slightly.
  9. Season with raw oil and serve, ensuring the mozzarella pieces are not too large for the child.
