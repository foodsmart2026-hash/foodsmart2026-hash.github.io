Mediterranean-style Potato and Pea Curry

💰 Save Euro · about €1.13 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 30 min · Medium

A simplified version of Indian Aloo Matar, adapted with ingredients easily found in any European supermarket. A nutritious and spicy one-pot meal.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes600 g
  • Frozen peas300 g
  • Tomato pulp (tinned crushed tomatoes)200 g
  • Brown onion1 pcs
  • Fresh ginger10 g
  • Curry powder10 g
  • Coconut milk (canned)200 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into cubes of about 2 cm for quick and even cooking.
  2. Sauté the chopped onion with the grated ginger and curry powder until the aroma is intensely released.
  3. Add the potatoes to the pan and let them absorb the spices for 3 minutes, stirring continuously.
  4. Pour in the tomato pulp and a glass of water, cover, and cook over medium heat for 15 minutes.
  5. Add the peas while still frozen (they will keep their bright green colour better) and continue cooking for another 5 minutes.
  6. Pour in the coconut milk to add creaminess and balance the spiciness of the curry.
  7. Let the sauce reduce over gentle heat for 5 minutes until the potatoes are tender but not falling apart.
  8. Turn off the heat when the consistency is thick and coating, almost creamy.
  9. Serve with basmati rice or toasted bread to complete the amino acid profile.
