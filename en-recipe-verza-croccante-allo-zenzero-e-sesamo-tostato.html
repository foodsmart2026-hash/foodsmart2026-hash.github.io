Crunchy Savoy Cabbage with Ginger and Toasted Sesame

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 8 min · Easy

An Asian-inspired side dish where Savoy cabbage is quickly stir-fried at high temperatures to preserve its texture and vibrant flavour.

Ingredients

  • Savoy cabbage500 g
  • Fresh ginger (root)20 g
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • White sesame seeds1 tbsp
  • Groundnut oil3 tbsp
  • Fresh chilli1/2
  • Sesame oil (optional)1 tsp

Allergens

Peanuts, Sesame, Soy

Method

  1. Shred the cabbage into strips about 1.5 cm wide after removing any tough white cores.
  2. Peel the ginger and cut it into very fine matchsticks or grate it finely.
  3. Deseed and finely chop the chilli.
  4. Heat the oil in a wok or a very large non-stick frying pan until it is almost smoking.
  5. Add the ginger and chilli, stir-frying for just 30 seconds to release the aromas without burning them.
  6. Toss the cabbage into the pan and stir-fry over a high heat for 5 minutes, moving it constantly with tongs or by tossing the pan.
  7. When the cabbage begins to wilt but still retains its bite, deglaze with the soy sauce and sesame oil.
  8. Cook for one more minute, turn off the heat and serve immediately, sprinkling with the sesame seeds previously toasted in a small dry pan.
