Ricotta and Banana Mousse with Spelt Flakes

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.41 per person

4 servings · Prep 25 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

A quick, no-cook cold breakfast, rich in calcium and potassium. The spelt flakes, left to rest in the cream, soften and become perfect for little ones to chew.

Ingredients

  • Fresh cow's milk ricotta (a creamy Italian whey cheese)200 g
  • Ripe banana1 pcs
  • Small wholegrain spelt flakes40 g
  • Orange juice10 ml

Allergens

Milk, Gluten

Method

  1. Peel the banana and mash it thoroughly in a bowl with the tablespoon of orange juice to prevent it from browning.
  2. Add the ricotta to the banana purée and whisk with an electric whisk or by hand for 2 minutes, until it becomes a light mousse.
  3. Fold the spelt flakes into the ricotta and banana mixture and stir with a spoon.
  4. Leave the mousse to rest in the fridge for at least 15-20 minutes: this step is essential as the flakes need to absorb the moisture from the ricotta to become tender.
  5. Take the mousse out of the fridge 10 minutes before serving so it is not too cold for the child.
  6. Stir the mousse again before portioning to restore its creaminess.
  7. Check that the spelt flakes are nice and soft by squashing one between your fingers.
  8. Serve in small bowls, optionally decorating with another pinch of flakes left whole for the adult portions only.
