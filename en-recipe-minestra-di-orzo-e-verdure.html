Pearl barley and vegetable soup

💰 Save Euro · about €1.36 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 40 min · Medium

A nutritious and satiating main course that combines the fibre of pearl barley with the plant-based proteins of pulses. This affordable preparation uses the staple vegetables of the Mediterranean diet for a balanced and warming meal.

Ingredients

  • Pearl barley240 g
  • Cooked cannellini beans240 g
  • Carrots150 g
  • Celery100 g
  • Golden onion100 g
  • Potatoes200 g
  • Fresh chard200 g
  • Water or vegetable stock1500 ml
  • Extra virgin olive oil40 ml
  • Iodised salt5 g
  • Fresh rosemary1 pz

Allergens

Gluten, Celery

Method

  1. Thoroughly wash the pearl barley under cold running water using a fine-mesh sieve to remove impurities and excess starch until the water runs clear.
  2. Prepare a regular fine dice (brunoise) with the carrot, celery and onion; heat 20 ml of extra virgin olive oil in a large pot and sweat the vegetables over low heat for about 7 minutes until the onion becomes translucent.
  3. Peel the potato and cut into cubes of about 1 cm, adding them to the sautéed base along with the whole rosemary sprig to release the essential oils without scattering the needles.
  4. Pour the drained barley into the pot and let it toast for 2 minutes, stirring continuously; this technique allows the grain to maintain a pleasant bite after cooking.
  5. Add the water or hot vegetable stock, salt moderately and bring to the boil; once boiling, lower the heat and partially cover with a lid.
  6. Continue cooking for 25-30 minutes, checking the consistency of the barley: it should be tender but springy, while the potatoes will begin to break down, creating a natural creaminess.
  7. Add the cannellini beans (previously rinsed of their canning liquid) and the chard cut into strips, cooking for another 5 minutes so the flavours integrate and the leafy greens wilt gently.
  8. Turn off the heat, remove the rosemary sprig and let the soup rest covered for 3-5 minutes to stabilise the temperature and harmonise the aromatic profile.
  9. Serve the soup in individual bowls and finish each portion with 5 ml of raw extra virgin olive oil and, if desired, a pinch of freshly ground black pepper.
