Steamed Swiss chard rolls with a mushroom and smoked tofu filling

🌿 Seasonal recipe

4 servings · Prep 30 min · Cooking 15 min · Medium

Large Swiss chard leaves enclosing a savoury, protein-rich filling, gently steamed to preserve colours and nutrients.

Ingredients

  • Very large Swiss chard leaves8
  • Smoked tofu250g
  • Finely chopped button mushrooms200g
  • Shallot1
  • Nutritional yeast flakes2 tbsp
  • Soy sauce (Tamari)1 tbsp
  • Sunflower oil2 tbsp
  • Grated fresh ginger1 tsp

Allergens

Soy
May contain traces of: Gluten.

Method

  1. Wash the Swiss chard leaves and blanch them whole in boiling salted water for just 30 seconds, then plunge them into iced water to stop the cooking.
  2. Lay the leaves out on a flat surface and use a small knife to remove the bottom part of the central rib if it is too thick, taking care not to puncture the leaf.
  3. In a frying pan, sauté the chopped shallot with the oil and the grated ginger.
  4. Add the chopped mushrooms and cook until they have released all their liquid.
  5. Crumble the smoked tofu with your hands or a fork and add it to the mushrooms in the pan.
  6. Season with the soy sauce and nutritional yeast, stirring well to combine the flavours.
  7. Place two tablespoons of the filling in the centre of each opened Swiss chard leaf.
  8. Fold the sides of the leaf inwards and roll from the bottom upwards to form a tightly sealed parcel.
  9. Arrange the rolls in a steamer basket lined with perforated baking parchment and cook for 10 minutes.
  10. Serve hot, optionally accompanied by a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil or extra soy sauce on the side.
