Shredded Chicken with Pea Velouté

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Chicken becomes very easy to eat when shredded and served on a bed of sweet peas. A colourful recipe rich in vitamins, ideal for a balanced meal.

🌿 Seasonal recipe💰 Save Euro⬇️ Big saving🌾 Gluten-free🐄 Dairy-free🥛 Lactose-free

A light and delicate dish, perfect for a nutritious dinner combining lean protein with the natural sweetness of legumes. The silky cream base pairs with the tender shredded chicken for a balanced comfort food experience.

Ingredients

  • Whole chicken breast200 g
  • Fine frozen peas200 g
  • White onion (small)1/4 pc
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml
  • Waterq.b.

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Start by bringing a large pot of unsalted water to the boil to cook your 200g whole chicken breast. Submerge the meat completely and lower the heat so the water simmers gently without boiling vigorously, cooking for about 20 minutes. Keep the chicken constantly covered by the liquid to prevent the surface from drying out. You will obtain uniformly cooked meat while retaining the internal juices that keep it tender. My advice is to avoid adding salt at this stage to prevent the muscle fibres from toughening excessively.
  2. While the chicken is cooking, prepare the aromatic base by finely chopping 1/4 of a white onion with a sharp knife. Transfer the onion to a non-stick saucepan with a tablespoon of water, letting it soften over a very low heat for about 4 minutes. You should achieve a transparent and soft onion, avoiding any browning which would make the cream bitter. This water-stewing method keeps the dish light and highly digestible. Remember that a well-stewed onion is the secret to an impeccable natural sweetness.
  3. Add the 200g of fine frozen peas to the softened onion and cover with a glass of water (approx. 200ml), bringing it to a moderate simmer. Cover with a lid and cook for about 15 minutes, checking that the liquid does not evaporate completely. The peas should be tender when pressed but retain a vibrant green colour and a fresh scent. If necessary, add a splash of water during cooking to maintain moisture. The secret to a bright colour is avoiding prolonged cooking at high heat which would oxidise the chlorophyll.
  4. Remove the chicken breast from the water with a slotted spoon and let it rest for a minute on a clean chopping board. While it is still very warm, use two forks to shred the meat along the grain, obtaining thin and short strands of about 2-3 centimetres. This task must be done precisely to ensure every bite is soft and easy to swallow, especially if served to children or the elderly. The warm meat will separate effortlessly, revealing an almost velvety texture. Do not wait for it to cool down, otherwise the fibres will become rigid and difficult to work with.
  5. Transfer the cooked peas and their cooking liquid into a mixer or use an immersion blender directly in the pan. Blend at maximum power for at least 2 minutes, using circular motions to incorporate air and make the mixture fluffy. The result should be a perfectly smooth, thick cream with an intense emerald green colour. If the consistency appears too grainy, add a tablespoon of the chicken cooking water. A well-blended cream is the perfect base to host the shredded meat.
  6. For a superior texture, pass the pea cream through a fine-mesh sieve, using the back of a spoon to filter the mixture. This step allows you to remove the outer skins of the legumes, making the cream silky and reducing the risk of bloating. Collect all the results in a clean saucepan, discarding the fibrous part left in the sieve. You will notice the cream becomes glossy and homogeneous, ready for the final touch. Do not skip this step if you want a result worthy of a top chef.
  7. Now add the shredded chicken to the sieved pea cream and place the pan over a very gentle heat. Stir carefully for about 2 minutes so that the heat is distributed evenly and the meat moistens with the cream. The meat should literally embrace the cream, absorbing its sweet flavour without falling apart further. You will obtain a harmonious mixture where the protein and vegetable components are perfectly balanced. Be careful not to let it boil to avoid ruining the delicacy of the preparation.
  8. Turn off the heat and add the 15ml of extra virgin olive oil by drizzling it over the hot preparation. Stir vigorously one last time to create an emulsion that will glaze the dish and enhance its aromas. Adding the oil cold is essential to preserve all its nutritional properties and fruity flavour. Serve the shredded chicken warm in small shallow bowls, decorating if you prefer with a pinch of pepper or fresh herbs. The final touch of cold oil provides that note of freshness that balances the sweetness of the peas.

Tips

  • Shred the chicken while still warm for finer and more regular fibers.
  • Use a fine-mesh sieve for the cream if you want a smooth, restaurant-quality result.
  • Store the cream separately from the chicken in the fridge for up to two days.

Variations

  • Replace the onion with leek for an even sweeter and more aromatic flavor.
  • Add fresh mint leaves to the pea cream for a refreshing note.
