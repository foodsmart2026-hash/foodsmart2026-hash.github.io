Shredded Chicken with Pea Velouté

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.74 per person

4 servings · Prep 10 min · Cooking 25 min · Medium

Chicken becomes very easy to eat when shredded and served on a bed of sweet peas. A colourful recipe rich in vitamins, ideal for a balanced meal.

Ingredients

  • Whole chicken breast200 g
  • Fine frozen peas200 g
  • White onion (small)1/4 pc
  • Extra virgin olive oil15 ml
  • Waterq.b.

Allergens

None of the 14 main allergens in the ingredients.

Method

  1. Boil the chicken breast in boiling water for about 20 minutes; it must be well cooked but not dry.
  2. In another small pot, cook the peas with the very finely chopped onion and a glass of water for 15 minutes.
  3. Drain the chicken and, while still hot, shred it using two forks to obtain thin, short fibres.
  4. Blend the peas (removing any large onion pieces) until you get a smooth, bright green cream.
  5. Pass the pea cream through a fine-mesh sieve if you want to completely remove the outer skins for younger children.
  6. Combine the shredded chicken with the pea velouté so that the meat is well moistened.
  7. Cook everything together for another 2 minutes to even out the temperatures and flavours.
  8. Season with the cold extra virgin olive oil and serve warm.
