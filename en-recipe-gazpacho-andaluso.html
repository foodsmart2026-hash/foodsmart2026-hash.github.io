Andalusian Gazpacho

Spain · 4 servings · Prep 20 min · Cooking 0 min · Easy

A cold Andalusian soup made with ripe tomatoes, green pepper, cucumber and bread: vegan.

Ingredients

  • Ripe tomatoes1000 g
  • Green pepper100 g
  • Cucumber150 g
  • Onion40 g
  • Garlic3 g
  • Stale bread60 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil60 ml
  • Sherry vinegar20 ml
  • Cold water150 ml
  • Fine salt6 g

Allergens

Gluten, Sulphites
May contain traces of: Sesame, Soy.

Method

  1. Begin by placing 60 g of stale bread in a small bowl and cover it with 150 ml of cold water, letting it soak for about 10 minutes. The bread must become completely soft so it can later blend perfectly with the other ingredients, providing the right body to the soup.
  2. While the bread rests, thoroughly wash 1000 g of ripe tomatoes and cut them into large chunks. Ensure the tomatoes are very red and juicy, as they determine the color base and the natural sweetness of your gazpacho.
  3. Prepare the other vegetables by peeling 150 g of cucumber and deseeding 100 g of green pepper, then cutting both into cubes. The green pepper provides a distinctive herbal note, while the cucumber brings that watery freshness essential for the dish's balance.
  4. Proceed by chopping 40 g of onion and 3 g of garlic, making sure to remove the germ from the garlic if you prefer a milder flavor. These aromatics create the savory foundation of the recipe; you will immediately smell a pungent yet harmonious aroma.
  5. Place the prepared tomatoes, pepper, cucumber, onion, and garlic into a blender. Add the bread (thoroughly squeezed to remove excess water), 6 g of fine salt, and 20 ml of Sherry vinegar, starting to blend at medium intensity until the mixture appears homogeneous.
  6. Now, with the blender still running, slowly drizzle in 60 ml of extra-virgin olive oil for about one minute. You will see the soup change color, turning toward a vibrant orange and taking on a velvety texture thanks to the emulsion of fats with the vegetable juices.
  7. To achieve a truly refined texture worthy of a professional, filter the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve, pressing down well with a spoon. This step serves to remove any leftover skins or seeds, making the gazpacho incredibly smooth on the palate.
  8. Place the cream in a glass container and let it rest in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours before consuming. Cold is essential not only for the serving temperature but also to allow the flavors to stabilize and fully meld together.
  9. Serve the gazpacho ice-cold in individual bowls, checking that the consistency is fluid yet full-bodied. Upon the first taste, you should perceive the brightness of the vinegar and the sweetness of the tomato in a perfect balance that immediately refreshes the palate.
