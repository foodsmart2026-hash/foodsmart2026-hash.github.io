Maccheroncini with Cream of Cannellini Beans and Sweet Fennel

Kid-friendly💰 Save Euro · about €0.46 per person

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cooking 15 min · Easy

Cannellini beans are the most delicate of pulses. Combined with cooked fennel, they create a fragrant and digestible cream.

Ingredients

  • Short pasta like maccheroncini (small macaroni)160 g
  • Cooked cannellini beans (canned or jarred)200 g
  • Large fennel bulb1 pc
  • Extra virgin olive oil20 ml
  • Fennel seeds (optional)1 pinch

Allergens

Gluten

Method

  1. Clean the fennel by removing the tough outer parts and slice it very thinly.
  2. Place the fennel in a small pan with a little water and cook until translucent and tender (about 12 minutes).
  3. Rinse the cannellini beans well under running water.
  4. Add the beans to the cooked fennel and blend everything with an immersion blender until a fluffy cream is formed.
  5. Pass the cream through a coarse sieve to remove any residual fennel fibres.
  6. Cook the maccheroncini in boiling unsalted water.
  7. Drain the pasta and season it with the cannellini and fennel cream.
  8. Add the raw extra virgin oil to complete the dish.
  9. Serve, ensuring the cream coats every piece of pasta well to make chewing easier.
